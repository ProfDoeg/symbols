SCRIPT. The Phoenician alphabet: writing system 4 of 17 in this set. Give at the top the type, the inventory, the direction, the period,
the region, the parent script and the daughters.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the 22 consonants, right to left, the Ahiram sarcophagus (c. 1000 BCE), the Byblos inscriptions, the Mesha stele, the Kilamuwa stele, the Carthaginian Punic and Neo-Punic; the mother of Greek, Aramaic, Hebrew, Arabic and, by descent, of Latin and Cyrillic; Herodotus on Cadmus and the phoinikeia grammata (5.58); the Ugaritic and Proto-Canaanite order; the letter names and their meanings; Naveh's early history.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented artefact or text, scholarly
reconstruction, tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention. Where a thread above names a
dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Give the sign table in Unicode where the
script is encoded. Absence of evidence is a finding.
