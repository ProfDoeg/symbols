SYSTEM. Hebrew gematria: the standard method (mispar hechrechi): gematria system 3 of 12 in this set. Give at the top the script, the period, the region and the table of
values.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the table aleph 1 to tav 400 and the five finals (with the extended values to 900 in mispar gadol), the Talmudic and midrashic uses (Eliezer 318 in Nedarim 32a and Rashi on Gen 14:14; the 'satan' 364 of Yoma 20a; Rabbi Eliezer's 32 rules of Aggadic interpretation, gematria as the 29th), the Sefer Yetzirah's letters, the classic equivalences (ahavah 13 = echad 13, YHVH 26; nachash 358 = mashiach 358; yayin 70 = sod 70, 'when the wine goes in the secret comes out'), the Zohar's uses, the Baal HaTurim's commentary on the Torah (1300s) as the systematic monument, Abulafia's tzeruf, the Hasidic and Lubavitch uses, the modern Torah-codes controversy (Witztum-Rips 1994 and the refutation by McKay et al. 1999) as gematria's cousin; Scholem and Idel on its history; the word's own etymology (Greek geometria or grammateia).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention. Show the arithmetic of every worked example.
Where a system claims antiquity it does not have (the 'Pythagorean' and 'Chaldean' name-numbers,
the modern English gematrias), say when it was actually invented and by whom. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
