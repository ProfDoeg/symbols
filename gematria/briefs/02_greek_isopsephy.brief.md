SYSTEM. Greek isopsephy: gematria system 2 of 12 in this set. Give at the top the script, the period, the region and the table of
values.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the alphabetic numerals as the base; the Hellenistic and Roman evidence: the Pompeii graffiti ('I love her whose number is 545', CIL IV 4839), Leonides of Alexandria's isopsephic epigrams for Nero, the Smyrna inscriptions, Lucian's Alexander the false prophet and his 'Glycon' (3663), Artemidorus's dream-book; Revelation 13:18 and the 666 (and 616) as Nero Caesar in Hebrew letters (Irenaeus V.30, the Oxyrhynchus 616), the Sibylline Oracles' 888 for Jesus (I.324-330), Irenaeus I.14-16 on Marcus the Magician (Jesus = 888, the dove = 801 = alpha + omega, the ogdoad of letters, the Aletheia body), the Pistis Sophia's numbers, the magical papyri (the 9999 and the seven vowels), Dornseiff 1922 and Barry 1999; the Byzantine and modern Greek survivals.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention. Show the arithmetic of every worked example.
Where a system claims antiquity it does not have (the 'Pythagorean' and 'Chaldean' name-numbers,
the modern English gematrias), say when it was actually invented and by whom. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
