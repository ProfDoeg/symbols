STANCE. Legend as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented text, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, disputed, legend, modern invention. Show the arithmetic of every worked example.

the modern English gematrias), say when it was actually invented and by whom. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
