HERO. Sinuhe (Egyptian (the tales) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the courtier who fled Egypt at Amenemhat I's death, the years in Retjenu, the duel with the champion, the letter of Senusret I and the return to die in Egypt (the Middle Kingdom's masterpiece, Berlin Papyrus 3022, the Ashmolean ostracon), Parkinson's edition, Waltari's Sinuhe the Egyptian (1945) and the film.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Middle Kingdom tales (Sinuhe, the Shipwrecked Sailor, the Eloquent Peasant), the Westcar Papyrus, the Tale of Two Brothers (Papyrus d'Orbiney), Wenamun (Papyrus Pushkin 120), the Setne Khamwas stories (the demotic papyri), Herodotus II, Manetho; Lichtheim's Ancient Egyptian Literature, Simpson's The Literature of Ancient Egypt, Parkinson's The Tale of Sinuhe, Ritner on Setne, the Khaemwaset monuments at Saqqara.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
