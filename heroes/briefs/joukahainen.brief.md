HERO. Joukahainen (Finnic (the Kalevala) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the young Lapp who challenged Väinämöinen in a singing contest and was sung into the swamp, the promise of his sister Aino, the revenge shot from the bushes that sent Väinämöinen into the sea (the beginning of the Pohjola journey), the Kalevala's runes 3 and 6.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Kalevala (Lönnrot 1835 and 1849; Bosley's translation) and the SKVR runes behind it (Suomen Kansan Vanhat Runot), the Kanteletar, Ganander's Mythologia Fennica, Castrén; Haavio's Väinämöinen: Eternal Sage, Siikala, Pentikäinen's Kalevala Mythology, Honko, Kaukonen on Lönnrot's method, Sibelius and Gallen-Kallela as reception, the Kalevala's role in Finnish nationhood.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
