HERO. Aslaug (Kráka) (Norse and Germanic heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the daughter of Sigurd and Brynhild hidden in a harp by Heimir, the peasant's foster-child who answered Ragnar's riddle (neither dressed nor undressed), the wife of Ragnar and mother of Ivar, the Aslaug of the TV series as reception.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Poetic Edda (the heroic poems), the Völsunga saga, the fornaldarsögur (Hervarar saga, Ragnars saga, Gautreks saga), Saxo Grammaticus, Beowulf (Klaeber's edition, Tolkien's essay) and Grettis saga, the Nibelungenlied and the Thidrekssaga, the Egils saga, the Icelandic family sagas; Simek, Turville-Petre, Andersson's The Legend of Brynhild, Tolkien, Orchard's Critical Companion to Beowulf, Rowe on the fornaldarsögur, the Skaldic Project.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
