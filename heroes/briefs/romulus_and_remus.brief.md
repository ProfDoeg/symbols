HERO. Romulus and Remus (Roman heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the twins of Rhea Silvia and Mars, the Tiber, the wolf and Faustulus, the augury and the wall, Remus killed, the Sabine women, the apotheosis as Quirinus (or the murder by the senators), the Lupercal (the cave found in 2007, disputed), the Capitoline Wolf's date (medieval, 2012 radiocarbon), Wiseman's Remus on the invention of the twin, the fascist and the modern Rome.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Virgil's Aeneid with Servius's commentary, Livy I-II, Dionysius of Halicarnassus, Ovid's Fasti and Metamorphoses, Plutarch's Romulus and Publicola, Dio, the Fasti Capitolini, the Lapis Niger, the Lupercal and the Lavinium heroon archaeology; Wiseman's Remus, Cornell's Beginnings of Rome, Dumézil, Grandazzi, Horsfall's commentaries, the Brill's New Pauly.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
