HERO. Emperor Jimmu (Japanese heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the first emperor, descendant of Amaterasu, the eastern expedition from Hyūga, the crow Yatagarasu and the golden kite, the enthronement at Kashihara in 660 BCE (the Nihon Shoki's date), the Kigensetsu holiday and the 2600th anniversary of 1940, the postwar doubt, the Kashihara shrine.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki (Yamato Takeru), the Heike monogatari and Gikeiki (Yoshitsune and Benkei), the otogizōshi and the Edo picture books (Momotarō, Kintarō, Urashima), the Nihon ryōiki and Konjaku, the Kabuki and Noh repertoires, the Meiji school readers that fixed Momotarō; Antoni, Reider, Foster, Kōji Kawamoto, McCullough's Heike, the Yoshitsune-as-Genghis-Khan legend as documented modern invention, the wartime Momotarō propaganda films.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
