HERO. Dobrynya Nikitich (Slavic (the byliny) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the courtly bogatyr who killed the dragon Zmey Gorynych and rescued Zabava, the diplomat and the harpist, the wife almost lost to Alyosha, the historical Dobrynya, Vladimir the Great's uncle, as the shadow, the Novgorod baptism 'by fire and sword'.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the byliny collections (Kirsha Danilov's of the 18th century, Rybnikov, Hilferding, Gilferding's Onega singers), the Kiev and Novgorod cycles, the Russian Primary Chronicle for the historical shadows, the lubok prints; Bailey and Ivanova's An Anthology of Russian Folk Epics, Propp's Russian Heroic Epic, Oinas, the Vasnetsov Bogatyrs (1898) and the Soviet reception, Rimsky-Korsakov's Sadko.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
