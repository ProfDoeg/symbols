HERO. Robin Hood (Medieval European legend (Germanic, Arthurian, folk) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the outlaw of Sherwood or Barnsdale: the ballads (Robin Hood and the Monk c. 1450, the Gest c. 1500), Little John, Will Scarlet, Friar Tuck and Maid Marian as accretions, the Sheriff and the king, the death at Kirklees by the prioress's bleeding, the candidates (Robert Hod of 1225, the Wakefield Robin), Holt's and Knight's studies, the May games, the modern Robin who robs the rich (a late turn: say so).

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Tschudi and the White Book of Sarnen for Tell, Gottfried's Tristan and Béroul and Thomas, Wolfram's Parzival and Chrétien's Perceval, the Tannhäuser ballad and the Venusberg, the Faustbuch of 1587 and Marlowe and Goethe, Till Eulenspiegel (1515), the Hamelin sources (the 1384 chronicle, the Lüneburg manuscript, Grimm), Lohengrin (the Schwanenritter), the Robin Hood ballads (Child, Holt), the Nine Worthies; Grimm's Deutsche Sagen, the Mittelhochdeutsche editions, the Arthurian Encyclopedia (Lacy).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
