HERO. Copil (Aztec and Toltec heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the son of Malinalxochitl who attacked the Mexica at Chapultepec to avenge his mother, killed and his heart thrown into the lake where the cactus grew (the founding of Tenochtitlan on the eagle's rock), the Crónica Mexicayotl, the Copil of the migration myth.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Anales de Cuauhtitlan, the Leyenda de los Soles, the Historia Tolteca-Chichimeca, Sahagún, Durán, Alvarado Tezozómoc's Crónica Mexicayotl, Ixtlilxochitl, the Codex Boturini and the migration codices, the Templo Mayor; Nicholson's Topiltzin Quetzalcoatl (2001), Davies's The Toltecs, Graulich, Carrasco, León-Portilla, Boone.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
