HERO. Solomon (Biblical (the mythic figures of the Hebrew Bible, between the atlas and the angels) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the son of David and Bathsheba, the wisdom asked for at Gibeon, the judgment of the two mothers, the Temple, the Queen of Sheba's visit (the atlas has her), the thousand wives and the turning to other gods, the Song of Songs and Ecclesiastes attributed, the Solomon of the Testament of Solomon and the ring that bound the demons (pantheon/goetia's ancestor), the Islamic Sulayman with the jinn and the hoopoe, the Ethiopian Menelik, the Seal of Solomon, the archaeology of the 'Solomonic' gates and the minimalist debate.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Hebrew Bible (the Masoretic text, the Septuagint and the Vulgate where they differ, the Dead Sea Scrolls), the Pseudepigrapha (1 Enoch, Jubilees, the Life of Adam and Eve, the Testaments), Josephus's Antiquities, the Talmud and the midrash (Genesis Rabbah, Pirke de-Rabbi Eliezer), Ginzberg's Legends of the Jews for the whole rabbinic layer, the Qur'an and the qisas al-anbiya (al-Tha'labi, al-Kisa'i) for the Islamic figure, the Church Fathers and the Glossa, the medieval mystery plays and the iconography (the Princeton Index), the archaeology where it bears (the minimalist debate: Finkelstein, Dever), the Anchor Bible Dictionary, the DDD, Alter's translation and commentary, Kugel's Traditions of the Bible, the Encyclopaedia Judaica.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
