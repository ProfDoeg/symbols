HERO. Enmerkar (Mesopotamian heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the king of Uruk who invented writing to threaten the lord of Aratta (Enmerkar and the Lord of Aratta), the contests of riddles and the goddess Inanna between them, Enmerkar and En-suhgir-ana, the Sumerian King List's founder of Uruk, the Nimrod identification of the scholars.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Etana epic (Kinnier Wilson, Novotny), the Sargon Birth Legend (Lewis's The Sargon Legend), the Sumerian King List, the Old Akkadian inscriptions and the Sargon legends of the first millennium (the King of Battle), the ETCSL and ORACC; Westenholz's Legends of the Kings of Akkade, Foster's Before the Muses, George on Gilgamesh for the comparanda.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
