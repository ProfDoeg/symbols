HERO. Galvarino (Mapuche (the epic and the chronicles) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the warrior whose hands were cut off by García Hurtado de Mendoza (1557) and who fought on with knives bound to the stumps, captured at Millarapue and hanged, Ercilla's Canto XXII, the Galvarino of the Chilean and Mapuche memory (the statue, the band names).

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Ercilla's La Araucana (1569-89) as the source of Caupolicán, Galvarino, Fresia and Guacolda (and the debate on what Ercilla invented), Pedro de Oña's Arauco domado, Góngora Marmolejo and Mariño de Lobera's chronicles, Núñez de Pineda, Rosales, the Mapuche oral tradition (Koessler-Ilg, Augusta), Bengoa's Historia del pueblo mapuche, the Chilean and Argentine national uses (the Caupolicán statue, Rubén Darío's sonnet).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
