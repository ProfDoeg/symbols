HERO. Pelayo (Iberian (the Spanish and Basque epics and legends) heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Visigothic noble who defeated the Moors at Covadonga (c. 722) and founded the kingdom of Asturias, the cave and the Virgin, the Crónica de Alfonso III's account and the Arabic silence, the Reconquista's founding myth, the Covadonga of the Carlists and of Franco, the tomb in the Santa Cueva.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Cantar de mio Cid (Menéndez Pidal, Montaner) and the Historia Roderici, the Chanson de Roland with the Roncevaux sources (Einhard, the Nota Emilianense), the romancero (the Infantes de Lara, Bernardo del Carpio, Don Rodrigo, Fernán González's Poema), the Crónica de Alfonso III and the Covadonga legend, Lope García de Salazar for Jaun Zuria, the Basque Bienandanzas e fortunas; Menéndez Pidal's La epopeya castellana, Fletcher's The Quest for El Cid, Deyermond, Lacarra, the nationalist uses (Franco's Cid, the Basque Jaun Zuria).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
