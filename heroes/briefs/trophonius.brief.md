HERO. Trophonius (Greek heroes). State at the top the figure's class as the tradition classes it (mortal
hero, demigod, heroized dead with cult, culture hero, epic king) and the earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the builder of Apollo's temple with Agamedes, the thief in the treasury of Hyrieus, swallowed by the earth at Lebadeia, the oracle in the cave with the honey cakes and the descent (Pausanias IX.39, the man who never laughed again), the springs of Lethe and Mnemosyne there.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Homer (Iliad, Odyssey), the Epic Cycle fragments (West's Loeb), Hesiod's Catalogue of Women, Pindar, the tragedians, Apollonius's Argonautica, Apollodorus's Bibliotheca, Pausanias, Hyginus, Ovid, the scholia, the LIMC for the vases; Gantz's Early Greek Myth, Nagy's The Best of the Achaeans and Greek Hero Cults, Farnell's Greek Hero Cults, Burkert, Ekroth on hero cult, Fowler's Early Greek Mythography, the Theoi.com source index, Perseus and the TLG.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named god or hero gets its own line (the other figure, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for the gods of this tradition in
symbols/pantheon and for the other heroes here, and the files cross-reference by name. Absence of
evidence is a finding.
