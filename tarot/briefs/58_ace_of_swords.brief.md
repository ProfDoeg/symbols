CARD. Ace of Swords: pip card of the suit of Swords. Give at the top the names across decks, the number, the earliest
surviving example, and the Golden Dawn attribution.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Ace as the Root of the Powers of Air in Book T; the suit itself: Spade, Épées; Air; the curved scimitars of the Marseille pattern; Vav; the Sola Busca and Marseille aces (the ace of coins as the maker's card with the printer's name and tax stamp, the ace of cups as a fountain/church); Waite's hand from the cloud; Crowley's ace; the Mamluk ancestor of the suit; the Ace's rank in play (high in tarocchi trumps? no: check).

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, esoteric attribution, disputed, legend, modern invention. Where a thread above carries
a question mark or '(verify)', treat it as a lead to check and report what you found. Quote the
occultists (Lévi, Papus, Waite, Crowley) in their own words with edition and page. Absence of
evidence is a finding: say what could not be traced.
