CARD. The Magician: trump I, Il Bagatto/Bagatello, Le Bateleur; a trump of the tarot, Marseille numbering unless noted. Give at the top the names across decks, the number, the earliest
surviving example, and the Golden Dawn attribution.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Beth, Mercury; the mountebank at his table in the fifteenth century (a conjurer, a craftsman?); the Marseille Bateleur's hat as a lemniscate; Waite's Magus with the four suit-signs and 'as above so below'; Crowley's three Magi paintings; Lévi's Magus with the wand; the Mercury/Hermes reading; the cups-and-balls game.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, esoteric attribution, disputed, legend, modern invention. Where a thread above carries
a question mark or '(verify)', treat it as a lead to check and report what you found. Quote the
occultists (Lévi, Papus, Waite, Crowley) in their own words with edition and page. Absence of
evidence is a finding: say what could not be traced.
