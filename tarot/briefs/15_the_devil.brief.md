CARD. The Devil: trump XV, Il Diavolo, Le Diable; a trump of the tarot, Marseille numbering unless noted. Give at the top the names across decks, the number, the earliest
surviving example, and the Golden Dawn attribution.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Ayin, Capricorn; no fifteenth-century Devil survives (the missing Visconti cards); the Marseille devil on a pedestal with two chained imps; Lévi's Baphomet of Mendes (1856) and its adoption by Waite (1909) with the inverted pentagram and the chained couple; Crowley's Devil as the goat of Mendes with the third eye, the testicles as the root, Pan; the Capricorn attribution; the Templar Baphomet.

STANCE. Myth as fully as fact. Every claim labeled: documented fact, scholarly reconstruction,
tradition, esoteric attribution, disputed, legend, modern invention. Where a thread above carries
a question mark or '(verify)', treat it as a lead to check and report what you found. Quote the
occultists (Lévi, Papus, Waite, Crowley) in their own words with edition and page. Absence of
evidence is a finding: say what could not be traced.
