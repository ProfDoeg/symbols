BEING. Loko (Vodou (the Haitian lwa, the Dahomean vodun behind them, and New Orleans) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the lwa of the trees and the priesthood, the Dahomean Loko (the iroko tree), the first houngan, the leaves and the asson's origin, St Joseph.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Herskovits's Dahomey (1938) and Life in a Haitian Valley, Maya Deren's Divine Horsemen, Métraux's Voodoo in Haiti, Milo Rigaud, Laënnec Hurbon, Karen McCarthy Brown's Mama Lola, Desmangles's Faces of the Gods, Michel and Bellegarde-Smith, Beauvoir-Dominique, the Bois Caïman documents and the 1791 debate (Geggus), Moreau de Saint-Méry (1797), the vèvè drawings, the Fon sources (Mercier, Maupoil's La géomancie à l'ancienne Côte des Esclaves, Le Hérissé), the Ouidah python temple and the Abomey palace records, Ellis's Ewe-Speaking Peoples; New Orleans: Tallant's Voodoo in New Orleans (with its errors), Long's Spiritual Merchants and A New Orleans Voudou Priestess (Marie Laveau, a person, only as reception), the Louisiana Writers' Project interviews.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
