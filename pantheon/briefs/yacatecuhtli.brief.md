BEING. Yacatecuhtli (Aztec (Mexica and central Mexican) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the nose lord, the god of the pochteca merchants, the staff bundle worshipped on the road, the merchants' secret feasts and the slave sacrifices at Tlatelolco, the pochteca as spies.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Florentine Codex (Sahagún, Anderson and Dibble; the Primeros Memoriales), Durán's Historia and Book of the Gods and Rites, the Codex Borgia and the Borgia group (Seler, Boone), the Codex Mendoza, the Codex Borbonicus, the Codex Magliabechiano, the Codex Telleriano-Remensis, the Leyenda de los Soles and the Histoyre du Mechique, the Anales de Cuauhtitlan, the Cantares Mexicanos, the Templo Mayor excavations (Matos Moctezuma, López Luján), the Coyolxauhqui stone (1978) and the Tlaltecuhtli monolith (2006), the Sun Stone; Nicholson's typology (1971), León-Portilla's Aztec Thought and Culture, Graulich, Townsend, Carrasco, Olivier's Mockeries and Metamorphoses of an Aztec God (Tezcatlipoca), Boone's Cycles of Time and Meaning, Miller and Taube's Gods and Symbols, the human sacrifice evidence (the tzompantli finds of 2015-2017) stated plainly.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
