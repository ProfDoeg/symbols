BEING. Ukko (Finnic and Sami pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the thunder god of the Finns (Ukko ylijumala), Agricola's list, the Ukon vakat feast with the beer, the hammer and the axe of the sky, the stone axes as Ukko's, the Ukko of the Kalevala's oaths, the Ukonkivi rock of Lake Inari.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Kalevala (Lönnrot 1835, 1849) and the SKVR runes behind it (the Suomen Kansan Vanhat Runot), Agricola's list of the Tavastian and Karelian gods (1551), Ganander's Mythologia Fennica (1789), Castrén, Haavio's Suomalainen mytologia, Siikala's Itämerensuomalaisten mytologia, Pentikäinen; for the Sami: the noaidi drums (Manker), Schefferus's Lapponia (1673), the missionary accounts (Randulf, Skanke), Rydving, Bäckman and Hultkrantz.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
