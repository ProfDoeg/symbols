BEING. Proserpina (Roman pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Persephone in Latin, the Secular Games with Dis, Claudian's De Raptu Proserpinae, the Locrian and Sicilian cults (Enna), Bernini's Rape, the name from proserpere.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Varro (via Augustine's City of God), Cicero's De Natura Deorum, Ovid's Fasti and Metamorphoses, Livy, Virgil, Festus, Macrobius, Servius, Arnobius and the Christian apologists as hostile witnesses, the Fasti calendars, the CIL inscriptions; Dumézil's Archaic Roman Religion, Wissowa's Religion und Kultus der Römer, Beard-North-Price's Religions of Rome, Scheid, Latte, the LIMC, the Brill's New Pauly; for Etruscan and Italic roots, Thulin and the Etruscan mirrors.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
