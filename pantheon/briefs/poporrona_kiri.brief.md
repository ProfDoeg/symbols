BEING. Poporrona' / Kíri (Quërí) (Amazonian (the Southern Arawak of eastern Peru, and the Peruvian selva folklore) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the evil child who never grows, the son of Ayots (grandson of Avíreri), who asks 'who is that one?' until his father transforms the person, who turned his baby cousin into rotten wood and bathed in the river his father used to flood and eat the people, who could not be killed by arrows or clubs until he asked for a balsa throne and a hardwood spike through his head, and became the peach palm (Bactris); the name Kíri from Kiristu (Cristo) in Santos-Granero's reading; the model of the child witch (amaseñeñ, máci) and the executions of accused children (1880s-1960s) documented in the article.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Santos-Granero's Saint Christopher in the Amazon (Ethnohistory 49:3, 2002) and The Power of Love (1991), Weiss's Campa Cosmology (1975), Smith's Deliverance from Chaos for a Song (1977, the Yanesha), Shaver and Dodd on the Nomatsiguenga (1990), Brown and Fernández's War of Shadows (1991), Renard-Casevitz on the Matsigenka, Rojas Zolezzi, the Franciscan chronicles (Izaguirre's Historia de las misiones franciscanas, Amich, Biedma, San Antonio 1750, Ordinaire 1885, Navarro 1924, Sala 1893), Pérez Marcio's Los hijos de la selva (1953), the Juan Santos Atahualpa sources (Loayza, Zarzar), Gow's Of Mixed Blood and An Amazonian Myth and its History (the Piro), Regan's Hacia la tierra sin mal (the Loreto folklore), Galeano's Iquitos collections, Cabrejos, Chirif, Dean's Urarina Society, the Amazonian folklore compilations of Loreto and Ucayali (the Bufeo, Chullachaqui and Yacuruna cycles), Weiss and Ansión on the Pishtaco, and the Shining Path years as documented history.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
