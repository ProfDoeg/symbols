BEING. The Muses (Greek pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the nine as one dossier (Calliope, Clio, Erato, Euterpe, Melpomene, Polyhymnia, Terpsichore, Thalia; Urania has her own file): Hesiod's proem on Helicon, the three older Muses of Pausanias, the Pierian and Heliconian cults, the Museum of Alexandria, the assignment of arts as Roman and later, the Sarcophagus of the Muses.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Homer, Hesiod (Theogony, Works and Days), the Homeric Hymns, Pindar, the tragedians, Apollodorus's Bibliotheca, Pausanias, Diodorus, Hyginus, the Orphic Hymns and fragments, the Greek Magical Papyri; Burkert's Greek Religion, Gantz's Early Greek Myth, Farnell's Cults of the Greek States, the LIMC for iconography, the Theoi.com source index, Perseus and the TLG for the texts, Kerényi and Graves as reception to be checked, the Pauly-Wissowa and the Brill's New Pauly.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
