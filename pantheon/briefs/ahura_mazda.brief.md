BEING. Ahura Mazda (Persian (Zoroastrian and older Iranian) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Wise Lord of the Gathas, the Achaemenid inscriptions (Behistun: 'by the favour of Ahuramazda'), the winged figure question, the Sasanian Ohrmazd and the investiture reliefs, the Bundahishn's cosmogony, the Zurvanite subordination, the Parsi and Iranian Zoroastrian worship today.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Avesta (Gathas, Yasna, Yashts, Vendidad, in Darmesteter, Mills, Humbach, Skjaervø), the Pahlavi books (Bundahishn, Denkard, Menog-i Khrad, Arda Viraf), the Achaemenid inscriptions (Behistun, Naqsh-e Rostam), Herodotus and Plutarch, Ferdowsi's Shahnameh, the Manichaean and Mithraic material where relevant; Boyce's History of Zoroastrianism, the Encyclopaedia Iranica online, Kellens, Malandra, Skjaervø, Hinnells, the Avestan Digital Archive.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
