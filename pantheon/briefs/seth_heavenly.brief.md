BEING. Seth (the heavenly Seth, the Great Seth) (Gnostic, with the Mandaean and Manichaean cousins pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the son of Adamas in the Pleroma and the father of the immovable race, the Second Treatise of the Great Seth, the Three Steles of Seth, the Gospel of the Egyptians (Seth incarnate as Jesus), the Sethians as named by Hippolytus and Epiphanius, the Seth of the Manichaeans and the Mandaean Shitil, Turner's Sethian Gnosticism.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Nag Hammadi codices (the Apocryphon of John in its long and short recensions, the Hypostasis of the Archons, On the Origin of the World, the Gospel of Truth, the Tripartite Tractate, the Gospel of the Egyptians, Zostrianos, Allogenes, the Trimorphic Protennoia, Eugnostos, the Discourse on the Eighth and Ninth; Robinson's and Meyer's editions, the Coptic in the Brill Coptic Gnostic Library), the Berlin Codex, the Pistis Sophia and the Books of Jeu (Askew and Bruce codices), the Gospel of Judas (Tchacos), the heresiologists (Irenaeus Against Heresies I, Hippolytus Refutatio, Epiphanius Panarion, Tertullian Against the Valentinians, Clement's Excerpta ex Theodoto), the Corpus Hermeticum (Nock-Festugière, Copenhaver) for Poimandres; the Mandaean Ginza Rabba (Lidzbarski, Drower's editions and Mandaeans of Iraq and Iran, Buckley); the Manichaean Kephalaia, the Cologne Mani Codex, the Turfan fragments, Augustine's anti-Manichaean works, the Fihrist of al-Nadim (Gardner and Lieu's sourcebook); Jonas's Gnostic Religion, Rudolph's Gnosis, Pearson, Layton's Gnostic Scriptures, Williams's Rethinking Gnosticism, King's What is Gnosticism, Turner on Sethianism, Thomassen on the Valentinians.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
