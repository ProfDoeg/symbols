BEING. Skan (Lakota (Oglala and the Sioux) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Sky, the moving power, the judge of the gods who gave each his place (the Skan of Walker's texts), the source of the ni (breath), the highest in the Sword account, the Taku Skanskan of the ethnographies.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: Walker's Lakota Belief and Ritual (the accounts of George Sword, Bad Wound, Finger, Thomas Tyon, collected 1896-1914) and Lakota Myth, Black Elk's accounts (Black Elk Speaks and The Sacred Pipe with DeMallie's The Sixth Grandfather on what Neihardt changed), Densmore's Teton Sioux Music, Ella Deloria's Dakota Texts, Lame Deer Seeker of Visions, Powers's Oglala Religion, DeMallie and Parks's Sioux Indian Religion, Dooling's The Sons of the Wind, the winter counts, Amiotte, Lakota language sources (Buechel's dictionary), the Ghost Dance of 1890 and Wounded Knee as documented history, the Sun Dance's ban (1883) and return, the sacred sites (Paha Sapa, Bear Butte, Devils Tower) in the present.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
