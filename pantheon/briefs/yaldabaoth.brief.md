BEING. Yaldabaoth (Abrahamic angelology and demonology, with the Gnostic figures pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the lion-faced demiurge born of Sophia's error (the Apocryphon of John, the Hypostasis of the Archons), the arrogant boast 'I am God and there is no other', Saklas and Samael as his names, the seven archons, the etymology debate, the Ophite diagram (Origen's Contra Celsum VI).

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Hebrew Bible and Septuagint, 1 Enoch, Jubilees, Tobit, the Dead Sea Scrolls, the Talmud and midrash, the Hekhalot literature, Sefer Raziel and the Zohar, the New Testament and apocrypha, Pseudo-Dionysius, Aquinas, the Qur'an and hadith and the tafsir, al-Tabari, the Nag Hammadi codices (Robinson, Meyer) and the heresiologists (Irenaeus, Hippolytus, Epiphanius), the grimoires (Lemegeton, Abramelin), Milton and Dante as reception; Davidson's Dictionary of Angels, the DDD, Bamberger's Fallen Angels, Kelly's Satan: A Biography, Russell's four-volume history of the Devil, Pearson and Rudolph on Gnosticism, the Encyclopaedia of Islam.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
