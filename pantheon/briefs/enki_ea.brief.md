BEING. Enki / Ea (Mesopotamian (Sumerian, Akkadian, Babylonian, Assyrian) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the god of fresh water, wisdom and craft at Eridu (the Abzu), Enki and Ninhursag (Dilmun), Enki and the World Order, Inanna and Enki (the me), the saviour of mankind in Atrahasis and the flood, Ea in the Enuma Elish (Apsu's killing, Marduk's father), the goat-fish and the flowing vase, Oannes.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the ETCSL (Electronic Text Corpus of Sumerian Literature), the Enuma Elish, Atrahasis, Gilgamesh (George's edition), Inanna's Descent, Enki and Ninmah, the god lists (An = Anum), the Assyrian royal inscriptions (RINAP), the ORACC projects including the Ancient Mesopotamian Gods and Goddesses site (AMGG), the CDLI tablets; Jacobsen's Treasures of Darkness, Black and Green's Gods, Demons and Symbols, Lambert's Babylonian Creation Myths, Frankfort on seals, the Reallexikon der Assyriologie.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
