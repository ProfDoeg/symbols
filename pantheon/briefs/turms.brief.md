BEING. Turms (Etruscan pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the Etruscan Hermes with the caduceus and the winged boots, the mirrors' Turms Aitas (Hermes of Hades), the psychopomp, the Turms in the Judgement of Paris scenes.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Liber Linteus of Zagreb, the Piacenza liver, the Tabula Capuana, the Pyrgi tablets, the mirrors (the CSE corpus) and their inscriptions, the Tomb of Orcus and the François Tomb, the Cippus Perusinus, Cicero's De divinatione on the haruspices, Varro and Festus, Pliny, Martianus Capella's list of the sixteen regions; Pallottino, de Grummond's Etruscan Myth, Sacred History and Legend, Bonfante's Etruscan Language, Thulin's Die etruskische Disciplin, Jannot's Religion in Ancient Etruria, Simon and de Grummond's The Religion of the Etruscans, the Brill's New Pauly.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
