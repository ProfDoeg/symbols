BEING. Mama Quilla (Andean and Inca pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Mother Moon, wife of Inti, the silver room of the Coricancha, the eclipses as attacks by a puma, the moon's calendar and the women's cult, the Coya's patroness, the Quilla of the Huarochirí.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the chroniclers (Cieza de León, Betanzos, Sarmiento de Gamboa, Cobo, Molina, Garcilaso de la Vega, Guaman Poma's Nueva corónica, Cristóbal de Albornoz, Arriaga's Extirpación), the Huarochirí Manuscript (Salomon and Urioste), the Huarochirí and Cajatambo idolatry trials; Rostworowski, Zuidema, Duviols, Urton, Bauer's Sacred Landscape of the Inca, Salomon, MacCormack's Religion in the Andes, Silverblatt, and the Peruvian and Bolivian archaeology reports; living practice in Cusco, the altiplano and the Argentine northwest.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
