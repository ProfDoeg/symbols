BEING. Kalki (Hindu and Vedic pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the future avatar on the white horse with the sword at the end of the Kali Yuga, the Kalki Purana, the Mahabharata's Kalki Vishnuyashas, the Kalki temple of Jaipur, the modern claimants and the Kalki Avatar cults, the Kalki of the Kalachakra Tantra.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Rigveda (Griffith, Jamison-Brereton), the Atharvaveda, the Brahmanas and Upanishads, the Mahabharata (van Buitenen, Ganguli) and Ramayana (Goldman), the Puranas (Bhagavata, Vishnu, Shiva, Markandeya with the Devi Mahatmya, Skanda, Linga), the Agamas and Tantras, the Tamil Sangam and bhakti corpus; Macdonell's Vedic Mythology, Gonda, Doniger, Kinsley's Hindu Goddesses, Eck, Fuller's Camphor Flame for living cult, the Sanskrit texts at GRETIL and sacred-texts.com, the ASI for temples.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
