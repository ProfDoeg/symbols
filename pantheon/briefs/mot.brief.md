BEING. Mot (Canaanite, Phoenician and Punic pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Death who swallows Baal, Anat's threshing and grinding of him, the seven-year cycle and the return (KTU 1.5-6), the Mot of the Bible's personified death (Isaiah 25:8, Hosea 13:14), the Muth of Philo of Byblos.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Ugaritic texts (KTU: the Baal Cycle, Kirta, Aqhat, in Smith and Pitard, Wyatt, Parker), the Amarna letters, the Hebrew Bible as hostile witness, Philo of Byblos via Eusebius, Lucian's De Dea Syria, the Phoenician and Punic inscriptions (KAI), the Carthage tophet and its excavation reports, Herodotus, Josephus; Smith's Early History of God and Origins of Biblical Monotheism, Day's Yahweh and the Gods and Goddesses of Canaan, the Dictionary of Deities and Demons in the Bible (DDD), Lipiński, Xella, Bonnet's Melqart.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
