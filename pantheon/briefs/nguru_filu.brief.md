# Ngürü-filu (the fox-snake): research brief

BEING. Ngürü-filu (the fox-snake) (Mapuche, with the Huilliche and Chiloé lore pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the river monster half fox half serpent that overturns boats, the Ngürüvilu of the river crossings, the machi's exorcism.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the early chroniclers (Ercilla's La Araucana, Núñez de Pineda's Cautiverio feliz, Rosales's Historia general, Febrés's and Havestadt's dictionaries, Augusta's Diccionario araucano and Lecturas araucanas), Lenz's Estudios araucanos, Guevara, Latcham, Moesbach's Vida y costumbres de los indígenas araucanos (Pascual Coña), Foerster's Introducción a la religiosidad mapuche, Bacigalupo on the machi, Faron's Hawks of the Sun, Grebe on the cosmovision, Dillehay's Monuments, Empires and Resistance, the Chiloé collectors (Cavada's Chiloé y los chilotes, Quintana, Plath's Geografía del mito y la leyenda chilena, Vicuña Cifuentes's Mitos y supersticiones), the nguillatun and machitun ethnographies, and the Argentine Neuquén and Río Negro material (Koessler-Ilg's Cuentan los araucanos).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
