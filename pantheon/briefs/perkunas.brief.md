BEING. Perkūnas (Pērkons) (Baltic (Lithuanian, Latvian, Old Prussian) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the thunder god with the axe and the oak, the Lithuanian Perkūnas and Latvian Pērkons of the dainas and the chronicles, the fight with Velnias, the Perkūnas of Lasicki and the Sudovian Book, the Slavic Perun and the Norse Fjörgyn cognates.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the dainas (Barons's Latvju dainas, the Lithuanian dainos), the Sudovian Book and the Prussian sources (Peter of Dusburg, Grunau's invented Prussian triad: say so), Lasicki's De diis Samagitarum (1615), Stryjkowski, Praetorius's Deliciae Prussicae, the Jesuit reports of the 17th century; Gimbutas, Vėlius's Baltų religijos ir mitologijos šaltiniai, Greimas's Of Gods and Men, Biezais on the Latvian gods, Beresnevičius; the Romuva and Dievturība revivals as documented reception.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
