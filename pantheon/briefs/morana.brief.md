BEING. Morana (Marzanna) (Slavic pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the winter and death goddess drowned or burned as an effigy at the end of winter (the Polish Marzanna, the Czech Morena, the Slovak Morena), Długosz's account, the Slavic Carnival rites, the mor/death root.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Primary Chronicle (Vladimir's pantheon of 980), Procopius on the Sclaveni, Helmold's Chronica Slavorum and Saxo on the Rugians (Svetovid at Arkona), Thietmar of Merseburg, the Zbruch idol, the Novgorod birch-bark letters, the byliny and the Russian fairy tales (Afanasyev), the dumy and the Polish and Czech chronicles (Długosz's list and its invention); Gieysztor's Mitologia Słowian, Ivanov and Toporov, Rybakov (with the critics), Zaroff, Álvarez-Pedrosa's Sources of Slavic Pre-Christian Religion, the Slavic Native Faith revival (Rodnovery) as documented reception.

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
