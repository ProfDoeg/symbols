BEING. The Tschäggättä (Alpine (Swiss, Tyrolean, Bavarian, Austrian) pantheon). State at the top the being's class (god, goddess, spirit, demon,
monster, collective) as the tradition classifies it, and its earliest secure attestation.

THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: the masked figures of the Lötschental (Valais) carnival with the carved wooden faces and animal skins, the origin legends (the Schurtendiebe robbers), the masks' makers, the run between Candlemas and Shrove Tuesday.

WHERE THE SOURCES ARE for this tradition: the Grimm brothers' Deutsche Sagen and Deutsche Mythologie, Vernaleken's Alpensagen (1858), Zingerle's Sagen aus Tirol, Heyl, Rochholz's Schweizersagen aus dem Aargau, Jecklin's Volkstümliches aus Graubünden, Kuoni's Sagen des Kantons St. Gallen, Müller's Sagen aus Uri, the Handwörterbuch des deutschen Aberglaubens, Wolfram's and Sebald's Perchten studies, the Krampus and Perchten runs' documentation (Salzburg, Gastein, the Lötschental Tschäggättä), the Museum der Völker and the Swiss folklore atlas (Atlas der schweizerischen Volkskunde).

STANCE. Legend as fully as fact; nothing discarded for being unprovable, everything labeled.
Where a thread above names a dispute, present both sides with their evidence. Every documented
interaction with another named divine being gets its own line (the other being, the nature of the
interaction, the source and date), since sibling dossiers exist for most of this pantheon and the
files cross-reference by name. Absence of evidence is a finding.
