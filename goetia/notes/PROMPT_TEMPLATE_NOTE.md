# One-off research notes attached to the Goetia set

Ad hoc questions Anthony raises about the 72 spirits, answered as research notes rather than
dossiers. `[NAME]` is the only substitution point; the brief carries the question.

---

[NAME]
Please investigate the question below as a research note. The point is to find out whether a
real, documented thread exists, and to say so plainly either way. Include:

- every specific claim you can find, with who made it, where (URL, video, forum, book), when,
  and what it rests on; quote the claim in its own words
- the primary facts the claim leans on, checked (the actual numbers, dates and texts)
- the genealogy of the idea if it has one (who first said it, who repeated it, how it changed)
- the honest verdict: documented tradition, modern invention with a traceable origin, a
  coincidence people have noticed, or nothing found; and what would count as evidence if
  someone wanted to pursue it further

Label every statement: documented, participant claim, folklore, coincidence, not found.
Do not pad; if there is nothing there, a short note saying so, with the searches you ran, is
the right answer. Structure: Question; What was searched; Claims found (each with source and
date); The facts checked; Genealogy; Verdict; Sources.
