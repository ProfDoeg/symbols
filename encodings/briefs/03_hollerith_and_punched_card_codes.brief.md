THREADS TO PULL, each to be verified against the sources and dated: Herman Hollerith's cards for the 1890 US census (the Jacquard and the railway ticket as models), the 80-column IBM card of 1928 with its zone and digit rows, the Hollerith code's letters (a zone punch plus a digit punch), the card as the data medium of the twentieth century, the BCDIC six-bit codes of the 1950s (IBM 704, 1401) that grew from it, the card's constraints that shaped EBCDIC, the 'do not fold, spindle or mutilate' culture, the end of the card in the 1980s.

STANCE. Folklore as fully as fact, labeled. Every claim labeled: documented standard or text,
participant recollection, scholarly reconstruction, disputed, folklore, modern invention. Give
the worked examples byte by byte. Where a story has one witness (the placemat, the 360's unused
ASCII bit), say so. Absence of evidence is a finding.
