# Dialog (Thiel's invitation-only conference network): Research Dossier

**Research cut-off:** 28 September 2026.

**Scope caution:** “Dialog,” “Dialog Retreat,” “Dialog Global,” and the membership community operated under that name are treated here as one network. The evidence identifies **Stonebrick LLC** as the legal operator doing business as Dialog. No related tax-exempt entity called “Dialog Foundation” could be verified; similarly named charities found in nonprofit databases are unrelated. “Dialog Weekend” does not appear to be a stable formal title in the located records.

## Evidence key

- **P — Primary/documentary record:** internal invitation, contemporaneous email, corporate registry, court pleading, organization website, or published participant form.
- **J — Reported and independently checked:** named journalists reporting from documents or identified sources.
- **S — Subject/participant account:** statement by Dialog, an organizer, invitee, or attendee.
- **L — Litigation allegation:** assertion in a complaint or courtroom submission not yet adjudicated.
- **R — Rumor/conspiracy narrative:** online claim for which supporting evidence was not located.
- **N — Negative, contradicted, or corrected:** claim contradicted by records, denied by the named person, corrected by a publisher, or unsupported after targeted searches.

---

## 1. Identification

Dialog is a private, paid, invitation-only international membership and conference network founded—on the best available record—in **2006**, not 2015, by technology investors **Auren Hoffman** and **Peter Thiel**. Its own current website describes it as a community of CEOs, founders, government leaders, investors, artists, and public intellectuals gathering for off-the-record conversations, and says it has no ideological agenda, partisanship, donations or investments, speeches, panels, on-record conversations, or self-promotion. Admission requires nomination after accomplishing “something amazing.” **Tier P/S, Dialog website, accessed 28 September 2026.** [Dialog’s current site](https://dialog.org/)

Dialog’s legal operator is **Stonebrick LLC**, a California LLC registered on 10 December 2002 and associated in California records with Auren Hoffman. In 2026 Irish litigation, Stonebrick expressly sued **trading as Dialog**. **Tier P/J/L, California registry-derived record and Irish court reporting, July–September 2026.** [California entity record](https://www.city-data.com/business-entities/CA/STONEBRICK-LLC-200234510118-CA.html), [Irish Commercial Court report](https://www.laois-nationalist.ie/news/powerscourt-conference-cancelled-after-threats-of-protests-and-blockades-court-hears_arid-106405.html)

Auren Hoffman is the chairman and operational center of the network. **Raffi Grinberg** is identified as executive director; **Juliette Levine** has been reported as part of the staff; **Simone Collins** previously served as managing director. Peter Thiel is properly described as cofounder, but evidence that he currently directs, owns, or finances Dialog is weak. Former attendees and an internal 2012 email describe him as absent or minimally involved. **Tier J/S/P, 2012–2026.** [Byline Times investigation](https://www.bylinesupplement.com/p/exclusive-peter-thiels-least-secret), [2012 primary invitation](https://epstein.media/files/house_oversight_013484/)

## 2. The Record

### Formation and early structure, 2002–2011

- **10 December 2002:** Stonebrick LLC registered in California, before Dialog’s creation. The record names Auren Hoffman as registered agent and shows the entity active. This establishes an older Hoffman vehicle, not that Dialog existed in 2002. **Tier P-derived.**
- **2006:** WIRED, Axios, the Guardian, Byline Times, and Hoffman biographies consistently identify 2006 as Dialog’s founding year, with Hoffman and Thiel as cofounders. No primary incorporation document for a separate “Dialog” entity was located. **Tier J, multiple-source convergence.**
- **2015 founding claim:** unsupported. It appears to confuse the year of a later event, public biography, or source publication with the network’s origin. **Tier N.**
- Early archived material reportedly contained member directories and “Around the Table” dinners in several cities. By 2026, Dialog materials claimed more than 150 such dinners annually. **Tier J based on archived/internal materials, Guardian and Byline Times, June 2026.**

### The 2012 invitation and 2013–14 retreats

On 28 November 2012, Auren Hoffman sent invitations for “Dialog 2014,” scheduled for **13–15 March 2014 at Sundance Resort, Utah**. The email:

- described a two-day bipartisan retreat “discussing how to change the world”;
- limited participation to 150;
- promised no speeches or panels, only moderated groups of 6–15;
- said the entire resort had been rented;
- made invitations personal and nontransferable;
- offered selected invitees discount codes, including `GovtEmployee` and `AcademicDialoger`;
- said fees would rise weekly;
- stated there were **“no sponsors”** and the event was **“100% off-the-record.”**

These are **Tier P** facts from a contemporaneous email later produced by the Epstein estate to Congress. [House Oversight document 013484](https://epstein.media/files/house_oversight_013484/), [parallel PDF copy](https://taxpolicy.org.uk/wp-content/mandelson-search/PDFs/EFTA01763941.pdf)

The invitation attached a partial list described as people participating in the **2013 Dialog Retreat**, used as references or social proof for the 2014 invitees. It is a participant claim in Dialog’s own email, not independent proof that every listed person ultimately attended. **Tier P as to the claim; attendance not independently verified.**

A September 2013 email shows biotechnology executive **Boris Nikolic** forwarding Jeffrey Epstein material from **Steve Wayne** concerning a Dialog retreat in Turkey and a breakout titled “Foreign policy implications for a computational currency like bitcoin.” This proves Epstein received material about a Dialog session; it does not prove he attended. **Tier P/J, September 2013.** [Miami New Times document review](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/news/peter-thiels-secret-society-turns-up-in-epstein-files-40559378/)

### Operating model

The record indicates that Dialog evolved into two related products:

1. **Membership:** private home dinners, global trips or “treks,” concierge services, a private chat, networking introductions, and dating or matchmaking.
2. **Retreats:** three- to four-day events of roughly 100–240 people historically and more than 200 in the planned 2026 event. Retreat participants need not be members.

Internal material reviewed by WIRED in June 2026 claimed more than **1,000 paying members** and more than **2,500 historical retreat participants**. Byline Times reported a target of 1,300 members by the end of 2026. In Irish litigation, Stonebrick itself said Dialog had about 1,000 members and held three large retreats annually, giving the approximate membership figure a litigation-record foundation. **Tier J/L, June–July 2026.** [WIRED ranking investigation](https://www.wired.com/story/how-peter-thiels-private-dialog-club-secretly-ranks-its-members/), [Irish court reporting](https://www.laois-nationalist.ie/news/powerscourt-conference-cancelled-after-threats-of-protests-and-blockades-court-hears_arid-106405.html)

### Pricing

- The 2012 invitation states that there were no sponsors, that participants paid retreat fees, and that prices rose as the event approached. **Tier P.**
- In February 2022, Columbia statistician **Andrew Gelman** published his invitation to the 1–4 December 2022 retreat at the Ritz-Carlton Bacara in Santa Barbara. The registration page displayed **$16,846**, reduced by **70 percent** with his code. Dialog told him it was “100% participant funded” and that everyone attending paid. **Tier P/S, published by the recipient in 2022.** [Gelman’s invitation and screenshots](https://statmodeling.stat.columbia.edu/2022/02/16/hey-i-got-an-exclusive-invitation-to-this-off-the-record-conference-but-i-think-ill-take-1907-jamaican-beef-patties-instead/)
- The 2026 database showed that grades affected price: lower-ranked attendees were placed on full-price tiers much more often than high-status invitees, and some charges reached tens of thousands of dollars. **Tier J from authenticated leaked records, WIRED, 18 June 2026.**
- Former European Parliament member **Marietje Schaake** told Byline Times she did not recall paying for her 2019 Venice retreat and believed her flight and hotel were covered. This conflicts with Dialog’s “everyone pays” statement, or suggests comping through discounts or another payer. **Tier S/J, 22 June 2026.**

### Ranking and engineered introductions

WIRED examined 192 internal dossiers:

- 130 were marked members; the rest included prospects labeled “First Time Dialoger” or “Warm.”
- Every dossier was assigned **A, B, or C**, with **C** apparently reserved for the most famous or influential.
- 141 of 192 were B; about one in seven were C.
- Staff recorded separate “value-add” scores from 1 to 4 and a “moderation tier.”
- Reasons for exclusion included “Value Add Too Low,” “Poor Culture Fit,” and “Grade Fell Too Low.”
- Staff performed “post-retreat code reviews.”
- An AI system helped prepare at least 26 dossiers, but staff could override it.
- Matching and seating considered wealth, public recognition, professional relevance, existing relationships, and do-not-pair rules.

These are **Tier J from internal records independently analyzed by WIRED, 18 June 2026**, not rumor. [WIRED ranking investigation](https://www.wired.com/story/how-peter-thiels-private-dialog-club-secretly-ranks-its-members/)

### Matchmaking and romantic data

The central claim in the item brief is substantiated, with qualifications.

The 2026 participant form asked whether a person was **“looking for love”** and offered “Single Man,” “Single Woman,” and “Other” respondents inclusion in “future matchmaking.” A separate `dating.dialog.org` application advertised “meaningful connections for exceptional people.” Roughly 10 percent of respondents opted into a singles pool. More than three-quarters of the records examined had algorithmically proposed matches, which staff appeared to refine manually. Some matching was professional; some was expressly romantic. **Tier J based on authenticated leaked forms and databases, WIRED, 16 and 18 June 2026.** [WIRED leak report](https://www.wired.com/story/leak-exposes-members-of-peter-thiels-secretive-dialog-society/)

What is **not** established:

- The exposed fields do not by themselves demonstrate collection of sexual orientation, sexual history, or intimate conduct beyond self-selected relationship/matchmaking categories.
- No evidence was located that the information was sold, supplied to Thiel, supplied to an intelligence agency, or used for blackmail.
- Byline Times’ suggestion that confessional information *could* be leverage, and security analyst Pierluigi Paganini’s warning that foreign intelligence services *could* exploit it, are risk assessments—not evidence that exploitation occurred. **Tier J opinion/speculation, not fact.**

### 2025 expansion plans

Axios reported on 7 August 2025 that Dialog was discussing purchase of land in Virginia near Washington, D.C., for a permanent campus and was considering acquisition of another membership organization. Axios’s source described plans, not a completed conveyance. No deed or corporate filing confirming a completed purchase was located. Claims that Dialog “purchased land in 2025” overstate the sourced record. **Tier J for negotiations; Tier N for a verified completed acquisition.** [Axios investigation](https://www.axios.com/2025/08/07/dialog-secret-network-thiel-hoffman)

### The 2026 leak

Two datasets must not be conflated:

1. **Public website directory:** roughly 113 names were embedded in `dialog.org` source code and accessible to anyone viewing the page source. Researcher **maia arson crimew** publicized it on 15 June 2026. WIRED and the Guardian independently verified the original material.
2. **2026 registration database:** a separate source provided WIRED with 222 registrants, including membership status, guest type, attendance history, biography, personal contact information, emergency contacts, allergies, political leaning, and access tokens.

Dialog reportedly stored records in **Airtable**. WIRED declined to publish credentials and private details. **Tier J, independently verified technical reporting, 16–18 June 2026.**

Stonebrick later alleged in Irish litigation that it had suffered a **“cyber-attack.”** That characterization is a **Tier L allegation**. Journalistic testing found at least the website directory openly available without authentication; thus “hacked” is not established as the sole or necessary mechanism of exposure.

### Ireland cancellation and lawsuit

Dialog Global 2026 was scheduled for **12–16/17 August 2026** at Powerscourt Hotel, Enniskerry, County Wicklow. After publication of the roster and agenda, Irish politicians and activists objected, principally over Thiel’s Palantir associations and secrecy surrounding attendance. Senator **Lynn Ruane**, who had accepted an invitation, withdrew and apologized for inadequate due diligence.

On **3 July 2026**, Powerscourt Hotel and Powerscourt Estate confirmed the event would not occur there. **Tier P/S, venue statements reported by RTÉ.** [RTÉ cancellation report](https://www.rte.ie/news/2026/0703/1581622-powerscourt-dialog/)

Stonebrick LLC, trading as Dialog, then sued:

- **Sugarloaf Ventures Ltd**, trading as Powerscourt Hotel;
- **Powerscourt Estates Ltd**;
- **Scalaheen Ltd**.

Stonebrick alleged breach of contract and wrongful inducement of breach. It said cancellation created approximately **€1.9 million** in refund exposure, that the retreat was a material source of annual revenue, and that 33 attendees had canceled. The hotel characterized the cancellation as force majeure after abusive communications and threats of protest and blockade. These remain opposing litigation positions. **Tier L, July 2026.**

On **2 September 2026**, Justice **Eileen Roberts** allowed the matter to proceed in Ireland’s Commercial Court. No final judgment or settlement was located by the research cut-off. **Tier J on public proceedings.** [Commercial Court update](https://thecurrency.news/articles/238462/dialog-case-against-powerscourt-hotel-to-be-heard-before-commercial-court/)

Reports said Dialog found or sought another location, but no reliable public record established where—or conclusively whether—the August retreat occurred. **Tier N/open question.**

## 3. The Network

### Source codes for the register

- **D1:** Name appeared in the June 2026 website-source directory. Original verified by WIRED and the Guardian; the list did not reliably distinguish members, invitees, speakers, or former attendees. **Tier J/P-derived, 15–20 June 2026.**
- **D1m:** Name appears in a preserved GitHub mirror claiming to reproduce the directory. This is a secondary archive, not pristine primary evidence. It contains at least one apparent role-label transposition and a discrepancy over Roger Myerson, whom WIRED described as absent from the public directory. **Tier J/archival lead.** [Preserved directory](https://github.com/nzaki-dev/dialog/blob/main/README.md)
- **R26:** Named in WIRED’s separately sourced 2026 registration records. A registration is stronger than directory appearance but does not prove eventual attendance, especially after cancellation. **Tier J, June 2026.**
- **H13:** Named in the 2012 invitation as a participant in the 2013 retreat and offered as a reference for 2014 invitees. **Tier P as to Dialog’s contemporaneous representation; actual attendance not independently checked.**
- **C:** Participation confirmed by the person or representative.
- **X:** Person denied membership or attendance, or the record was corrected.

## 4. Money and Power

### Documented inflows

- Membership dues and retreat registration charges are the principal documented revenue source.
- The 2022 advertised retreat price was $16,846 before individualized discounts.
- Internal records show prices altered by status grading and ability or willingness to pay.
- Dialog told Andrew Gelman it was entirely participant-funded.
- The 2012 invitation said there were no sponsors.
- Dialog’s present website says it accepts no donations or investments.

### Documented outflows or liabilities

- Venue rental, travel or hotel support for selected invitees, event staff, security, database and application services, and concierge/networking operations are evident categories, but no audited accounts are public.
- Schaake believed her 2019 flight and hotel were paid, showing that at least some costs may have been absorbed by Dialog or a third party.
- Stonebrick claimed approximately €1.9 million in refund liability from the Powerscourt cancellation.
- Stonebrick said Dialog Global was a material source of annual revenue.

### Peter Thiel’s financing

No grant, investment, loan, sponsorship amount, tax filing, bank record, or contract showing that Peter Thiel funded Dialog was located.

The record supports:

- Thiel’s cofounder status;
- use of his name on invitations;
- occasional reported attendance;
- association with numerous invitees through other companies and political networks.

The record cuts against claims of continuing financial or managerial control:

- 2012: Ian Osborne wrote that Thiel did not attend and should stop the organizers using his name.
- 2018–2024 attendees said they did not see him.
- Sophia Bush said she later learned he had not been involved for about 15 years.
- Byline Times sources described Dialog as Hoffman-run and operationally separate from Thiel.
- Stonebrick’s Irish pleading said Thiel had no involvement organizing the Powerscourt event.

Therefore “Thiel-funded Dialog” is a common journalistic shorthand, but **not established by a disclosed money trail**. **Tier N/open financial question.**

### Ownership and nonprofit status

Dialog is not documented as a foundation or public charity. The operator is a private LLC; its accounts and owners’ economics are not publicly disclosed like Form 990 filings. A similarly named **Dialogue Foundation** in IRS databases publishes a Mormon periodical and is unrelated.

### Power mechanism

Dialog’s demonstrable power lies in selection and access:

1. nominees are screened and graded;
2. wealth, fame, office, institutional position, and perceived “value add” influence status and price;
3. seating and introductions are algorithmically suggested and manually curated;
4. private dinners and treks extend connections beyond the annual retreat;
5. off-record rules reduce external visibility;
6. staff track which relationships already exist and which pairings should be avoided.

No evidence establishes that Dialog formally commands participants, bargains collectively, directs government policy, or exercises security clearances. The documented mechanism is curated social access, not a proven chain of command.

## 5. Ideas and Programs

Dialog is not a research institute and has no located record of original cybernetics, AI, behavioral-science, or human-subject experiments.

Its program is a method of elite convening:

- moderated small groups rather than plenary speeches;
- agenda construction around participant interests;
- assigned seating;
- off-record or non-attribution rules;
- “Soapbox” sessions and workshops;
- ideological and professional diversity as an organizational claim;
- personal discussions involving relationships, family, sex, caregiving, mental health, religion, mortality, and the afterlife;
- topical discussions involving AI, regulation, energy, nuclear power, war, Iran, NATO, battlefield technologies, political realignment, and institutional trust.

The 2026 leaked schedule included titles such as:

- “Money (Does?) Buy Happiness”
- “Bring Back Nuclear”
- “Navigating WWIII”
- “Battlefield Technologies”
- “How’s Your Sex Life?”
- “Build-a-Cult”
- “Build-a-Party”

The provocative titles establish proposed subject matter, not the opinions, agreements, or conduct of attendees.

### Human-subject and behavioral-research question

No research protocol, institutional review board record, experiment, clinical study, or consent process was located. Dialog’s collection and use of personal data for matching is a commercial/community data practice, not documented scientific research. Characterizing it as an experiment on human subjects would exceed the evidence.

### Comparison with Edge dinners

**Similarities**

- both mix technology executives, investors, government figures, scientists, writers, and cultural figures;
- both use private dinners and exclusive invitations as cross-sector networking mechanisms;
- both grew around a powerful connector: Auren Hoffman for Dialog, literary agent **John Brockman** for Edge;
- both offered intellectual programming alongside social access.

**Differences**

- Edge operated through the tax-exempt **Edge Foundation Inc.**, maintained a public intellectual website, published many event accounts, and filed public Form 990-PF returns. Dialog operates through a private LLC with much less financial disclosure. [Edge Foundation IRS data](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/133528667)
- Edge’s dinners often piggybacked on TED and publicly celebrated attendees, although some names were omitted. Dialog’s principal retreats were systematically off-record and historically did not publish rosters.
- Jeffrey Epstein was a documented Edge donor and participant: media and nonprofit filings indicate approximately **$638,000** in donations from his foundations between 2001 and 2015; emails document post-conviction invitations and attendance. No equivalent donation or attendance has been established for Dialog. [Guardian’s Edge investigation](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/feb/03/epstein-silicon-valley)
- No documentary evidence was found that Edge Foundation created, funded, controlled, or merged with Dialog. Overlapping attendees and the broad salon model are not proof of an institutional tie.

## 6. Scandal, Controversy and Contest

### Privacy and security failure

**Documented:** Dialog exposed a directory through website source code and, in separate records, sensitive participant data including personal contact details, political leanings, emergency contacts, allergies, attendance history, rankings, and matchmaking selections. Access tokens were also exposed. **Tier J, WIRED, June 2026.**

**Disputed:** Stonebrick calls the event a cyberattack. Journalists found at least part of the material openly retrievable without authentication. The precise acquisition route for the separately supplied registration database has not been publicly established.

**Outcome:** Public exposure, participant controversy, withdrawal by invitees, cancellation of the Irish venue, and litigation.

### Secret grading and differential pricing

**Documented:** A/B/C grades, status criteria, “value-add” scores, post-retreat reviews, political classifications, manual price adjustments, AI-assisted dossiers, and exclusion rules. **Tier J from leaked internal records.**

**Contested interpretation:** Calling this a caste system or social-engineering operation is interpretive. The underlying ranking and matching machinery is documented.

### Political and regulatory proximity

The roster places data brokers, defense contractors, financiers, cabinet officers, legislators, military commanders, journalists, regulators, and academics within one off-record network. This is a documented structural fact. Claims that a particular contract, regulatory decision, military operation, or political appointment resulted from Dialog require separate evidence; none was located.

### Ireland controversy

Opponents argued the conference should not be held because of Thiel’s politics and Palantir’s relationships with the Israeli and U.S. militaries. Those are political arguments about an associated cofounder and company, not proof that Dialog itself contracted with a military or participated in warfare.

The hotel cancellation produced an unresolved contract dispute. As of the cut-off:

- Stonebrick alleges wrongful cancellation and inducement of breach.
- The hotel invokes force majeure and security/protest concerns.
- No court has determined liability.

### Epstein narrative

**Documented**

- Epstein received two forwarded Dialog invitations in November 2012.
- He advised Lisa Randall that Sundance was pleasant and she should go.
- Boris Nikolic forwarded him Dialog bitcoin-session materials in 2013.
- Epstein later discussed an unspecified “secret society idea” with Joi Ito and referenced Thiel.

**Not documented**

- Epstein’s attendance at Dialog.
- Epstein’s membership.
- Epstein funding Dialog.
- Dialog facilitating abuse or trafficking.
- Dialog’s dating program supplying partners to Epstein.
- A Dialog blackmail operation.
- A shared Edge–Dialog institution controlled by Epstein.

### Intelligence-agency narrative

Documented participant records include former or serving intelligence, defense, military, national-security, law-enforcement, and oversight figures, including Turki Al Faisal Al Saud, Alexus Grynkewich, Stanley McChrystal, Dan Driscoll, Jim Himes, Hallie Hoffman, and others.

No primary record was located establishing Dialog as a CIA, NSA, FBI, Mossad, Saudi intelligence, NATO, or military program. No security-clearance sponsorship, classified contract, FOIA release, appropriations record, or agency charter for Dialog was found. Claims that it is an intelligence front remain **Tier R/N**.

### MKUltra, cybernetics, and Kaczynski

The requested contested-material search found no documentary connection between Dialog and:

- MKUltra;
- Theodore Kaczynski;
- Henry Murray’s Harvard research;
- the Macy Conferences;
- Norbert Wiener’s cybernetics program;
- LSD research;
- organized crime;
- human-subject experimentation.

Popular videos appear to connect Dialog to this history through Peter Thiel, data companies, Edge-style salons, Epstein, and overlapping technological elites. That is a chain of association, not a documented institutional lineage. The video title supplied in the brief was not located in reliably indexed sources, so its exact narration could not be audited line by line.

### “The people who rule the world”

This narrative circulated on Reddit and similar forums after the leak. The evidence supports a private transnational network containing powerful individuals. It does not establish unified ideology, collective decision-making, or a controlling world government. Public denials and sharply opposed political views among attendees contradict a simple unitary-command account. **Tier R, with underlying roster fact distinguished from the narrative.**

## 7. Reception and Afterlife

Before 2025, Dialog’s public footprint consisted mainly of invitations, participant biographies, occasional attendee references, and archived event pages.

- **2016:** Michael Lazerow publicly mentioned a Utah Dialog retreat while discussing his disagreement with Thiel.
- **2022:** Andrew Gelman published and mocked the pricing and exclusivity of his invitation.
- **2025:** Axios first gave the network broad national attention through its report on a proposed Washington-area campus.
- **June 2026:** WIRED’s two reports converted Dialog from a little-known retreat into a major privacy, influence-network, and data-security story.
- **20 June 2026:** The Guardian placed the directory in the context of transnational elite networking and corrected an initial implication concerning Jeffrey Epstein: the historical “Jeff Epstein” participant was Oracle’s former CFO.
- **22 June 2026:** Byline Times argued that Dialog was principally a Hoffman-run influence network trading on Thiel’s name, rather than an organization personally directed by Thiel.
- **July 2026:** Irish reporting centered on protest, the Powerscourt cancellation, Palantir, and the resulting legal dispute.
- **August 2026:** Ezra Klein reiterated that his experience was of a conference, not a secret society, and that Thiel was absent.
- **September 2026:** the Commercial Court proceeding made Stonebrick’s legal identity, approximate membership, retreat frequency, and claimed financial exposure part of the public record.

The public image consequently moved through three stages:

1. obscure off-record retreat;
2. “Thiel secret society” after the leak;
3. a more qualified picture of a large, fee-based Hoffman-operated network that uses status ranking and engineered introductions, while retaining Thiel’s name and substantial overlap with his wider social and business world.

## 9. Findings

1. **Founding:** 2006 is the supported date. The suggested 2015 date is not.
2. **Legal vehicle:** Stonebrick LLC, controlled or owned by Auren Hoffman, operates as Dialog. No related “Dialog Foundation” was found.
3. **Thiel:** cofounder status is documented. Continuing management and funding are not; several records indicate long-term distance from operations.
4. **Matchmaking:** documented, not rumor. Dialog collected opt-ins for romantic introductions and operated a dating application, alongside professional matching.
5. **Sensitive-data collection:** documented. The database included political, personal, emergency, health-related, attendance, ranking, and relationship fields.
6. **Leak mechanics:** public exposure is documented; Stonebrick’s characterization of a cyberattack remains a litigation allegation.
7. **Membership lists:** directory appearance, registration, invitation, and attendance are distinct. Reporting that treats every listed person as an active member is unreliable.
8. **Epstein:** documentary contact exists through forwarded invitations and retreat material. Attendance, membership, funding, abuse, or operational involvement are unproved.
9. **Intelligence/MKUltra/cybernetics:** powerful national-security figures attended or were listed, but no institutional CIA, MKUltra, Kaczynski, or cybernetics-program link was located.
10. **Edge comparison:** structurally useful but not genealogical. Edge has a documented nonprofit vehicle and Epstein funding; Dialog does not.
11. **Money:** fees, rankings, discounts, and litigation exposure are documented. Full income, profits, ownership economics, and payer identities remain private.
12. **Legal status:** the Powerscourt dispute remained pending as of 28 September 2026.
