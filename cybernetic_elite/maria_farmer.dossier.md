# Maria Farmer: Research Dossier

*Record current through September 28, 2026.*

## Evidentiary key

- **[P — primary record]** Court filing, sworn affidavit, police/FBI record, government report, institutional record, or contemporaneous document. A filing proves that a statement was made, not necessarily that it was true.
- **[L — litigation allegation]** Alleged in a complaint, claim, or affidavit but not finally adjudicated.
- **[J — reported]** Reported by identified journalists, ordinarily after interviews or document review.
- **[S — subject account]** Claimed by Maria Farmer in an interview or recorded conversation.
- **[D — disputed/denied]** Expressly contradicted by another participant.
- **[R — rumor/conspiracy]** Circulated without adequate documentary corroboration.
- **[N — negative finding]** No reliable record supporting the asserted connection was located.

## 1. Identification

Maria Kristine Farmer is an American figurative artist, born November 28, 1969, and the author of the earliest presently documented complaint to the Federal Bureau of Investigation concerning Jeffrey Edward Epstein. A Santa Clara University commencement record places “Maria Kristine Farmer” in its 1992 graduating class; court records say she moved to New York in 1993 and received an MFA from the New York Academy of Art in 1995. Her October 2023 federal claim listed her as single. No documented alias, spouse, child, corporate office, investment fund, board seat, government position, security clearance, or intelligence-agency employment was found. [[P] Santa Clara University, 1992](https://cdm17268.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/commencement/id/84/download); [[P/L] 2023 SF-95 claim](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Maria-Farmer-FTCA-Claim-Form-2023.pdf); [[P/L] amended estate complaint, March 9, 2020](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/farmer-v-indyke-119-cv-10474-sdny-2019-022.pdf)

Farmer’s historical significance rests on five overlapping roles:

1. an artist whom Epstein employed as an art scout, purchaser, receptionist, and general assistant in the mid-1990s;
2. a woman who alleges that Epstein and Ghislaine Maxwell sexually assaulted her in Ohio in 1996;
3. the older sister of Annie Farmer, who later testified at Maxwell’s criminal trial;
4. the complainant behind an FBI intake dated September 3, 1996 and categorized as “child pornography”; and
5. a litigant and public advocate challenging institutional failures by the FBI, Justice Department, media, and art world.

The recently released FBI record substantially confirms the narrow but crucial proposition that federal law enforcement received her warning in 1996. It does not, by itself, prove every later detail of her account. Her alleged Ohio assault was never tried, and the confidential payment she later accepted from the Epstein Victims’ Compensation Program was not an admission of liability.

## 2. The Record

### Origins, education, and art

Farmer was raised in Kentucky in a family that, according to her 2020 complaint, experienced financial difficulty after her parents divorced. Journalistic accounts identify her parents as Frank Farmer and Janice Farmer and describe two younger sisters and two younger brothers. The primary record establishes her date of birth and Kentucky citizenship but is less specific about her precise birthplace. [[P/L] amended complaint, 2020](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/farmer-v-indyke-119-cv-10474-sdny-2019-022.pdf)

After graduating from Santa Clara University in 1992, she entered the New York Academy of Art and completed its figurative-art MFA program in 1995. That summer she attended a Santa Fe Art Institute workshop associated with painter Eric Fischl. [[P] Santa Clara commencement program, 1992](https://cdm17268.contentdm.oclc.org/digital/api/collection/commencement/id/84/download); [[J] Artnet, August 26, 2019](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/maria-farmer-new-york-art-academy-1610506)

Farmer has said she was selling paintings for more than $20,000 before working for Epstein. The best-documented transaction is more complicated: she says a German buyer had offered $12,000 for a thesis-show painting, but Epstein obtained it for $6,000 after promising to help her career. Farmer alleged that then-dean Eileen Guggenheim pressured her to accept the discounted sale. Guggenheim denied forcing a sale and later denied making the introduction. [[S/J/D] ABC News, January 9, 2020](https://abcnews.com/US/jeffrey-epstein-survivor-paints-portraits-survivors-happened/story?id=68148224); [[J/D] Artnet, August 26, 2019](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/maria-farmer-new-york-art-academy-1610506)

### Entry into the Epstein household

Farmer met Epstein and Maxwell at, or in connection with, her 1995 MFA thesis exhibition. Epstein bought her painting and subsequently employed her. According to her sworn affidavit, civil complaints, and interviews, her duties progressed from recommending and purchasing art to receiving visitors and keeping entry records at Epstein’s Manhattan residence. She has identified Chuck Bowdish and Damian Loeb as artists whose work she persuaded Epstein to purchase. The amount of her salary has not been published. [[P/L] 2019 affidavit](https://games-cdn.washingtonpost.com/notes/prod/default/documents/70dad243-9cd9-4e60-b745-e7d4563d41bb/note/5e3b63a6-2013-400e-acb2-47a16b554b49.pdf); [[J] Artnet, 2019](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/maria-farmer-new-york-art-academy-1610506)

Farmer says she saw school-aged girls—some in uniforms—enter and be escorted upstairs. Maxwell allegedly described them as candidates for Victoria’s Secret modeling work and called some “nubiles.” Farmer says she accompanied Maxwell on drives during which Maxwell approached girls in public places. These are Farmer’s observations, recorded under oath and in interviews; no contemporaneous visitor ledger produced by Farmer has been publicly authenticated. L Brands subsequently said it did not believe Epstein was ever employed by or authorized to represent the company. [[P/L] Giuffre complaint quoting Farmer affidavit, April 16, 2019](https://openargs.com/wp-content/uploads/Giuffre-v.-Dershowitz.pdf); [[S/J/D] CBS News, November 19, 2019](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/jeffrey-epstein-accuser-maria-farmer-says-ghislaine-maxwell-threatened-her-life-after-assault-fbi-failed/); [[S/J/D] ABC News, 2020](https://abcnews.com/US/jeffrey-epstein-survivor-paints-portraits-survivors-happened/story?id=68148224)

Farmer also says Epstein showed her a room containing screens fed by cameras in bedrooms, bathrooms, showers, and toilets, told her the house was extensively recorded, and said recordings were kept in a safe. This is a repeated subject account and a litigation allegation, not a publicly authenticated archive of 1995–96 recordings. [[S] CBS News, 2019](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/jeffrey-epstein-accuser-maria-farmer-says-ghislaine-maxwell-threatened-her-life-after-assault-fbi-failed/); [[S] ABC News, 2020](https://abcnews.com/US/jeffrey-epstein-survivor-paints-portraits-survivors-happened/story?id=68148224)

### Annie Farmer

Epstein paid for Annie Farmer, Maria’s teenage sister, to visit New York and later his New Mexico ranch. Maria’s complaint alleges that Epstein groped Annie during a film and that Epstein and Maxwell subsequently abused her in New Mexico. Annie later gave her own account under oath and testified at Maxwell’s criminal trial. Maria’s original introduction of Annie to Epstein later became a point of hostile scrutiny; it does not establish that Maria knew Epstein’s intentions. [[L] amended complaint, 2020](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/farmer-v-indyke-119-cv-10474-sdny-2019-022.pdf); [[P — defense filing identifying Annie as alleged Minor Victim 2] Maxwell subpoena motion, 2021](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/United%20States%20v.%20Maxwell%2C%20No.%20120-cr-00330%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202020%29/336.pdf)

### The Ohio residence and Leslie Wexner

In summer 1996, while producing paintings commissioned for the film *As Good as It Gets*, Farmer accepted Epstein’s offer to work at a large New Albany, Ohio house. Her pleadings repeatedly call it a guesthouse on Leslie Wexner’s estate. Property records reviewed by *The Washington Post*, however, showed that Wexner had deeded that house to Epstein in 1992: Epstein owned the house in 1996. The practical separation was less clear. It was surrounded by Wexner-developed land, and a former security officer told the newspaper that it was effectively the main estate’s guesthouse and guarded by Wexner personnel. [[J] Washington Post, October 5, 2019](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/economy/epstein-accuser-holds-victorias-secret-billionaire-responsible-as-he-keeps-his-distance/2019/10/05/1b6baf6c-d0d3-11e9-b29b-a528dc82154a_story.html)

Farmer obtained an Ohio driver’s license carrying the nearby Wexner-mansion address, reportedly so she could collect Epstein and Maxwell from the airport. She says security personnel required her to obtain permission before leaving or even jogging outside. A former security employee corroborated the armed security, guard dogs, and plausibility of requiring permission, but said he did not remember the alleged assault or confinement. [[J/D] Washington Post, 2019](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/economy/epstein-accuser-holds-victorias-secret-billionaire-responsible-as-he-keeps-his-distance/2019/10/05/1b6baf6c-d0d3-11e9-b29b-a528dc82154a_story.html)

Farmer alleges that on Epstein and Maxwell’s final visit, Epstein requested a foot massage and the pair then physically and sexually assaulted her. She escaped the room and barricaded herself elsewhere in the house. She says local emergency calls were hung up or placed on hold; someone told her law enforcement “worked for Wexner”; security refused to let her leave for approximately twelve hours; and her father eventually drove from Kentucky to retrieve her. Her father confirmed receiving a distressed call and encountering difficulty at the gate. The Franklin County Sheriff’s Office confirmed that deputies worked contract security for Wexner but had no surviving record of Farmer’s reported call; its retention policy would not have preserved such records for more than two years. [[L/J/D] amended complaint](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/farmer-v-indyke-119-cv-10474-sdny-2019-022.pdf); [[J] Washington Post, 2019](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/economy/epstein-accuser-holds-victorias-secret-billionaire-responsible-as-he-keeps-his-distance/2019/10/05/1b6baf6c-d0d3-11e9-b29b-a528dc82154a_story.html)

Leslie and Abigail Wexner said they had no knowledge of Farmer before the press coverage, had never met or spoken with her, and had never discussed her with Epstein. Farmer herself told CBS that she never saw Leslie Wexner. She claims she spoke to someone she understood to be Abigail Wexner by telephone; the Wexners deny it was Abigail. No evidence located shows that either Wexner personally participated in, ordered, or knew of the alleged assault or confinement. [[D/J] CBS News, 2019](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/jeffrey-epstein-accuser-maria-farmer-says-ghislaine-maxwell-threatened-her-life-after-assault-fbi-failed/); [[D/J] Washington Post, 2019](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/economy/epstein-accuser-holds-victorias-secret-billionaire-responsible-as-he-keeps-his-distance/2019/10/05/1b6baf6c-d0d3-11e9-b29b-a528dc82154a_story.html)

### Threats, photographs, and the 1996 reports

After Ohio, Farmer says Epstein and Maxwell retained or stole photographs and negatives of her minor sisters, including partially clothed images made as studies for her art. She alleges that Maxwell threatened that her artwork and career would be destroyed and later warned that there were many ways to die near the West Side Highway. The alleged sexual assault and Maxwell’s threats were not recorded in the surviving short FBI synopsis, but the theft of the sisters’ images and a threat to burn Farmer’s house were. [[P/L] FBI FD-71, September 3, 1996](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%204/EFTA00006107.pdf); [[S/L] CBS News, 2019](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/jeffrey-epstein-accuser-maria-farmer-says-ghislaine-maxwell-threatened-her-life-after-assault-fbi-failed/)

The documentary sequence is:

1. **August 29, 1996 — NYPD.** A complaint report identified in Farmer’s 2023 claim as No. 1996-0067241 recorded aggravated-harassment allegations: Epstein allegedly threatened to burn her paintings and send her photographs of the burned works, and possessed keys to her apartment. The NYPD reportedly referred the out-of-state and federal allegations to the FBI. The full original report is not presently in the public court exhibit set, but its text appears in the sworn federal claim and was independently obtained by CBS in 2025. [[P/L] 2023 FTCA claim](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Maria-Farmer-FTCA-Claim-Form-2023.pdf)

2. **September 3, 1996 — FBI FD-71.** The now-public intake is headed “child pornography.” It records that a professional artist said Epstein stole photographs and negatives of her 12- and 16-year-old sisters; she believed he sold them to “potential buyers”; Epstein had asked her to photograph girls at swimming pools; and he threatened to burn her house if she disclosed the matter. The complainant’s name is redacted in the released form, but Farmer’s counsel has confirmed it is her complaint, and a congressional letter treats it as such. [[P] DOJ-released FD-71](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/files/DataSet%204/EFTA00006107.pdf); [[P/J] House Oversight Democrats letter, December 23, 2025](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/imo/media/doc/garcia_et_al_to_doj_oig_re_maria_farmer.pdf)

3. **What the short intake does not say.** It does not name Maxwell, memorialize the Ohio assault, describe a trafficking ring, identify the “potential buyers,” or name Bill Clinton or Donald Trump. Farmer says she supplied all or some of that information orally and that an FBI agent hung up mid-call. Those broader details remain Farmer’s allegation; they are not captured in the released one-page intake. [[S/L/J] Time, December 20, 2025](https://time.com/7342045/epstein-files-release-trump-bill-clinton-maria-farmer-1996-redactions/); [[L] 2025 complaint](https://www.jamesmarshlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Doc-1-Complaint-05-29-2025.pdf)

4. **No documented 1996 follow-up.** No evidence of a substantive investigation, interview, search, or referral following the intake has emerged. Farmer says the FBI did not contact her again until 2006. A 1997–98 personal journal says she had “turn[ed] them into the FBI”; 2006 FBI notes record that the NYPD Sixth Precinct told “MF” to call the FBI. [[P/L] 2023 federal claim](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Maria-Farmer-FTCA-Claim-Form-2023.pdf); [[P] FBI Vault, Jeffrey Epstein Part 18](https://vault.fbi.gov/jeffrey-epstein/Jeffrey%20Epstein%20Part%2018/view)

The release therefore verifies a 1996 FBI warning but not every allegation Farmer says she conveyed. It also leaves unexplained what, if anything, agents did with the intake.

### Vanity Fair, 2002–03

The video proposition supplied in the brief—Farmer and Annie went on the record to Vicky Ward in 2002 and their allegations were removed from the resulting article—is **substantially supported**.

Maria Farmer, Annie Farmer, and their mother spoke on the record to Ward in 2002. Ward also contacted David Schafer and Eric Fischl; Fischl later said Maria had called from Ohio in distress and that he urged her to leave. The profile published as “The Talented Mr. Epstein” in March 2003 omitted the sisters’ claims. [[J] New Yorker, February 8, 2022](https://www.newyorker.com/news/annals-of-communications/why-didnt-vanity-fair-break-the-jeffrey-epstein-story); [[P — published article] Vanity Fair, March 2003](https://www.vanityfair.com/news/2003/03/jeffrey-epstein-200303)

Why they were omitted is contested:

- **Ward’s version:** Epstein threatened her, communicated with editor Graydon Carter, and persuaded Carter to remove the allegations.
- **Carter’s versions:** Ward introduced inadequately substantiated allegations too late; editorial, research, and legal staff did not trust the material; later he conceded that emails showed the allegations had been presented weeks before closing.
- **Documentary correction:** Emails reviewed by *The New Yorker* show Ward had indeed supplied the allegations in early December 2002, contradicting Carter’s “last-minute” account.
- **Ward’s own later conduct:** In 2011 she published a largely flattering discussion of Epstein and Maxwell, said she had been unsure whom to believe, and referred euphemistically to Epstein’s sexual conduct. Ward later blamed editors, but her draft contained substantially the same language. Her draft included an abbreviated version of the Farmer allegations, while omitting serious elements of Annie’s account. [[J/D] New Yorker, 2022](https://www.newyorker.com/news/annals-of-communications/why-didnt-vanity-fair-break-the-jeffrey-epstein-story)

Thus, removal is documented; an Epstein-directed suppression order is plausible and supported by Ward and former contributor John Connolly, but is not conclusively proved. Farmer says the omission left Epstein and Maxwell aware she had spoken while depriving her of the protection of publication.

### 2019 affidavit and litigation

On April 12, 2019, Farmer signed a sworn affidavit, filed April 16 as an exhibit in *Giuffre v. Dershowitz*. She described her Epstein employment, school-aged visitors, her and Annie’s alleged assaults, threats, and the FBI report. It was her first major public, on-the-record court statement. [[P/L] affidavit](https://games-cdn.washingtonpost.com/notes/prod/default/documents/70dad243-9cd9-4e60-b745-e7d4563d41bb/note/5e3b63a6-2013-400e-acb2-47a16b554b49.pdf)

She also alleged that attorney Alan Dershowitz visited Epstein’s house and went upstairs while underage girls were present. Farmer did **not** allege that she personally witnessed Dershowitz assault anyone. Dershowitz denied her account and said he had not met Epstein before Farmer stopped working there. In December 2019, Judge Naomi Reice Buchwald struck the allegation from Farmer’s estate complaint because it was immaterial to her claims; the order expressly said it was a pleading ruling, not a determination of evidentiary truth. [[L/D] Giuffre complaint](https://openargs.com/wp-content/uploads/Giuffre-v.-Dershowitz.pdf); [[P] December 23, 2019 order](https://app.midpage.ai/document/farmer-v-indyke-1000118284404)

Farmer filed *Farmer v. Indyke*, No. 19-cv-10474, in November 2019 and amended it March 9, 2020. The defendants were Darren K. Indyke and Richard D. Kahn **in their capacities as executors of Epstein’s estate**.

**Correction to the item brief:** Maria Farmer did not name Ghislaine Maxwell as a defendant in this action. Maxwell appeared in the factual allegations, but the lawsuit was against the estate’s executors. Annie Farmer’s parallel case, No. 19-cv-10475, did name Maxwell. [[P/L] Maria’s amended complaint](https://media-cdn.rollcall.com/epstein-files/farmer-v-indyke-119-cv-10474-sdny-2019-022.pdf)

Maria alleged battery and intentional infliction of emotional distress and placed more than $75,000 in controversy, without stating a specific demand. On April 1, 2021, the parties dismissed the action with prejudice after she accepted an offer from the Epstein Victims’ Compensation Program. The payment amount remains confidential. Acceptance was not a judicial finding that the allegations were true, and dismissal was not an exoneration of the estate. [[P] joint dismissal, April 1, 2021](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/Farmer%20v.%20Indyke%2C%20No.%20119-cv-10474%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202019%29/033.pdf)

Maria did not testify at Maxwell’s 2021 criminal trial. Annie did. Maxwell’s defense sought subpoenas for Maria’s alleged physical evidence, including envelopes she said carried Epstein’s and Maxwell’s touch DNA. The public motion records the request and Farmer’s earlier claim that the FBI declined the envelopes; no public forensic test validating those claims was located. [[P/S] Maxwell subpoena motion](https://www.justice.gov/multimedia/Court%20Records/United%20States%20v.%20Maxwell%2C%20No.%20120-cr-00330%20%28S.D.N.Y.%202020%29/336.pdf)

### Art-world dispute

In 2019 Farmer accused the New York Academy of Art and Eileen Guggenheim of placing student access at the service of wealthy patrons, introducing her to Epstein, pressuring the discounted sale, taking her to his New Mexico ranch, and responding dismissively when she reported what happened in Ohio. Guggenheim denied forcing the sale, initially denied visiting the ranch, and disputed the timing and substance of the alleged disclosure. She later acknowledged visiting the ranch after another participant corroborated the trip. [[J/D] Artnet, 2019](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/maria-farmer-new-york-art-academy-1610506)

A 2020 investigation commissioned by the Academy and conducted by Walden Macht & Haran interviewed about twenty people and reviewed thousands of communications. It concluded that “critical aspects” of Farmer’s allegations against Guggenheim were untrue. It did not purport to disprove the Epstein/Maxwell assaults and said it presumed those allegations true. The report itself acknowledged it found no information proving or disproving the disputed Ohio telephone call. [[J/D] Artnet, June 30, 2020](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/new-york-academy-art-investigation-epstein-maria-farmer-1890683)

The investigation drew accusations of victim-blaming because it blamed Farmer for introducing Annie to Epstein and treated minor variations—daytime versus “dinner,” clothing colors, and which painting was sold—as credibility issues. After trustee resignations and a student-alumni protest, the Academy issued a “profound apology,” directed the firm to remove victim-blaming conclusions, and promised a $30,000 donation matching Epstein’s scholarship support to a survivor charity. [[J] Art Newspaper, August 10, 2020](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2020/08/10/new-york-academy-of-art-issues-apology-to-epstein-accuser-and-alumna-maria-farmer-after-claims-of-victim-blaming)

In March 2026 the Academy disclosed further post-conviction Epstein support and said it would donate another $65,900 to Girls Educational and Mentoring Services. It acknowledged “serious failures in judgment and governance.” This institutional reversal does not resolve every Farmer–Guggenheim factual disagreement, but it contradicts the Academy’s earlier effort to close the matter as essentially unfounded. [[J] Hyperallergic, March 16, 2026](https://hyperallergic.com/new-york-academy-of-art-says-it-will-donate-epstein-money/)

### Health and return to art

Farmer said the experience destroyed her confidence in painting and drove her from New York. She later sold antiques and restored houses. In 2019 she disclosed a rare brain tumor; in May 2020 *The Daily Beast* reported that she was also preparing for treatment for Hodgkin lymphoma. These are subject and journalistic reports, not published medical records. A survivor-organized fundraiser reportedly raised more than $43,000 for her treatment. [[S/J] Daily Beast, May 17, 2020](https://www.thedailybeast.com/epstein-victim-is-painting-his-enablers-like-ghislaine-maxwell-and-alan-dershowitz-as-murderous-lizard-people/)

Her return produced the pastel *Survivors Project*, *The Open Secret*, the large allegorical *Setiles*, and later *EPSTEIN’S DECK*. Her lizard, snake, flying-saucer, tarot, and “FBI diagram” images are satirical and allegorical. They are not evidence that the depicted subjects are literally nonhuman, occult actors, or extraterrestrials. [[S/J] ABC News, 2020](https://abcnews.com/US/jeffrey-epstein-survivor-paints-portraits-survivors-happened/story?id=68148224); [[S/J] Daily Beast, 2020](https://www.thedailybeast.com/epstein-victim-is-painting-his-enablers-like-ghislaine-maxwell-and-alan-dershowitz-as-murderous-lizard-people/)

### Federal claim and lawsuit

Farmer submitted federal administrative claims in 2022 and 2023. Her October 26, 2023 Standard Form 95 demanded **$600 million** in personal-injury damages from the FBI. That figure was a demand, not an award or valuation accepted by the government. [[P/L] SF-95](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Maria-Farmer-FTCA-Claim-Form-2023.pdf)

On May 29, 2025, she sued the United States under the Federal Tort Claims Act in the District of Columbia. The complaint pleads negligence and negligent infliction of emotional distress based on the alleged failures of the FBI, DOJ, and U.S. Attorney’s Offices. It seeks trial-determined damages rather than a judgment already fixed at $600 million. The government has not been found liable. [[P/L] complaint](https://www.jamesmarshlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Doc-1-Complaint-05-29-2025.pdf)

The complaint also documents her unsuccessful efforts to obtain records: a January 2025 FBI response estimated completion of a FOIA search in November 2027, while a December 2024 DOJ internal-affairs response said it had not opened an investigation but considered her complaint “addressed.” These statements appear as exhibits or allegations in her filing; no merits judgment has yet evaluated them. [[P/L] 2025 complaint](https://www.jamesmarshlaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Doc-1-Complaint-05-29-2025.pdf)

On September 25, 2026, Judge Reggie B. Walton ordered the case transferred to the Southern District of Florida because it substantially overlapped another survivor action previously transferred there. He did not decide whether the government was negligent. As of this dossier’s cutoff, the merits remain unresolved. [[P] transfer opinion](https://docs.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/district-of-columbia/dcdce/1%3A2025cv01709/281128/16)

### The 2020 OPR report: a necessary correction

The DOJ Office of Professional Responsibility’s November 2020 review concerned the Southern District of Florida’s **2006–08 investigation and non-prosecution agreement**. It found no professional misconduct by DOJ attorneys but found that Alexander Acosta exercised poor judgment and that victims were not treated with expected forthrightness and sensitivity. [[P] DOJ statement, November 12, 2020](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/statement-doj-office-professional-responsibility-report-jeffrey-epstein-2006-2008)

It did **not** investigate or document the FBI’s handling of Farmer’s 1996 intake. A December 2025 congressional letter expressly criticized the report for failing to mention the 1996 complaint and asked the DOJ Inspector General to investigate that omission and the original FBI failure. Therefore, describing the 1996 inaction as “documented in the OPR report” is inaccurate; what is documented is OPR’s omission of it. [[P] House Oversight Democrats letter, December 23, 2025](https://oversightdemocrats.house.gov/imo/media/doc/garcia_et_al_to_doj_oig_re_maria_farmer.pdf)

## 9. Findings

The strongest documented conclusions are:

1. Farmer worked for Epstein in the mid-1990s.
2. She was at the Epstein-owned New Albany house within the security environment surrounding Wexner’s properties.
3. She made an NYPD report in August 1996 and an FBI complaint recorded September 3, 1996.
4. The FBI complaint expressly concerned possible child pornography, stolen photographs of minor sisters, possible buyers, requested pool photographs, and threats.
5. No substantive 1996 FBI investigation has been documented.
6. Maria and Annie spoke on the record to Vicky Ward in 2002; their allegations were omitted from the March 2003 *Vanity Fair* profile.
7. The reason for the omission is disputed, and Ward’s later account contains documented inaccuracies.
8. Farmer’s allegations of assault, detention, blacklisting, and broader trafficking information remain allegations supported in parts by collateral records and witnesses but were never adjudicated in her own case.
9. Maria sued the Epstein estate’s executors, not Maxwell personally; Annie’s parallel case named Maxwell.
10. The 2020 OPR report did not examine the 1996 failure.
11. The FBI record released in 2025 vindicates Farmer’s central assertion that she warned the Bureau in 1996; it does not validate every wider political or intelligence claim later attached to her story.
12. No documentary Farmer tie to CIA, Mossad, MKUltra, cybernetics, computing programs, Ted Kaczynski, or John Brockman was found.
13. Her current federal negligence case remains unresolved following its September 2026 transfer.
