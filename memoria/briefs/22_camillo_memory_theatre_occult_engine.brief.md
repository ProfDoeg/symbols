Yates's interpretation of the Theatre's "secret" (Ch. VI pp. 151–158; Ch. VII pp. 160–163): the Sun series, the memory images as inner talismans, Ficino's and Pico's precepts, and the Dantesque residue.

**Principle.** The great central series of Sol shows Ficinian sun mysticism with magical solarianism: the Sun as *statua Dei* and image of the Trinity, the light hierarchy Sol, Lux, Lumen, Splendor, Color, Generatio, Argus as the world vivified by astral spirit, the Golden Chain under the Sandals, the Cock and Lion story proving Camillo's "solar virtue", the solar spiritus identified with the spirit of Christ. The basic planetary images are supposed to be inner talismans drawing astral spiritus into memory, their power running through the subsidiary images of each series and unifying memory from its causes; Camillo's own gloss on the Egyptian statues locates the magic in perfect proportion, so the magic of a memory image would be its proportions. Ficino's letter adds proportion, harmony and connection to the classical demand for order, his projected *imago mundi* supplies an astral memory building, Pico's "theatre of the world" may have suggested the form; so Camillo developed rather than invented occult memory. Beneath the surface the old places survive: Cybele vomiting fire is Hell, Europa and the Bull true religion, the Mouth of Tartarus Purgatory, the sphere of ten circles the Earthly Paradise, the wolf–lion–dog heads Prudence's three times; these coffers "must have been full of sermons. Or of cantos of the Divine Comedy."
