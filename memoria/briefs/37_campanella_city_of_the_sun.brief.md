Tommaso Campanella, *Città del Sole*, written c. 1602 in the Inquisition's prisons at Naples, first published in Latin 1623; his letters repeatedly offering the City or a model of it for local memory.

**Principle.** A round Utopian city with a round temple at its centre depicting all the stars with their relations to things below, and concentric circular walls on which are painted all mathematical figures, animals, birds, fishes, metals, human inventions and activities, statues of great men and founders of religions on the outermost wall: an encyclopaedic lay-out with a "celestial" organising basis, "using the world as a book", the children learning by walking the walls of images dependent on the central causal sun-temple images. Static in a City as Camillo's is in a Theatre, and so far simpler than Bruno's "awful complexities"; both Bruno and Campanella trained in the Dominican convent at Naples. Its Solarian pedagogy is, by Rossi's and Yates's account, the direct source of Comenius's *Orbis pictus* (system 44).

**Components.**
- Campanella's City of the Sun used as "local memory" `[3.44]`

**Yates.** Ch. XIII, pp. 297–298; Ch. XVII, pp. 377–378; compared with Yates's Camillo plan at p. 130 n. 4.

**In Yates's words.**
- "his City of the Sun, or perhaps some model of it, could be used for 'local memory', as a very quick way of knowing everything 'using the world as a book'." (p. 298)
- "It is much simpler than Bruno's systems because static in a City (as Camillo's system is static in a Theatre) and does not attempt Bruno's awful complexities." (p. 298)
- "The pedagogic method of the highly occult Solarians, and the whole plan of their City and its images, was a form of local memory, with its places and images." (p. 378)

**Descends from / leads to.** From 21 (Camillo), 26–28, 36 (*Shadows*; the "Alta Astra" altar of *Images*). To 40 (Campanella's parallel rejection of the metaphysical apex, p. 340), 44 (Comenius's *Orbis pictus* "came straight out of Campanella's City of the Sun"; Andreae's *Christianopolis*).
