# The Path of Lamed: Research Dossier

## Evidentiary method and labels

This dossier distinguishes six kinds of statement:

- **[DT — documented text]** A claim stated in an identifiable primary text.
- **[SR — scholarly reconstruction]** A conclusion advanced by modern historical or philological scholarship.
- **[KT — kabbalistic tradition]** A received Jewish interpretation that may be later than the text to which it is attached.
- **[HO — Hermetic-occult attribution]** A correspondence belonging to Christian Cabala, the Golden Dawn, Thelema, BOTA, or related occult systems.
- **[DIS — disputed]** A claim for which sources, recensions, or scholars materially disagree.
- **[MI — modern invention or synthesis]** A comparatively recent construction presented in some occult literature as ancient.

“Lamed,” “Path 22,” “Libra,” “Justice,” and “Geburah–Tiphareth” belong to several different historical layers. They should not be treated as one immemorial doctrine.

# The name

## Hebrew spelling, pronunciation, and meaning

**[DT]** The letter is written **ל** in square Hebrew. Its name appears as **לָמֶד** or **לָמֵד**, conventionally transliterated *Lamed*, *Lāmed*, or older scholarly *Lamedh*. It denotes the lateral consonant /l/.

Three related but non-identical explanations must be separated:

1. **[DT] The Hebrew root למד, l-m-d**, means “learn,” and in causative forms “teach.” The semantic relation between the root and the ancient letter-name is plausible but does not by itself establish the original pictorial meaning.

2. **[DT/KT] מַלְמַד, malmad**, is a cattle-goad or ox-goad. Judges 3:31 describes Shamgar striking Philistines with **מַלְמַד הַבָּקָר**, “an ox-goad.” This supports the traditional gloss “ox-goad,” though *malmad* is not simply the unaltered letter-name. [Judges 3:31, Hebrew text](https://www.sefaria.org/Judges.3.31)

3. **[SR/DIS]** Reconstructions of the earliest alphabet frequently identify the ancestral sign as a crooked staff, goad, or shepherd’s crook. This identification is probable rather than demonstrable: early alphabetic signs are not accompanied by ancient glossaries telling us what their pictures meant.

The occult gloss “ox-goad” therefore has a genuine Hebrew lexical basis, but the further ideas—discipline, karma, the lash of equilibrium, directing the aspirant—are **Hermetic developments**, not meanings inherent in the ancient letter.

## Shape and graphic history

The broad historical sequence is:

| Period/script | Approximate form and development | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Proto-Sinaitic/early alphabetic | A crooked staff or goad-like sign is commonly proposed | **[SR/DIS]** Acrophonic reconstruction |
| Proto-Canaanite/Phoenician | **𐤋**, an angular hooked or staff-like character | **[DT/SR]** Epigraphic attestations |
| Paleo-Hebrew | Closely related angular form | **[DT]** Iron Age Hebrew inscriptions |
| Imperial Aramaic | Increasingly cursive ancestor of the later Jewish character | **[DT/SR]** |
| Jewish square script | **ל**, distinguished by its tall ascender above the normal writing line | **[DT]** Developed through the Aramaic book hand |
| Greek and Latin collateral descendants | Greek lambda, Λ/λ; via Greek/Etruscan, Latin L | **[SR]** Comparative palaeography |

The history of the alphabet from Proto-Sinaitic through Canaanite, Phoenician, Aramaic, and Hebrew is surveyed in the Oriental Institute’s scholarly treatment of the early alphabet. [Oriental Institute, *Visible Language*](https://oi.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/oimp32.pdf)

**[KT]** Later Jewish homiletics make much of Lamed’s ascender. It becomes a “tower flying in the air,” aspiration above ordinary consciousness, or the upward point of learning. A widely repeated construction sees ל as formed from a kaf surmounted by a vav. These are symbolically productive readings of the square letter; they are not explanations of the Proto-Sinaitic sign. [Chabad, “Lamed”](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/137084/jewish/Lamed.htm)

## Numerical value and gematria

**[DT]** Lamed has the value **30**. It can consequently represent the number thirty in Hebrew notation.

**[KT]** Common homiletical combinations include:

- **ל + ב = לב**, *lev*, “heart,” 30 + 2 = 32. The word’s value becomes suggestive beside the “thirty-two paths of wisdom.”
- **א + ל = אל**, *El*, 1 + 30 = 31, a biblical divine name.
- Lamed also occurs in **אלהים**, *Elohim*, and numerous theophoric names.

These observations do not make Lamed itself a divine name. Nor does the presence of ל as the preposition “to/for” in sacred phrases establish a specific Lamed-path doctrine.

## The biblical matrix—and its limits

### 1 Chronicles 29:11

The verse recited in *Vayevarekh David* reads:

> לְךָ יְהוָה הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת וְהַנֵּצַח וְהַהוֹד ... לְךָ יְהוָה הַמַּמְלָכָה

“To You, YHWH, belong greatness, power, beauty, victory, and majesty … to You, YHWH, belongs kingship.” [1 Chronicles 29:11](https://www.sefaria.org/I_Chronicles.29.11)

**[KT]** Kabbalists read its sequence as the seven lower Sephiroth:

- *Gedullah* = Chesed
- Geburah
- Tiphareth
- Netzach
- Hod
- *kol* = Yesod
- *mamlakhah* = Malkhut

**[Important negative finding]** The prefixed ל in **לְךָ**, “to You,” is grammatical. The verse supports the names of the lower Sephiroth, not the historical existence of a Geburah–Tiphareth “Path of Lamed.”

### Proverbs 3

Proverbs 3:18 calls Wisdom/Torah “a tree of life.” Verse 19 says:

> יְהוָה בְּחָכְמָה יָסַד־אָרֶץ כּוֹנֵן שָׁמַיִם בִּתְבוּנָה

“YHWH founded the earth by wisdom; He established the heavens by understanding.” [Proverbs 3:18–20](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.3.19)

**[KT/SR]** This is an important biblical matrix for *Etz Chaim*, Chokhmah, Binah, and creation through wisdom. It is not an etymological source for Lamed or evidence for its later position.

### Job 28 and Isaiah

**[DT]** Job 28 asks where Wisdom may be found and concludes with fear of God and avoidance of evil. Isaiah 11:2 joins “wisdom and understanding,” counsel, might, knowledge, and fear of YHWH.

**[KT/SR]** Such wisdom texts helped furnish the scriptural vocabulary within which later Jewish cosmology was read. No early source has been located that derives Lamed’s Geburah–Tiphareth connection from Job or Isaiah.

# The Jewish tradition

## 1. *Sefer Yetzirah*: text, date, and recension

### Date

**[DIS]** Proposed dates extend from late antiquity into the early Islamic period. Older scholarship often placed a core between roughly the third and sixth centuries; other scholars have argued for a seventh-century setting. The first securely recoverable textual evidence and the three principal recensional families belong to approximately the tenth century.

Peter Hayman’s critical edition declines to manufacture a single pristine text from incompatible witnesses. It presents the Short, Long, and Saadyan recensions comparatively and describes the work as emerging into view around the early tenth century. [Hayman, publisher’s description](https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/sefer-yesira-9783161587955/) [Hayman, searchable critical-edition scan](https://www.scribd.com/doc/92433457/Hayman-A-Peter-Sefer-Yesira-Edition-Translation-and-Text-critical-Commentary-TSAJ-104-Tubingen-2004-split)

### The thirty-two paths and letter classes

The work opens with creation by “thirty-two wondrous paths of wisdom,” classically analyzed as:

- ten **sefirot belimah**
- twenty-two foundational letters
- three mothers: א מ ש
- seven doubles
- twelve simples

The injunction “ten and not nine, ten and not eleven” is particularly important in the later Da’at controversy. [Westcott’s *Sepher Yetzirah*, chapter 1](https://sacred-texts.com/jud/yetzirah.htm)

**[DT]** Lamed belongs to the twelve simples. In the Gra recension:

> הִמְלִיךְ אוֹת ל׳ בַּתַּשְׁמִישׁ ... מֹאזְנַיִם בָּעוֹלָם וְתִשְׁרֵי בַּשָּׁנָה

“He made Lamed king over coition … Libra in the world and Tishrei in the year.” [*Sefer Yetzirah*, Gra Version 5:10](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah_Gra_Version.5.10)

This is a cosmological correspondence among letter, zodiacal sign, month, human faculty, and organ. It does **not** say that Lamed joins Geburah to Tiphareth.

### Recensional variants

| Recension or edition | Lamed | Month | Faculty | Body |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saadyan family | Libra | Tishrei | Variant ordering among the simple-letter faculties | Recensional variation |
| Short/Long witnesses represented by Hayman | Libra | Tishrei | Sexual intercourse versus impotence appears as a polarity in an expanded formulation | Intestines in Hayman’s reconstructed line |
| Gra version | Libra | Tishrei | Coition/intercourse | Gall or gall bladder |
| Common Warsaw/Sefaria text | Libra | Tishrei | Often omitted or displaced in that paragraph | Liver |
| Westcott, 1887 | Libra | Tishri | Sexual desire | Gall |
| Bill Heidrick’s occult adaptation | Libra | Tishri | “Work,” with a note that the Kaplan text says coition | Gall |

The most stable triad is **Lamed–Libra–Tishrei**. Faculty and anatomy are not stable. Hayman’s detailed line reads, in translation, “There was formed with Lamed: Libra, Tishri, the intestines, sexual intercourse and impotence.” [Hayman critical text](https://www.scribd.com/doc/92433457/Hayman-A-Peter-Sefer-Yesira-Edition-Translation-and-Text-critical-Commentary-TSAJ-104-Tubingen-2004-split)

**[DIS]** Terms such as “Saadia version,” “Gra version,” and “Ari version” do not describe three autograph manuscripts written by those individuals. They identify textual/commentarial families transmitted under their authority.

**[MI warning]** Some internet editions silently harmonize *Sefer Yetzirah* with Golden Dawn tables. Heidrick explicitly signals alterations of this kind; such editions are useful evidence for modern reception, not for establishing the ancient text. [Heidrick adaptation](https://sacred-texts.com/oto/yetzirah.htm)

## 2. The “Faithful Intelligence”

The Hebrew tract known as the *Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom* gives:

> נתיב כ״ב נקרא שכל נאמן

“The twenty-second path is called the Faithful Intelligence.” [Hebrew *Thirty-Two Paths* text](https://www.mr-kaplan.com/Hebrew/32-paths-of-wisdom.html)

Its continuation says that spiritual powers are increased or fostered through it for those dwelling under its shadow.

Kircher printed:

> *Vigesima secunda semita dicitur Intelligentia fidelis …*

“The twenty-second path is called the Faithful Intelligence.” His fuller Latin says spiritual virtues are entrusted to and fostered by it for those beneath its shadow. [Kircher, *Oedipus Aegyptiacus* II.1, p. 315](https://www.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm)

Westcott’s familiar rendering is:

> “The Twenty-second Path is the Faithful Intelligence…”

and explains that spiritual virtues increase through it.

**[SR/DIS]** This numbered catalogue is not the same composition as the core *Sefer Yetzirah*. It circulated as a separate medieval text and was appended to, printed with, or treated as an exposition of *Sefer Yetzirah*. Its exact date and authorship remain uncertain. The equation

> 22nd Intelligence = Lamed = Geburah–Tiphareth = Libra = Justice

is therefore a composite of at least four layers:

1. the core *Sefer Yetzirah* letter/sign correspondence;
2. the separate numbered Intelligences;
3. Kircher’s placement of letters on a diagram;
4. the Golden Dawn’s tarot and colour system.

## 3. *Bahir*

**[DT]** The *Bahir*, appearing in Provence in the late twelfth century but containing older materials, treats letters, divine qualities, the Tree, cosmic directions, and the ten *ma’amarot* or sefirotic powers.

**[DT: absence]** No passage located in the *Bahir* names a “Path of Lamed,” calls Lamed the Faithful Intelligence, assigns it to Geburah–Tiphareth, or associates it with a tarot image. Modern charts that place such data under a “Bahir” heading are retrospective harmonizations. [Sefaria, *Sefer HaBahir*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir)

## 4. Talmudic and alphabet-midrash lore

### Shabbat 104a

**[DT]** Shabbat 104a preserves children’s or mnemonic interpretations of alphabetic sequences. Its exposition around ז–ח–ט–י–כ–ל reads the letters as a promise that God will sustain, favor, benefit, give inheritance, and crown the righteous. Elsewhere, א–ב becomes *alef binah*, “learn understanding.” [Shabbat 104a](https://www.sefaria.org/Shabbat.104a)

This is genuine rabbinic letter lore, but not a doctrine of a geometric Lamed path.

### *Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva*

**[KT]** The alphabet midrash transmitted under Rabbi Akiva’s name treats each letter as a cosmic sign. One Lamed interpretation plays on **למד** and the understanding heart: “Do not read *lamed* but a heart understanding knowledge,” linking it with **לב**, heart. The work is pseudepigraphic and composite; attribution to the second-century Rabbi Akiva is traditional rather than historical. [*Otzar Midrashim*, Alphabet of Rabbi Akiva](https://www.sefaria.org/Otzar_Midrashim%2C_Midrashim_of_Rabbi_Akiba%2C_Aleph_Bet_of_Rabbi_Akiba_%28Version_1%29.38)

## 5. Zoharic letter lore

### The letters petition to begin creation

**[DT]** In the Zohar’s prologue, the Hebrew letters appear before God in reverse order, each requesting that creation begin with it. God ultimately chooses Bet.

Lamed does not receive an independent petition scene. When Mem appeals through **מלך**, *melekh*, “king,” it is told to return to its place together with Lamed and final Kaf because the world cannot exist without a king. Lamed’s role is consequently embedded in **מ־ל־ך**, not in a Justice-path doctrine. [Zohar, Introduction 6:22](https://www.sefaria.org/Zohar%2C_Introduction.6.22) [Zohar.com, Prologue 22–39](https://www.zohar.com/zohar/Prologue/verses/22-39)

### Authorship and dating

- **[KT]** The Zohar presents itself through Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai and his circle.
- **[SR]** The main corpus emerged in late-thirteenth-century Castile. Moses de León was central to its production and circulation.
- **[DIS]** Scholem argued substantially for de León’s authorship; later scholarship associated with Yehuda Liebes, Ronit Meroz, Daniel Matt, Elliot Wolfson, and others increasingly treats it as layered and connected with a creative circle rather than as a simple one-man forgery.

Daniel Matt’s Pritzker introduction summarizes the late-thirteenth-century appearance of the text and the problem of de León’s role. [Pritzker Zohar, front matter and introduction](https://supress.sites-pro.stanford.edu/sites/supress/files/media/file/4810_Front_Matter.pdf)

## 6. Gikatilla and classical sefirah correspondences

Joseph Gikatilla’s *Sha’arei Orah* organizes discussion around the divine names associated with the Sephiroth. It is crucial for understanding how Jewish Kabbalists related names, biblical verses, angels, and emanations.

**[DT: absence]** It does not provide a classical table in which Lamed-as-path has its own divine name, archangel, angelic order, planet, vice, virtue, perfume, tarot card, or colour scale. Those are later Hermetic categories.

For the two Sephiroth later joined by Lamed in the Kircher tree:

- Geburah is associated with judgment, power, and restrictive force.
- Tiphareth mediates and harmonizes divine qualities.

Their later occult names—*Elohim Gibor*, Khamael, Seraphim, Mars; and *YHWH Eloah ve-Da‘ath*, Raphael, Malakhim, Sun—should not be retrojected wholesale into Gikatilla’s system. [Sefaria search entry for *Sha’arei Orah*](https://www.sefaria.org/Shaarei_Orah)

## 7. Cordovero: *Pardes Rimmonim* and *Or Ne’erav*

Moses Cordovero completed *Pardes Rimmonim* in 1548. He systematized earlier Kabbalah before the ascendancy of the Lurianic school.

**[DT/DIS]** Cordovero’s descriptions of the channels are not identical across works:

- *Pardes Rimmonim*, Gate 7, describes a set that can yield twenty-four connections if two unnumbered channels are counted.
- *Or Ne’erav*, part 6, chapter 2, gives a twenty-two-channel account.
- Printed diagrams appearing after Cordovero’s death vary.
- The diagrams do not furnish the complete Golden Dawn assignment of Hebrew letters, astrology, and tarot.

Accordingly, it is unsafe to call the Kircher tree simply “Cordovero’s tree.” [Sefaria, *Pardes Rimmonim*](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimonim?lang=he&tab=schema)

## 8. Luria through Hayyim Vital

**[DT/KT]** The Lurianic system preserved by Hayyim Vital centers on:

- *tzimtzum*, divine contraction or concealment;
- the *kav*, ray or line entering the vacated space;
- Adam Kadmon;
- the four worlds—Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah, Assiah;
- the primordial configurations of points, bands, and rectification;
- *shevirat ha-kelim*, the shattering of the vessels;
- sparks entrapped among the *qelippot*;
- *tikkun*, restoration;
- the *partzufim*, reconfigured divine personae.

[Introduction to *Etz Chaim*](https://www.myjewishlearning.com/article/what-is-the-tree-of-life-etz-chaim/) [*Etz Chaim*: the line or *kav*](https://fringe.hekhal.org/texts/etz-chaim-the-line)

**[DT: absence]** Vital’s *Etz Chaim* contains no tarot trump and no autonomous “Path of Lamed” joining Geburah to Tiphareth. Attempts to interpret Adjustment as *tikkun* may be spiritually suggestive, but they are modern comparative readings.

## 9. Ari and Gra tree arrangements

In the widely reproduced reconstructions derived from Aryeh Kaplan’s comparison:

| System | Structural principle | Lamed placement |
|---|---|---|
| Ari-derived | Three mothers horizontal, seven doubles vertical, twelve simples diagonal | Hod–Yesod |
| Gra-derived | Same general letter-class geometry, with different ordering/connection details | Chesed–Yesod |
| Kircher/Golden Dawn | Letters assigned successively to a specific early-modern 22-line diagram | Geburah–Tiphareth |

[Comparative Ari/Gra/Kircher table](https://arcane-archive.org/tarot/qbl-and-tarot-1.php)

**[DIS]** “The Ari tree” and “the Gra tree” are convenient labels. Surviving editions, diagrammatic conventions, and the process of turning verbal rules into a single drawing complicate the notion that either master left one definitive modern-style chart.

The crucial difference is methodological: the Ari/Gra arrangements attempt to honor *Sefer Yetzirah’s* three morphological classes geometrically; Kircher’s placement follows the routes on his diagram and does not keep all mothers horizontal, doubles vertical, and simples diagonal.

## 10. Hasidic readings

### *Tanya*

**[DT: absence]** There is no chapter of *Tanya* devoted to the “Path of Lamed.” *Tanya* discusses the ten soul powers, divine and animal souls, letters of divine speech, Torah, mitzvot, and the upper and lower unities; it does not adopt Kircher’s twenty-two letter-path placements or tarot. [Sefaria, *Tanya*](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya)

A Hasidic interpreter may legitimately meditate on Lamed as learning, heart, aspiration, or Tishrei, but calling this “the Tanya’s Lamed chapter” is a modern imposition.

### Nachman of Bratslav

**[DT: absence]** Rabbi Nachman’s teachings abound in letter symbolism, Torah, melody, repentance, Tishrei, and the heart. No canonical text was located that identifies the Golden Dawn path 22 as a Nachman teaching.

## 11. Prayer, Omer, calendar, tribe, and Torah

### Prayer

Lamed occurs constantly as the Hebrew prefix “to/for.” In **לְךָ יְהוָה**, “to You, YHWH,” its grammatical role should not be confused with an esoteric path attribution.

### Psalm 119

**[DT]** Psalm 119:89–96 is the Lamed acrostic stanza. It begins **לְעוֹלָם יְהוָה**, “Forever, YHWH, Your word stands in heaven.” This provides a genuine Jewish Lamed–Torah association but no Tree placement. [Psalm 119:89](https://www.sefaria.org/Psalms.119.89)

### Counting the Omer

**[DT: absence]** Classical Omer practice counts forty-nine days and is frequently interpreted through seven lower Sephiroth in a seven-by-seven grid. There is no standard classical assignment of the Path of Lamed to a particular Omer day.

### Calendar

**[DT]** Lamed corresponds to **Tishrei** and **Libra/Moznayim** in the principal *Sefer Yetzirah* families.

### Tribe

**[DIS/KT]** Ephraim is a common later assignment to Tishrei/Lamed, especially in tables influenced by later commentarial traditions. But *Sefer Yetzirah* itself does not name tribes in its simple-letter creation paragraphs, and Jewish month–tribe tables disagree. Ephraim must therefore be labeled a later traditional attribution, not part of the earliest recoverable text. [Chabad on Tishrei and Libra](https://www.chabad.org/theJewishWoman/article_cdo/aid/5634787/jewish/Why-the-Zodiac-for-the-Month-of-Tishrei-Is-Scales-Libra.htm) [*Bnei Yissaschar*, Tishrei](https://www.sefaria.org/Bnei_Yissaschar%2C_Tishrei)

# The Hermetic and occult tradition

## 1. Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Pico’s *Conclusiones* included 900 theses, among them propositions he identified as Cabalistic. He interpreted Kabbalah as confirming Christian mysteries.

- **[DT]** Pico introduced Jewish kabbalistic materials into a Latin Christian philosophical program.
- **[SR]** His conclusions helped inaugurate Christian Cabala.
- **[DT: absence]** Pico gives no Lamed–Justice correspondence and no path 22 joining Geburah–Tiphareth.
- **[Critical context]** Pico’s procedure is confessional appropriation: Jewish texts become evidence for Christian claims that their Jewish transmitters did not make.

[Pico, Latin *Conclusiones*](https://www2.esotericarchives.com/pico/conclus.htm)

## 2. *Portae Lucis*, 1516

Paulus Ricius’s Latin translation of portions of Gikatilla’s *Sha’arei Orah*, printed as *Portae Lucis*, carries one of the earliest printed Tree diagrams.

- **[DT]** It is an important landmark in the graphic and Latin reception of the Sephiroth.
- **[SR]** Earlier manuscript diagrams exist; it is therefore the earliest famous printed tree, not the first Tree of Life image of any kind.
- **[DT: absence]** Its diagram is not the complete Golden Dawn letter-path system.

## 3. Reuchlin, 1517

Johannes Reuchlin’s *De arte cabalistica libri tres* is a Christian-Cabalist dialogue joining Hebrew learning, Pythagorean philosophy, divine names, and Christian apologetics.

- Latin title: *De arte cabalistica*, “On the Cabalistic Art.”
- Hebrew letters are treated as bearers of number, sound, and divine mystery.
- **[DT: absence]** No tarot correspondence for Lamed and no “Justice path.”
- **[DT]** The first edition was printed at Hagenau by Thomas Anshelm in March 1517. [Badische Landesbibliothek digitization](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/blbihd/content/titleinfo/7174105) [Bavarian State Library copy](https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/en/details/bsb00017488)

## 4. Kircher, 1652

Athanasius Kircher’s *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, volume II, part 1, contains the decisive early-modern synthesis.

### The diagram

**[DT]** Kircher’s Tree placed twenty-two Hebrew letters on twenty-two inter-sefirah channels. In this diagram Lamed occupies the line between Geburah and Tiphareth.

**[SR]** The immediate diagrammatic ancestry probably includes Philippe d’Aquin’s 1625 *Behinat Olam* material and earlier Christian-Cabalist diagrams. Kircher nevertheless became the most influential printed transmitter.

**[Important qualification]** Kircher’s letter placement—not all his planetary arrangements—was adopted by the Golden Dawn. The Golden Dawn drew planetary-sefirah correspondences from a broader Renaissance occult lineage, including Agrippa.

### The Latin path text

Kircher prints the twenty-second path as:

> *Vigesima secunda semita dicitur Intelligentia fidelis…*

His account continues that spiritual virtues are entrusted to and fostered through it for those dwelling beneath its shadow. [Kircher, p. 315](https://www.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm)

### Appropriation and polemic

Kircher simultaneously mined Jewish sources and denounced what he called rabbinic superstition and imposture. His preface claims an ancient Mosaic truth while subordinating actual Jewish Kabbalah to Christian and Egyptianizing interpretation. That contradiction is documented in his own Latin preface, not merely imposed by present-day criticism. [Kircher, preface, pp. 209–212](https://www.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm)

## 5. Knorr von Rosenroth, 1677–1684

Christian Knorr von Rosenroth’s *Kabbala Denudata* translated and excerpted Zoharic, Cordoverian, and Lurianic materials into Latin.

- **[DT]** It greatly expanded Christian European access to Kabbalah.
- **[SR]** It became a major source for later occultists who could not read the Hebrew/Aramaic originals.
- **[DT: absence]** It is not the first source for Lamed–Justice and does not make tarot part of Jewish Kabbalah.
- Its importance for later Qliphoth tables lies in transmission and recombination rather than in a single original, universally fixed “demonic Tree.”

[Wellcome catalogue and digital record](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/j9weuw35) [Google Books scan](https://books.google.co.uk/books?id=yIlOquc5yOUC)

## 6. Éliphas Lévi, 1854–1856

Lévi’s *Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie* systematically correlated the twenty-two Hebrew letters and twenty-two tarot trumps.

**[HO]** This was a watershed in occult tarot.

**[DIS]** Lévi’s sequence is not the later Golden Dawn sequence. In the Lévi/Papus-style ordinal alignment, the twelfth Hebrew letter Lamed corresponds to trump XII, the Hanged Man. Papus explicitly preserves this association while also identifying Lamed as a simple letter connected with Libra. [Papus, “The Hanged Man”](https://sacred-texts.com/book/the-tarot-of-the-bohemians/shell/12-the-hanged-man) [Lévi, French scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dogme_et_rituel_de_la_haute_magie_(IA_b24884340_0001).pdf)

Thus the equation Lamed = Justice was not simply inherited from Lévi. It resulted from the Golden Dawn’s own synthesis of Kircher’s path order, *Sefer Yetzirah’s* astrology, and tarot.

## 7. The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn

### The attribution

The Golden Dawn teaches:

- Path number: 22
- Letter: Lamed
- Meaning: ox-goad
- Connection: Geburah–Tiphareth
- Sign: Libra
- Trump: Justice
- Title: “Daughter of the Lords of Truth; Ruler of the Balance”
- Yetziratic title: Faithful Intelligence

[*Book T* scan](https://benebellwen.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/mathers-and-felkin-golden-dawn-book-t-the-tarot-1888.pdf)

### Reasoning

The result combines:

1. Kircher’s placement of the twelfth letter on the 22nd path;
2. *Sefer Yetzirah’s* Lamed–Libra correspondence;
3. Justice’s scales;
4. Libra’s scales;
5. the numerical and initiatory scheme of twenty-two paths below the ten Sephiroth.

The scales correspondence is visually compelling, but it should not be mistaken for proof that ancient Jewish Kabbalists associated a tarot card with Lamed.

### Justice and Strength numbering

- Older tarot ordering commonly numbered Justice VIII and Strength XI.
- The Golden Dawn retained Justice as VIII while assigning it to Lamed/Libra.
- Arthur Edward Waite’s published deck numbered Strength VIII and Justice XI, bringing their printed positions into the Hebrew-letter sequence Teth then Lamed.
- Crowley returned Adjustment to VIII and Lust to XI while keeping Lamed–Libra and Teth–Leo.

### Colour scales

The four worlds are represented by four colour scales:

| World/scale | Lamed colour |
|---|---|
| Atzilut / King | Emerald green |
| Beriah / Queen | Blue |
| Yetzirah / Emperor | Deep blue-green |
| Assiah / Empress | Pale green |

The occasional plain “green” for the Emperor scale is a transcriptional simplification; Golden Dawn and Gareth Knight sources give deep blue-green. [Golden Dawn compendium scan](https://www.labirintoermetico.com/06Numerologia_Cabala/I.Regardie_Complete_Golden_Dawn_%28II%20ed.deluxe%29.pdf)

## 8. Crowley

### *Liber 777*

Crowley’s *777* was first published in 1909 and depends heavily on Golden Dawn tabular material. It consolidates rather than independently discovers most Lamed correspondences.

The key row is 22. Its principal nonblank path-specific entries are recorded in the correspondence section below. [*777* online table](https://hermeticus.info/doku.php?id=table_01) [Scan of *777*](https://www.invisiblehouse.org/pdf/liber777.pdf)

### *Liber 231*

In the positive sequence, verse 11 corresponds to the tarot’s zero-based sequence leading to Lamed/Justice:

> “Also the lady Maat with her feather and her sword abode to judge the righteous.”

The next sentence says Fate was already established. The appended ROTA note calls Lamed “the severe discipline of the Path.” [*Liber CCXXXI*](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/publication/english/Liber_CCXXXI.html)

### *The Book of Thoth*

Crowley renamed Justice **Adjustment** because the card represents dynamic and exact equilibration rather than merely human legal judgment. His principal ideas include:

- Libra and the balance;
- Venus as ruler of Libra;
- Saturn exalted in Libra;
- continual compensation and adjustment;
- the feminine complement of the Fool;
- the conjunction of Aleph and Lamed as a special Thelemic key.

These are **Thelemic interpretations**, not readings found in *Sefer Yetzirah*.

### The Tzaddi/Heh question

**[DT/HO]** Crowley’s “Tzaddi is not the Star” revision exchanges the Hebrew-letter attributions of the Emperor and Star:

- Emperor: from Heh to Tzaddi
- Star: from Tzaddi to Heh

It does not change Lamed, Libra, or Adjustment. Treating Lamed as part of the Tzaddi controversy is an error.

### Thelemic grades

On the Golden Dawn/A∴A∴ Tree, Lamed connects:

- Tiphareth: Adeptus Minor, 5°=6□
- Geburah: Adeptus Major, 6°=5□

**[HO]** The path can therefore symbolize movement between those grades, especially severity disciplined by solar equilibrium. There is no independent formal “Lamed grade.”

## 9. Dion Fortune

Dion Fortune’s *The Mystical Qabalah* appeared in 1935.

**[Important negative finding]** It contains chapters on the ten Sephiroth, not full chapters on each of the twenty-two letter paths. There is therefore no “chapter on the Path of Lamed” to reproduce. The request for that chapter appears to confuse Fortune’s sefirah-by-sefirah structure with later pathworking manuals. [Complete-text edition](https://www.globalgreyebooks.com/online-ebooks/dion-fortune_mystical-qabalah_complete-text.html)

Fortune nevertheless helped popularize the Kircher/Golden Dawn Tree and its psychological reading.

## 10. Israel Regardie

Regardie’s *A Garden of Pomegranates* identifies:

- Lamed as the twelfth letter, value 30;
- path 22;
- Geburah–Tiphareth;
- ox-goad or whip;
- Libra;
- Justice;
- aloe, emerald, elephant, and spider.

His later publication of Golden Dawn papers made the system broadly accessible. [*A Garden of Pomegranates* scan](https://www.labirintoermetico.com/06numerologia_cabala/Isreael_Regardie_A_Garden_Of_Pomegranates_2Nd%20Ed.pdf)

## 11. Paul Foster Case

Case and the Builders of the Adytum preserved Lamed–Libra–Justice but changed or refined some directional and meditational details.

In *The Tarot*:

- Lamed is associated with action or work;
- Justice is Key 11;
- the direction is northwest;
- Case acknowledges correction of an earlier directional assignment.

[Paul Foster Case, *The Tarot*](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1947__case___the_tarot.pdf)

BOTA’s Justice imagery places particular emphasis on equilibration, law, subconscious response, and karmic consequence. These are twentieth-century occult teachings.

## 12. Gareth Knight

Knight’s *Practical Guide to Qabalistic Symbolism* presents the established Golden Dawn synthesis:

- path 22, Tiphareth–Geburah;
- Lamed, ox-goad;
- Libra;
- Justice;
- the four colour scales;
- Faithful Intelligence;
- balance, karma, and rectification.

[Gareth Knight, volume II](https://maestrosdelsaber.com/wp-content/uploads/ftp-files/Kabbalah/Kab%20en%20General/Kab%20Knight%2C%20Gareth%20-%20A%20Practical%20Guide%20to%20Qabalistic%20Symbolism%20Vol%202.pdf)

## 13. Pathworking traditions

**[HO/MI]** Modern pathworking developed from Golden Dawn astral vision, tarot contemplation, and later guided-imagery practice. Common methods include:

- contemplating Justice or Adjustment as an “astral door”;
- entering a balanced hall, tribunal, or Maat-like scene;
- encountering scales, sword, feather, throne, or twin pillars;
- examining the relationship between severity and solar integration;
- testing imagery against the prescribed letter, colour, divine names of the endpoints, and tarot symbolism.

Regardie and later Golden Dawn authors emphasize testing visions rather than treating every spontaneous image as authoritative.

Case’s approach is more meditative and doctrinal. Knight stresses karma and exact rectification. Grant’s practice deliberately reverses the imagery into a nightside tunnel of disequilibrium. None of these practices is described in the classical Jewish sources.

# The Qliphah or tunnel

## Jewish qelippot versus the occult adverse Tree

**[DT/KT]** Jewish Kabbalah possesses real doctrines of *qelippot*, “shells” or “husks,” the *sitra ahra*, impurity, and powers nourished by displaced holiness. Lurianic teaching links them with the shattered vessels and entrapped sparks.

**[MI/HO]** The fixed mirrored Tree with ten named demonic orders, one demon prince per Sephirah, twenty-two tunnels, tarot attributions, colours, and sigils is a Western occult synthesis. It should not be advertised as a chart copied intact from the Zohar.

## The ten adverse orders and rulers

The following is the standard later Golden Dawn/Crowley-derived table; spellings vary considerably:

| Sephirah opposed | Qliphothic order | Common gloss | Later occult ruler(s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kether | Thaumiel | Divided or contending powers | Satan and Moloch |
| Chokhmah | Ghagiel/Ogiel | Hinderers/confusion | Beelzebub |
| Binah | Satariel | Concealers | Lucifuge Rofocale |
| Chesed | Gha’agsheblah/Gamchicoth | Devourers or breakers | Astaroth |
| Geburah | Golachab/Golab | Burners or incendiaries | Asmodeus |
| Tiphareth | Thagirion/Tagiriron | Disputers or false/ugly beauty | Belphegor |
| Netzach | A’arab Zaraq/Harab Serapel | Ravens of dispersion | Baal |
| Hod | Samael | Poison or falsehood of God | Adrammelech |
| Yesod | Gamaliel | Obscene or polluted forms | Lilith |
| Malkhut | Lilith/Nahemoth | Night spectres or whisperers | Naamah in many versions |

[Golden Dawn Qliphoth tables](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Qliphoth.html)

**[DIS]** The tenth order is especially unstable: Lilith, Nahemoth, Nehemoth, and related names may identify the order, ruler, or one of several female demonic figures depending on the table.

## Lamed’s three adverse designations

Three different things are often confused:

1. **A’abiriron** — the *Liber 777* order assigned to Libra, glossed “the Clayey.”
2. **Golachab and Thagirion** — the adverse shells corresponding to the two Sephiroth at the path’s endpoints.
3. **Lafcursiax** — the genius guarding the Lamed tunnel in Crowley’s sigil-table and Kenneth Grant’s development.

These are not synonyms.

## Crowley’s adverse genius

*Liber XXII: Liber Carcerorum* gives:

- Hebrew letter: Lamed
- Formula/name: **Lusanaherandraton**
- Genius: **Lafcursiax**

[*Liber XXII*, Lamed entry](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/publication/english/Liber_XXII.html)

Its Lamed sigil reverses the equilibrated Justice image: disordered scales, a sword, an abnormal human or demonic form, and a yod-like element appear in a deliberately unbalanced composition.

## Kenneth Grant’s tunnel

In *Nightside of Eden* (1977), Grant places the tunnel of Lafcursiax behind the Tree between the nightside analogues of Geburah and Tiphareth.

Its themes include:

- exact equilibrium inverted into perilous instability;
- the crooked or fallen balance;
- judgment distorted into mechanical fate;
- the “funambulatory” ability to cross a razor-thin line between positive and negative;
- creative chaos and the danger of disequilibrium;
- musical attribution F-sharp in an upper register;
- the number 671 in Grant’s extended numerological treatment.

[Kenneth Grant, *Nightside of Eden* scan](https://s3.us-west-1.wasabisys.com/luminist/EB/G/Grant%20-%20Nightside%20of%20Eden.pdf)

**[Historical judgment]** Lafcursiax is neither a Talmudic demon nor a Zoharic prince. Its documentary lineage begins with Crowley’s twentieth-century Class A sigil/name table; Grant converts it into a fully described tunnel decades later.

# Controversies and disputes

## 1. Dating *Sefer Yetzirah*

- **Traditional:** Abraham is sometimes named as author or recipient.
- **Older scholarly proposals:** late antiquity, often third–sixth centuries.
- **Alternative reconstruction:** approximately seventh century.
- **Text-critical evidence:** main recoverable recensions and quotations emerge around the tenth century.
- **Conclusion:** Abrahamic authorship is traditional; the exact date of composition remains disputed; the extant text is demonstrably layered.

## 2. Is the “Faithful Intelligence” part of *Sefer Yetzirah*?

**No, not in the strict text-critical sense.** It belongs to a separate medieval list of thirty-two Intelligences that later circulated with the work. Westcott’s placement encourages readers to treat it as part of *Sefer Yetzirah* proper. Kircher likewise integrated it into his presentation.

## 3. Zoharic authorship

- **Traditional:** Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai, second century.
- **Scholem:** Moses de León, late thirteenth century, substantially responsible.
- **More recent scholarship:** layered composition and a Castilian circle, with de León central but not necessarily the only creative agent.

The Zohar’s Lamed lore must therefore be dated both within its literary fiction and within its documented medieval emergence.

## 4. Kircher versus Jewish trees

Kircher’s diagram is not “the Jewish Tree” in an exclusive or normative sense. Jewish Kabbalists produced multiple verbal and graphic arrangements.

The Kircher/Golden Dawn Lamed is Geburah–Tiphareth. Ari- and Gra-derived arrangements place Lamed elsewhere because they organize mother, double, and simple letters geometrically. Modern occult books often omit this dispute.

## 5. Did the Golden Dawn merely adopt Kircher?

It adopted the broad twenty-two-path layout and letter placement associated with Kircher, but not his system wholesale. It combined:

- Kircher’s diagram;
- one form of *Sefer Yetzirah* astrology;
- Renaissance planetary magic;
- Lévi’s tarot thesis;
- Masonic and Rosicrucian grade symbolism;
- Egyptianizing ritual imagery;
- its own colour and initiatory schemes.

“Adopted Kircher’s tree” is substantially correct if understood as shorthand, not as a claim of complete copying.

## 6. Justice, Strength, and the tarot sequence

There is no ancient Jewish tarot correspondence.

- Lévi/Papus: ordinal system could yield Lamed–Hanged Man.
- Golden Dawn: Lamed–Justice–Libra.
- Waite: renumbered Justice XI and Strength VIII.
- Crowley: Adjustment VIII and Lust XI while retaining Lamed–Libra.

The changes are editorial and esoteric choices, not recovery of a demonstrable ancient tarot alphabet.

## 7. Tzaddi/Heh

Crowley’s exchange concerns the Emperor and Star. It does not change Lamed. Any “Tzaddi/Heh problem of Lamed” is a category error.

## 8. Da’at

*Sefer Yetzirah* insists upon ten Sephiroth, not eleven. Da’at can function in later Kabbalah as:

- knowledge;
- a state produced by the union of Chokhmah and Binah;
- a noncounted or hidden locus;
- a functional sefirah when Keter is not counted in a given configuration.

Treating Da’at as an uncomplicated permanent eleventh Sephirah contradicts the classical tenfold formula. Kircher and occult diagrams may draw or label it without making it numerically equivalent to the ten.

## 9. “Ox-goad” as prehistoric certainty

The gloss has strong lexical and traditional support. The pictorial reconstruction of the earliest sign as an ox-goad is plausible, not certain. “The Proto-Sinaitic letter unquestionably depicts an ox-goad” overstates the evidence.

## 10. Lamed as the “only letter above the line”

This is true of familiar square-script typography in a practical sense, but it is not true across every ancient Hebrew script or handwriting style. Its elevated mystical meaning is a later meditation on the received form.

## 11. Tribe of Ephraim

Ephraim is a documented later correspondence in one influential stream. Competing Jewish month–tribe systems exist, and the tribes are absent from the relevant *Sefer Yetzirah* paragraph. Therefore:

> Lamed–Tishrei–Libra: textually strong.  
> Lamed–Tishrei–Ephraim: later and disputed.

## 12. The Qliphoth list

Jewish Kabbalah contains qelippot; it does not contain Kenneth Grant’s twenty-two tunnels. The fixed order/prince chart is a Western synthesis, and Lafcursiax is specifically Crowleyan/Grantian.

## 13. Christian Cabala and appropriation

Pico, Reuchlin, Kircher, and Knorr preserved and transmitted important Jewish material, sometimes with formidable linguistic labor. They also reinterpreted it within Christian truth claims, often making Judaism a preliminary witness to Christianity.

Kircher’s overt hostility to rabbinic Kabbalah makes the appropriative dynamic especially visible. Historical acknowledgment of transmission does not erase the theological asymmetry.

# Open questions

1. **Date and provenance of the *Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom*.** Its relation to particular medieval commentarial circles remains less secure than popular occult histories imply.

2. **Exact pre-Kircher genealogy of the familiar diagram.** Philippe d’Aquin is a strong lead, but a complete stemma of manuscript and printed diagrams remains difficult.

3. **Cordovero’s intended definitive path layout.** His verbal accounts and posthumous diagrams are not fully concordant.

4. **Recensional priority within *Sefer Yetzirah*.** “Short” need not automatically mean earliest, and “Long” need not simply be an expansion of one pristine text.

5. **Original referent of the early Lamed sign.** Goad, crook, or staff remains an acrophonic reconstruction rather than an ancient caption.

6. **The source of every minor Golden Dawn decan name.** Some forms are textually corrupt, and later tables repeat spelling errors without checking Arabic, Hebrew, Coptic, or astrological originals.

7. **Authorship of individual Golden Dawn path lectures.** Regardie’s published corpus includes different historical strata; not every explanatory sentence belongs to the Order’s earliest documents.

8. **Origin of A’abiriron’s spelling and gloss.** Editions of *777* and derivative tables vary.

9. **Crowley’s precise process in producing the *Liber 231/XXII* names and sigils.** The text asserts inspired or Class A status, while historical reconstruction can document publication and manuscript context but cannot verify supernatural origin.

10. **Relationship between Grant’s published tunnel descriptions and private initiatory work.** His system is deliberately visionary and associative; historical verification can establish literary dependence, not the objective reality of the tunnel.
