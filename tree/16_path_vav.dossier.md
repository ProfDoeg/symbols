# The Path of Vav: Research Dossier

## Method and evidentiary labels

This dossier distinguishes six kinds of claim:

- **[Documented text]** — stated in a named primary text or visible in a historical artifact.
- **[Scholarly reconstruction]** — a conclusion of historical, philological, or manuscript scholarship.
- **[Kabbalistic tradition]** — a claim internal to Jewish mystical reception, without necessarily being historically demonstrable.
- **[Hermetic-occult attribution]** — a correspondence belonging to Christian Cabala, Hermetic Qabalah, ceremonial magic, Tarot, or Thelema.
- **[Disputed]** — contested dating, authorship, textual reading, or attribution.
- **[Modern invention]** — a relatively recent synthesis presented as esoteric practice rather than as ancient textual tradition.

The central historical finding is this:

> **“The Path of Vav” is not a single ancient Jewish datum.** Three originally separate things were eventually superimposed:
>
> 1. the *Sefer Yetzirah’s* thirty-two paths;
> 2. Vav among its twelve simple letters;
> 3. the separate medieval list of thirty-two named “Intelligences.”
>
> Kircher’s seventeenth-century diagram put Vav on the Chokmah–Chesed connector. The Golden Dawn subsequently combined that placement with Taurus, the Hierophant, four colour scales, magical images, and ceremonial pathworking.

## The name

### 1. Hebrew form, pronunciation, and meaning

**[Documented language]** The name is וָו, *vav* or historically *waw*. The consonant represented Proto-Semitic */w/*. In much European Jewish pronunciation it shifted toward /v/, now standard in Israeli Hebrew.

Vav has several linguistic functions:

- a consonant, historically /w/ and commonly /v/;
- a *mater lectionis* indicating *o* or *u*;
- the conjunction ו־, “and”;
- in Biblical Hebrew narrative, part of constructions traditionally called *vav conversive* or *vav consecutive*.

Its dictionary meaning is a hook, peg, or fastening. Biblical וָוִים, *vavim*, are the hooks or pegs of the Tabernacle, especially in Exodus 26–27 and 38. “Nail” is the conventional occult gloss, but “hook” or “peg” is lexically more exact. See the [Klein Dictionary entry](https://www.sefaria.org/Klein_Dictionary%2C_%D7%95) and the older [Jewish Encyclopedia article](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/14797-waw).

**[Kabbalistic and occult interpretation]** Because ו־ means “and” and a physical *vav* joins material, both Jewish homilists and occultists interpret Vav as conjunction, continuity, transmission, and the fastening of higher to lower worlds. The symbolism is meaningful as exegesis; it is not proof that the alphabet was designed for the Kircher path.

### 2. Letter-form and historical development

A simplified palaeographic sequence is:

```text
possible pictorial ancestor → Proto-Sinaitic/Proto-Canaanite
                           → Phoenician 𐤅
                           → Imperial Aramaic 𐡅
                           → Jewish square script ו
```

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** Early alphabetic writing developed in the second millennium BCE through acrophonic adaptation of Egyptian signs. Waw’s exact pictorial ancestor is not settled. Older popular accounts confidently call it a tent-peg or nail; proposals in palaeographic scholarship include an oar or mace-like sign. Consequently:

- “Vav means hook/peg” is lexically secure.
- “The Proto-Sinaitic picture was definitely a nail” is **[Disputed]**.
- Connecting that supposed picture directly to the Chokmah–Chesed path is **[Modern occult interpretation]**.

The modern square ו descends through Aramaic script adopted by Judeans in the Second Temple period. It consists principally of a head and descending vertical stroke. Its difference from י can become halakhically significant: [Menachot 29b](https://www.sefaria.org/Menachot.29b) considers whether a damaged Vav remains legible as Vav.

### 3. Gematria and Torah lore

**[Documented rabbinic text]** Vav has numerical value six. In [Kiddushin 30a](https://www.sefaria.org/Kiddushin.30a), the sages discuss whether the Vav of גָּחוֹן, “belly” in Leviticus 11:42, lies on one or the other side of the Torah’s midpoint. The passage itself observes that later sages no longer possessed complete certainty about defective and plene spelling. Popular claims that this Vav is demonstrably the exact middle letter of every Torah text therefore overstate the Talmud.

**[Medieval scribal custom]** *Vavei ha-amudim*, “hooks of the columns,” came to denote the practice of beginning most Torah-scroll columns with Vav. The custom is attested in the medieval period; it is not an ancient rule of Moses, and some authorities opposed distortions introduced merely to force the layout. See the textual-history discussion in [“Scribing the Tabernacle”](https://www.thetorah.com/article/scribing-the-tabernacle-a-visual-midrash-embedded-in-the-torah-scroll).

### 4. Vav in the divine Name

In יהוה, Vav is the third letter.

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** A widespread fourfold reading maps:

| Letter | World | Configuration |
|---|---|---|
| Yod | Atzilut | Chokmah/Abba |
| First Heh | Beriah | Binah/Imma |
| Vav | Yetzirah | Zeir Anpin, the six middot |
| Final Heh | Assiah | Malkhut/Nukva |

These mappings occur in several variations, so they should not be treated as a single immutable table. Vital’s [*Etz Chaim* 1:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.5.1) explicitly construes Vav as the totality of six Sephiroth, while the final Heh is Malkhut.

## Biblical matrices

The following verses helped generate Sephirotic vocabulary, but none names a “Path of Vav.”

### 1 Chronicles 29:11

> לְךָ יְהוָה הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת וְהַנֵּצַח וְהַהוֹד כִּי־כֹל בַּשָּׁמַיִם וּבָאָרֶץ...

“To You, YHWH, belong greatness, power, beauty, victory, and majesty, for all that is in heaven and earth…”

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** The sequence was read as Gedullah/Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, Netzach, Hod, “all” as Yesod, and kingdom as Malkhut. It is a principal scriptural support for the seven lower Sephiroth. [Hebrew and translation](https://www.sefaria.org/I_Chronicles.29.11).

### Proverbs 3:19

> יְהוָה בְּחָכְמָה יָסַד־אָרֶץ כּוֹנֵן שָׁמַיִם בִּתְבוּנָה

“YHWH founded the earth by wisdom; He established heaven by understanding.”  
This supplied biblical language for Chokmah and Binah. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.3.19).

### Job 28 and Job 26:7

Job 28 asks where Wisdom and Understanding may be found; God alone knows wisdom’s way. Job 26:7 contains בְלִימָה, *belimah*, “nothingness” or “without anything,” later echoed in *sefirot belimah*. [Job 28](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.28); [Job 26:7](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.26.7).

### Isaiah

Isaiah 11:2 enumerates spirit, wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge, and fear of YHWH. Isaiah 57:15 supplies the “high and exalted,” “inhabiting eternity,” and “holy is His name” vocabulary incorporated into versions of *Sefer Yetzirah*. [Isaiah 11:2](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.11.2); [Isaiah 57:15](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.57.15).

# The Jewish tradition

## I. *Sefer Yetzirah*

### Date, form, and recensions

**[Disputed]** Traditional attributions include Abraham and, in some transmission histories, Rabbi Akiva. Neither attribution is accepted as a historical authorship statement by critical scholarship.

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** Proposed dates range from Late Antiquity into the early Islamic period. Some layers may be earlier, but the work is demonstrably present in multiple forms by the tenth century. A prudent formulation is:

- core materials may reflect approximately third- to sixth-century intellectual worlds;
- the received work took shape no later than roughly the seventh to ninth centuries;
- Short, Long, and Saadian versions were already distinguishable by the tenth century.

Peter Hayman’s critical edition attempts to reconstruct the earliest recoverable wording rather than treating any later recension as the original. Its catalog record is [here](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb40115170h); a public adaptation of Hayman’s reconstructed text appears at [Open Siddur](https://opensiddur.org/readings-and-sourcetexts/mekorot/non-canonical/esoteric/sefer-yetsirah/sefer-yetsirah-earliest-recoverable-text-by-a-peter-hayman-adapted-by-aharon-varady/).

### The thirty-two paths

**[Documented text]** The work opens with God engraving creation through “thirty-two wondrous paths of Wisdom”: ten *sefirot belimah* and twenty-two foundational letters.

The letters are divided into:

- three mothers: א מ ש;
- seven doubles: ב ג ד כ פ ר ת;
- twelve simples: ה ו ז ח ט י ל נ ס ע צ ק.

Vav is therefore a **simple**, not a mother or double.

The text insists that there are “ten and not nine, ten and not eleven.” This becomes important to the later dispute over Da’at.

### What the earliest recoverable text says

**[Documented reconstructed text]** Hayman’s earliest recoverable layer names Vav among the twelve simple letters and collectively correlates the simples with twelve constellations, months, human functions, organs, and diagonal boundaries. It does **not** preserve a secure one-to-one statement identifying Vav specifically with Taurus.

Thus:

- Vav’s membership among the twelve simples is early.
- The fully individualized Vav–Taurus–Iyar–organ formula belongs to the developed recension history.
- “Path 16 joins Chokmah–Chesed” is absent.

### The received Vav formula

In the Gra recension, *Sefer Yetzirah* 5:7 reads:

> הִמְלִיךְ אוֹת ו׳ בְּהִרְהוּר וְקָשַׁר לוֹ כֶתֶר וְצֵרְפָן זֶה בָזֶה וְצָר בָּהֶם שׁוֹר בָּעוֹלָם וְאִיָּר בַּשָּׁנָה וְכוּלְיָא יְמָנִית בַּנֶּפֶשׁ זָכָר וּנְקֵבָה.

“He made the letter Vav king over thought/reflection, bound a crown to it, combined one with another, and with them formed Taurus in the universe, Iyar in the year, and the right kidney in the soul, male and female.”

See [*Sefer Yetzirah*, Gra version, chapter 5](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah_Gra_Version.5).

### Recensional variants

Aryeh Kaplan’s synopsis, especially his table on p. 219, gives the following readings:

| Recension | Sign/month | Human faculty | Organ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gra | Taurus/Iyar | Thought or reflection | Right kidney |
| “Ari” | Taurus/Iyar | Thought or reflection | Right kidney |
| Short | Taurus/Iyar | Hearing in some witnesses | Left hand |
| Long | Taurus/Iyar | Thought | Gall |
| Saadia | Taurus/Iyar | Hearing | Gall |
| Cordoverian/Ramak | Taurus/Iyar | Hearing | Left hand |

Source: Aryeh Kaplan, [*Sefer Yetzirah: The Book of Creation*](https://leonbahrmanministries.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Aryeh-Kaplan-Sefer-Yetzirah2.pdf), table 48, p. 219.

**Finding:** Taurus and Iyar are relatively stable across these later recension families; faculty and anatomy are not.

The [Saadia recension](https://hermetics.net/media-library/kabbalah/sepher-yetzirah-book-of-formation-saadia-version/) therefore does not simply reproduce the Gra text. The familiar Golden Dawn statement “Vav = hearing” agrees with the Saadian/Cordoverian stream, whereas “Vav = thought” agrees with the Gra/Ari stream.

### Tribe

**[Later Kabbalistic attribution]** Vav/Iyar is commonly assigned to **Issachar**, partly through arrangements of the tribal encampments, the zodiac, and the months. The tribe is not named in the core *Sefer Yetzirah* Vav formula. A Vital-oriented modern digest gives Vav–Iyar–Taurus–Issachar together, but this is a reception table, not proof that the equation stood in the earliest text.

### What Vav joins on Jewish Trees

The received *Sefer Yetzirah* speaks of twelve “diagonal boundaries,” but does not supply Kircher’s numbered planar diagram.

Diagrams commonly transmitted as the Ari and Gra Trees apply the classes as follows:

- three mothers to three horizontal relations;
- seven doubles to seven vertical relations;
- twelve simples to diagonals.

In both commonly printed Ari and Gra allocations, **Vav joins Keter and Binah**. It does not join Chokmah and Chesed. The two Trees differ elsewhere in the placement and geometry of Sephiroth and paths.

**[Disputed attribution history]** “Ari Tree” is best understood as a diagrammatic scheme transmitted under Lurianic authority. No surviving autograph by Isaac Luria proves that every modern graphic detail was drawn by him. The same caution applies when treating a later engraved “Gra Tree” as if the printed plate were itself the Vilna Gaon’s original sketch.

## II. The *Bahir*

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** *Sefer ha-Bahir* appeared in Provence in the late twelfth century, incorporating earlier motifs but attaining its known literary form in medieval Europe. Gershom Scholem’s historical account remains foundational, though later scholars have revised parts of his reconstruction. See [Scholem, *Origins of the Kabbalah*](https://kronadaran.am/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/G.-Scholem.-Origins-of-the-Kabbalah.pdf) and the [Posen Library overview](https://www.posenlibrary.com/entry/sefer-ha-bahir-book-brilliance).

**[Documented text]** The *Bahir* develops:

- letter mysticism;
- ten divine powers;
- the tree, channels, light, limbs, and cosmic form;
- the symbolism of six directions and lower divine powers.

[Bahir §147](https://fringe.hekhal.org/texts/sefer-ha-bahir-147-targum) derives the thirty-two paths from hidden light and Torah.

**Negative finding:** The *Bahir* contains no documented Kircher-style “Path of Vav” joining Chokmah to Chesed and no Vav–Hierophant correspondence.

## III. Rabbinic and medieval letter legends

### *Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva*

**[Documented medieval text]** This anonymous alphabetic midrash survives in multiple versions and is not historically a work by the tannaitic Rabbi Akiva.

In version B, Vav comes before God and asks to begin creation because the praise:

> וְאַתָּה קָדוֹשׁ יוֹשֵׁב תְּהִלּוֹת יִשְׂרָאֵל  
> “And You are holy, enthroned upon the praises of Israel” (Psalm 22:4)

begins with it. Vav is refused because it also begins the wording of a destructive verse:

> וַיַּךְ יְהוָה בָּעָם מַכָּה רַבָּה מְאֹד  
> “YHWH struck the people with a very great blow” (2 Chronicles 13:17).

The Hebrew text is preserved in the [Historical Dictionary Project database](https://maagarim.hebrew-academy.org.il/Pages/PMain.aspx?mishibbur=74000&mismilla=19&mm15=000000001141+00).

This is a distinct legend from the Zoharic procession of letters.

### Talmudic form-lore

The Talmud uses the shapes and legibility of letters homiletically and halakhically. Vav is specifically relevant where shortening it could turn it into Yod. [Menachot 29b](https://www.sefaria.org/Menachot.29b) tests the damaged Vav through a child’s reading.

**Negative finding:** There is no Talmudic doctrine of “Vav as the sixteenth path from Chokmah to Chesed.” Claims to that effect conflate later Hermetic diagrams with rabbinic letter lore.

## IV. The Zohar

### Date and authorship

- **[Kabbalistic tradition]** The Zohar records teachings of Rabbi Shimon bar Yochai and his circle in second-century Galilee.
- **[Scholarly reconstruction]** Its principal literary emergence was in late-thirteenth-century Castile. Moses de León was a major author, redactor, or transmitter, probably within a broader circle; many scholars now speak of layered or multiple authorship rather than a single act of fabrication.
- **[Disputed]** The precise division among de León, preceding materials, collaborators, and later strata remains debated.

A representative academic discussion is the [UCL study of Zoharic authorship](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10088406/) and scholarship on the [*Sifra di-Tseni‘uta* as a sample text](https://cris.tau.ac.il/en/publications/the-archaeology-of-the-zohar-sifra-ditseni%CA%BDuta-as-a-sample-text/).

### The letters petition to begin the Torah

In the Zohar’s introduction, letters approach God in reverse order. When Vav and Heh appear, God tells them that because they belong to the divine Name, they should remain there and should not become the instrument with which the world is created. See [Zohar, Introduction 6:34](https://www.sefaria.org/Zohar%2C_Introduction.6.34) and the parallel [Zohar Online text](https://en.zoharonline.org/Zohar-prologue).

This differs materially from *Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva*. The stories share an alphabetic-contest genre but should not be collapsed into one original narrative.

### Vav as six

**[Documented Zoharic tradition]** Vav’s value six makes it an image of the six central divine qualities, later formulated as Zeir Anpin: Chesed through Yesod. It can represent the “son,” the masculine configuration, the central column, or the connective extension of divine influx.

**Negative finding:** The Zohar does not identify Vav with Tarot, the Hierophant, or a numbered Chokmah–Chesed connector.

## V. The thirty-two named Intelligences

A short medieval Hebrew tract assigns a descriptive “Intelligence” to each of thirty-two paths. It circulated independently and was later appended to *Sefer Yetzirah* editions.

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** It belongs to medieval speculative Kabbalah, often associated with materials conventionally grouped around the Circle of Iyyun. Both its date and the coherence of an “Iyyun circle” remain subjects of research.

The sixteenth entry is:

> הַנָּתִיב הַשִּׁשָּׁה עָשָׂר נִקְרָא שֵׂכֶל נִצְחִי ... וְנִקְרָא גַּן עֵדֶן הַמּוּכָן לַצַּדִּיקִים.

“The sixteenth path is called the Enduring Intelligence … and it is called the Garden of Eden prepared for the righteous.”

The wording varies among witnesses. נצחי, *nitzḥi*, can mean enduring, perpetual, eternal, or—through the root נצח, victory—triumphal/victorious.

**Critical distinction:** The tract names its sixteenth **Intelligence**, but it does not thereby identify that Intelligence with the sixth Hebrew letter. That identification depends upon a later diagram that orders the twenty-two letters upon connectors.

## VI. Cordovero

### *Pardes Rimmonim* (completed 1548)

Moses Cordovero synthesized much of pre-Lurianic Kabbalah in the *Pardes Rimmonim*. Its twelfth gate transmits and comments upon the named paths. The relevant text is [*Pardes Rimmonim* 12:2–3](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.12.2).

**[Documented text]** Cordovero knows the sixteenth as שכל נצחי, the Enduring Intelligence.

**Negative finding:** This does not establish the later equation:

```text
sixteenth Intelligence = sixth letter Vav = Chokmah–Chesed
```

Those equations require an additional path-ordering diagram.

### *Or Ne’erav*

In [*Or Ne’erav*, part VI](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_VI.5), Cordovero interprets the Tetragrammaton so that Vav includes six Sephiroth. This is a direct Jewish Vav symbolism, but it concerns the six middot/central configuration, not Kircher’s path 16.

## VII. Gikatilla and Sephirotic names

Joseph Gikatilla’s *Sha‘arei Orah* organizes the Sephiroth through divine Names.

Relevant endpoint associations include:

- Chokmah: יה, Yah;
- Chesed/Gedullah: אל, El.

The work’s Sephirotic treatment can be consulted at [*Sha‘arei Orah*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha%27arei_Orah), including its [second-Sephirah section](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha%27arei_Orah%2C_Ninth_Gate%2C_Second_Sefirah.1).

**Negative finding:** Gikatilla does not provide a classical Jewish table in which the Chokmah–Chesed line has its own divine name, archangel, angelic order, planet, Tarot card, perfume, or colour scale. Those are later Hermetic constructions.

## VIII. Luria through Vital

Isaac Luria, the Ari (1534–1572), taught chiefly orally. Hayyim Vital (1542–1620) recorded and organized the teachings; *Etz Chaim* is a post-Lurianic textual construction from Vital’s writings and their editorial transmission.

### The Lurianic cosmological sequence

**[Kabbalistic tradition/documented Vitalian corpus]**

1. **Ein Sof** — the limitless divine.
2. **Tzimtzum** — contraction or concealment.
3. **Kav** — a ray entering the vacated space.
4. **Adam Kadmon** — primordial configuration.
5. **Olam ha-Nekudim/Tohu** — an unstable world of points.
6. **Shevirat ha-kelim** — shattering of vessels.
7. **Nitzotzot and kelippot** — sparks become entangled amid shells.
8. **Tikkun** — repair and reconfiguration.
9. **Partzufim** — dynamic “faces” or configurations: Arikh Anpin, Abba, Imma, Zeir Anpin, Nukva.
10. **Four worlds** — Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah, Assiah.

See [*Etz Chaim* 1](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.1.1), its treatment of [circles and Adam Kadmon](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.4.11), and the [broken vessels](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.26.1).

Vav commonly signifies **Zeir Anpin**, the configuration of six Sephiroth from Chesed through Yesod. Its vertical stroke can symbolize extension or influx.

**Negative finding:** Vital does not teach “the Hierophant path,” “pathworking Vav,” four colour scales, or Grant’s Uriens tunnel.

## IX. The Gra

The Vilna Gaon’s *Sefer Yetzirah* commentary became the basis of an influential recension and Tree arrangement. In the commonly transmitted Gra diagram:

- Vav is a simple letter;
- Vav retains Taurus and Iyar;
- Vav is located on Keter–Binah;
- Da’at is not simply inserted as an eleventh counted Sephirah.

The Gra’s geometric arrangement differs substantially from the Kircher/Golden Dawn Tree. Translating Golden Dawn Tarot cards onto it is a modern experiment, not a historical teaching of the Gra.

## X. Hasidic readings

### Tanya

There is no chapter of *Tanya* entitled “The Path of Vav.”

Relevant passages instead teach:

- Vav as the six emotional attributes or Zeir Anpin;
- Vav/final Heh as the revealed worlds;
- the divine Name as a model of emanation.

See [*Tanya*, Iggeret ha-Kodesh 5](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.5), [Iggeret ha-Teshuvah 4](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_III%3B_Iggeret_HaTeshuvah.4), and [Likkutei Amarim 40](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_I%3B_Likkutei_Amarim.40).

### Nachman of Bratslav

Nachman can treat Vav as a staff, line, or connective Torah-symbol and repeatedly describes Torah as divine Names. [*Likkutei Moharan* I:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Likutei_Moharan.1.4) is representative.

**Negative finding:** Nachman does not supply a Golden Dawn-style Vav pathworking.

### Prayer

Vav is omnipresent in liturgical Hebrew as “and.” Later Hasidic interpretation treats it as the joining of apparent opposites; one example is the Vav of *ve-ha-nora*, “and the Awesome,” in the Amidah’s first blessing. A Chabad presentation connects this Vav with Zeir Anpin and truth: [“The Letter Vav Is the Letter of Emes”](https://www.chabad.org/therebbe/article_cdo/aid/4721130/jewish/The-Letter-Vav-Is-the-Letter-of-Emes-Truth.htm).

This is Hasidic homily, not a universally normative technical attribution.

### Counting the Omer

The Kabbalistic Omer counts forty-nine combinations of the seven lower Sephiroth:

```text
Chesed through Malkhut
×
Chesed through Malkhut
= 49 combinations
```

Iyar contains most of the Omer and Vav is assigned to Iyar in received *Sefer Yetzirah*. Nevertheless:

- there is no single classical “Vav day” covering the Omer;
- the Omer practice is Sephirotic, not a ritual traversal of Kircher’s path 16;
- attempts to turn the whole month into a Hierophant pathworking are modern occult synthesis.

# The Hermetic and occult tradition

## I. Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Pico’s *Conclusiones nongentae* included forty-seven theses “according to the opinion of the Hebrew sages” and seventy-two Kabbalistic conclusions presented as his own synthesis.

**[Documented text]** Pico Christianized Kabbalistic concepts as supposed confirmation of Christological doctrines. His theses helped launch Christian Cabala. The Latin text is available in the [*Conclusiones*](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Conclusiones_philosophicae%2C_cabalisticae_et_theologicae), with individual theses in the [Brown Pico Project](https://cds.library.brown.edu/projects/pico/singleThesis.php?expand=sub10528&myID=p1052833).

**Negative finding:** Pico does not attest Vav as Tarot V, as path 16, or as a Chokmah–Chesed connector. Any modern statement that “Pico taught the Path of Vav” is anachronistic.

**[Scholarly assessment]** Pico depended heavily upon Flavius Mithridates’ Latin translations and read Jewish material through Christian prisca-theologia and apologetic aims. Whether termed appropriation, transformation, or interreligious translation, it involved extracting Jewish esotericism from its ritual and communal context.

---

## II. *Portae Lucis*, 1516

Paulus Ricius’ Latin partial translation of Gikatilla, *Portae Lucis*, included a famous title-page woodcut attributed to Hans Burgkmair.

**[Documented artifact]** This is among the earliest printed depictions of a Sephirotic Tree. See the [Center for Jewish Art record](https://cja.huji.ac.il/index.php?id=40577&mode=set), [historical study](https://www.sephardichorizons.org/Volume15/Issue1/Heller-Gikatilla.html), and [title-page image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3APortae_Lucis_1516.jpg).

Its form is not the later Kircher/Golden Dawn path diagram. It should not be cited as evidence for Vav–Hierophant or even for Kircher’s precise twenty-two-connector arrangement.

---

## III. Johannes Reuchlin, 1517

Reuchlin’s *De arte cabalistica* elaborated Christian Cabala through a dialogue among Jewish, Muslim, and Christian speakers. [Digitized 1517 edition](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/blbihd/content/titleinfo/7174105).

Latin spellings include *Vau* or related transliterations for ו. Reuchlin elevated Hebrew as a sacred language and treated divine Names, letter permutation, number, and angelology.

**Negative finding:** Reuchlin does not supply the later unit “Vau, sixteenth path, Chokmah–Chesed, Taurus, Hierophant.” His Cabala is an important intellectual ancestor, not an attestation of the completed attribution.

---

## IV. Athanasius Kircher, 1652–1653

Kircher’s *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, vol. II.1, contains the pivotal diagram.

### What Kircher changed

**[Documented artifact]** Kircher printed:

- ten Sephiroth;
- twenty-two explicit connecting lines;
- Hebrew letters distributed in alphabetic order over those connectors;
- Vav as the sixth lettered connector;
- that connector between Chokmah and Chesed.

His configuration is the direct visual ancestor of the Tree used in modern Hermetic Qabalah, although later occultists also received it through intervening compilations. See the [Heidelberg scan](https://digi.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/diglit/kircher1653bd2_1/0096), [Cornell plate](https://digital.library.cornell.edu/catalog/ss%3A550272), and [transcribed excerpts](https://www.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm).

### Kircher’s Latin sixteenth Intelligence

> *Decima sexta semita dicitur Intelligentia triumphans & æterna, eò quòd illa est voluptas gloriæ … & vocatur paradisus voluptatis, qui paratus est iustis.*

“The sixteenth path is called the triumphal and eternal Intelligence, because it is the delight of Glory … and it is called the paradise of delight prepared for the righteous.”

Kircher also calls it a path of victory against evil spirits.

This Latin explains Westcott’s “Triumphal or Eternal Intelligence.” Kircher translated or adapted the medieval Hebrew שכל נצחי but combined it with his connector diagram.

### Difference from Ari and Gra Trees

| System | Vav joins | Organizing principle |
|---|---|---|
| Kircher | Chokmah–Chesed | Hebrew alphabet assigned successively to a twenty-two-line planar Tree |
| Ari diagram as commonly transmitted | Keter–Binah | mothers horizontal, doubles vertical, simples diagonal |
| Gra diagram as commonly transmitted | Keter–Binah | same letter classes, different overall Sephirotic geometry from Ari |
| Golden Dawn | Chokmah–Chesed | Kircher-type connector map plus astrological and Tarot correspondences |

Thus the “Path of Vav” described in the brief is specifically the **Kircher–Golden Dawn path**, not a consensus Jewish path.

---

## V. Knorr von Rosenroth, 1677–1684

Christian Knorr von Rosenroth’s *Kabbala Denudata* translated and rearranged substantial Jewish Kabbalistic material in Latin, including Zoharic and Lurianic texts. [Digitized edition](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/10759091).

It disseminated:

- *sephiroth* and divine configurations;
- Adam Kadmon;
- Lurianic emanation and restoration;
- the Kings of Edom and shells;
- Latinized spellings such as *Vau/Vav*.

**Negative finding:** Knorr did not originate Vav–Hierophant; Tarot correspondences were still absent. His importance is transmission and Christian reframing.

---

## VI. Éliphas Lévi, 1854–1856

Lévi’s *Dogme et rituel de la haute magie* made the equation of twenty-two Hebrew letters and twenty-two Tarot trumps foundational to the French occult revival. [French text](https://www.arbredor.com/ebooks/Dogme.pdf).

But Lévi’s ordering differs from the Golden Dawn:

- the Magician corresponds to Aleph/Trump I;
- the Fool is displaced toward the end;
- **Vav corresponds to Trump VI, the Lovers**, not Trump V, the Hierophant.

Therefore:

> “Vav has always been the Hierophant” is false even within nineteenth-century occultism.

Papus largely continued the Lévi-style correspondence in [*The Tarot of the Bohemians*](https://www.labirintoermetico.com/02Tarocchi/Papus_The_Tarot_of_the_Bohemians.pdf).

---

## VII. The Golden Dawn, c. 1888–1900

The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn produced the now-standard Hermetic system:

```text
Vav
= sixth Hebrew letter
= sixteenth connector
= Chokmah–Chesed
= Taurus
= Trump V, the Hierophant
= “Magus of the Eternal”
```

The system is visible in the Knowledge Lectures, *Book T*, ritual papers, and later Israel Regardie’s publication of the order’s corpus. See the [First Knowledge Lecture](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Knowledge_Lecture_01_Neophyte.html), [*Book T*](https://jantetteroo.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Mathers_and_felkin_golden_dawn_book_t_th.pdf), and [Regardie’s *Complete Golden Dawn*](https://s3.us-west-1.wasabisys.com/luminist/EB/R/Regardie%20-%20The%20Complete%20Golden%20Dawn.pdf).

### Why the Hierophant?

**[Hermetic-occult reasoning]**

- Vav is the fastening nail or connective “and.”
- The Hierophant connects a tradition to initiates and mediates sacred teaching.
- Taurus supplies stability, embodiment, endurance, and institutional form.
- Taurus is fixed earth and ruled by Venus.
- The path joins Chokmah, archetypal Wisdom, to Chesed, ordered beneficence or Mercy.
- The “Eternal Intelligence” is interpreted as the transmission of supernal Wisdom into stable religious or magical form.

This is a coherent internal symbolic argument. It is not evidence that the author of *Sefer Yetzirah* possessed Tarot cards.

### Yetziratic text in Westcott

W. Wynn Westcott translated:

> “The Sixteenth Path is the Triumphal or Eternal Intelligence, so called because it is the pleasure of the Glory, beyond which is no other Glory like to it, and it is called also the Paradise prepared for the Righteous.”

See Westcott’s [*Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom*](https://hermetics.net/media-library/kabbalah/william-wynn-westcott-the-thirty-two-paths-of-wisdom/) and his 1893 [*Sepher Yetzirah* edition](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1893__westcott___sepher_yetzirah.pdf).

Westcott’s presentation encourages readers to think that the named Intelligences form part of *Sefer Yetzirah* proper. Text-critical study does not support that simplification.

### Four colour scales

| World/Golden Dawn scale | Colour |
|---|---|
| Atziluth / King | Red-orange |
| Beriah / Queen | Deep indigo |
| Yetzirah / Prince | Deep warm olive |
| Assiah / Princess | Rich brown |

These are Golden Dawn magical colours, not Jewish textual attributions.

## VIII. Crowley

### *Liber 777*

Crowley’s *777* systematized Golden Dawn tables while adding material from comparative religion, astrology, ceremonial magic, and his own choices. The first edition appeared in 1909. [Original scan](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1909__crowley___777.pdf).

Crowley retained Vav–Taurus–Hierophant.

### *The Book of Thoth*

Crowley’s Hierophant preserves Vav and Taurus. The card contains:

- a bull or Taurus symbolism;
- elephants;
- four Kerubic creatures;
- nine nails around the oriel;
- the child of the New Aeon;
- the Scarlet Woman;
- imagery of the conjunction of microcosm and macrocosm.

See [Crowley’s Atu commentary](https://hermetic.com/crowley/book-of-thoth/atu).

### The Tzaddi/Heh question

Liber AL I:57 declares, “All these old letters of my Book are aright; but צ is not the Star.” Crowley consequently revised the Emperor/Star, Heh/Tzaddi correspondences.

**Finding:** This dispute does **not** change Vav. Crowley continued to assign:

```text
Vav → Taurus → Hierophant
```

Claims that Crowley replaced Vav or made the Hierophant an Aquarian card usually arise from confusion over conflicting summary charts or the Heh–Tzaddi exchange.

### *Liber 231*

The Vav-associated verse reads:

> “Also is the Star of Flame exalted, bringing benediction to the universe.”

See the 1912 [*Equinox* printing](https://100thmonkeypress.com/biblio/acrowley/books/equinox_1_7_1912/equinox_v1_7_text.pdf).

**[Hermetic/Thelemic visionary text]** *Liber 231* is Class A in Crowley’s system. Its verses and sigils are Thelemic revelation, not recoveries of ancient Jewish path texts.

### Thelemic grades

The path’s endpoint grades are:

- Chokmah: 9°=2□, Magus;
- Chesed: 7°=4□, Adeptus Exemptus.

Because Kircher’s Vav connector spans the supernal/lower division, later pathworkers call it a conduit “across the Abyss.” There is no separate A∴A∴ grade called the “Grade of Vav.” The grades belong to Sephiroth, not connectors.

## IX. Dion Fortune, Regardie, Case, and Knight

### Dion Fortune

*The Mystical Qabalah* (1935) is organized primarily by Sephiroth, not by twenty-two chapters devoted to individual letter-paths. [Text](https://about.openlibhums.org/_pdfs/textbooks/DHpr5I/Dion-Fortune-Mystical-Qabalah.pdf).

**Negative finding:** There is no “chapter on the Sephira Vav” to reproduce. Vav is not a Sephirah, and Fortune did not write a full chapter entitled “The Path of Vav.” Her Western Tree nonetheless presupposes the Golden Dawn/Kircher arrangement.

### Israel Regardie

Regardie’s *A Garden of Pomegranates* treats Vav as:

- the Hierophant;
- Taurus;
- a mediating path from Wisdom to Mercy;
- an initiatory and religious symbol;
- deep indigo in the Queen scale.

See [*A Garden of Pomegranates*](https://www.labirintoermetico.com/06numerologia_cabala/Isreael_Regardie_A_Garden_Of_Pomegranates_2Nd%20Ed.pdf).

His publication of Golden Dawn papers made the system broadly accessible, but it also encouraged readers to call a specifically Hermetic synthesis simply “the Qabalah.”

### Paul Foster Case

Case retained Vav–Taurus–Hierophant and emphasized:

- hearing or interior audition;
- intuition;
- the union implied by a nail or hook;
- the crossed keys;
- musical note C-sharp;
- red-orange.

See [Case, *The Tarot*, p. 78 onward](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1947__case___the_tarot.pdf) and the [BOTA Hierophant page](https://botaineurope.org/the-tarot/5-hierophant).

His “hearing” is compatible with the Saadia/Cordovero recension family, whereas the Gra/Ari versions assign thought.

### Gareth Knight

Knight’s *Practical Guide to Qabalistic Symbolism* and later pathworking literature interpret Vav as the stable transmission of Chokmah’s force into Chesed’s spiritual authority. These are sophisticated twentieth-century Hermetic developments, not classical Jewish commentary.

## X. Pathworking

### Golden Dawn-derived method

A typical pathworking proceeds as follows:

1. establish a ritual temple or inner astral space;
2. invoke the endpoint Sephiroth;
3. visualize the Hebrew Vav and Taurus glyph;
4. enter through the Hierophant image;
5. test figures with divine names and symbols;
6. encounter a temple, teacher, bull, keys, or initiatory mystery;
7. return through the same route and record the vision.

Modern examples often include red-orange light at the threshold, deep indigo as the central atmosphere, Taurus landscapes, temples, thrones, bulls, and an initiator.

**[Modern occult practice]** These visions are shaped by prior symbolic priming. They may be spiritually meaningful to practitioners but are not independent historical evidence for an ancient path doctrine.

The Ciceros’ [*Skrying on the Tree of Life*](https://logoilibrary.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/skrying_on_the_tree_of_life-tabatha_cicero.pdf) explicitly makes path 16 a high initiatory ascent between Chokmah and Chesed.

# The Qliphah and the tunnel

## Jewish shells versus the occult “shadow Tree”

Jewish sources develop *kelippah*, “shell” or “husk,” especially through Zoharic and Lurianic treatments of the *sitra aḥra*, the “other side.” Lurianic Kabbalah relates shells to the broken vessels and entrapped sparks.

However, classical Jewish literature does not present:

- a Tarot-negative of Vav;
- a tunnel named Uriens;
- twenty-two nightside tunnels matching the Hermetic paths;
- a canonical demon prince assigned to every connector.

Those belong to later occult taxonomy.

## Ten Qliphothic orders

The following list was stabilized through nineteenth- and twentieth-century Hermetic tables, drawing on scattered Jewish, Christian-demonological, and Latin Kabbalistic materials:

| Sephirah | Qliphah/order | Crowley’s “prince of evil” correspondence |
|---|---|---|
| Keter | Thaumiel | Satan and Moloch |
| Chokmah | Ghagiel/Ghogiel | Beelzebub |
| Binah | Satariel | Lucifuge |
| Chesed | Gha’agsheblah/Gamchicoth | Astaroth |
| Gevurah | Golachab | Asmodeus |
| Tiferet | Thagirion | Belphegor |
| Netzach | A’arab Zaraq/Harab Serapel | Baal |
| Hod | Samael | Adrammelech |
| Yesod | Gamaliel | Lilith |
| Malkhut | Lilith/Nahemoth | Naamah |

**[Hermetic compilation]** Spellings and rulers vary sharply between manuscripts and later tables. This is not a single list quoted unchanged from the Zohar. The Golden Dawn’s qliphothic descriptions can be read in its [“Evil and Averse Sephiroth” paper](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Qliphoth.html).

## The Vav tunnel

Because Vav joins Chokmah and Chesed on the Hermetic Tree, its nightside tunnel lies between their shadows:

```text
Ghagiel — Uriens — Gha’agsheblah
```

### Kenneth Grant, *Nightside of Eden* (1977)

Grant names the tunnel **Uriens**. Its complex includes:

- the letter Vav;
- the sixteenth path;
- the pitch C-sharp;
- Taurus and a bull’s roar;
- the Adimiron or “Bloody” qliphothic order;
- the shoulders, Atlas, and burden-bearing;
- Gorgons and Minotaurs;
- a fiery sigil placed on a brown triangle;
- sexual and Set–Lilith symbolism;
- the *Liber 231* “Star of Flame.”

See Grant, [*Nightside of Eden*, pp. 188–189](https://s3.us-west-1.wasabisys.com/luminist/EB/G/Grant%20-%20Nightside%20of%20Eden.pdf).

**[Modern invention/Typhonian attribution]** Uriens is Grant’s occult tunnel-name, not an entity attested in *Sefer Yetzirah*, Bahir, Zohar, Cordovero, or Vital. Calling it “the traditional Jewish demon of Vav” is incorrect.

At the Sephirotic endpoints, Crowley’s table gives Beelzebub to Ghagiel and Astaroth to the Chesed shell. Neither is the prince “of Vav” in a classical source. Grant’s tunnel entity and Crowley’s endpoint princes belong to different levels of the later system.

- Lévi/Papus ordering: VI, Lovers.

## Diagrams and manuscripts

### Earliest Trees

Tree-like Sephirotic arrangements appeared gradually in medieval manuscripts; there is no single uncontested “first Tree of Life.” Early diagrams often differ radically from the later three-pillar glyph.

### *Portae Lucis*, 1516

The title-page woodcut is an early printed Sephirotic Tree but not the Golden Dawn path map.

### Kircher’s plate

Kircher’s plate is historically decisive because it clearly coordinates ten nodes, twenty-two lines, and alphabetic letter order. Vav consequently falls on Chokmah–Chesed.

### Golden Dawn coloured Trees

Golden Dawn manuscripts multiplied the Tree through four colour scales and four worlds. Their colour theory is ceremonial technology, not a reproduction of medieval Jewish illuminated manuscripts.

## Tarot artists

- **Pamela Colman Smith**, under Waite’s direction, created the influential 1909 Hierophant: enthroned religious teacher, two acolytes, crossed keys.
- **Frieda Harris** painted Crowley’s Thoth Hierophant during 1938–1943; the deck was published posthumously.
- **Paul Foster Case and Jessie Burns Parke** developed the BOTA Tarot imagery.
- **Robert Wang**, working with Regardie’s published system, produced an influential Golden Dawn Tarot.

## Poets and novelists

Crowley was himself a poet, and Dion Fortune and Kenneth Grant were novelists as well as occult writers. Their imaginative writing fed their esoteric systems.

**Negative finding:** There is no defensible canonical list of major poets or novelists who demonstrably worked with Vav path 16 specifically. Many modern novels use Kabbalistic Trees generally, but assigning a work to Vav without authorial evidence is interpretive speculation.

# Controversies and disputes

## 1. Date of *Sefer Yetzirah*

Traditional Abrahamic authorship is a sacred attribution. Critical dates extend across several centuries because the text is layered and recensionally unstable. The existence of astrological and anatomical material in received editions does not establish that every one-to-one attribution was present in the earliest recoverable form.

## 2. Zoharic authorship

The traditional Shimon bar Yochai frame and the historical emergence of the text in thirteenth-century Spain answer different questions. Historical evidence favours medieval composition and redaction, with Moses de León central but not necessarily alone.

## 3. Is the list of thirty-two Intelligences part of *Sefer Yetzirah*?

Textually, no: it is an independent medieval tract appended to editions. Westcott and occult publishers helped normalize the combined presentation.

## 4. Kircher versus Jewish Trees

Kircher’s diagram is not “the Tree exactly as taught by the Ari.” The letter-to-connector order conflicts with the Ari and Gra schemes. Vav’s location is the clearest test case:

- Kircher/GD: Chokmah–Chesed.
- Ari/Gra: Keter–Binah.

## 5. Tarot attributions

Lévi’s letter order makes Vav the Lovers. The Golden Dawn’s insertion of the Fool as zero before the Magician makes Vav the Hierophant. Neither scheme is present in ancient or medieval Jewish sources.

## 6. The Tzaddi/Heh controversy

Crowley’s reassignment concerns the Emperor and Star, not Vav. Invoking “Tzaddi is not the Star” to dislodge Vav–Hierophant is a category error unless one is adopting an entirely revised modern system.

## 7. Da’at

*Sefer Yetzirah* says ten, not eleven. Later Kabbalah treats Da’at as:

- knowledge emerging from Chokmah and Binah;
- a state or conjunction;
- an apparent position when Keter is not counted;
- a quasi-Sephirah in particular diagrams.

Calling Da’at an ordinary eleventh Sephirah contradicts the formal tenfold count. Calling it wholly “fake” ignores substantial Jewish Kabbalistic use. Its status is conditional and system-dependent.

## 8. Christian appropriation

Pico, Reuchlin, Kircher, and Knorr preserved and publicized material that might otherwise have had a narrower European readership. They also:

- subordinated Jewish readings to Christian theology;
- detached practices from halakhic and communal settings;
- treated Kabbalah as confirmation of Christianity;
- introduced syncretic Egyptian, Neoplatonic, and magical frameworks.

Both transmission and appropriation are historically documented; one does not cancel the other.

## 9. “Planet of Vav”

Vav is zodiacal, not planetary, in the Golden Dawn classification. Venus is relevant because it rules Taurus; the Moon is exalted there. Neither should be listed as if Vav were one of the seven double letters assigned directly to planets.

## 10. “The ancient nail pictograph”

The Hebrew word *vav* does mean hook or peg. The exact Proto-Sinaitic model for the letter remains disputed. Modern diagrams that show a recognizable iron nail in 1800 BCE go beyond the evidence.

## 11. Qliphothic princes

The ten demon-prince table is a Hermetic compilation. Spellings, pairings, and ranks vary. Uriens is a twentieth-century Grantian tunnel, not the Jewish demon of Vav.

---

# Open questions

1. **How early was Vav specifically assigned to Taurus?**  
   All major received recension families retain it, but the earliest recoverable text does not securely preserve the individualized equation.

2. **Who first superimposed the named Intelligences on an alphabetically lettered Tree?**  
   Kircher is the decisive printed witness for the familiar form, but manuscript intermediaries and earlier Christian diagrams require more codicological study.

3. **How directly did the Golden Dawn copy Kircher?**  
   The structural dependence is clear; the precise chain may include Rosenroth, nineteenth-century French occultists, Masonic diagrams, and Mathers’ manuscript research.

4. **Did Luria personally authorize the diagram now called the Ari Tree?**  
   The scheme is authentically embedded in Lurianic reception, but the graphic and editorial history is less secure than popular presentations suggest.

5. **What is the archetype of Proto-Sinaitic Waw?**  
   Hook, peg, oar, and mace proposals remain in circulation. Lexical meaning and pictorial origin should be kept separate.

6. **Can “Triumphal” be preferred over “Eternal”?**  
   Hebrew נצחי permits endurance/eternity and evokes victory. Westcott’s double translation reflects a real semantic range, while Kircher’s *triumphans et aeterna* makes the Latin reception explicit.

7. **Can experiential pathworkings count as evidence?**  
   They are evidence for modern religious experience and reception history, but not for the contents of ancient Jewish texts.

# Conclusion

The historically defensible genealogy is:

```text
Hebrew Vav:
hook/peg, conjunction, value 6
        ↓
Sefer Yetzirah:
one of twelve simple letters;
received attribution Taurus–Iyar;
faculty and organ vary
        ↓
Zoharic/Cordoverian/Lurianic tradition:
Vav as the six middot, Zeir Anpin,
and third letter of YHWH
        ↓
Medieval independent tract:
path 16 = Sekhel Nitzḥi,
the Enduring Intelligence
        ↓
Kircher, 1652–53:
alphabetic connector diagram;
Vav placed Chokmah–Chesed
        ↓
Lévi:
letters joined to Tarot,
but Vav = Lovers
        ↓
Golden Dawn:
Vav = path 16 = Chokmah–Chesed
= Taurus = Hierophant
+ four colour scales
        ↓
Crowley, Case, Fortune, Regardie, Knight:
expansion and pathworking
        ↓
Kenneth Grant, 1977:
nightside tunnel Uriens
```

The Path of Vav is therefore historically real as a major **Hermetic-Qabalistic tradition**, but it is not an unchanged doctrine transmitted from Abraham, *Sefer Yetzirah*, the Zohar, or Luria. Its Jewish ingredients—Vav, Taurus, Iyar, sixfold divine symbolism, and the Enduring Intelligence—are genuine but originally belong to different textual and diagrammatic settings.
