# The Path of Tav: Research Dossier

## Scope and evidence legend

This dossier distinguishes six kinds of claim:

- **[DT — documented text]** Directly attested in a cited primary text, manuscript, early printed edition, ritual, or correspondence table.
- **[SR — scholarly reconstruction]** A historical or philological conclusion argued by modern scholars.
- **[KT — kabbalistic tradition]** A received Jewish interpretation whose authority is traditional rather than historical-critical.
- **[HO — Hermetic-occult attribution]** A correspondence or doctrine documented in Christian Cabala, the Golden Dawn, Thelema, or their descendants.
- **[D — disputed]** The sources or scholarly interpretations conflict.
- **[MI — modern invention]** An attribution first demonstrable in modern occult literature, despite occasional claims of antiquity.

The central finding is decisive:

> **“Tav as Path 32, joining Yesod to Malkhut, ruled by Saturn and represented by the World/Universe” is a Hermetic-occult synthesis, not a unitary teaching of the ancient or medieval Jewish sources.**

The individual components have different histories:

- Tav is ancient.
- Tav’s classification among the seven double letters is early *Sefer Yetzirah* material.
- “Thirty-two paths” is early *Sefer Yetzirah* language.
- The named Thirty-second Intelligence is a later medieval appendix.
- Identifying the twenty-two letters with twenty-two particular lines between the ten sefirot is medieval and diagram-dependent.
- Kircher’s particular arrangement was published in the seventeenth century.
- Tarot XXI was joined to Tav by nineteenth-century occultists.
- Saturn–Tav in its now-standard Western form was fixed by the Golden Dawn.
- Earth as Tav’s secondary attribution is a Golden Dawn/Crowley supplement.
- Thantifaxath is first documented in Crowley’s *Liber 231* and turned into a “tunnel” by Kenneth Grant in 1977.

# The name

## Hebrew spelling and pronunciation

**[DT]** The letter is **ת**, named **תָּו**.

Common forms include:

- **Tav** — standard modern Israeli and many contemporary Jewish transliterations.
- **Taw** — a closer representation of Tiberian Hebrew *tāw* and common in Semitic philology.
- **Tau** — Greek-derived or older Western occult spelling.
- **Thau** — early-modern Latin and nineteenth-century occult spelling emphasizing the historical spirant pronunciation.
- Traditional Ashkenazi pronunciation distinguishes dotted **תּ** as /t/ and undotted **ת** as /s/. Tiberian Hebrew distinguished stop /t/ from fricative /θ/. Modern Israeli Hebrew normally pronounces both as /t/.

The doubling classification in *Sefer Yetzirah*—ב ג ד כ פ ר ת—reflects the older stop/fricative behavior of the **begadkefat** letters. It does not originally mean that each letter possessed two occult personalities.

## Meaning and etymology

**[SR]** The usual derivation connects the letter-name with Hebrew **תָּו**, “mark” or “sign.” The clearest biblical example is Ezekiel 9:4:

> וְהִתְוִיתָ תָּו עַל־מִצְחוֹת הָאֲנָשִׁים  
> “Put a mark upon the foreheads of the people.”

The consonant’s old Northwest Semitic form is a cross or intersecting mark. A standard early palaeographic summary describes Taw as a “mark” represented by a cross in the Proto-Sinaitic repertoire. The broad relationship between the name and the biblical noun is widely accepted; the precise pictorial ancestry, orientation, and route through the earliest alphabetic inscriptions remain matters of palaeographic reconstruction. [Ezekiel 9:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Ezekiel.9.4), [University of Chicago palaeographic study](https://isac.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/uploads/shared/docs/oic12.pdf), [recent alphabetic-sign study](https://cipl-cloud37.segi.ulg.ac.be/index.php/hieroglyphs/article/download/15/17/108)

### Reconstructable formal sequence

**[SR]**

1. A crossed or tally-like Proto-Sinaitic sign is conventionally reconstructed as the ancestor.
2. Phoenician/Paleo-Hebrew **𐤕** retained a cross-like form.
3. Imperial Aramaic handwriting progressively opened and curved the sign.
4. Jewish “square” script descended from the Aramaic branch and eventually produced **ת**.
5. Phoenician Taw was adopted as Greek **Τ, tau**, whence Etruscan and Latin **T**.

The standard Unicode history notes that Paleo-Hebrew is represented through the Phoenician character set, while Imperial Aramaic was the immediate graphic ancestor of square Hebrew. [Unicode, Phoenician and Aramaic scripts](https://unicode.org/versions/Unicode17.0.0/core-spec/chapter-10/), [Unicode Phoenician names list](https://www.unicode.org/charts/nameslist/n_10900.html)

**[D]** “The original Tav was literally a crucifix” is too strong. The sign was cross-shaped long before Christianity; Christian Cabalists later read this retrospectively as a prophecy of the Christian cross.

## Numerical value

**[DT]** Tav has the alphabetic numerical value **400**. Accordingly:

- ת = 400.
- א–ת symbolizes the alphabetic totality “from Alef to Tav.”
- אמת, *emet*, “truth,” has the value 441 and ends in Tav.
- את, the Hebrew direct-object marker, has the value 401 and consists of the first and last letters of the alphabet.

**[KT]** Rabbinic and later kabbalistic interpretation often treats Alef–Tav as totality. That symbolism should not be confused with the later Christian Alpha–Omega or occult World-card doctrine, even though those traditions frequently compare them.

## Tav in biblical and rabbinic lore

### Ezekiel’s mark

**[DT]** Ezekiel 9:4–6 describes a mark, תָּו, placed on foreheads before Jerusalem’s destruction. The verse does not explicitly say that the mark was shaped like the later square letter ת.

**[KT]** Babylonian Talmud, Shabbat 55a, develops the sign into a letter-symbol:

- a Tav in ink for the righteous;
- a Tav in blood for the wicked;
- ת for **תִּחְיֶה**, “you shall live”;
- ת for **תָּמוּת**, “you shall die.”

Reish Lakish further explains Tav as the final letter of God’s seal, **אמת**, truth. [Shabbat 55a](https://www.sefaria.org/Shabbat.55a)

### Truth and falsehood

**[DT/KT]** Shabbat 104a contrasts the firm-footed letters of אמת with the unstable letters of שקר. Tav thus participates in a graphic moral exegesis: truth stands; falsehood totters. [Shabbat 104a](https://www.sefaria.org/Shabbat.104a)

### The letters petition to begin the Torah

**[KT]** In *Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva* and, later, the Zohar’s introduction, the letters appear before God in reverse order. Tav comes first because it is the final letter. It argues from its place in **Torah** or from its status as the seal of אמת. God rejects it because Tav is also the mark associated with death in Ezekiel and ends **מות**, *mavet*, “death.”

The story’s point is not that Tav is an evil letter. It dramatizes the insufficiency of even “truth” when it stands alone and explains why creation begins with Bet. [Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva](https://www.sefaria.org/Otzar_Midrashim,_Otiot_D'Rabbi_Akiva.1), [Zohar introduction, Sulam presentation](https://www.sefaria.org/Sulam_on_Zohar%2C_Introduction.23)

**[D]** The precise relationship between the alphabet midrash and the Zoharic version is part of the textual history of medieval letter lore. It is not evidence that the Golden Dawn’s Path 32 doctrine existed in rabbinic antiquity.

## Latin and occult spellings

- **Pico:** generally transliterates the letter as *tau* or uses reversed Hebrew *ta* for את.
- **Reuchlin:** *Tau* or *Thau*.
- **Kircher:** *Thau*.
- **Knorr von Rosenroth:** *Tau/Thau* in Latinizing tables.
- **Mathers and the Golden Dawn:** normally *Tau*.
- **Crowley:** *Tau* in prose and correspondence tables, though modern Thelemic writers also use *Tav*.
- **Modern Jewish scholarship:** usually *Tav* or *Taw*.

Kircher wrote of the Samaritan form:

> “in signo Thau in charactere Samaratico figuram perfectam Crucis habere”

That is: “in the sign Thau, in the Samaritan character, [it] has the perfect figure of a cross.” This is a Christian typological observation, not ancient Jewish doctrine. [Kircher, *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, vol. II](https://www.esonet.org/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/Oedipus_Aegyptiacus02.pdf)

# The Jewish tradition

## Biblical foundations claimed for the Tree

The following verses became important to sefirah symbolism, but none names “the Path of Tav.”

### 1 Chronicles 29:11

**[DT]**

> לְךָ יְהוָה הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת וְהַנֵּצַח וְהַהוֹד כִּי־כֹל בַּשָּׁמַיִם וּבָאָרֶץ לְךָ יְהוָה הַמַּמְלָכָה

**[KT]** Kabbalists read the sequence as:

- Gedulah/Chesed
- Gevurah
- Tiferet
- Netzach
- Hod
- *Kol*, interpreted as Yesod
- Mamlakhah/Malkhut

This is a scriptural anchoring of the seven lower sefirot. It is not an enumeration of seven letter-paths and does not single out Tav. [1 Chronicles 29:11](https://www.sefaria.org/I_Chronicles.29.11?with=Liturgy)

### Proverbs 3:18–20

**[DT]** Wisdom is called “a tree of life”; God founds the earth with Wisdom, establishes heaven with Understanding, and divides the depths through Knowledge. [Proverbs 3:18–20](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.3.19)

**[KT]** These verses became major warrants for Chokhmah, Binah, and Da’at language. They do not supply Tav’s name, Saturn attribution, or path position.

### Job 28

**[DT]** Job 28 asks where wisdom and understanding may be found and says that God knows its “way.” It supplied kabbalists with scriptural language for concealed wisdom and paths, but it does not enumerate the thirty-two paths or identify Tav.

### Isaiah

Relevant motifs include:

- Isaiah 44:6 and 48:12: God as first and last.
- Isaiah 11:2: wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge, and fear.
- Isaiah 28:29: wondrous counsel and great wisdom.

**[KT]** These passages were woven into later systems of divine attributes. None says “Tav is Saturn” or “Tav joins Yesod to Malkhut.”

## *Sefer Yetzirah*

### Date and textual state

**[SR/D]** *Sefer Yetzirah* is first securely attested through tenth-century texts and commentaries. Proposed dates have ranged from the second century to the early Islamic period. Many contemporary scholars place its formation somewhere in late antiquity or the early Islamic centuries, while declining to assign a single exact date.

Peter Hayman’s critical edition prints the short, long, and Saadian recensions synoptically. More recent work by Avishai Bar-Asher argues that the so-called Saadian form has earlier Genizah attestation than older scholarship allowed. [Hayman edition description](https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/sefer-yesira-9783161587955/), [Bar-Asher, “The Earliest Version”](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/the-earliest-version-of-sefer-yeirah-text-format-structure-and-ge/), [Genizah and recension abstract](https://jstudies.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/jstudies/files/tarbiz_-_8902_-_book.pdf)

**[KT]** Jewish tradition frequently attributes the work to Abraham, sometimes treating it as knowledge transmitted orally before being written. This is a traditional attribution, not the conclusion of historical criticism.

### The thirty-two paths

The opening reads:

> בשלשים ושתים נתיבות פליאות חכמה חקק יה יהוה צבאות אלהי ישראל אלהים חיים ומלך עולם אל שדי רחום וחנון רם ונשא שוכן עד וקדוש שמו

“In thirty-two wondrous paths of wisdom Yah … engraved.”

The text then divides these thirty-two into:

- ten **sefirot belimah**;
- twenty-two foundational letters;
- the letters subdivided into three mothers, seven doubles, and twelve simples.

[Hebrew opening and recension map](https://hekhal.org/texts/sefer-yetzirah-1-1-2-targum-v2/), [Westcott’s public-domain translation](https://sacred-texts.com/jud/yetzirah.htm)

### What “thirty-two paths” originally means

**[DT/SR]** In the textual argument of *Sefer Yetzirah*, the thirty-two are the ten sefirot plus twenty-two letters.

**[D]** The text does not unambiguously provide the now-familiar Kircher diagram and then number its lines 11–32. “Tav is Path 32” follows when later interpreters:

1. treat the ten sefirot as Paths 1–10;
2. place the letters in alphabetic order as Paths 11–32;
3. assign those letters to a particular twenty-two-line diagram.

That convention is coherent within Hermetic Qabalah but is not a verbatim instruction in the earliest *Sefer Yetzirah* witnesses.

### Ten sefirot belimah

**[DT]** The book repeatedly insists on “ten and not nine, ten and not eleven.” Its sefirot are closer to primordial numbers, dimensions, or determinations than to the fully personified sefirot of medieval Kabbalah.

That difference matters for Da’at and for attempts to project the later Tree backward onto the book.

### Tav among the seven doubles

**[DT]**

> שבע כפולות בג״ד כפר״ת

The seven doubles are Bet, Gimel, Dalet, Kaf, Pe, Resh, and Tav. They are associated collectively with:

- seven planets;
- seven heavens;
- seven days;
- seven gates or bodily apertures;
- seven pairs of contrary conditions.

**[D]** The manuscript recensions do not support one original, uncontested Tav–planet allocation.

### Recension comparison

| Textual family | Tav’s planet | Day/body/opposite where explicit | Assessment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Earliest recoverable common core | No secure individual pairing | Collective sevens are present | **[SR]** Hayman’s reconstruction cautions against treating later tables as original |
| Long/Saadian-related order | Moon | Friday; left ear; dominion–subjection | **[DT/D]** Explicit but not Saturn |
| Donash/related early tables | Moon | Similar sequence, with local variants | **[DT/D]** |

A concise comparison based on Hayman and the principal recension tables is available in the [planetary-attribution synopsis](https://www.psyche.com/psyche/yetsira/sy_planetaryattributions.html). Aryeh Kaplan’s appendix prints the Saadian form with Tav as Moon, not Saturn. [Kaplan scan](https://leonbahrmanministries.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Aryeh-Kaplan-Sefer-Yetzirah2.pdf)

### The pairs of opposites

In the early long/Saadian sequence the seven pairs commonly run:

1. life/death;
2. peace/evil or war;
3. wisdom/folly;
4. wealth/poverty;
5. fruitfulness/desolation;
6. beauty/ugliness;
7. dominion or mastery/subjection or servitude.

Because Tav is seventh in that sequence, it receives dominion/subjection.

**[D]** Later recension reorderings move planets, body gates, and qualities together or separately. Thus “Tav = beauty/ugliness” is true in some late Western tables, while “Tav = power/servitude” reflects another order. Neither should be presented as universal.

### Tav, month, and tribe

**[DT]** Tav is a **double**, not a simple letter. Therefore:

- it has no native month assignment in *Sefer Yetzirah*;
- it has no native tribe assignment;
- it belongs to the seven-planet/day/gate structure.

Any month or tribe assigned directly to Tav is a later conflation or private system.

## The “Thirty-two Paths of Wisdom”

### It is not the original *Sefer Yetzirah*

**[SR]** The tract listing thirty-two named “Intelligences” is a separate medieval composition or exegetical appendix. Westcott printed it after his *Sepher Yetzirah*, which encouraged English occultists to treat it as part of the ancient book. Westcott himself presented it as supplementary material. [Westcott 1893 scan](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1893__westcott___sepher_yetzirah.pdf), [survey of English editions](https://www.themathesontrust.org/papers/judaism/karr-notes-on-sy-2015.pdf)

### Hebrew name of the thirty-second

**[DT]**

> הנתיב ל״ב נקרא שכל נעבד

“The thirty-second path is called *sekhel neʿevad*.”

The continuation in the Raavad-associated recension reads substantially:

> ונקרא כן מפני שהוא מתוקן לכל המשתמשים בעבודת שבעה כוכבי לכת לחבלם

A cautious translation is:

> “It is so called because it is prepared for those who make use of the service/worship of the seven planets, to their harm.”

The wording varies slightly in witnesses and is not entirely transparent. [Raavad-associated Hebrew text](https://www.sefaria.org/Ra%27avad_on_Sefer_Yetzirah%2C_Introduction%2C_The_Thirty_Two_Paths_of_Wisdom%2C_The_Thirty_Two_Paths_Explained.7), [Hebrew PDF witness](https://or-breslev.co.il/wp-content/uploads/%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%91%D7%93.pdf)

### What does נֶעֱבָד mean?

Possible senses include:

- worked or cultivated;
- served;
- worshipped;
- made use of;
- subjected to operation.

**[D]** “Administrative” is not the most literal translation. Westcott’s rendering interprets the path as an intelligence that directs planetary operations:

> “The Thirty-second Path is the Administrative Intelligence … because it directs and associates … the seven planets.”

Westcott continues that the planets are guided in their due courses. [Westcott text](https://sacred-texts.com/jud/yetzirah.htm)

The difference is historically important:

- the Hebrew gloss appears concerned with human use or worship of the seven planets;
- Westcott turns the intelligence itself into a planetary administrator;
- Golden Dawn writers then apply that administrative quality to Saturn–Tav.

## *Sefer ha-Bahir*

**[DT]** The *Bahir*, emerging in Provence in the late twelfth century though containing older materials, discusses:

- the thirty-two paths;
- the numerical value of **לב**, *lev*, “heart,” as 32;
- letters, sefirot, divine powers, and symbolic trees.

[Bahir 7–8](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir.8)

**Absence-of-evidence finding:** The *Bahir* does not present the Golden Dawn’s “Tav, Saturn, World, Yesod–Malkhut, Administrative Intelligence” complex. Its thirty-two-path language belongs to the formative Jewish history of the idea, but a discrete Path of Tav dossier cannot be extracted from it.

## The Zohar

### Historical placement

**[KT]** Traditional attribution assigns the Zohar to Rabbi Shimon bar Yohai, a second-century sage.

**[SR/D]** Gershom Scholem argued that Moses de León was its principal thirteenth-century author. Later scholars—including Yehuda Liebes, Ronit Meroz, Daniel Matt, and others—have increasingly described a Castilian literary circle and multiple compositional strata rather than a single simple authorship event. [National Library of Israel overview](https://www.nli.org.il/en/discover/judaism/jewish-bookshelf/zohar), [Pritzker Edition front matter](https://supress.sites-pro.stanford.edu/sites/supress/files/media/file/4810_Front_Matter.pdf), [study of the authorship question](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10088406/)

### Tav petitions God

**[DT/KT]** In the Zohar’s alphabet prologue, Tav presents itself first. Its claims and rejection turn on:

- the final letter of אמת, truth;
- God’s seal;
- the mark in Ezekiel;
- the last letter of מות, death.

This is genuine Tav lore. It still does not identify Tav with Saturn, Tarot XXI, or a Yesod–Malkhut line. [Zohar introduction with Sulam](https://www.sefaria.org/Sulam_on_Zohar%2C_Introduction.23)

### Tav and Lilith

**[D]** Later Zoharic commentary, especially the twentieth-century *Sulam*, can connect the Tav of מות with the feminine demonic side and Lilith. That does not make “Tav = Lilith = Thantifaxath” a doctrine of the original Zohar. The latter combination belongs to modern occult synthesis. [Sulam on the Zoharic prologue](https://www.sefaria.org/Sulam_on_Zohar%2C_Introduction.213)

## Gikatilla’s *Sha’arei Orah*

Joseph Gikatilla’s *Gates of Light*, composed in the late thirteenth century, organizes divine names through the ten sefirot.

Relevant endpoint associations include:

- Malkhut: **Adonai** and kingship;
- Yesod: **El Chai** and **Shaddai**, divine vitality and covenantal foundation.

**[DT/KT]** These are sefirah and divine-name relationships, not a Saturn–Tav path attribution. Later Christian and Hermetic tables extracted and regularized the names into fixed columns of “divine name, archangel, angelic order, planet.” The original work is more exegetical and dynamic than those charts imply. [*Sha’arei Orah* on Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/Shaarei_Orah?tab=contents)

## Cordovero

### *Pardes Rimmonim* (completed 1548)

Moses Cordovero systematized earlier Kabbalah in thirty-two gates. His discussions include:

- the ten sefirot;
- channels or paths between them;
- divine names;
- angels;
- colours;
- the relation of letter-wisdom to emanation.

In Gate 12 he explains that paths are called **seklim**, intelligences, because the flow of Chokhmah is apprehended through them. [*Pardes Rimmonim* 12:3](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.12.3), [contents and index](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim%2C_Index)

**[D]** Surviving manuscripts and printed diagrams do not yield a single uncontested Cordoverian twenty-two-line map. Descriptions in *Pardes Rimmonim* and *Or Ne’erav* have been interpreted as giving somewhat different totals unless implicit or uncounted channels are supplied.

**Absence-of-evidence finding:** Cordovero does not teach the World tarot card, Golden Dawn colours, or Thantifaxath. “Tav = Saturn” is also not a safe attribution to him without specifying a particular *Sefer Yetzirah* table.

### *Or Ne’erav*

The section indexed “Letter Tav” is a lexical index or exposition of Tav-initial terms—including desire, produce, Torah, and related concepts. It is not a Hermetic Path 32 chapter. [*Or Ne’erav*, Tav](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_VII%2C_Letter_Tav)

## Luria through Hayyim Vital

### Lurianic doctrines

**[DT/KT]** Hayyim Vital’s *Etz Chaim* records the Lurianic complex of:

- Ein Sof and the withdrawal or concealment called *tzimtzum*;
- primordial ray and vessels;
- Adam Kadmon;
- the worlds of Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah, and Assiah;
- the shattering of the vessels, *shevirat ha-kelim*;
- sparks embedded in the lower realms;
- partzufim;
- rectification, *tikkun*.

[Sefer *Etz Chaim*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.4.11), [University of Pennsylvania Vital manuscript](https://openn.library.upenn.edu/Data/0021/html/HebrewMss32.html)

### Does Vital assign Tav to the Yesod–Malkhut path?

**[D]** Modern diagrams often call a particular one-path-to-Malkhut structure the “Ari Tree” and place:

- the three mothers on horizontal paths;
- seven doubles on vertical paths;
- twelve simples on diagonals;
- Tav on the last central vertical, Yesod–Malkhut.

But this tidy letter-map should not be represented as a straightforward plate drawn and labeled by Isaac Luria in Vital’s *Etz Chaim*. Lurianic ilanot were transmitted, expanded, and recombined by Vital’s successors, including Israel Sarug, Jacob Tzemah, and Meir Poppers.

The safest formulation is:

- **[KT]** The “Ari tree” is a traditional reconstruction consonant with later Lurianic diagram practice.
- **[D]** Its exact letter labels are not uniformly attested in Vital’s principal text.
- **[HO]** Western occult writers frequently contrast this reconstructed tree with Kircher as though both were fixed authorial diagrams. The actual manuscript history is more complicated.

### Structural difference from Kircher

In the commonly printed Ari-type tree:

- Malkhut is connected only to Yesod.
- Two upper diagonal channels connect Chokhmah–Gevurah and Binah–Chesed.

In Kircher’s tree:

- the two crossed upper diagonals are absent;
- Netzach–Malkhut and Hod–Malkhut are present;
- Malkhut therefore has three incoming paths.

In both commonly reconstructed layouts, Tav itself remains Yesod–Malkhut. What changes are the neighboring simple-letter paths and the overall channel logic.

## The Gra tree

The Vilna Gaon’s recension and diagrams seek a geometrically systematic implementation of *Sefer Yetzirah*:

- three mothers on horizontal axes;
- seven doubles on verticals;
- twelve simples on diagonals.

In a standard reconstruction:

- Bet enters Chesed;
- Gimel enters Gevurah;
- Dalet enters Tiferet;
- Kaf enters Netzach;
- Pe enters Hod;
- Resh enters Yesod;
- Tav enters Malkhut from Yesod.

Thus:

> **Ari reconstruction: Tav = Yesod–Malkhut.**  
> **Gra reconstruction: Tav = Yesod–Malkhut.**  
> **Kircher/Golden Dawn: Tav = Yesod–Malkhut.**

The dispute is not Tav’s endpoints in these three standard reconstructions; it is the provenance, surrounding geometry, and planetary/trump attribution. ## Prayer, Omer, calendar, and Torah

### Prayer

**[DT]** Tav appears throughout the Hebrew liturgy simply as a letter. Especially relevant is the final alphabetic stanza of Psalm 119, verses 169–176, whose eight verses begin with Tav.

**[KT]** אמת, ending in Tav, is a major liturgical word: God’s seal is truth, and אמת joins the end of the Shema paragraph to the redemption blessing.

**Absence-of-evidence finding:** There is no universal Jewish prayer assigned to “Path 32 Tav.”

### Counting the Omer

**[KT]** Kabbalistic Omer practice maps the forty-nine days as seven lower sefirot within one another.

**[DT]** *Sefer Yetzirah* also mentions seven weeks among the manifestations of the seven doubles.

**Absence-of-evidence finding:** No classical standard assigns a particular Omer day to Tav. The resemblance “seven doubles/seven weeks” is real, but a Tav day is a modern extrapolation.

### Calendar

Tav is a double letter; month and tribe belong to the twelve simples. Its calendrical possibilities are therefore weekday and planet, not month and tribe.

### Torah

Tav is:

- the final letter of the alphabet;
- part of בראשית and many opening words of Genesis;
- the final letter of אמת;
- joined with Alef in את, the direct-object marker prominent in Genesis 1:1.

**[KT]** Kabbalists may read את as encompassing the full alphabet. **[D]** Pico and Christian Cabalists transformed this into cosmological and Christological claims that are not equivalent to ordinary Hebrew grammar.

# The Hermetic and occult tradition

## Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Pico’s *Conclusiones* contains forty-seven conclusions “according to the doctrine of the Hebrew cabalists” and seventy-one according to his own Cabalistic opinion.

He distinguishes Cabala into sciences of sefirot and names and discusses “alphabetic revolutions.” He also interprets the two instances of את in Genesis 1:1:

> “Per dictionem ta quae bis ponitur…”

He argues that the two occurrences signify prior intellectual and animate natures. [Latin *Conclusiones*](https://www2.esotericarchives.com/pico/conclus.htm), [Brown critical project](https://cds.library.brown.edu/projects/pico/latest.php)

**[DT]** Pico used Hebrew letter exegesis.

**[SR]** His principal mediator was the convert Flavius Mithridates, whose Latin translations could alter their Jewish originals.

**[D]** Pico does not yet provide the later Tav–Saturn–World–Yesod/Malkhut package. Claims that he knew the Golden Dawn path system are anachronistic.

**[SR]** Pico’s Christian Cabala deliberately reinterpreted Jewish materials to confirm the Trinity and Christ. His conclusion that every Jewish Cabalist must concede Christian Trinitarian truth documents the confessional purpose of the appropriation. [Stanford Encyclopedia on Pico](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2013/entries/pico-della-mirandola/)

## *Portae Lucis*, 1516

Paolo Riccio’s Latin *Portae Lucis*, derived from Gikatilla’s *Sha’arei Orah*, was printed at Augsburg in 1516.

Its title-page woodcut, by Hans Burgkmair the Elder, is generally identified as the first printed depiction of the sefirot. It presents divine emanation in a Christian printed environment but does not yet constitute the Golden Dawn’s twenty-two-letter tarot map. ## Johannes Reuchlin, 1517

Reuchlin’s *De arte cabalistica* discusses:

- thirty-two paths;
- ten sefirot;
- Hebrew letters and names;
- the three divine-name eras of Shaddai, YHWH, and the Christianized pentagrammaton IHSVH.

**[DT]** Reuchlin helped establish Latin Cabalistic terminology, including *semitae* for paths and *Tau/Thau* for Tav. **[D]** Reuchlin’s early printed tree has fewer connecting lines than the later Kircher figure. It is not the Golden Dawn tree.

**Absence-of-evidence finding:** Reuchlin gives neither Tarot XXI nor the Golden Dawn four colour scales to Tav.

## Athanasius Kircher, 1652–1654

Kircher’s *Oedipus Aegyptiacus* published one of the most influential early-modern trees.

### The plate

The tree shows:

- ten numbered sefirot;
- twenty-two connecting channels;
- Hebrew letters on those channels;
- divine names and angelic material;
- a three-pillared geometry recognizable as the ancestor of the standard Western occult Tree.

[Kircher plate, Cornell](https://digital.library.cornell.edu/catalog/ss%3A550272), [Kircher scan](https://www.esonet.org/wp-content/uploads/2010/06/Oedipus_Aegyptiacus02.pdf), [Wikimedia image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tree_of_Life_%28Kircher_1653%29.jpg)

### Tav’s position

**[DT/HO]** Tav is placed on the lowest central channel between Yesod and Malkhut.

### Was Kircher’s tree purely his invention?

**[D]** No simple answer is adequate.

- Kircher printed and canonized the exact configuration later convenient to Western occultists.
- He drew on earlier Christian Cabalists, including Philippe d’Aquin.
- Jewish manuscript diagrams already displayed varied channel patterns; three paths into Malkhut cannot safely be called an invention ex nihilo.
- The plate’s synthesis and subsequent influence are nevertheless specifically Christian Cabalistic.

Scholarship on Kircher’s diagrams emphasizes compilation, translation, and rearrangement rather than a pristine transmission from one “original Tree.” [Daniel Stolzenberg, discussion of Kircher’s trees](https://setnakh.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Four_Trees_Some_Amulets_and_the_Seventy.pdf)

## Knorr von Rosenroth, 1677–1684

*Kabbala Denudata* translated and excerpted Jewish kabbalistic texts into Latin, including materials related to:

- the Zohar;
- Luria;
- Abraham Cohen de Herrera;
- the sefirot;
- the qlippot;
- divine names and partzufim.

[Mathers’s later English selection](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1912__mathers___kabbala_denudata.pdf), [online index](https://www.billheidrick.com/Orpd/KRKD/index.htm)

The Latin tradition uses **Tau/Thau**, value 400. Mathers’s later English tables gloss it as “sign of the cross,” but that exact English expression should be attributed to Mathers’s presentation rather than automatically quoted as Knorr’s wording.

**[SR]** Knorr was a crucial conduit between Jewish Kabbalah, Christian Cabala, and the nineteenth-century occult revival.

**Absence-of-evidence finding:** *Kabbala Denudata* does not itself establish the Golden Dawn’s World card or its colour scales.

## Éliphas Lévi, 1854–1856

Lévi’s *Dogme et rituel de la haute magie* made the systematic twenty-two-letter/twenty-two-trump equivalence central to French occultism.

### Tav and the World

**[HO]** Through ordinal alignment, the last Hebrew letter corresponds to the final numbered trump, the World.

Lévi’s tarot ordering differs from the Golden Dawn’s:

- the Fool is placed between Judgement and the World or treated as unnumbered;
- Justice and Strength retain the French historical numbering;
- the Golden Dawn later relocates the Fool to Alef/0 and adjusts the trump-letter sequence.

[French original](https://www.cercle-papus.com/ouvrages/eliphas-levi-dogme-et-rituel-tome-1.pdf), [English public-domain translation](https://magicgatebg.com/Books/Dogma%20et%20Rituel%20de%20la%20Haute%20Magie%20Part%20II%20by%20Eliphas%20Levi.pdf)

**[SR]** Historical tarot arose in fifteenth-century Italy. There is no evidence that its original makers encoded the Hebrew alphabet. Lévi’s linkage is a nineteenth-century occult construction, not recovered Renaissance card doctrine. ## The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, from 1888

The Golden Dawn fused:

- Kircher’s tree;
- *Sefer Yetzirah*’s letter classes;
- Lévi’s tarot principle;
- astrology and alchemy;
- Enochian magic;
- four-world colour symbolism;
- graded initiation.

### Tav’s fixed Golden Dawn identity

**[HO]**

- Path: 32
- Letter: Tau
- Endpoints: Malkhut–Yesod
- Planet: Saturn
- Secondary element: Earth
- Tarot: XXI, the Universe or World
- Intelligence: Administrative
- Esoteric title: “The Great One of the Night of Time”
- Function: passage from the material sphere into the astral or formative sphere

### Why Saturn?

The Golden Dawn’s planetary sequence follows its own Hermetic organization, not the Saadian or Gra recension. Saturn suits Tav because it signifies:

- boundary;
- finality;
- time;
- crystallization;
- material limitation;
- completion of a cycle.

This is symbolic consistency internal to the Golden Dawn. It is not proof that the ancient author of *Sefer Yetzirah* meant Saturn.

### Why Earth as well?

**[HO]** The Golden Dawn and Crowley treat Tav/Saturn as Path 32 and add **32-bis** for Earth, just as Shin/Fire receives **31-bis** for Spirit.

Crowley later admitted that Earth and Spirit were additions forced onto a twenty-two-letter scheme. Tav’s Saturn attribution is primary within the system; Earth is a supplementary “bis” attribution.

### Initiatory use

The Golden Dawn’s Theoricus ritual says the path of Tau is the only opened path leading from the Zelator grade toward Theoricus. It interprets the World card through:

- the four Kerubim;
- four elements;
- the astral plane;
- the Qliphoth below;
- Saturn reflected through manifestation.

[Golden Dawn Theoricus ritual](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Ritual_Theoricus.html)

The Portal ritual calls Tau a cross and describes the path from the “airy quarter of Malkuth” into Yesod, with Spirit acting upon matter. [Golden Dawn Portal ritual](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Ritual_Portal_Initiation.html)

### Book T

Book T identifies XXI as:

- the Universe or World;
- “The Great One of the Night of Time”;
- the foundation or synthesis of the cosmic elements and material world.

Its imagery includes a central female figure, an oval or wreath, and the four Kerubic emblems.

The card’s ordinary divinatory range includes completion, synthesis, success, matter, delay, inertia, obstruction, perseverance, and patience, modified by dignity and surrounding cards. [Golden Dawn tarot notes](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Tarot_Notes.html), [Crowley’s publication of Book T material](https://hermetic.com/crowley/libers/lib78)

## Westcott

William Wynn Westcott translated *Sefer Yetzirah* in 1887 and revised it in 1893. His version was exceptionally influential in English occultism.

His supplement gives Tav:

- Jupiter;

## Pathworking traditions

### Golden Dawn antecedent

The Golden Dawn’s grade ceremonies are the earliest major ritual dramatizations of this particular path complex. Techniques include:

- contemplation of the Universe card;
- visualization of Tav and Saturnian colours;
- movement from Malkhut toward Yesod;
- examination of elemental and Kerubic symbolism;
- astral skrying.

### Twentieth-century development

Fortune, Regardie, Case, Knight, Dolores Ashcroft-Nowicki, and later occult schools turned pathworking into a regular guided-imagery practice.

Recurring reports include:

- a dark gateway, cave, tunnel, or veil;
- passage from physical heaviness to lunar imagery;
- confrontation with fear of death, isolation, time, or bodily limitation;
- realization that material form is neither self-sufficient nor disposable;
- return with increased discrimination about fantasy and embodiment.

**[HO/MI]** These are initiatory phenomenologies produced within a trained symbolic framework. They are not independently verifiable descriptions of an objective astronomical road.

**Jewish distinction:** Classical Jewish Kabbalah certainly includes contemplative prayer, letter permutation, visualization, kavvanot, ascent traditions, and meditations on divine names. Modern tarot-based “Path 32 pathworking” is nevertheless a Western occult form, not a standard Jewish rite.

# The Qliphah or tunnel

## Jewish qelippot

Hebrew **קְלִפּוֹת**, *qelippot*, means husks or shells.

**[KT]** Zoharic and Lurianic traditions use qelippah language for forces of concealment, impurity, death, or self-enclosed existence. Lurianic teaching commonly distinguishes:

- three completely impure qelippot;
- qelippat nogah, the mixed or translucent shell through which ordinary permitted existence may be elevated.

This is not identical with the later Western chart of ten infernal spheres, one demon prince per sphere, plus twenty-two tunnels.

## Thantifaxath

### Crowley

- Thantifaxath’s name: **documented in Crowley**.

## Diagrams and manuscripts

### Earliest Jewish diagrams

Kabbalistic manuscripts from the late thirteenth and fourteenth centuries contain diagrams called *ilanot*. From the fourteenth century, long parchment scrolls increasingly mapped the sefirot, worlds, divine names, and cosmological processes. The layouts were diverse; there was no single universally fixed “original Tree.” [Ilanot Project](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/site/), [Ilanot database](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/Ilanot_Site/database.html), [survey of Italian ilanot](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/site/?p=585)

### *Portae Lucis*, 1516

The Augsburg title page is the first printed sefirah diagram. It is not yet the Golden Dawn Tree.

### Cordoverian and Lurianic diagrams

Manuscripts of *Pardes Rimmonim* and later Lurianic scrolls show multiple diagrammatic solutions. Seventeenth-century Lurianic ilanot could combine textual modules from Vital, Sarug, Tzemah, Poppers, and others.

### Kircher’s plate

Kircher’s plate became the dominant non-Hebrew printed template because it was reproducible and accessible to Christian readers.

### Golden Dawn coloured trees

The Golden Dawn’s *Minutum Mundum* and related diagrams added:

- four colour scales;
- tarot;
- astrological signs;
- planets and elements;
- grades;
- divine and angelic names.

These are late nineteenth-century esoteric artworks, not facsimiles of ancient Jewish manuscripts.

## Tarot artists

### Pamela Colman Smith

Her World card depicts a dancing central figure within a wreath and the four living-creature emblems. Waite concealed most explicit Golden Dawn attributions in his published text, but the deck emerged from that milieu.

### Frieda Harris

Crowley and Harris transformed the scene into a vortex of cosmic completion:

- the dancing female form;
- serpent;
- zodiacal ellipse;
- four Kerubic guardians;
- interpenetrating geometric fields;
- a more luminous palette than the older “Night of Time” might suggest.

Crowley explicitly says the New Aeon card should be brighter and more dynamic than the old subfusc Saturnian treatment.

## Poets and novelists

Writers such as William Butler Yeats, Arthur Machen, Gustav Meyrink, Jorge Luis Borges, Umberto Eco, and Alan Moore worked with Kabbalah, occult alphabets, tarot, or the Tree of Life.

**Evidence qualification:** Tav-specific influence is clearest in Golden Dawn members and tarot artists. For Borges’s “The Golem,” Meyrink’s *The Golem*, Eco’s *Foucault’s Pendulum*, or Moore’s *Promethea*, general kabbalistic engagement is demonstrable; a direct dependence on the technical Path 32 table must be established work by work and should not be presumed.

# Controversies and disputes

## 1. Date of *Sefer Yetzirah*

- **[KT]** Abrahamic authorship.
- **[SR]** Composition in late antiquity or the early Islamic period.
- **[D]** Proposed dates span several centuries.
- **Secure point:** the surviving manuscript/commentarial record is tenth century onward.

The diversity already visible in the earliest witnesses implies an earlier developmental history, but it does not identify a precise author or year.

## 2. Which recension is “Ari,” “Gra,” or “Saadia”?

Labels often conceal editorial history.

- “Saadia recension” names the text Saadia commented upon, not necessarily one he authored.
- “Gra recension” is a deliberate eighteenth-century reconstruction.
- “Ari recension” often means a later printed text aligned with Lurianic interpretive tradition, not an autograph by Luria.
- The short and long recensions contain both older and later readings.

Consequently, no responsible dossier should state simply “*Sefer Yetzirah* assigns Tav to Saturn.”

## 3. Is the Thirty-second Intelligence part of *Sefer Yetzirah*?

No, not in the strict text-critical sense.

The named “Intelligences” are a medieval tract appended to later editions. Westcott’s publishing decision fused the works for many occult readers.

## 4. “Administrative” versus *neʿevad*

The Hebrew means something like worked, served, worshipped, or made operational. Westcott’s “Administrative Intelligence” expresses the gloss’s planetary-governance idea but obscures the lexical and possibly polemical reference to planetary worship.

## 5. Zohar authorship

- **[KT]** Rabbi Shimon bar Yohai.
- **[SR]** Late thirteenth-century Castile.
- **[D]** Moses de León as single author versus a Zoharic circle and multiple strata.
- **Relevant Tav finding:** the alphabet-petition narrative is genuinely Zoharic, regardless of which authorship model one adopts.

## 6. Christian Cabalistic appropriation

Pico, Reuchlin, Kircher, and Knorr preserved, translated, and disseminated major bodies of Jewish learning. They also transformed that material for Christian apologetic purposes.

Calling the process merely “influence” understates the asymmetry; calling it mere fabrication overlooks the real Jewish texts and teachers involved. It was simultaneously:

- textual transmission;
- translation;
- conversionary polemic;
- creative reinterpretation;
- appropriation under unequal religious conditions.

A scholarly overview notes that this was not a disinterested exchange. [Princeton chapter on Christian Kabbalah](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i9572.pdf)

## 7. Kircher versus Jewish trees

The slogan “Kircher invented the occult Tree” is an oversimplification.

- Jewish diagrams preceded Kircher.
- Their channel arrangements varied.
- Kircher’s exact synthesis became dominant in Western print.
- The Golden Dawn adopted its geometry while applying a new tarot/astrology system.
- Ari and Gra trees impose different relations among mothers, doubles, and simples.

For Tav specifically, all three common reconstructions place it Yesod–Malkhut. The major dispute concerns why it is there and what, if anything, that line means.

## 8. Tarot’s antiquity

**[SR]** Tarot emerged as a European card game in fifteenth-century Italy.

**[HO legend]** Lévi, Papus, and later occultists associated it with Egypt, Hermes, Moses, and primordial wisdom.

There is no historical evidence for an ancient Hebrew–tarot key. The system may be symbolically productive without being ancient.

## 9. Saturn versus Earth

Tav’s Golden Dawn Saturn attribution and its Earth supplement are not identical:

- Path 32 = Tav/Saturn.
- 32-bis = Earth.
- Crowley interprets their overlap through heaviness and completion.
- Some later books casually say “Tav is Earth,” erasing the primary planetary structure.

## 10. Tzaddi/Heh

Crowley’s switch affects the Emperor and Star. It does not change Tav. Claims that Crowley reassigned all twenty-two trumps are incorrect.

## 11. Da’at’s status

*Sefer Yetzirah* insists upon ten sefirot, not eleven.

In later Kabbalah, Da’at may be:

- the unifying knowledge of Chokhmah and Binah;
- a state appearing when Keter is not counted;
- an internal partzufic configuration;
- an apparent or “invisible” position rather than an independent eleventh sefirah.

The Golden Dawn’s Abyss symbolism intensified Da’at’s diagrammatic role. Tav lies far below this dispute and does not depend on treating Da’at as an eleventh sefirah.

## 12. Qliphothic rulers

The ten qliphah names have roots in Jewish and early-modern materials, but their neat one-to-one demon-prince table is not a single ancient list. Later occultists harmonized:

- Zoharic/Lurianic shells;
- Knorr;
- Golden Dawn papers;
- Crowley’s *777*;
- grimoire demonology;
- Grant and Karlsson.

## 13. Is Thantifaxath an ancient demon?

No evidence found.

The name is documented in Crowley’s *Liber 231*. Grant’s tunnel interpretation is 1977. Claims of ancient Jewish provenance are modern invention unless new manuscript evidence is produced.

# Open questions

1. **What is the earliest manuscript of the “Thirty-two Paths of Wisdom”?**  
   The tract circulates with medieval commentaries, but its exact author, date, and earliest recoverable wording require a dedicated manuscript stemma.

2. **How should שכל נעבד be translated?**  
   “Administrative” captures Westcott’s interpretation but not its literal morphology. “Served,” “worshipped,” “worked,” and “operational” preserve different aspects.

3. **What is the relationship between the Tav petition in alphabet midrash and the Zohar?**  
   The narrative clearly belongs to a wider medieval letter-story complex. Exact direction of borrowing and redactional stages remain research questions.

4. **Which planetary pairing, if any, belonged to the earliest *Sefer Yetzirah*?**  
   The common core secures the collective relation of seven doubles to seven planets but not a universally agreed letter-by-letter table.

5. **How early is the Yesod–Malkhut Tav label?**  
   The line itself appears naturally in several later geometries, but dating the earliest surviving diagram with Tav explicitly written on it requires manuscript-by-manuscript investigation.

6. **How much did Kircher depend on Philippe d’Aquin and Jewish informants?**  
   The general chain is demonstrable; the precise source of each line and label is not always recoverable.

7. **Were all Golden Dawn colour scales fixed at one moment?**  
   Surviving instructional papers and members’ decks show small variations. The four Tav colours are stable in the Crowley/Regardie transmission, but physical pigment choices varied.

8. **What is the exact textual genealogy of the demon-prince list?**  
   Modern charts often combine Golden Dawn and later grimoire materials without identifying which names are in Knorr, Mathers, Crowley, Grant, or Karlsson.

9. **How should experiential pathworking reports be evaluated?**  
   They document the effects of ritualized imagination and initiatory training but cannot by themselves decide historical or metaphysical questions.

10. **Can Jewish contemplative practices be compared with pathworking without collapsing them?**  
    Comparison is possible at the level of letters, visualization, ascent, and disciplined imagination. Genealogical identity has not been demonstrated.

# Conclusions

The fullest defensible reconstruction is a history of successive layers:

1. **Ancient Northwest Semitic alphabet:** Taw is a cross-like mark.
2. **Hebrew Bible:** תָּו means a mark; Tav becomes the final alphabetic letter.
3. **Rabbinic lore:** Tav signifies life/death, truth, God’s seal, and alphabetic completion.
4. ***Sefer Yetzirah*:** Tav is one of seven double letters, but its planet varies across recensions; Saturn is not the universally original attribution.
5. **Medieval alphabet mysticism:** Tav petitions to begin creation and is rejected because its truth-symbolism is inseparable from judgment and death.
6. **Medieval “Thirty-two Paths” tract:** the thirty-second is שכל נעבד, later rendered “Administrative Intelligence.”
7. **Medieval and early-modern trees:** diverse Jewish and Christian diagrams place letters on sefirah channels.
8. **Kircher:** publishes the layout that will dominate Western occultism; Tav lies between Yesod and Malkhut.
9. **Lévi:** gives the twenty-two Hebrew letters a tarot sequence; Tav receives the World.
10. **Golden Dawn:** fixes Tav as Path 32, Saturn/Earth, Universe, Administrative Intelligence, with four colour scales and an initiatory ritual.
11. **Crowley:** expands the correspondences, renames the trump the Universe, and supplies Thantifaxath in *Liber 231*.
12. **Grant:** turns Thantifaxath into the thirty-second Tunnel of Set.
13. **Modern practice:** Tav becomes an experiential symbol of embodiment, time, boundary, death, completion, and passage between material and astral consciousness.

The Jewish and Hermetic readings can be placed beside one another, but they cannot responsibly be collapsed. Jewish Tav is first a letter, mark, double consonant, numerical sign, and vehicle of scriptural exegesis. Hermetic Tav is a deliberately assembled initiatory path whose Saturnian, terrestrial, tarot, chromatic, and qliphothic correspondences accumulated between the seventeenth and twentieth centuries.
