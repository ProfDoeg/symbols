# The Path of Mem: Research Dossier

## Method and evidentiary key

This dossier keeps three historically different objects separate:

1. **Mem in Jewish letter-mysticism**: the thirteenth Hebrew letter, one of the three “mothers” in *Sefer Yetzirah*, attributed there to water.
2. **The twenty-third “Stable Intelligence”**: a numbered item in the separate medieval list commonly called the *Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom*.
3. **The Hermetic Path of Mem**: the Geburah–Hod line on the Kircher/Golden Dawn Tree, furnished with Water, the Hanged Man, colours, gods, magical implements and pathworking practices.

The first is ancient or early medieval Jewish material. The second is medieval Jewish material of uncertain authorship and transmission. Their identification with the third is an early-modern and especially nineteenth-century Hermetic synthesis.

Labels used below:

- **[TEXT]** Directly documented in a named primary text.
- **[TRADITION]** A traditional Jewish interpretation accepted within a particular lineage.
- **[SCHOLARSHIP]** Historical or philological reconstruction.
- **[HERMETIC]** A Christian-Cabalist or Western-occult attribution.
- **[DISPUTED]** Evidence or interpretation is contested.
- **[MODERN]** A comparatively recent synthesis, expansion or invention.
- **[NEGATIVE FINDING]** The proposed attribution was not found in the relevant primary source.

# The name

## Hebrew spelling and pronunciation

**[TEXT]** The two graphic forms are:

- מ — open or medial *mem*, used initially and medially.
- ם — closed or final *mem*, normally used at the end of a word.

The name is vocalized מֵם, approximately *mēm*. Common transliterations include **Mem**, **Mēm**, **Mim** and, in older occult books, **Mem/Maim**. Crowley sometimes placed מים, *mayim*, “water,” in the name column rather than distinguishing the letter-name from its elemental keyword.

The [Jewish Encyclopedia entry on Mem](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/10611-mem) identifies it as the thirteenth letter, gives 40 as its value and relates the name to water.

## Meaning and etymology

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** The letter descends through the Northwest Semitic alphabetic family:

> probable Proto-Sinaitic water-sign → Proto-Canaanite/Phoenician *mēm* → Paleo-Hebrew מ-type forms → Aramaic-derived Jewish square מ/ם.

The conventional reconstruction connects the name with West Semitic words for water, including Hebrew מַיִם, *mayim*. Its remote ancestor is often identified with an Egyptian rippling-water hieroglyph, conventionally numbered N35. That is a palaeographic reconstruction, not a statement contained in rabbinic or kabbalistic literature.

The earliest alphabetic form was angular or zigzag-like. Phoenician and Paleo-Hebrew made it increasingly linear. Jewish square script arose from Imperial Aramaic handwriting in the Persian and Hellenistic periods. The medial form remained open while a word-final form became closed. Unicode accordingly treats Paleo-Hebrew inscriptions through the Phoenician block and modern Jewish writing through Hebrew; see the [Unicode Hebrew and Phoenician specification](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode16.0.0/core-spec/chapter-10/).

**[DISPUTED]** It is too strong to say that every stage is a directly demonstrated copying of the Egyptian water hieroglyph. The water-sign/acrophonic theory is the standard explanatory model, but Proto-Sinaitic inscriptions are sparse, inconsistently oriented and often difficult to decipher.

## Shape lore

**[TEXT]** Babylonian Talmud, Shabbat 104a, distinguishes open and closed Mem:

> מ״ם פתוחה מ״ם סתומה — מאמר פתוח מאמר סתום

“Open Mem, closed Mem: an open utterance and a sealed utterance.”

This is letter-exegesis, not historical palaeography. The same passage discusses the five final letters and the miraculous stability of the letters Mem and Samekh in the tablets. See [Shabbat 104a](https://www.sefaria.org/Shabbat.104a?lang=en).

**[TRADITION]** Later Jewish homily reads open Mem as revealed Torah and closed Mem as concealed Torah. It also associates the enclosing final form with gestation, concealment, *mikveh*, messianic potential and the closed Mem in Isaiah 9:6. These are productive exegetical meanings, not the reason historical scribes originally developed final letters.

## The anomalous closed Mem in Isaiah

**[TEXT]** Isaiah 9:6 writes לְמַרְבֵּה, “of the increase,” with a closed ם inside the word. Sanhedrin 94a asks why an ordinary medial Mem is closed and develops a messianic interpretation. Rashi preserves the anomaly in his commentary: [Rashi on Isaiah 9:6](https://www.sefaria.org/Rashi_on_Isaiah.9.6).

**[TRADITION]** The closed Mem became a symbol of a withheld or sealed redemption. Christian Cabalists later used it christologically, while Jewish commentators did not thereby identify it with Jesus or the Hanged Man.

## Gematria

**[TEXT/TRADITION]**

- מ = 40.
- ם normally remains 40 in standard gematria.
- In *mispar gadol*, the five finals may be extended to 500–900, making ם = 600.
- The spelling מם totals 80 if both letters are counted ordinarily.
- מים, *mayim*, totals 90.
- ארבעים, “forty,” is naturally linked to biblical periods of transition: the flood’s forty days, Moses’ forty days on Sinai, the spies’ forty days and Israel’s forty years.

**[TRADITION]** These biblical forties made Mem a convenient emblem of gestation, ordeal, purification and transformation. The connection is exegetical; the biblical narratives do not say that their forty-day periods encode Mem.

# Biblical background

## The Sephirotic proof-texts

These verses concern the vocabulary and structure of the Sephiroth more than Mem itself.

### 1 Chronicles 29:11

**[TEXT/TRADITION]**

> לְךָ יְהוָה הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת וְהַנֵּצַח וְהַהוֹד … לְךָ יְהוָה הַמַּמְלָכָה

Jewish kabbalists read the sequence as Gedullah/Chesed, Geburah, Tiferet, Netzach, Hod, “all” as Yesod, and kingdom as Malkhut. It supports the names of the seven lower Sephiroth, not a Geburah–Hod Mem path.

### Proverbs 3:19

> יְהוָה בְּחָכְמָה יָסַד־אָרֶץ כּוֹנֵן שָׁמַיִם בִּתְבוּנָה

“The LORD founded the earth by wisdom; established heaven by understanding.”

**[TRADITION]** This became a major proof-text for Chokhmah and Binah. It also resonates with *Sefer Yetzirah*’s earth/heaven arrangements, but does not assign Mem to a line between two Sephiroth.

### Job 28

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** Job’s poem asks where wisdom may be found, distinguishes it from mines and precious substances, and says that God comprehends its way. Kabbalists and Christian Cabalists used it as a scriptural warrant for hidden Wisdom. It contains no Path-of-Mem doctrine.

### Isaiah

Relevant passages include:

- Isaiah 9:6, the anomalous closed Mem.
- Isaiah 11:2, the spirit of wisdom, understanding, counsel, strength, knowledge and fear of God.
- Isaiah 55:1, “all who thirst, come to water,” traditionally interpreted as Torah.
- Isaiah 44:3, divine water poured upon the thirsty and spirit upon offspring.

These provide a biblical vocabulary of water, wisdom, spirit and concealment, but the composite Path 23 symbolism is post-biblical.

# The Jewish tradition

## 1. *Sefer Yetzirah*

### Date and recensions

**[TEXT]** *Sefer Yetzirah* opens with God engraving creation through “thirty-two wondrous paths of Wisdom,” then identifies its basic inventory as ten Sephiroth *belimah* and twenty-two foundation letters.

**[SCHOLARSHIP/DISPUTED]** Proposed dates range from late antiquity to the early Islamic period. Peter Hayman’s critical edition emphasizes that the work first becomes securely visible in the tenth century and that its wording must be reconstructed from divergent manuscripts. Saadia Gaon’s Arabic commentary, dated 931, supplies a firm terminus ante quem.

The principal textual families are:

- Short recension.
- Long recension.
- Saadian recension.
- Later redactions conventionally called Ari and Gra versions.

Hayman collated nineteen manuscripts and treated Short, Long and Saadian materials as distinguishable recensional evidence; see [Hayman, *Sefer Yeṣira: Edition, Translation and Text-Critical Commentary*](https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/sefer-yesira-9783161587955/) and its [publisher’s preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783161587955_A40613273/preview-9783161587955_A40613273.pdf).

**[DISPUTED]** “Ari recension” and “Gra recension” should not be imagined as autograph manuscripts written personally by Isaac Luria or Elijah of Vilna. They are edited traditional forms aligned with the readings and commentaries associated with those masters.

### The thirty-two paths

**[TEXT]** The opening arithmetic is:

- Ten Sephiroth *belimah*.
- Twenty-two foundation letters.
- Three mothers.
- Seven doubles.
- Twelve simples.

See [*Sefer Yetzirah* 2:6](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah.2.6).

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** The work does not say:

- “Mem is path 23.”
- “Mem joins Geburah and Hod.”
- “Mem is the Stable Intelligence.”
- “Mem corresponds to the Hanged Man.”

Nor does the ancient core give the later standard names Keter through Malkhut to ten diagrammatic nodes and then number the lettered connectors 11–32. Those are interpretive developments.

### Mem as a mother

**[TEXT]** The core statement is:

> שלש אמות אמ״ש בעולם אויר מים אש

“Three mothers, Aleph-Mem-Shin, in the world: air, water, fire.”

Mem is therefore the watery mother. The text contrasts it with hissing fire and places Aleph between them as a reconciler. The relevant Hebrew passages are collected in [Sefaria’s Three Mothers source sheet](https://voices.sefaria.org/sheets/263162).

A fuller creation formula reads:

> המליך אות מ״ם במים וקשר לו כתר וצרפן זה בזה

“He made the letter Mem king over water, bound a crown to it, and combined them with one another.”

Mem then forms:

- earth in the world,
- cold in the year,
- belly in the human being,
- with male and female permutations.

### Recensional variants

**[TEXT/SCHOLARSHIP]** Short, Long, Saadian, Ari and Gra witnesses vary in wording, ordering, grammatical person, the arrangement of organs and directions, and permutation formulas. The stable core is that Mem belongs to the mother triad and water.

Saadia’s version likewise treats Mem as the quiet, heavy or downward member of the triad. His commentary explains the shapes and sounds philosophically rather than attaching Mem to Geburah and Hod. A Hebrew/French presentation is available in [Lambert’s edition of Saadia’s commentary, chapter 3](https://www.biblioj.fr/saadia-gaon-sefer-yetzira-commentaire-lambert-chapitre-3/).

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** No recension makes Mem a month, tribe, planet or pair of moral opposites:

- Month and tribe belong to the twelve simple letters.
- Planet and opposing quality belong to the seven doubles.
- Element belongs to the mothers.

### Water and earth

A frequent modern misunderstanding is that assigning Mem to water and saying it “formed earth” is contradictory. *Sefer Yetzirah* works with nested triads:

| Domain | Aleph | Mem | Shin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primordial attribution | Air | Water | Fire |
| World | air/atmospheric mediation | Earth from water | Heaven from fire |
| Year | temperate mediation | Cold | Heat |
| Person | chest or mediating torso | Belly | Head |

Exact anatomical wording varies, but water remains Mem’s elemental power.

## 2. The separate *Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom*

The short list of thirty-two named “Intelligences” circulated as an appendix to some editions of *Sefer Yetzirah*. In Western transmission it was especially influential through Joannes Stephanus Rittangelius’s Hebrew-Latin edition of 1642 and Westcott’s English translation.

### Path 23 in Hebrew

**[TEXT]**

> נתיב כ״ג נקרא שכל קיים. ונקרא כן מפני שהוא כח קיום כל הספירות

“The twenty-third path is called the Enduring Intelligence, because it is the sustaining power of all the Sephiroth.”

A variant continues that it sustains those who hold fast to it and clothes them with the holy spirit. The Hebrew list is reproduced at [The Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom](https://www.mr-kaplan.com/Hebrew/32-paths-of-wisdom.html).

### Westcott’s translation

**[TEXT/HERMETIC TRANSMISSION]** W. Wynn Westcott translated:

> “The Twenty-third Path is the Stable Intelligence … because it has the virtue of consistency among all numerations.”

See Westcott’s [*Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom*](https://hermetics.net/media-library/kabbalah/william-wynn-westcott-the-thirty-two-paths-of-wisdom/).

“Numerations” is Westcott’s way of rendering the Sephiroth; it should not be taken to mean ordinary arithmetic.

### Textual status

**[SCHOLARSHIP/DISPUTED]**

- This list is not the same composition as the core *Sefer Yetzirah*.
- Its author and exact date are uncertain.
- Medieval manuscripts and commentaries transmitted variant forms.
- Western occult editions often print it as though it were the final chapter of *Sefer Yetzirah*, obscuring its separate history.
- The identification “item 23 = Mem” follows the convenient arithmetic of ten Sephiroth followed by Aleph as 11, Beth as 12, and so on. It is not stated in the Hebrew description of *Sekhel Qayyām* itself.

## 3. *Sefer ha-Bahir*

**[TEXT/SCHOLARSHIP]** The *Bahir* appeared in Provence near the end of the twelfth century, though tradition attributes it to the first-century sage Neḥunya ben ha-Qanah.

It develops:

- the thirty-two paths,
- letters as creative powers,
- water as Torah and blessing,
- right/left polarities,
- ten divine powers that become the classical Sephiroth.

Section 147 connects the hidden primordial light, the thirty-two paths and Oral Torah; see [*Bahir* §147](https://fringe.hekhal.org/texts/sefer-ha-bahir-147-targum).

Another passage associates Michael and water with the right and Gabriel and fire with the left: [*Bahir* §8](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir.8).

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** The *Bahir* does not identify Mem with a numbered Geburah–Hod path, the Stable Intelligence or the Hanged Man. Its right-hand association of water may actually sit uneasily with the later Hermetic placement of watery Mem down the left Pillar of Severity.

## 4. Alphabet legends

### *Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva*

**[TRADITION/SCHOLARSHIP]** The *Alphabet of Rabbi Akiva* is a pseudonymous early-medieval alphabet midrash, not a surviving work authored by the second-century Rabbi Akiva. It assigns cosmic meanings to letter names, shapes and crowns.

In its alphabetic petitions, Mem appeals through words such as *melekh*, “king,” or *malkhut*, “kingdom.” God does not choose it to begin creation. Versions differ substantially; a modern English presentation is available through [Power Sefer Press](https://powerseferpress.com/ak1/), with bibliographical orientation at the [National Library tradition summarized here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alphabet_of_Rabbi_Akiva).

### Letters petitioning to begin the Torah

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** In the Zoharic prologue the letters approach God in reverse order, each asking that creation begin with it. Mem argues from מֶלֶךְ, *melekh*, “king.” God replies that the world needs a king and sends Mem back together with Lamed and Kaf, which spell מלך.

The episode appears in [*Sulam* on the Zoharic Introduction §30](https://www.sefaria.org/Sulam_on_Zohar%2C_Introduction.30).

This is a literary theosophy of the alphabet. It is not an account of Mem joining Geburah and Hod.

## 5. The Zohar

### Date and authorship

**[TRADITION]** The Zohar speaks in the voice and circle of Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai, a second-century tanna.

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** Its principal strata emerged in late-thirteenth-century Castile. Gershom Scholem argued that Moses de León composed the principal Zoharic body, while later scholars have refined the model toward layered composition, collaboration and different authorial circles. Scholem’s evidence includes its medieval vocabulary, sources, Aramaic, allusions to contemporary debates and circulation through de León. His discussion is available in [*Kabbalah*](https://www.betemunah.org/kabbalah-gershom-scholem.pdf), pp. 233–249 of the scan.

Daniel Matt’s Pritzker edition treats the work as a stratified literary corpus while translating its Aramaic in historical context: [The Zohar: Pritzker Edition](https://www.sup.org/books/jewish-studies/zohar-pritzker-edition-volume-one).

### Mem imagery

**[TEXT]** Zoharic Mem lore includes:

- the alphabetic petition;
- open and closed aspects of divine speech;
- water as Torah and supernal influx;
- *mayin dekhurin* and *mayin nukvin*, masculine and feminine waters;
- the closed Mem as enclosure, womb and concealed royal power;
- water flowing through the Sephirotic structure.

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** Neither the main Zohar nor the later *Tikkunim* supplies the complete Golden Dawn object “Path 23 = Mem = Geburah–Hod = Hanged Man.”

## 6. Gikatilla’s *Sha‘arei Orah*

Joseph Gikatilla wrote *Sha‘arei Orah* in Castile around 1290. Its ten gates organize divine names around the Sephiroth and became one of the most important bridges between biblical names and Sephirotic theosophy.

For the endpoints relevant to the later Hermetic path:

| Sephirah | Common Gikatillian associations |
|---|---|
| Geburah | Judgment, fear, power, the left arm; names related to Elohim and divine severity |
| Hod | Prophetic splendour and the lower left side/leg; divine hosts and channels of prophetic reception |

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** Gikatilla does not establish a special Mem channel between them, nor assign that channel Gabriel, Water or a tarot image.

Paulus Ricius’s Latin translation, *Portae Lucis* (Augsburg, 1516), placed an influential Sephirotic diagram on its title page. It is an early printed Tree, but not yet Kircher’s lettered twenty-two-path grid. See [Marvin Heller on the 1516 *Portae Lucis*](https://www.sephardichorizons.org/Volume15/Issue1/Heller-Gikatilla.html).

## 7. Cordovero

### *Pardes Rimmonim* (completed 1548)

Moses Cordovero systematized earlier Kabbalah before the emergence of Lurianism.

**[TEXT]** His discussions include:

- Sephirotic names and their scriptural foundations;
- channels between Sephiroth;
- divine names, angels, colours and bodily correspondences;
- water on the right, principally Chesed;
- upper and lower waters;
- pure waters and, by contrast, bitter or impure waters in the shells.

See:

- [*Pardes Rimmonim* 8:11](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.8.11), on elements and Sephirotic sides.
- [*Pardes Rimmonim* 23:13:36](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.23.13.36), on upper/lower and pure/impure waters.
- [*Pardes Rimmonim* 1:2](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.1.2), on scriptural and conceptual foundations.

### Cordovero’s path diagrams

**[TEXT/DISPUTED]** Cordovero describes channels verbally, but surviving descriptions and diagrams do not yield a single universally consistent twenty-two-line arrangement. *Pardes Rimmonim* and *Or Ne‘erav* have been read as giving different totals or configurations depending on what counts as a channel.

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** No securely identified passage in either work calls the Geburah–Hod connector “Mem” or “the twenty-third Stable Intelligence.”

### *Or Ne‘erav*

*Or Ne‘erav* is an accessible introduction to Cordoverian study and devotional preparation. It stresses disciplined Torah study and proper reception of Kabbalah. It is not a manual of letter-path vision work.

## 8. Luria and Vital

Isaac Luria taught in Safed in the 1570s. His disciples, especially Ḥayyim Vital, wrote and redacted the teachings. *Etz Ḥayyim* is therefore a Vitalian literary presentation of Lurianic instruction rather than a book written straight through by Luria.

### Principal Lurianic doctrines

**[TEXT/TRADITION]**

- *Tzimtzum*: the primordial contraction or concealment.
- the ray entering the vacated space;
- *Adam Qadmon*;
- the four worlds: Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah, Assiah;
- the primordial configurations of points, lines and integration;
- the breaking of the vessels, *shevirat ha-kelim*;
- sparks fallen among the shells;
- the *partzufim*, reconfigured divine countenances;
- *tikkun*, restoration through divine and human action.

See [*Sefer Etz Chaim* 25:1](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.25.1) and the [linear-configuration excerpt](https://fringe.hekhal.org/texts/etz-chaim-the-linear-configuration).

### Mem in Lurianic material

Mem appears in letter permutations, names and water symbolism. It is not assigned the Golden Dawn complex of Hanged Man, Stable Intelligence and Geburah–Hod pathworking.

### The “Ari Tree”

**[TRADITION/MODERN DIAGRAMMATIC RECONSTRUCTION]** Diagrams derived from Ari-oriented *Sefer Yetzirah* interpretation place the three mothers on horizontal balances:

- Shin between Chokhmah and Binah;
- Aleph between Chesed and Geburah;
- Mem between Netzach and Hod.

This expresses fire above, mediating air in the middle and water below. It is very different from Kircher’s sequential alphabet, where Mem lies between Geburah and Hod.

**[CAUTION]** Calling this “the tree drawn by Luria” is misleading. It is better described as a later diagrammatic reconstruction of Ari-associated rules and directions. *Etz Chaim* itself does not contain a Golden Dawn-style route map for twenty-two tarot pathworkings.

## 9. The Gra

Elijah ben Solomon Zalman, the Vilna Gaon (1720–1797), produced an influential commentary and recension of *Sefer Yetzirah*.

**[TRADITION]** Gra-associated diagrams likewise organize the mothers as balancing or horizontal relationships, the doubles as structural lines and the simples as diagonals. In commonly reproduced Gra diagrams, Mem occupies the lower horizontal relation, Netzach–Hod.

**[DISPUTED]** The printed Warsaw 1884 diagram reproduced by Aryeh Kaplan contains typographical or duplicative anomalies, so modern redrawn “Gra Trees” should not automatically be treated as autograph evidence. Kaplan reproduces and discusses Gra- and Ari-related directional systems in [*Sefer Yetzirah: The Book of Creation*](https://leonbahrmanministries.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Aryeh-Kaplan-Sefer-Yetzirah2.pdf).

| System | Mem connection |
|---|---|
| Kircher/Golden Dawn | Geburah–Hod |
| Common Ari reconstruction | Netzach–Hod |
| Common Gra reconstruction | Netzach–Hod |
| *Sefer Yetzirah* itself | No pair of named Sephiroth specified |

## 10. Hasidic readings

### *Tanya*

**[TEXT]** *Tanya*, Likkutei Amarim chapter 1, uses the four-element psychology found in earlier ethical and kabbalistic literature. Water is the source of appetitive pleasure because water causes pleasurable things to grow.

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** *Tanya* contains no dedicated “chapter on Mem” and no discussion of a twenty-third Geburah–Hod path. A source sheet for the relevant chapter is [Tanya chapter 1, four elements](https://www.sefaria.org/sheets/285871).

Later Chabad teaching develops Mem through:

- *mayim*, water;
- *mikveh*, immersion;
- Moses’ forty days;
- the open and closed forms;
- revealed and concealed Torah.

A representative modern Chabad treatment is [“Mem—The Thirteenth Letter”](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/137085/jewish/Mem.htm). It should not be projected backward as a quotation from *Tanya*.

### Naḥman of Breslov

Rabbi Naḥman frequently uses water, springs, rivers, immersion, tears and Torah-as-water. *Likkutei Moharan* 51, for example, contrasts true “clear water” with salty water as falsehood, drawing upon rabbinic warnings surrounding mystical ascent. See [*Likkutei Moharan* 51](https://breslov.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/Lm-lesson-51.pdf).

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** No primary Breslov source located in this research establishes the Golden Dawn Path of Mem. “The Water Castle” in the tale of the Seven Beggars belongs to Breslov narrative and later commentary, not a Geburah–Hod tarot path.

# The Hermetic and occult tradition

## 1. Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Giovanni Pico’s *Conclusiones nongentae* included forty-seven “Cabalistic conclusions according to my own opinion,” helping introduce Kabbalah into Christian Renaissance philosophy.

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]** Pico used the closed Mem christologically, arguing that a Cabalistic mystery of Mem could disclose why Christ sent the Paraclete and connecting messianic purification with water. The Latin appears in [Pico’s *Conclusiones*](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Conclusiones_philosophicae%2C_cabalisticae_et_theologicae); a searchable bilingual presentation is at [Spiritus Mundi](https://spiritusmundi.com/es/obras/conclusiones-nongentae).

**[HISTORICAL JUDGMENT]** Pico’s reading is Christian Cabala: it borrows Jewish letter symbolism but interprets it as encrypted Christian doctrine. It does not yet give Mem a tarot trump or Kircher’s position.

## 2. Reuchlin, 1517

Johannes Reuchlin’s *De arte cabalistica* presents Cabala through a fictional dialogue and treats Hebrew names and letters as a prisca-theologia confirming Christianity.

A scan is held by the [Badische Landesbibliothek](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/blbihd/content/titleinfo/7174105); a searchable Latin/Spanish transcription is available at [Spiritus Mundi](https://spiritusmundi.com/es/obras/de-arte-cabalistica).

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** Reuchlin does not yet give the later package:

> Mem = Path 23 = Geburah–Hod = Water = Hanged Man.

His Tree tradition and the 1516 *Portae Lucis* image are important antecedents to diagrammatic Christian Cabala, but not the Golden Dawn path system.

## 3. Kircher, 1652

Athanasius Kircher published his “Systema Sephiroticum” in *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, volume II, part I.

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]** Kircher’s plate:

- arranges ten Sephiroth in three pillars;
- connects them with twenty-two lines;
- places Hebrew letters on the lines in alphabetic sequence;
- supplies divine names, angels and astrological material;
- places Mem on the connector from Geburah to Hod.

The primary plate and adjacent Latin text can be examined in the [1652 *Oedipus Aegyptiacus* scan](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/13487220.pdf) and in the [Esoteric Archives transcription and plate](https://www2.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm).

### Why Kircher matters

Kircher provides the direct diagrammatic ancestor of the Tree later used by the Golden Dawn. The Golden Dawn changed and expanded its correspondences but retained its essential line layout and alphabetical sequencing.

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** Kircher probably adapted earlier Christian-Cabalist diagrams, particularly material associated with the Jewish convert Philippe d’Aquin’s *Interprétation de l’arbre de la Cabale* (1625). Moshe Idel has reportedly described the sequential assignment as compatible with a known but minor Jewish tradition. That is different from claiming it was the normative Ari or Gra arrangement.

### Kircher versus Ari/Gra

| Question | Kircher | Ari/Gra reconstructions |
|---|---|---|
| Ordering principle | Letters proceed broadly in alphabetic path order | Letter classes govern geometry |
| Mothers | Distributed in the sequential network | Horizontal balancing links |
| Mem | Geburah–Hod | Commonly Netzach–Hod |
| Malkhut | Three approach paths in the Kircher-derived form | Often only the central approach in Jewish reconstructions |
| Function | Christian universal synthesis | Commentary-driven Jewish cosmology |
| Tarot | None in 1652 | None |

## 4. Knorr von Rosenroth, 1677–1684

Christian Knorr von Rosenroth’s *Kabbala Denudata* translated and rearranged Jewish materials, including Zoharic, Cordoverian and Lurianic texts. It transmitted Kabbalah to Latin-reading Christian intellectuals.

Primary scans are available through [e-rara](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/10759091) and a digital index at [Bill Heidrick’s *Kabbala Denudata* archive](https://www.billheidrick.com/Orpd/KRKD/index.htm).

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]**

- Latin terminology includes forms such as *Mem* and *aqua*.
- The work reproduces several *ilanot*, some derived from Jewish Lurianic diagrams and some rearranged by Knorr.
- It helped transmit the Sephirotic and qliphothic materials later used by Mathers.

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** Rosenroth is not the origin of the Hanged Man attribution. Nor should all diagrams in *Kabbala Denudata* be collapsed into Kircher’s single Tree.

## 5. Éliphas Lévi, 1854–1856

Lévi’s *Dogme et rituel de la haute magie* argued that the twenty-two tarot trumps correspond to the twenty-two Hebrew letters. The French primary text is available in this [scan of *Dogme et rituel*](https://www.cercle-papus.com/ouvrages/eliphas-levi-dogme-et-rituel-tome-1.pdf).

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]** Lévi began with the Magician as Aleph and placed the Fool late in the sequence. Consequently, in Lévi’s ordering, the Hanged Man was not Mem. It corresponded to Lamed, while Mem corresponded to Death.

This is a major correction to claims that “Lévi first assigned the Hanged Man to Mem.” He initiated the alphabet–trump synthesis; the Golden Dawn altered the order by putting the Fool/Aleph first.

## 6. The Golden Dawn, approximately 1887–1900

### Construction of the correspondence

**[HERMETIC]** The Golden Dawn combined:

- Kircher’s lettered Tree;
- *Sefer Yetzirah*’s letter classes and elemental/planetary/zodiacal attributes;
- the Rittangelius/Westcott *Thirty-Two Paths*;
- a reordered tarot sequence;
- four-world colour scales;
- Egyptian, Greek, Christian and magical-symbolic material.

This produced:

> Path 23 = Mem = Water = Geburah–Hod = XII, the Hanged Man = Stable Intelligence.

### Why the Hanged Man became Mem

The Golden Dawn made the Fool the unnumbered/zero trump and placed it before the Magician:

| Hebrew letter | Trump |
|---|---|
| Aleph | Fool |
| Beth | Magician |
| … | … |
| Lamed | Justice/Adjustment |
| Mem | Hanged Man |
| Nun | Death |

Thus Mem, thirteenth letter, aligns with the thirteenth position in a Fool-first sequence even though the printed card bears XII.

This was not deduced from ancient Jewish sources; it was an occult reordering of tarot.

### Book T

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]** Golden Dawn *Book T* calls the Hanged Man:

> “The Spirit of the Mighty Waters.”

The card and path represent sacrifice, reversal, suspension, baptism, death and rebirth. Public access to the order’s tarot teachings came through Crowley’s *Equinox* publication and Israel Regardie’s later compilations. Relevant material is preserved in [Regardie’s *Complete Golden Dawn System of Magic*](https://www.invisiblehouse.org/ref/Complete_Golden_Dawn_System_of_Magic.pdf).

### Knowledge Lectures and ritual use

Golden Dawn instruction describes:

- Mem as a mother and Water;
- the Geburah–Hod path;
- the drowned or suspended adept;
- the rainbow above the ocean;
- the “cup and cross of suffering”;
- the reconciliation of Geburah’s force with Hod’s formal intellect.

The Adeptus Major material uses the Hanged Man as a symbol of sacrifice and passage into the martial sphere: [Golden Dawn Adeptus Major ritual](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Ritual_Adeptus_Major.html).

### Four colour scales

| World/scale | Mem colour |
|---|---|
| Atziluth / King | Deep blue |
| Beriah / Queen | Sea-green |
| Yetzirah / Prince or Emperor | Deep olive-green |
| Assiah / Princess or Empress | White flecked purple |

The equation “King = Atziluth, Queen = Beriah, Prince = Yetzirah, Princess = Assiah” is Golden Dawn colour theory, not a colour list in *Sefer Yetzirah*.

### *The Book of Thoth*

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]** Crowley retained Mem and Water but reinterpreted sacrifice for the New Aeon. He calls the card’s symbolism the spiritual function of water in initiation—a baptism that is also death. He treats the traditional Hanged Man as an Osirian or dying-god formula whose compulsory self-sacrifice has become problematic in the Aeon of Horus.

The primary chapter is [“The Hanged Man,” *The Book of Thoth*](https://hermetic.com/crowley/book-of-thoth/atu).

Crowley also observes that:

- ordinary מ resembles a wave;
- final ם resembles water enclosed or at rest;
- Mem’s 40 develops the elemental formula;
- the figure hangs from an ankh-like or Tau structure over watery forms.

### Tzaddi is not the Star

**[TEXT/HERMETIC]** *Liber AL* I:57 declares, “All these old letters of my Book are aright; but צ is not the Star.”

Crowley eventually exchanged the astrological positions of the Emperor and Star, interpreting Tzaddi as the Emperor and Heh as the Star within his revised scheme.

**Finding:** This controversy does **not** alter Mem or the Hanged Man. Claims that Crowley moved the Hanged Man because of the Tzaddi/Heh revelation are false. See [*Liber AL vel Legis*](https://hermetic.com/legis/index).

### *Liber 231*

*Liber Arcanorum* presents twenty-two “houses” and twenty-two qliphothic “prisons” through sigils and cryptic verses. It was published in *The Equinox* I:7 in 1912: [*Liber 231*](https://hermetic.com/crowley/libers/lib231).

The Mem formula is associated with the slain or drowned son and watery death. The qliphothic sigil later called Malkunofat supplied Kenneth Grant with one basis for the “tunnel” concept.

**[CAUTION]** Crowley did not publish Kenneth Grant’s complete Tunnels-of-Set doctrine. Treating *Liber 231* as though it already contained *Nightside of Eden*’s detailed tunnel initiations is retrospective.

## 8. Dion Fortune

*The Mystical Qabalah* was published in 1935.

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** It has no chapter devoted exclusively to Mem. Fortune’s main chapters treat the ten Sephiroth. Her chapter on the paths explains that the twenty-two connectors represent equilibrated relations between Sephirotic powers.

The complete text is accessible at [Global Grey’s *Mystical Qabalah*](https://www.globalgreyebooks.com/online-ebooks/dion-fortune_mystical-qabalah_complete-text.html).

The request to reproduce “the chapter on this Sephira in full” is therefore inapplicable twice over:

1. Mem is a letter/path, not a Sephirah.
2. Fortune did not write a discrete Mem chapter.

Fortune’s contribution was to popularize the Tree as a psychological and initiatory map, making later pathworking accounts possible.

## 9. Israel Regardie

### *A Garden of Pomegranates*

Regardie’s early exposition gives one of the clearest twentieth-century summaries:

- path 23 joins Geburah and Hod;
- Mem means water;
- the intelligence is Stable;
- Poseidon and Neptune correspond;
- sea-green is central to the path;
- the cup, wine and water plants are its symbols;
- aquamarine and beryl are stones;
- onycha and myrrh are perfumes.

See [Regardie, *A Garden of Pomegranates*](https://www.labirintoermetico.com/06Numerologia_Cabala/Isreael_Regardie_A_Garden_Of_Pomegranates_2Nd%20Ed.pdf), pp. 82–83 in the scanned edition.

### Golden Dawn publication

Regardie’s publication of Golden Dawn papers in the 1930s and later expanded editions moved formerly initiatory material into public occultism. It is through Regardie, more than direct study of Kircher, that many modern practitioners learned the Path of Mem.

## 10. Paul Foster Case

Case retained:

- Mem as water;
- Hanged Man as Key 12;
- reversal and suspension;
- stillness of mind;
- the watery “root” or mother-substance;
- surrender of personal will to universal life.

His *Book of Tokens* gives a poetic meditation for each Hebrew letter. The Mem meditation is a twentieth-century esoteric composition spoken in the voice of cosmic consciousness, not an ancient Hebrew text. Bibliographical details are available for [Case, *The Book of Tokens*](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Book_of_Tokens.html?hl=en&id=bFHPtHtPAlYC&output=html_text).

B.O.T.A. additionally relates the Hanged Man to Neptune. This is a modern astrological gloss; the Golden Dawn’s fundamental attribution is elemental Water.

## 11. Gareth Knight

Gareth Knight’s *Practical Guide to Qabalistic Symbolism*, first published in the 1960s, consolidates the twentieth-century British pathworking tradition.

For path 23 he gives:

- Hod–Geburah;

- Hanged Man;
- Stable Intelligence;

## 12. Pathworking traditions

### Golden Dawn mode

A typical pathworking proceeds by:

1. ritual purification and banishing;
2. establishing the Tree and the two endpoint spheres;
3. visualizing the Mem letter in the correct colour;
4. vibrating elemental or divine names;
5. passing through a tarot- or letter-marked portal;
6. encountering watery, sacrificial or inverted imagery;
7. testing figures by names and symbols;
8. returning, closing and recording the vision.

### Common imagery

- deep or moonless ocean;
- drowned giant or suspended adept;
- rainbow over water;
- cup, cross and wine;
- baptismal death;
- maternal womb;
- reflected or inverted world;
- surrender of rigid intellect;
- reconciliation of Geburah’s fire with Hod’s formal mind.

### Modern published examples

Chic and Tabatha Cicero’s *Skrying on the Tree of Life* describes the path as a purifying return to “the eternal womb” and stresses stillness and fixed purpose. See [Cicero, *Skrying on the Tree of Life*](https://logoilibrary.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/skrying_on_the_tree_of_life-tabatha_cicero.pdf), p. 212 onward.

**[MODERN]** Such guided visions are creative ritual literature. Similar images across reports may result from shared scripts, tarot imagery and expectations rather than independent confirmation of an objective astral geography.

## 13. Thelemic grades

In the A∴A∴ grade structure:

- Hod corresponds to Practicus, 3°=8□.
- Geburah corresponds to Adeptus Major, 6°=5□.

Thus the Mem line can be interpreted as spanning the intellectual-formative grade and the martial adept grade.

**[CAUTION]** A∴A∴ grades attach formally to Sephiroth. Saying that a candidate “holds the grade of Path 23” is not standard. The path is a transition or formula between grade-stations.

# The Qliphah or tunnel

## Jewish qelippot versus the occult reverse Tree

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** In Jewish Kabbalah, *qelippah* means shell, husk or rind. Zoharic and Lurianic writings describe impurity, the *sitra aḥra*, demonic powers and sparks trapped among shells.

**[HISTORICAL DISTINCTION]** Jewish sources do not ordinarily present a standardized inverse Tree with ten English occult titles, one demon prince per node and twenty-two named “tunnels” used for initiatory pathworking. That form grew from Christian-Cabalist, Golden Dawn, Crowleyan and post-Crowleyan tabulation.

## The ten orders

Crowley’s *777 Revised* gives the following sequence:

| Sephirah | Occult qliphothic order | Approximate gloss |
|---|---|---|
| Keter | Thaumiel | Twins/contending forces of God |
| Chokhmah | Ghagiel/Ghogiel | Hinderers |
| Binah | Satariel | Concealers |
| Chesed | Gha‘agsheklah | Smiters/breakers in pieces |
| Geburah | Golachab | Burners/flaming ones |
| Tiferet | Thagirion | Disputers/litigation |
| Netzach | A‘arab Zaraq | Ravens of dispersion |
| Hod | Samael | False accusers/deceivers |
| Yesod | Gamaliel | Obscene ones |
| Malkhut | Lilith/Nahemoth variants | Woman of night; whisperers |

Spellings differ between Hebrew manuscripts, Latin transliterations, Mathers, Crowley, Grant and modern occult authors. The table is an occult reception-history object, not a critical edition of one ancient Jewish list.

## Endpoint shells

Path 23 connects the Sephiroth Geburah and Hod, so its reverse-side endpoints are conventionally:

- **Golachab**, the shell of Geburah.
- **Samael**, the shell of Hod.

Golden Dawn imagery describes Golachab as volcanic or burning heads and Samael as deceiving, dog-headed or juggler-like forms. See the [Golden Dawn paper “The Qliphoth”](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Qliphoth.html).

Later ruler tables often assign:

- Asmodeus to Golachab.
- Adramelech to Samael.

**[DISPUTED/MODERN]** The demon-prince tables are unstable. Crowley’s columns, Mathers-derived papers and modern Left-Hand-Path books do not always agree; Hebrew spellings are frequently corrupt. The assignment should not be presented as a settled Zoharic doctrine.

## Malkunofat

Kenneth Grant’s *Nightside of Eden* appeared in 1977. Grant read the qliphothic sigils of *Liber 231* as tunnels running behind the Tree.

**[TEXT/MODERN TYPHONIAN]**

- Tunnel: Malkunofat.
- Path reflected: Mem/23.
- Element: abyssal water.
- Generic arch-devil: Leviathan.
- Inhabitants: the “Deep Ones.”
- Magical themes: drowning, dream-depth, atavistic consciousness, sexual/womb symbolism, dissolution of form and dangerous astral skrying.

Grant states that Malkunofat is the tunnel of the Deep Ones and uses Leviathan as their generic emblem. See [Grant, *Nightside of Eden*](https://s3.us-west-1.wasabisys.com/luminist/EB/G/Grant%20-%20Nightside%20of%20Eden.pdf).

### Status of the attribution

- **Not Zoharic:** No Zoharic text names a tunnel Malkunofat.
- **Not Lurianic:** Vital’s *Etz Chaim* contains no Tunnels-of-Set practice.
- **Partly Crowley-derived:** The name/sigil has roots in the qliphothic half of *Liber 231*.
- **Grant’s invention:** The tunnel geography, Deep Ones, sonic keys, Lovecraftian resonances and much of the sexual-Typhonian interpretation belong to Grant.
- **Literary borrowing:** “Deep Ones” unmistakably invokes H. P. Lovecraft’s fiction. Grant treats literary imagery as occult revelation; historians cannot accept that as evidence of antiquity.

# The letter’s form and symbolic anatomy

## Open and closed

| Form | Rabbinic symbolism | Later kabbalistic/Hasidic symbolism | Occult symbolism |
|---|---|---|---|
| מ | Open utterance | Revealed Torah, flowing water | Wave, active water |
| ם | Sealed utterance | Concealed Torah, womb, messianic enclosure | Still sea, sealed abyss, latent consciousness |

Crowley’s contrast between the waved medial letter and quiet final Mem is a plausible visual meditation but not historical palaeography.

## Numerical symbolism of forty

Common traditional clusters include:

- forty days of the flood;
- forty days of Moses on Sinai;
- forty days of the spies;
- forty years in the wilderness;
- forty measures connected with ritual immersion in later homily;
- forty days traditionally associated with embryonic formation;
- forty lashes minus one in rabbinic penal practice.

The shared number supports a family of transition symbols—purification, gestation, judgment and revelation—but does not demonstrate that every biblical “forty” was deliberately written as a Mem cipher.

## Artists and literature

### Pamela Colman Smith

Her Rider–Waite Hanged Man shows a serene suspended figure with a halo and crossed leg. Waite’s published imagery is indebted to Golden Dawn training, although his public text minimizes explicit order secrets.

### Frieda Harris

Crowley’s Thoth Hanged Man is more explicitly aquatic and initiatory. The figure hangs from the ankh, descends into elemental water and is entangled with serpentine life/death symbolism.

### Occult fiction

Dion Fortune’s novels employ initiatory, elemental and Tree-of-Life symbolism, but no single novel is securely documented as a sustained literary treatment of Path 23 alone.

**[NEGATIVE FINDING]** No adequately sourced evidence was found for a major premodern Jewish poet or novelist explicitly working with “the Geburah–Hod Path of Mem.” Modern lists often infer such connections from any use of water, hanging or the number forty; that is insufficient evidence.

# Controversies and disputes

## 1. Dating *Sefer Yetzirah*

| Position | Evidence | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Patriarch Abraham | Traditional attribution | Theological/pseudepigraphic, not historical evidence |
| Tannaitic, c. second century | Parallels with early rabbinic and cosmological language | Possible early materials, but no manuscript evidence that early |
| Third–sixth centuries | Linguistic and conceptual reconstruction | Common broad scholarly proposal |
| Seventh–ninth centuries | Islamic-period philosophical context and textual emergence | Strong candidate for compilation/redaction |
| Before 931 | Saadia’s commentary | Secure terminus ante quem |

Hayman’s conservative conclusion—that the recoverable book becomes visible in the tenth century while possibly containing older layers—is safer than assigning one precise date.

## 2. Zoharic authorship

- **Kabbalistic tradition:** revelations of Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and his circle.
- **Scholem:** main body composed by Moses de León in late-thirteenth-century Castile.
- **Later scholarship:** multiple strata, compositional circles and later *Tikkunim*; de León remains central but “single author of every line” may be too simple.
- **Evidence:** medieval Aramaic, contemporary sources, circulation history, de León’s Hebrew works and Isaac of Acre’s report concerning de León’s widow.

The religious authority of the Zohar and the historical dating of its literary form are separate questions.

## 3. Is the Stable Intelligence part of *Sefer Yetzirah*?

**No, not in the straightforward textual sense.** It is part of an appended list transmitted with some editions. Westcott’s typography encouraged readers to treat the texts as one document.

Therefore:

- “Mem is Water” is directly Yetziratic.
- “Path 23 is Stable Intelligence” belongs to the separate path list.
- “Mem is Path 23” is a numbering synthesis.
- “Path 23 connects Geburah and Hod” belongs to the Kircher diagram.
- “Its trump is the Hanged Man” belongs to the Golden Dawn.

## 4. Kircher versus Jewish Trees

Kircher’s diagram should not be dismissed merely as random fabrication: it drew upon earlier Cabalist diagrams and perhaps a minority Jewish arrangement. But neither should it be presented as “the” ancient Jewish Tree.

Jewish *ilanot* vary in:

- the number and geometry of channels;
- whether Malkhut has one or several direct connections;
- whether Da‘at is drawn;
- how partzufim and worlds are nested;
- whether letters are placed on connectors at all.

The Kircher/GD Tree became standard in Western occultism because it was printable, regular and exceptionally good at holding large correspondence tables.

## 5. Lévi versus Golden Dawn tarot

| System | Mem |
|---|---|
| Lévi’s sequential tarot | Death |
| Golden Dawn | Hanged Man |
| Crowley | Hanged Man |
| Rider–Waite visual deck | Hanged Man, with esoteric letter unpublished on the card |
| Continental systems following Lévi/Papus | Often Death or another shifted allocation |

There was no single ancient Hebrew–tarot correspondence waiting to be rediscovered. Tarot trumps emerged in fifteenth-century Italy; the Hebrew-letter equation is nineteenth-century occultism.

## 6. The Tzaddi/Heh problem

It concerns Emperor and Star, not Mem. Golden Dawn and Crowley both keep Hanged Man–Mem. Crowley’s later exchange of Heh and Tzaddi did not produce a rival Mem attribution.

## 7. Da‘at’s status

**[JEWISH TRADITION]** Da‘at, knowledge, can represent:

- the integration of Chokhmah and Binah;
- a concealed or emergent Sephirotic function;
- a state appearing when Keter is not counted;
- the locus of consciousness or union.

The governing rule remains “ten and not eleven” in *Sefer Yetzirah*. Thus Da‘at is normally not an eleventh independent Sephirah alongside all ten.

**[HERMETIC]** Occult Trees often draw Daath as an unnumbered sphere in the Abyss. This affects paths crossing the upper Tree but does not change the physical location of Mem on the Geburah–Hod line.

## 8. Christian appropriation

Christian Cabalists preserved, translated and circulated Jewish texts, but regularly reframed them as proof of:

- Trinity;
- Incarnation;
- Jesus as Messiah;
- Christian sacraments;
- universal ancient theology.

The problem is not simply “influence.” It is that Jewish texts were often detached from halakhic, linguistic and communal settings and made to confess doctrines their Jewish authors rejected. The Hermetic Path of Mem should accordingly be described as a creative Christian/occult reception, not as the secret unanimous teaching of Judaism.

## 9. Qliphothic historicity

Jewish Kabbalah has ancient and medieval traditions concerning demons, the *sitra aḥra* and shells. But the clean ten-order table and especially Grant’s twenty-two tunnels are later occult constructions.

Malkunofat’s strongest documentary chain is:

> *Liber 231* sigil/name → Crowleyan occultism → Kenneth Grant’s 1977 tunnel interpretation.

It cannot be traced as a named path to the Zohar, Cordovero or Vital.

## 10. Neptune

Neptune was discovered in 1846. Therefore any Mem–Neptune correspondence is necessarily modern. It may serve as an occult analogy for oceanic consciousness, suspension and dissolution, but it cannot be an original Golden Dawn reading of *Sefer Yetzirah*’s ancient cosmology in the strict historical sense.

# Dedicated contested-material pass

Searches were conducted around “Path of Mem,” attribution, dispute, dating, Kircher, Golden Dawn, Ari, Gra, Hanged Man and Malkunofat. The recurring contested claims resolve as follows:

| Claim encountered | Finding |
|---|---|
| “*Sefer Yetzirah* says Mem is the path from Geburah to Hod.” | False. It says Mem is a mother and Water; it names no such connector. |
| “Path 23 and Mem were always identical.” | Unsupported. The identity depends on numbering the ten Sephiroth before the letters. |
| “The Stable Intelligence is a verse of *Sefer Yetzirah*.” | Misleading. It belongs to the separately transmitted *Thirty-Two Paths* list. |
| “Kircher invented the entire Tree.” | Too strong. He synthesized earlier Jewish and Christian-Cabalist diagram traditions, but his lettered arrangement is not normative Jewish Kabbalah. |
| “The Golden Dawn simply copied Lévi.” | False in detail. It retained the letter–tarot idea but changed the Fool’s position, moving Hanged Man to Mem. |
| “Lévi assigned Hanged Man to Mem.” | False in Lévi’s original sequence; his Mem corresponds to Death. |
| “Crowley changed Mem because Tzaddi is not the Star.” | False. His Tzaddi/Heh revision does not touch Mem. |
| “Mem has Pisces or Scorpio as its original sign.” | False for *Sefer Yetzirah*. Mem is elemental Water; Pisces/Scorpio are later analogies or confusion with zodiacal letters. |
| “Mem has a month and tribe.” | Category error. Those belong to simple letters. |
| “Mem has a classical planet.” | Category error. Planets belong to doubles. Neptune is modern. |
| “Grant’s Malkunofat is Zoharic.” | False. It is a twentieth-century Typhonian elaboration of Crowleyan material. |
| “The Qliphah of Mem is Samael.” | Imprecise. Samael is the shell of Hod; the path’s reverse tunnel is Malkunofat in Grant. |
| “The Qliphah of Mem is Golachab.” | Likewise imprecise. Golachab is the Geburah endpoint shell. |
| “Dion Fortune wrote a full chapter on Mem.” | False; she wrote path theory and Sephiroth chapters, not a Mem chapter. |
| “*Tanya* has a Path-of-Mem chapter.” | False; it discusses water within four-element psychology. |
| “Ari and Gra use Mem on Geburah–Hod.” | Generally false in their common reconstructed Trees; Mem is the lower horizontal Netzach–Hod balance. |
| “There is one canonical Jewish Tree.” | False historically. Manuscript *ilanot* show extensive variety. |

# Open questions

1. **Date and provenance of the *Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom*.** Its medieval Hebrew transmission needs a full stemmatic study independent of occult editions.

2. **Exact source of Kircher’s alphabetical sequencing.** Philippe d’Aquin is a strong proximate source, but the precise Jewish manuscript or oral precedent remains incompletely reconstructed.

3. **Ari and Gra diagram transmission.** Many popular diagrams depend on nineteenth- and twentieth-century printed editions. Earlier manuscripts should be compared before treating every modern layout as authoritative.

4. **Recensional anatomy in *Sefer Yetzirah*.** The exact placement of head, belly, chest and sexed permutations varies and deserves manuscript-by-manuscript presentation.

5. **Mem’s petition traditions.** *Otiyot de-Rabbi Akiva*, Zoharic prologues and later alphabet books share motifs, but their precise literary dependence remains complex.

6. **The demon-prince tables.** Asmodeus–Golachab and Adramelech–Samael are common modern assignments, yet manuscript spellings and Golden Dawn columns do not form a perfectly stable tradition.

7. **Authorship within the Golden Dawn.** Westcott, Mathers and the cipher manuscripts each contributed to the system; exact responsibility for individual tarot and colour decisions is not always demonstrable.

8. **Liber 231 and Grant.** A critical edition should distinguish Crowley’s original sigils, captions and verses from Grant’s names, tunnel geography and later Typhonian interpretations.

9. **Experiential pathworking reports.** Published accounts are historically useful evidence for modern practice but cannot adjudicate metaphysical claims about objective astral beings.

10. **Proto-Sinaitic ancestry.** The water-sign derivation remains likely, but newly discovered inscriptions or revised decipherments could alter the detailed genealogy.

---

# Conclusion

The oldest recoverable nucleus is simple and powerful:

> Mem is a mother letter; Mem rules water.

The familiar “Path of Mem” is a historical composite assembled much later:

1. *Sefer Yetzirah* supplied Mem and Water.
2. A medieval appendix supplied the twenty-third “Stable Intelligence.”
3. Early Jewish and Christian diagrams supplied increasingly elaborate Trees.
4. Kircher fixed Mem on the Geburah–Hod connector.
5. Lévi joined tarot to the alphabet but initially gave Mem to Death.
6. The Golden Dawn reordered the trumps and assigned Mem the Hanged Man.
7. Crowley, Regardie, Case, Fortune’s successors and Knight interpreted the path psychologically and initiatorily.
8. Kenneth Grant constructed its Nightside counterpart, Malkunofat, under Leviathan.

That composite is historically real and enormously influential within Hermetic Qabalah. It is not, however, a single doctrine found whole in *Sefer Yetzirah*, the *Bahir*, the Zohar, Cordovero or Vital. Keeping those layers distinct preserves both Jewish Kabbalah’s own textual history and the originality—along with the appropriations—of Western occultism.
