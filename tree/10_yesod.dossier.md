# Yesod: Research Dossier

## Scope, method, and evidence labels

This dossier treats Jewish Kabbalah, Christian Cabala, Hermetic Qabalah, and later occultism as historically related but non-identical traditions. Similar vocabulary does not imply identical doctrine.

Evidence labels used throughout:

- **[TEXT]** Directly attested in a cited primary text.
- **[TRADITION]** A claim made within Jewish kabbalistic tradition, without asserting historical authorship or supernatural truth.
- **[SCHOLARSHIP]** A modern historical or philological reconstruction.
- **[CHRISTIAN CABALA]** A Renaissance or early-modern Christian adaptation.
- **[HERMETIC]** A Golden Dawn, Thelemic, or related Western-occult attribution.
- **[DISPUTED]** A claim for which sources, recensions, or scholarly interpretations conflict.
- **[MODERN]** A development not demonstrated in the premodern sources examined.
- **[ABSENCE]** A searched-for attribution that is not present in the relevant source.

Quotations are short because modern translations and twentieth-century books remain copyrighted. In particular, Dion Fortune’s entire Yesod chapter cannot be reproduced verbatim; a detailed analytical précis is supplied instead.

# The name

## Hebrew form and etymology

**[TEXT]** The word is spelled **יְסוֹד**, consonantally **יסוד**: yod–samekh–vav–dalet. It is a Hebrew noun meaning a foundation, base, supporting structure, or act/result of founding. Its verbal root is **יסד**, “to found,” “establish,” or “lay a foundation.”

Representative pronunciations and transliterations include:

- Modern Israeli: approximately *yesód*.
- Academic: *yĕsôd*, *yəsōd*, or *Yesod*.
- Older Christian Latin: *Iesod*, later *Jesod*.
- Golden Dawn/Crowley transliteration: *Yesod*, sometimes consonantally **ISVD**.

The gematria is **80**. This becomes relevant to the interpretation of **כִּי כֹל**, *ki khol*, “for all,” in 1 Chronicles 29:11: כי = 30 and כל = 50, also totaling 80.

## Biblical strata

### The ordinary biblical word

**[TEXT]** In the Hebrew Bible, *yesod* and forms of the root יסד are architectural and metaphorical words before they are technical mystical terms. Examples include foundations of buildings or altars, the founding of the earth, and a secure foundation established by God.

The principal mystical proof-text is [Proverbs 10:25](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.10.25):

> וְצַדִּיק יְסוֹד עוֹלָם  
> “The righteous is an everlasting foundation.”

The Hebrew also permits the traditional rendering “the righteous is the foundation of the world.” The grammatical ambiguity matters: the later kabbalistic doctrine is an exegetical development, not the transparent lexical meaning of the verse.

### 1 Chronicles 29:11 and the seven lower sefirot

**[TEXT]** [1 Chronicles 29:11](https://www.sefaria.org/I_Chronicles.29.11?with=Kabbalah) contains the sequence:

> הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת וְהַנֵּצַח וְהַהוֹד

“Greatness, power, beauty, victory, and majesty.”

**[TRADITION]** Kabbalists read these as Gedulah/Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, Netzach, and Hod. The verse then says **כִּי כֹל**, “for all,” which is interpreted as Yesod, followed by **הַמַּמְלָכָה**, “the kingdom,” corresponding to Malkhut. Thus the seven lower sefirot are obtained exegetically:

1. Gedulah/Chesed
2. Gevurah
3. Tiferet
4. Netzach
5. Hod
6. *Ki khol* → Yesod
7. Mamlakhah → Malkhut

**[TRADITION]** The identification of *ki khol* with Yesod is supported by gematria: both equal 80. It is also supported by the Zoharic epithet **Kol**, “All,” for the channel that gathers the preceding powers.

**[QUALIFICATION]** Yesod is not literally written as a sefirah-name in the verse. The connection is kabbalistic exegesis.

### Proverbs 3:19

[Proverbs 3:19](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.3.19) says:

> יְהוָה בְּחָכְמָה יָסַד־אָרֶץ  
> “YHWH founded the earth by wisdom.”

**[TEXT]** The verb *yasad*, “founded,” belongs to the Yesod root.

**[TRADITION]** Kabbalists could read the verse as an account of emanational construction: divine Wisdom founds or establishes the lower world.

**[ABSENCE]** The verse itself does not name the ninth sefirah.

### Job 28 and *belimah*

**[TEXT]** [Job 28](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.28) is a wisdom poem asking where Wisdom can be found. It does not mention Yesod as a sefirah. It contributes instead to the broader biblical vocabulary from which later theories of hidden Wisdom, depth, mining, measure, and cosmic construction were developed.

More directly relevant to *Sefer Yetzirah* is [Job 26:7](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.26.7), where God “hangs the earth upon **belimah**.” *Sefer Yetzirah* calls its ten numerations **sefirot belimah**. Interpretations of *belimah* include “nothing,” “without-what,” “restraint,” or the restraint of the mouth.

### Isaiah

Three Isaianic passages are especially relevant:

- [Isaiah 28:16](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.28.16): God lays in Zion a tested stone and “sure foundation.”
- [Isaiah 48:13](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.48.13): “My hand founded the earth.”
- [Isaiah 33:6](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.33.6): wisdom and knowledge are the stability of the times.

**[TRADITION]** Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah* connects Zion with Yesod: Zion is the concentrated point through which divine influx reaches the lower realm.

**[ABSENCE]** These verses provide vocabulary and imagery, not a self-evident ten-sefirot table.

# The Jewish tradition

## 1. Late-antique background: *Sefer Yetzirah*

### Date and recensions

**[SCHOLARSHIP/DISPUTED]** *Sefer Yetzirah* cannot be dated with precision. Proposed dates range from the early centuries CE to the early Islamic period. Older scholarship often placed its core between the third and sixth centuries. Tzahi Weiss and other recent scholars have argued for later or more layered contexts. The first firmly datable extensive witnesses are medieval.

Peter Hayman’s critical edition identifies three principal early textual recensions:

1. Short recension
2. Long recension
3. Saadyan recension

Hayman prints nineteen manuscripts synoptically. The common popular category “Gra–Ari recension” is a much later edited/commentarial form, not a fourth ancient manuscript family of equal standing. [Hayman’s edition description](https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/sefer-yesira-9783161587955/).

**[DISPUTED]** Earlier scholarship sometimes treated the Saadyan text as secondary. A recent reconstruction argues that parts of it may preserve the earliest recoverable form. This reverses a common assumption and remains under discussion: [Hebrew University research record](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/the-earliest-version-of-sefer-yeirah-text-format-structure-and-ge/).

### The thirty-two paths

The opening of [*Sefer Yetzirah*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah.1) presents:

- Ten **sefirot belimah**
- Twenty-two “letters of foundation”
- Together, thirty-two “wondrous paths of Wisdom”

The twenty-two letters divide into:

- Three Mothers: א מ ש
- Seven Doubles: ב ג ד כ פ ר ת
- Twelve Simples: ה ו ז ח ט י ל נ ס ע צ ק

The ten sefirot are described through number, measure, spirit, water, fire, and six spatial extremities. They are not the later sequence Keter–Chokhmah–Binah–…–Yesod–Malkhut.

**[ABSENCE]** There is no named ninth sefirah Yesod in the early text. Consequently:

- It gives Yesod no divine name.
- It gives Yesod no archangel.
- It gives Yesod no angelic order.
- It gives Yesod no planet.
- It gives Yesod no virtue, color, tarot card, or magical image.

The word “foundation” belongs instead to the phrase **עשרים ושתים אותיות יסוד**, “twenty-two letters of foundation.”

### The covenant in *Sefer Yetzirah*

**[TEXT]** The ten are compared with ten fingers, “five opposite five,” with the covenant of unity in the middle:

- Covenant of the tongue
- Covenant of circumcision

This becomes retrospectively important to Yesod. In the ancient text, however, it does not explicitly say that the circumcised organ is the ninth sefirah.

**[TRADITION]** Cordovero and later commentators interpret the covenant of circumcision through Yesod, while the covenant of the tongue relates to the central harmonizing principle.

### Saadia, Gra, and Ari forms

**[TEXT/SCHOLARSHIP]** Saadia Gaon’s tenth-century Judeo-Arabic commentary treats *Sefer Yetzirah* philosophically and cosmologically. It does not simply reproduce the later Zoharic Tree.

**[TRADITION]** The “Ari version” refers to a Lurianic textual and diagrammatic reception. The Vilna Gaon’s eighteenth-century recension and commentary further regularized the text according to his understanding.

**[DISPUTED]** Popular books frequently speak of “Saadia, Short, Long, and Gra/Ari” as four coeval editions. That is convenient for practice, but misleading as textual history. Hayman’s manuscript work recognizes Short, Long, and Saadyan as the principal early recension families.

## 2. The *Bahir*: the righteous pillar

The *Bahir* emerged publicly in Provence in the later twelfth century, probably around 1150–1200. Traditional attribution associates it with the first-century sage Neḥunya ben HaKanah; historical scholarship treats it as a medieval compilation containing older motifs.

[*Bahir* §102](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir.102) describes a single pillar extending from heaven to earth. Its name is “Righteous,” and it sustains the world, invoking Proverbs 10:25.

**[TEXT]** The passage gives the foundational cluster:

- A single cosmic pillar
- Heaven-to-earth transmission
- The name Tzaddik, “Righteous”
- The foundation of the world

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** In the *Bahir* and some early thirteenth-century sources, the power later called Yesod is not always counted ninth. It can appear as the seventh lower potency. The fully standardized position as ninth emerged during the thirteenth-century development of the Gerona school and related systems. The historical overview is preserved in the *Encyclopaedia Judaica* article on Kabbalah: [Jewish Virtual Library transcription](https://www.jewishvirtuallibrary.org/jsource/judaica/ejud_0002_0011_0_10514.html).

This is an important correction to the popular claim that a fixed modern Tree has existed unchanged since antiquity.

## 3. Thirteenth-century consolidation

### Gerona and the ninth position

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** Azriel of Gerona, Ezra of Gerona, and related thirteenth-century kabbalists developed systematic sequences of ten divine powers. In this environment Yesod became securely placed between Netzach/Hod and Malkhut.

Its functions crystallized as:

- Collector of the upper influx
- Mediator to the last sefirah
- Male generative power
- Covenant
- Righteous pillar
- “All,” because it gathers the preceding powers
- Peace, because it unites giver and receiver

### Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah*

Joseph Gikatilla composed *Shaʿarei Orah* toward the end of the thirteenth century. Gate Two begins with Adonai/Malkhut and proceeds into the divine names associated with Yesod.

In [*Shaʿarei Orah*, Gate Two](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha%27arei_Orah%2C_Second_Gate%2C_Ninth_Sefirah.5), Gikatilla says that a house rests upon its foundation: similarly, Adonai/Malkhut rests upon Yesod. Divine influx reaches the lower kingdom through **El Chai**. He also links **Shaddai** with the divine limiting word *dai*, “enough.”

**[TEXT]** Gikatilla therefore supplies:

- Yesod as literal foundation
- El Chai
- Shaddai/El Shaddai
- Covenant of circumcision
- Sabbath as sign
- Zion
- The sustaining channel to Malkhut

**[QUALIFICATION]** Gikatilla’s discussion does not amount to a Golden Dawn table. Gabriel, Kerubim, Moon, purple colors, tarot, and Theoricus are absent.

### Zion

**[TRADITION]** Gikatilla reads Zion not only geographically but as a mystical locus of concentrated divine generativity. Isaiah’s foundation stone becomes part of the language through which the foundational channel is contemplated.

The association with Joseph is reinforced by traditional numerical and verbal connections. Joseph is the human exemplar of controlled sexual power and faithful transmission.

## 4. The Zoharic corpus

### Date and authorship

**[TRADITION]** The Zohar presents its teachings through Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and companions of the second century.

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** The work surfaced in late thirteenth-century Castile through Moses de León. Gershom Scholem argued that de León was its principal author. Current scholarship more often treats “Zoharic literature” as a stratified product of a literary circle, with de León central but not necessarily responsible for every stratum. See [The Aramaic of the Zohar: status quaestionis](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10088406/) and Scholem’s [*Major Trends in Jewish Mysticism*](https://www.bard.edu/library/pdfs/archives/Scholem-Major_Trends_in_Jewish_Mysticism.pdf).

### Yesod as Tzaddik

**[TEXT]** The Zohar repeatedly identifies the righteous one with the foundational channel. “Tzaddik” is simultaneously:

- A divine potency
- A righteous human being
- Joseph
- The covenantal organ
- A channel of blessing

This permits movement between theology, ethics, body, biblical narrative, and ritual.

### Joseph the Righteous

In [Zohar, *Miketz*](https://www.sefaria.org/Zohar%2C_Miketz.4.35), Joseph’s resistance to Potiphar’s wife makes him guardian of the covenant and a “chariot” for Yesod.

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** Joseph’s sexuality is not negated; it is controlled, sanctified, and made capable of transmitting blessing. His epithet **Yosef ha-Tzaddik** therefore carries a technical kabbalistic meaning.

**[TRADITION]** Joseph’s economic distribution during famine also mirrors Yesod’s cosmic function: he gathers abundance and apportions it to the recipient world.

### Covenant and phallus

**[TEXT]** In Zoharic symbolism, the “sign of the covenant” is the circumcised male organ. Yesod is the divine analogue of this organ.

**[IMPORTANT QUALIFICATION]** The symbolism is not reducible to a modern anatomical chart. It joins:

- Circumcision
- Sexual fidelity
- Ejaculation or withholding
- River and water
- Divine naming
- Sabbath
- Union of masculine and feminine aspects of the Godhead
- Generation of souls and blessing

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** Elliot Wolfson has shown that circumcision, visionary experience, gender, and the divine name form a single symbolic field in medieval Kabbalah. The imagery is frequently androcentric: the male organ is imagined as covenantal transmitter, while the feminine Shekhinah receives and manifests. Modern egalitarian rereadings should be identified as reinterpretations, not silently projected backward.

### Yesod, Tiferet, and Shekhinah

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** Yesod is usually the terminal male channel of the configuration whose central body is Tiferet. It transmits into Malkhut/Shekhinah.

This is why Yesod is called:

- **Kol**, All
- **Tzaddik**, Righteous
- **Brit**, Covenant
- **Shalom**, Peace
- A river, stream, spring, or fountain
- The “end of the body”

**[TEXT]** *Petaḥ Eliyahu* in the *Tikkunei Zohar* describes Netzach and Hod as the legs and Yesod as “the end of the body, the sign of the holy covenant”: [Sefaria source sheet](https://www.sefaria.org.il/sheets/418649).

### Moon and dreams

**[TEXT]** The Zohar connects Gabriel with dreams in places such as [Zohar, *Vayetzei*](https://www.sefaria.org/Zohar%2C_Vayetzei.8.45).

**[QUALIFICATION]** This does not by itself establish the complete equation Yesod = Gabriel = Moon. In much Zoharic symbolism, the Moon represents the receptive Shekhinah/Malkhut, while Yesod is the channel through which she receives. The later Hermetic table compresses and reorganizes these overlapping motifs.

## 5. Prayer

### Redemption joined to prayer

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** Zoharic interpretation connects the blessing **Gaʾal Yisrael**, “Who redeemed Israel,” with Yesod and the following Amidah with Malkhut. Liturgically joining redemption to prayer enacts their union. See [Zohar, *Terumah*, verses 42–59](https://www.zohar.com/zohar/Trumah/verses/42-59).

### The blessing of peace

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** The concluding peace blessing of the Amidah corresponds to Yesod in a Zoharic enumeration: [Zohar, *Terumah*, chapter 7](https://www.zohar.com/zohar/Trumah/chapters/7).

This fits Yesod as *shalom*, the harmonizing bond. Counting varies because the ancient “Eighteen Benedictions” has nineteen blessings in standard later forms.

### No universal “Yesod prayer”

**[ABSENCE]** Classical Judaism has no single universally obligatory prayer addressed to the sefirah Yesod. Kabbalistic prayer assigns divine processes to existing liturgical words; it does not normally replace God with the sefirot as independent objects of worship.

## 6. Cordovero

### *Pardes Rimmonim* (completed 1548)

Moses Cordovero systematized earlier Kabbalah before the mature dissemination of Lurianism.

In [*Pardes Rimmonim* 1:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.1.4.4), Yesod harmonizes Netzach and Hod. It is the covenantal mediator through which the lower powers are unified.

In [*Pardes Rimmonim* 1:2](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.1.2), Cordovero connects the covenantal genital organ with Yesod.

In [*Pardes Rimmonim* 1:10](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.1.10), the names are explained:

- Yesod is **El Chai** because life issues from it.
- It is **Shaddai** because divine supply is measured or told “enough.”
- The righteous one sustains the world.

In [*Pardes Rimmonim* 23:10](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.23.10.2), Yesod feeds the worlds and sustains Malkhut.

In [*Pardes Rimmonim* 15:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.15.4), Joseph and Benjamin can represent direct and reflected relations between Yesod and Malkhut.

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** Cordovero’s Yesod is therefore:

- Ninth
- A harmonizer
- A reservoir
- A channel
- Life-giving
- Sexual and covenantal
- Ethically realized through the righteous person

### *Or Neʿerav*

[*Or Neʿerav* VI](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_VI.5.12) assigns Tzevaot names to Netzach/Hod and discusses Shaddai and El Chai for Yesod.

[*Or Neʿerav* III](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_III.3.10) offers a homiletic etymology in which Yesod suggests the yod of covenant and *sod*, “mystery.”

**[PHILOLOGICAL QUALIFICATION]** This is symbolic wordplay, not the historical etymology of יסוד.

[*Or Neʿerav* III.5](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_III.5.31) connects the holy sign of circumcision with adhesion to Yesod.

## 7. Luria through Ḥayyim Vital

### Historical setting

Isaac Luria, the Ari, taught in Safed until his death in 1572. He wrote little systematic prose. His doctrines survive principally through disciples, especially Ḥayyim Vital.

**[TEXTUAL QUALIFICATION]** “The *Etz Chaim* of Vital” is not a single authorially finalized book in the modern sense. Manuscripts, Vital’s notebooks, the work of his son Shmuel Vital, and the redaction of Meir Poppers all shaped the transmitted corpus.

### Adam Kadmon

[*Etz Chaim* 2:2](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.2.2) discusses ten sefirot within Adam Kadmon.

**[TRADITION]** Yesod is consequently both:

- A cosmic power
- A limb or structural station within primordial humanity
- A reproductive/transmissive organ within later partzufim

### Contraction, vessels, and shattering

The Lurianic sequence includes:

1. *Tzimtzum*, contraction or concealment
2. The ray entering the vacated space
3. Adam Kadmon
4. Emanations through the “eyes,” producing the world of Nekudim
5. Vessels unable to contain the light
6. Shattering of the vessels
7. Descent of fragments and sparks
8. Rectification through ordered partzufim

[*Etz Chaim* 1:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.4.11) places the broken kings and their repair within the emergence of the worlds. [*Etz Chaim* 9:2](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.9.2) connects the seven kings with the lower structure and the emergence of residues nourishing the shells.

**[TRADITION]** Yesod participates in the shattering insofar as it belongs to the seven lower emotive powers. In tikkun, its energies are reorganized within Zeir Anpin and related to Nukva in a controlled, face-to-face configuration.

### Partzufim

In mature Lurianic Kabbalah the principal partzufim include:

- Arikh Anpin
- Abba
- Imma
- Zeir Anpin
- Nukva

Zeir Anpin contains the six sefirot Chesed through Yesod. Yesod is its terminal generative organ and the immediate channel into Nukva/Malkhut.

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** [*Etz Chaim* 40:13](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.40.13) treats the letter vav as encompassing six powers through Yesod. [*Etz Chaim* 1:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.5) relates the final heh of the divine name to Malkhut/Nukva.

### Four worlds

The four worlds are:

1. Atzilut — Emanation
2. Beriah — Creation
3. Yetzirah — Formation
4. Asiyah — Action

**[TRADITION]** A complete tenfold structure can be contemplated within each world. It is therefore misleading to say simply that Yesod “is Yetzirah.” In Golden Dawn tables Yesod has a special affinity with the formative or astral world, but Jewish Lurianic Kabbalah presents Yesod within each world and partzufic system.

### Tikkun and ethics

**[TRADITION]** Repairing Yesod involves:

- Sexual discipline
- Sanctification of the covenant
- Proper marital union
- Restraint of fantasy and emission
- Attachment to the righteous
- Correct prayer intention
- Raising and transmitting sparks

The modern slogan “Yesod means sexual energy” is therefore incomplete. Sexual symbolism is fundamental, but it belongs to a broader theology of transmission, covenant, ethical restraint, generation, language, and cosmic repair.

## 8. The Vilna Gaon’s tree

Elijah ben Solomon Zalman, the Vilna Gaon or Gra, produced an eighteenth-century recension and commentary on *Sefer Yetzirah*.

**[TRADITION]** Gra diagrams attempt to embody:

- The ten sefirot
- Three Mothers
- Seven Doubles
- Twelve Simples
- Spatial and cosmological relationships described in *Sefer Yetzirah*

**[DISPUTED]** The modern label “the Gra tree” can conceal variation among editions and reconstructions. It is not simply Kircher’s tree with different labels.

**[ABSENCE]** The Gra did not validate the Golden Dawn tarot overlay. The Golden Dawn’s assignment of trumps to the lines is a nineteenth-century Hermetic operation.

## 9. Hasidic readings

### *Tanya*

The most direct Chabad discussion is not a chapter of *Likkutei Amarim* titled “Yesod.” It is [*Iggeret HaKodesh* 15](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.15.15).

**[TEXT]** Rabbi Shneur Zalman explains Yesod through the analogy of a father binding his intellect to his son “face to face,” with love and desire that the son understand.

The emphasis is relational:

- Knowledge not merely possessed but communicated
- Emotional attachment joining teacher and recipient
- Contraction of an expansive idea into transmissible form
- Interpersonal bonding as an analogue of divine transmission

**[ABSENCE]** There is no separate canonical “Tanya chapter on Yesod” in the first part corresponding to the way modern handbooks sometimes imply.

### Naḥman of Bratslav

[Likutei Moharan 34:3](https://www.sefaria.org/Likutei_Moharan.34.3.2) identifies Joseph, covenant, and Yesod and treats the righteous person’s mouth as a channel of abundance.

[Likutei Moharan 7:6](https://www.sefaria.org/Likutei_Moharan.7.6.6) connects generative seed, mind, kidneys, covenant, and attachment to the tzaddik.

**[TRADITION]** Bratslav shifts Yesod toward the living charisma of the tzaddik: speech, counsel, prayer, sexuality, and communal attachment become channels of divine vitality.

## 10. Omer, calendar, tribe, and Torah

### Counting the Omer

**[TRADITION]** In the widespread kabbalistic Omer scheme, the seven weeks correspond to:

1. Chesed
2. Gevurah
3. Tiferet
4. Netzach
5. Hod
6. **Yesod**
7. Malkhut

Yesod’s week comprises days 36–42. Each day combines Yesod with one of the seven lower sefirot:

- Chesed shebe-Yesod
- Gevurah shebe-Yesod
- Tiferet shebe-Yesod
- Netzach shebe-Yesod
- Hod shebe-Yesod
- Yesod shebe-Yesod
- Malkhut shebe-Yesod

See [Chabad’s week-six Omer resource](https://www.chabad.org/multimedia/audio_cdo/aid/3998458/jewish/Counting-the-Omer-Week-6.htm).

**[QUALIFICATION]** This contemplative overlay is not the plain biblical commandment to count forty-nine days. It is a later kabbalistic devotional structure.

### Month and tribe

**[NOT APPLICABLE]** A month and tribe are assigned in *Sefer Yetzirah* systems to each of the twelve **simple letters**, not to Yesod as a sefirah. Yesod therefore has no single canonical month or tribe under that system.

Later occultists sometimes derive lunar months or Joseph’s tribes, Ephraim and Manasseh, from other correspondences. These should not be confused with the twelve-simple-letter table.

### Torah

**[TRADITION]** The Torah as a whole is more often associated with Tiferet, the written Torah, or with Chokhmah in other contexts. Yesod’s Torah relation is specifically covenantal and transmissive.

The Zoharic claim that guarding the covenant is equivalent to guarding the Torah expresses a totalizing ethical symbolism; it does not make Yesod the exclusive “sefirah of Torah.”

# The Hermetic and occult tradition

## 1. Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Pico’s *Conclusiones nongentae* proposed 900 theses for public debate. Among them were conclusions “according to the secret teaching of the Hebrew cabalists.”

**[CHRISTIAN CABALA]** Pico treated Kabbalah as a primordial theology capable of confirming Christian claims. His project combined Jewish materials with Platonism, Neoplatonism, scholastic philosophy, magic, and Christian theology.

The Latin primary text is available at [Esoteric Archives](https://www.esotericarchives.com/pico/conclus.htm).

**[ABSENCE]** The searchable text does not provide a later-style Yesod entry with *Iesod*, *Fundamentum*, Gabriel, Kerubim, Moon, and a fixed tree position. Pico refers to numbered “numerations,” including the ninth, but his work is not a Golden Dawn correspondence manual.

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** Pico’s importance is therefore not that he completed the Yesod table. He established the cultural project in which Jewish Kabbalah was reinterpreted as proof of Christianity. This appropriation was later criticized by Jewish writers such as Leon Modena: [Yaacob Dweck, “A Jewish Response to Christian Kabbalah”](https://doi.org/10.23943/princeton/9780691145082.003.0006).

## 2. Johannes Reuchlin, 1517

Reuchlin’s *De arte cabalistica* is the first extensive printed Latin defense and exposition of Kabbalah.

**[CHRISTIAN CABALA/TEXT]** Its Yesod cluster appears in Latin approximately as:

> **Iesod, Fundamentum … Sadai … fundamentum mundi … deus vivus … Joseph Justus**

That is: Yesod, Foundation; Shaddai; foundation of the world; living God; Joseph the Righteous.

Reuchlin’s work is available through the [German Digital Library record and scan](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/item/CV4BIAVTHUELB57ALX33HUCVJOKVSBO7).

Reuchlin also places the **Kerubim** with the ninth emanation and the **Ishim** with the tenth.

**[HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE]** Here the medieval Jewish name, divine names, Joseph, biblical foundation, and angelic hierarchy enter a durable Latin table.

**[QUALIFICATION]** Reuchlin’s system is Christian Cabala. It should not be cited as though it were an untouched rabbinic consensus.

## 3. The *Portae Lucis* title page, 1516

Paulus Ricius’s Latin *Portae Lucis*, derived from Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah*, appeared in Augsburg in 1516.

Its title page contains what is often identified as the first printed representation of a kabbalistic tree: [Center for Jewish Art record](https://cja.huji.ac.il/browser.php?id=40577).

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** It stands between Jewish manuscript diagrams and Christian print culture. It does not yet establish the exact later Golden Dawn path system.

## 4. Agrippa

**[CHRISTIAN/HERMETIC TRANSMISSION]** Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa’s *De occulta philosophia*, published in final form in 1533, transmitted Reuchlinian sefirah, divine-name, angelic, and planetary correspondences into ritual magic.

Agrippa is a major bridge between Christian Cabala and later ceremonial magic even though the user’s named sequence begins with Pico and Reuchlin.

## 5. Kircher, 1652–1654

Athanasius Kircher published *Oedipus Aegyptiacus* in Rome between 1652 and 1654. His *Cabala Hebraeorum* is available in Latin at [Esoteric Archives](https://www.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm); catalog and scan links are collected by the [Online Books Page](https://onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/book/lookupid?key=olbp67641).

### Latin name

Kircher’s table gives:

> **יסוד — Iesod — Fundamentum**

and associates the row with **El Chai**. His table does not necessarily present the exact later compound “Shaddai El Chai.”

### The Kircher tree

The famous plate appears in *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, volume II, part I, around pp. 288–290: [Cornell digital image](https://digital.library.cornell.edu/catalog/ss%3A550272).

**[SCHOLARSHIP]** Kircher drew partly on earlier diagrams, including Philippe d’Aquin’s 1625 *Interprétation de l’arbre de la cabale*. The precise genealogy of individual lines is still being refined, but Kircher’s plate became the decisive printed ancestor of the standard Hermetic tree.

### Why its paths matter

Kircher arranged twenty-two connecting lines so that the Hebrew letters could be placed in alphabetical sequence. The Golden Dawn retained this basic topology and overlaid:

- *Sefer Yetzirah* letter classes
- Astrology
- Tarot trumps
- Colors
- Initiatory grades
- Egyptian god-forms
- Enochian and alchemical symbolism

**[DISPUTED]** Kircher’s arrangement is not “the ancient Jewish Tree.” Jewish manuscript *ilanot*, Cordoveran diagrams, Lurianic trees, and Gra diagrams differ in topology and purpose.

## 6. Knorr von Rosenroth, 1677–1684

Christian Knorr von Rosenroth’s *Kabbala Denudata* made extensive Jewish and Lurianic writings available in Latin.

A scanned edition is available through [e-rara](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/1283857.pdf), with relevant supplementary material in [this volume](https://www.e-rara.ch/download/pdf/1283919.pdf).

The Yesod passages include ideas such as:

- Adonai/Malkhut resting upon Yesod
- Yesod called El Chai
- The world standing upon one pillar named Tzaddik
- Proverbs 10:25
- The genital member of both sexes
- Mercurial or reproductive imagery in later Latin discussion

Representative Latin includes:

> **Mundus … super unica columna cui nomen Zaddik**

“The world [stands] upon a single pillar whose name is Tzaddik.”

**[CHRISTIAN CABALA]** Rosenroth’s translations transmitted authentic Jewish and Lurianic textual material, but within an explicitly Christian intellectual project. Recent scholarship also shows that his work depended on Hebrew manuscripts and Jewish assistance rather than solitary “decipherment”: [2024 study](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/asch-2024-2016/html).

## 7. Éliphas Lévi, 1854–1856

Lévi’s *Dogme et rituel de la haute magie* popularized the formula that the twenty-two tarot trumps correspond to the twenty-two Hebrew letters.

**[HISTORICAL QUALIFICATION]** Antoine Court de Gébelin and the Comte de Mellet had already connected tarot with ancient wisdom and Hebrew letters in the eighteenth century. Lévi did not invent every element, but he made the synthesis programmatic and influential.

A public-domain English scan is available here: [*Transcendental Magic*](https://www.rahoorkhuit.net/library/ceremonial/classics/pdf/transcendental/dogma_et_rituel_part_2.pdf).

**[DISPUTED]** Lévi’s ordering is not the Golden Dawn’s. He associated Aleph with the Magician rather than the Fool. The Golden Dawn rearranged the system to harmonize its chosen astrology, Hebrew order, and tarot numbering.

For Yesod, Lévi’s deeper contribution is the **astral light**: a subtle, plastic medium receiving images and mediating between mind and matter. Later writers, especially Fortune and Regardie, identified that medium strongly with Yesod.

## 8. The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn

### Knowledge Lectures

The Golden Dawn’s late nineteenth-century Knowledge Lectures standardized the modern occult Yesod package:

- Ninth sefirah
- Foundation
- Pure Intelligence
- Shaddai El Chai
- Gabriel
- Kerubim
- Levanah/Moon
- Reproductive organs
- Four color scales
- Four Nines of tarot
- Theoricus 2°=9□

Israel Regardie later printed the system in *The Golden Dawn*: [scanned edition](https://scriptaetveritas.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/ISRAEL_REGARDIE_COMPLETE_GOLDEN_DAWN_SYSTEM_OF_MAGIC.pdf).

### Theoricus 2°=9□

The Golden Dawn’s [Theoricus ritual](https://hermetic.com/crowley/equinox/i/ii/eqi02020f) explicitly says that the grade:

- Is attributed to Air
- Refers to the Moon
- Is attached to the thirty-second path of Tav
- Concerns the four elements, Kerubim, Qliphoth, astral plane, and reflected Saturn

**[HERMETIC]** The candidate travels from Malkhut to Yesod along Tav and enters the lunar sphere.

**[QUALIFICATION]** Air is the grade’s elemental curriculum; Moon is the sefirah’s planet. These are not a claim that the Moon is physically composed of Air.

### “Pure Intelligence”

William Wynn Westcott’s 1887 translation appends a list of the Thirty-Two Paths. The ninth is:

> “The Ninth Path is the Pure Intelligence.”

It is said to purify the numerations and preserve their unity. See [Westcott’s *Sepher Yetzirah*](https://sacred-texts.com/jud/yetzirah.htm).

**[CRITICAL FINDING]** The named Intelligences are not part of the early core recensions of *Sefer Yetzirah*. They derive from a later medieval “Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom” tradition. Calling this “the Yetziratic text of Yesod” is Golden Dawn usage, not a quotation in which ancient *Sefer Yetzirah* names Yesod.

### Book T and the four Nines

The Golden Dawn’s *Book T* assigns the four Nines to Yesod:

| Suit | Golden Dawn title | Astrology |
|---|---|---|
| Nine of Wands | Lord of Great Strength | Moon in Sagittarius |
| Nine of Cups | Lord of Material Happiness | Jupiter in Pisces |
| Nine of Swords | Lord of Despair and Cruelty | Mars in Gemini |
| Nine of Pentacles | Lord of Material Gain | Venus in Virgo |

Primary scan: [Mathers/Felkin, *Book T*](https://benebellwen.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/mathers-and-felkin-golden-dawn-book-t-the-tarot-1888.pdf).

The cards express Yesod as crystallization immediately before manifestation:

- Fire: strength stabilized
- Water: emotional abundance
- Air: thought solidified into torment
- Earth: material gain nearing completion

### *Liber 777*

Crowley published *777* anonymously in 1909. It reorganized Golden Dawn correspondence material into a large tabular reference. Primary scans include [Hermetic Library](https://hermetic.com/_media/93beast.fea.st/files/section1/777/liber_777.pdf), [IAPSOP](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1909__crowley___777.pdf), and [Keep Silence](https://keepsilence.org/the-equinox/777/table-of-correspondences_low.pdf).

The Yesod row preserves:

- Kerubim

A complete nonblank-row inventory appears in the Correspondences section below.

### *Liber LVIII*

Crowley writes that Netzach and Hod produce:

> “ISVD, Yesod, the Foundation or Basis”

represented by El Chai and Shaddai. See [*Liber LVIII*](https://hermetic.com/crowley/libers/lib58).

He also notes the triangular number:

**[HERMETIC]** Forty-five is then available for comparison with Adam, אדם, which also equals 45 in ordinary gematria.

### The four Nines in *The Book of Thoth*

Crowley describes Yesod as a low but decisive “crystallization of Energy.” The direct ray from Tiferet saves it from the imbalance of Netzach and Hod.

His shortened card titles are:

- Nine of Swords — Cruelty

See [*The Book of Thoth*, Small Cards](https://hermetic.com/crowley/book-of-thoth/small-cards).

### A∴A∴ grade

In the A∴A∴ system the 2°=9□ grade is **Zelator**, not Theoricus. [*Liber XIII*](https://larabell.org/mirrors/maroney.org/CrowleyIntro/Texts/Book_13.html) assigns the Zelator posture, breath-control, meditation, and magical-instrument work.

Thus:

- Golden Dawn: Theoricus 2=9
- A∴A∴: Zelator 2=9

Confusing the two grade-names collapses distinct organizations.

### *Liber CCXXXI*

[*Liber CCXXXI*](https://www.bibliotecapleyades.net/crowley/liber/libers/liber231.pdf) presents twenty-two “houses” of the Mercury genii and twenty-two “prisons” of the Qliphoth, corresponding to tarot trumps and Hebrew letters.

**[ABSENCE]** It is not a prose chapter about Yesod. Its relevance is indirect:

- It provides averse path spirits.
- Kenneth Grant later used them to construct the Tunnels of Set.
- Four such tunnels touch the nightside counterpart of Yesod.

Calling Liber 231 an ancient qliphothic map is incorrect. It is a Crowleyan revelation/technical text first published in the early twentieth century.

## 10. Dion Fortune, *The Mystical Qabalah* (1935)

Fortune’s Yesod treatment is Chapter XXIV in standard editions. A scan is available at [Awakening Intuition](https://www.awakening-intuition.com/FortuneDion-TheMysticalQabbalah.pdf).

### Fortune’s opening table

She gives:

- Title: Yesod, Foundation
- Magical image: beautiful naked man, very strong
- Position: base of the Middle Pillar
- Yetziratic text: Pure Intelligence
- God-name: Shaddai El Chai
- Archangel: Gabriel
- Order: Kerubim
- Mundane chakra: Levanah, Moon
- Spiritual experience: Vision of the Machinery of the Universe
- Virtue: independence
- Vice: idleness
- Body: reproductive organs
- Symbols: perfumes and sandals

### Detailed analytical précis of the chapter

**[HERMETIC]** Fortune calls Yesod exceptionally important to the practical occultist because it is the immediate nonphysical foundation of Malkuth.

Her argument proceeds through several linked claims:

1. **Yesod as formative matrix.** Physical events do not arise unmediated. Images, patterns, and subtle forces are organized in Yesod before they appear in Malkuth.

2. **Astral light.** Fortune identifies Yesod with the plastic, image-bearing medium earlier occultists called the astral light.

3. **Treasure House of Images.** Yesod contains forms deposited by personal and collective imagination. These include dreams, symbols, fantasies, emotional images, ritual forms, and inherited psychic residues.

4. **Maya or illusion.** Yesod is not “unreal” in the sense of nonexistent. It is deceptive because images can be mistaken for independent realities or spiritual truths.

5. **Reflection.** As moonlight reflects sunlight, Yesod reflects forces descending from higher levels. Its apparent autonomy can therefore be misleading.

6. **Unconscious mind.** Fortune equates much Yesodic activity with instinct, dream, fantasy, repression, memory, and the subconscious. This is a twentieth-century psychological recoding of the older “astral” doctrine.

7. **Netzach and Hod.** Emotional force from Netzach and conceptual form from Hod meet in Yesod, where a workable image is produced.

8. **Reproduction.** Sexual organs symbolize Yesod because they give form and continuity to life. Sexual images are one manifestation of a more universal generative function.

9. **Magical images.** Ritual magic acts through deliberate construction, charging, and dissolution of Yesodic forms. Uncontrolled fantasy produces glamour; disciplined imagination produces an instrument.

10. **Psychic hygiene.** Because Yesod contains images and desires, the occultist must distinguish intuition from projection and symbolic perception from wish-fulfillment.

11. **The Moon.** Lunar periodicity explains flux, tides, fertility, sleep, dream, and the cyclic alteration of psychic sensitivity.

12. **Machinery of the universe.** The “machinery” is not merely mechanical hardware. It is the lawful formative process by which subtle forces become appearances and events.

13. **Perfumes.** Odor works directly and associatively upon consciousness; incense provides a material medium for evoking a subtle state.

14. **Sandals.** Sandals signify the capacity to travel securely through the astral field and to remain insulated or properly grounded.

15. **Practical warning.** Yesod must be passed through, not enthroned as ultimate. Fascination with visions, spirits, sexuality, or fantasy can imprison the practitioner within reflected imagery.

**[CRITICAL QUALIFICATION]** “Treasure House of Images,” “astral light,” “subconscious,” and “machinery of the universe” are Fortune’s Hermetic-psychological synthesis. They should not be put into the mouth of the *Bahir*, Zohar, or Vital without qualification.

# The Qliphah or tunnel

## Jewish kelippah doctrine versus the inverse tree

**[TEXT/TRADITION]** Jewish Kabbalah uses **kelippah/kelippot**, “shell/husk/shells,” for forces of concealment, obstruction, waste, judgment, or evil. Zoharic and Lurianic sources contain elaborate doctrines of the Other Side.

**[CRITICAL DISTINCTION]** A perfectly symmetrical inverse Tree with ten named demonic spheres, one demon prince per sphere, twenty-two named tunnels, tarot trumps, and modern pathworking instructions is not a classical Jewish diagram. It is a layered Western-occult construction.

## Gamaliel

Crowley’s *777* assigns the ninth qliphothic order to:

> **Gamaliel — “Obscene Ones”**

The primary table places Gamaliel opposite Yesod: [*Liber 777* scan](https://astrumargentum.org/ht/libri/Liber_777.pdf).

The Golden Dawn paper [“The Qliphoth”](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Qliphoth.html) calls them “obscene bull-men, linked together.”

### Philological warning

**[TEXT]** **Gamaliel**, גמליאל, is an ordinary Hebrew personal name known from Jewish and Christian antiquity. It is normally understood along the lines of “God has recompensed/rewarded me.”

**[HERMETIC/DISPUTED]** “Obscene Ones,” “Polluted of God,” and similar glosses are occult titles, not convincing translations of the Hebrew name. They may represent corruption, wordplay, or an inherited misunderstanding in the Latin and English tabular tradition.

## Lilith as ruler

The historical evidence is untidy:

- **[HERMETIC]** Crowley’s qliphoth column places Gamaliel at nine and Lilith as the order at ten.
- **[HERMETIC]** Some infernal-ruler columns pair Lilith with the ninth level.
- **[HERMETIC]** The Golden Dawn prose paper describes Lilith under Malkuth while describing Gamaliel separately under Yesod.
- **[MODERN]** Later qliphothic systems standardize “Gamaliel ruled by Lilith.”

Therefore the statement “the ancient Kabbalah teaches that Lilith is the demon prince of Gamaliel” is unsupported.

A historically responsible formulation is:

> **Gamaliel is the Golden Dawn/Crowley qliphothic order opposed to Yesod; Lilith became its standard ruler in later occult syntheses, although earlier tables duplicate or relocate her.**

|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Thaumiel | Keter |
| 2 | Ghagiel | Chokhmah |
| 3 | Satariel | Binah |
| 4 | Gha’agsheklah / Gha’agsheblah | Chesed |
| 5 | Golachab | Gevurah |
| 6 | Thagirion | Tiferet |
| 7 | Aʿarab Zaraq | Netzach |
| 8 | Samael | Hod |
| 9 | **Gamaliel** | **Yesod** |
| 10 | Lilith | Malkhut |

**[DISPUTED]** Spellings vary drastically because Hebrew and Aramaic terms passed through manuscript, Latin, German, French, and English transmission.

## Grant’s adjoining tunnels

On the usual nightside reflection of the Kircher/Golden Dawn tree, four tunnels meet Gamaliel:

| Letter/path | Tunnel | Qliphoth joined |
|---|---|---|
| Samekh | **Saksaksalim** | Thagirion–Gamaliel |

| Resh | **Raflifu** | Samael–Gamaliel |
| Tav | **Thantifaxath** | Gamaliel–Nahemoth/Lilith |

**[MODERN OCCULT]** Names and sigils ultimately derive from Crowley’s Liber 231, but their organization as tunnels and Grant’s descriptions date to *Nightside of Eden*.

# Paths adjoining Yesod

Because Yesod is a sefirah rather than a letter-path, it has no letter shape, numerical value, or tarot trump of its own. On the Kircher/Golden Dawn tree four paths touch it.

| Path | Letter | Value and literal name | *Sefer Yetzirah* class | Golden Dawn attribution | Joins |
|---:|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 25 | ס Samekh | 60; “support/prop” | Simple | Sagittarius; Temperance, Crowley’s Art | Tiferet–Yesod |
| 28 | צ Tzaddi | 90; final ץ; traditional name etymology uncertain, occult “fish-hook” | Simple | Aquarius; Star in Golden Dawn, Emperor in Crowley | Netzach–Yesod |
| 30 | ר Resh | 200; “head” | Double | Sun; The Sun | Hod–Yesod |
| 32 | ת Tav | 400; “mark/sign” | Double | Saturn and Earth; World/Universe | Yesod–Malkhut |

## Recensional issues

**[TEXT/DISPUTED]** The identification of the seven double letters with the seven planets varies among *Sefer Yetzirah* recensions and commentaries. The Golden Dawn follows a chosen later arrangement and supplements Tav with Earth as well as Saturn.

**[CRITICAL FINDING]** The ancient text classifies Samekh and Tzaddi as simple letters and Resh and Tav as doubles. It does not say that these letters join the four specific pairs of sefirot on Kircher’s diagram.

## Ari and Gra trees

**[TRADITION/DISPUTED]** Ari and Gra diagrams apply mother/double/simple categories according to different organizing principles. They do not offer a single universally agreed path-to-sefirah mapping corresponding to the Golden Dawn one.

Consequently, “What does Tzaddi join on the Ari tree?” may receive different answers depending on:

- Which printed reconstruction is used
- Whether the diagram represents *iggulim* or *yosher*
- Whether paths mean causal emanation, letter operation, bodily channels, or graphic adjacency
- Whether later editors have imposed Golden Dawn-style line counting

The safe historical conclusion is that the Kircher/Golden Dawn four-path neighborhood of Yesod is not transferable wholesale to Ari or Gra diagrams.

## Tzaddi and Heh

Golden Dawn:

- Heh = Aries = Emperor
- Tzaddi = Aquarius = Star

Crowley’s *Book of the Law* I:57 says:

> “Tzaddi is not the Star.”

In *The Book of Thoth*, Crowley resolved this by assigning:

- Tzaddi/Aquarius to the Emperor
- Heh/Aries to the Star

See the primary [*Book of Thoth* scan](https://www.rahoorkhuit.net/library/crowley/equinox/volume3/vol_3_no_05.pdf).

**[DISPUTED]** This is a Thelemic revelation and interpretive solution, not a philological correction to *Sefer Yetzirah*. Golden Dawn, Rider–Waite–Smith, B.O.T.A., and Thoth practitioners therefore use competing systems.

## Pathworking traditions

### Tav: Malkhut to Yesod

The Golden Dawn Theoricus rite is the foundational pathworking:

- Candidate leaves the material grade
- Encounters elemental/kerubic guardians
- Passes through Saturnian limitation and the four elements
- Enters lunar Yesod

Later guided pathworkings emphasize dreams, thresholds, caves, temples, mirrors, and lunar landscapes.

### Samekh: Yesod to Tiferet

Samekh is the central ascent toward Tiferet. In Thelema it becomes especially important through *Liber Samekh* and the Knowledge and Conversation of the Holy Guardian Angel.

### Resh: Hod to Yesod

Resh joins intellect, language, and solar consciousness to the image-making foundation. Pathworkings explore the illumination or exposure of unconscious contents.

### Tzaddi: Netzach to Yesod

Golden Dawn practice joins Venusian feeling and lunar imagery under Aquarius and the Star. Crowley’s swap changes the trump to the Emperor, producing a different psychological and ritual reading.

**[MODERN]** Detailed scripts describing exact temples, beings, passwords, or visionary stages generally derive from twentieth-century pathworking manuals, not medieval Jewish sources.

# People and works

## Jewish kabbalists and texts

### Biblical authors and sages

- Proverbs supplied **tzaddik yesod olam**.
- Chronicles supplied the lower-sefirot vocabulary and *ki khol*.
- Isaiah supplied foundation, Zion, and cosmic-building imagery.
- Job supplied Wisdom and *belimah* language.

### *Sefer Yetzirah* commentators

- Saadia Gaon: philosophical-cosmological recension and commentary.
- Dunash ibn Tamim: early commentary.
- Shabbetai Donnolo: cosmological and medical reception.
- Judah ben Barzillai: extensive medieval commentary.
- Abraham Abulafia: linguistic and ecstatic interpretation.
- Vilna Gaon: recension, commentary, and diagrammatic reorganization.

### *Bahir* and early theosophical Kabbalah

- Anonymous *Bahir* compilers
- Isaac the Blind
- Azriel and Ezra of Gerona
- Naḥmanides and related Gerona circles

These developed the pillar, righteous-one, covenant, and emanational system in which Yesod became fixed.

### Joseph Gikatilla

*Shaʿarei Orah* provided one of the clearest medieval explanations of Yesod through divine names, Zion, covenant, and the dependence of Malkhut upon its foundation.

### Zoharic circle

- Traditional protagonist: Shimon bar Yoḥai
- Historical transmitter/likely central author: Moses de León
- Probable wider literary circle and later strata

Daniel C. Matt’s Pritzker translation is the major current annotated English edition.

### Cordovero

- *Pardes Rimmonim*, completed 1548
- *Or Neʿerav*

Cordovero organized the symbolic material into a relatively systematic theology of mediation and divine names.

### Luria and Vital

- Isaac Luria
- Ḥayyim Vital
- Shmuel Vital
- Meir Poppers

Their textual transmission transformed Yesod from one sefirah in a linear order into a repeated organ-function within worlds, partzufim, Adam Kadmon, shattering, and repair.

### Hasidic masters

- Shneur Zalman of Liadi: interpersonal bonding and transmission
- Naḥman of Bratslav: tzaddik, covenant, speech, and abundance

## Christian Cabalists and Hermetic magicians

### Pico della Mirandola

Made Kabbalah a central component of Renaissance Christian concordism.

### Johannes Reuchlin

Printed the enduring Latin Yesod/Fundamentum–Shaddai–El Chai–Joseph–Kerubim complex.

### Paulus Ricius

Translated and Christianized Gikatilla; *Portae Lucis* helped transfer the Tree into print.

### Agrippa

Integrated Cabala with planetary, angelic, talismanic, and ceremonial magic.

### Athanasius Kircher

Published the diagram that became the principal ancestor of the Hermetic tree.

### Knorr von Rosenroth

Made Zoharic and Lurianic materials available in Latin and influenced Mathers, the Golden Dawn, and European esotericism.

### Éliphas Lévi

Popularized Hebrew-letter/tarot correspondence and astral-light theory.

### Westcott and Mathers

- Westcott translated *Sefer Yetzirah* and the Thirty-Two Paths appendix.
- Mathers transmitted Rosenroth and formulated Golden Dawn teaching and tarot correspondences.

### Aleister Crowley

Systematized the correspondence table in *777*, modified tarot through the Tzaddi–Heh exchange, and mapped Yesod to A∴A∴ Zelator.

### Dion Fortune

Made Yesod a theory of the astral light, unconscious, reproductive forces, magical imagery, and practical psychic hygiene.

### Israel Regardie

Published Golden Dawn teachings and consolidated the modern psychological-astral interpretation.

### Paul Foster Case

Reframed lunar Yesod as subconscious formative power within B.O.T.A.

### Gareth Knight

Expanded Fortune’s etheric and psychological model into a comparative pathworking system.

### Kenneth Grant

Constructed the qliphothic “Nightside,” Gamaliel’s lunar-sexual imagery, and its tunnels.

## Scholars

### Gershom Scholem

Established the principal historical framework for early Kabbalah, Zoharic authorship, and the history of symbolism. Some conclusions, especially the single-author Zohar model, have since been refined.

### Moshe Idel

Critiqued overly linear and exclusively theosophical histories, emphasizing ecstatic, linguistic, magical, and experiential Kabbalah.

### Elliot R. Wolfson

Analyzed circumcision, phallic symbolism, gender, vision, and the divine name. Particularly important for avoiding sanitized summaries of Yesod.

### Joseph Dan

Provided histories of Jewish mysticism that distinguish *Sefer Yetzirah*, early Kabbalah, Zoharic literature, Christian Cabala, and modern occultism.

### Peter Hayman

Produced the first comprehensive critical synoptic edition of *Sefer Yetzirah*’s principal early recensions.

### Daniel C. Matt

Produced the annotated Pritzker Zohar translation, integrating textual witnesses and modern scholarship.

### J. H. Chajes

Reconstructed the history of *ilanot*, showing that kabbalistic trees are historically changing material artifacts rather than a single immemorial diagram. See [Ilanot: Maps of God](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/site/) and [*The Kabbalistic Tree*](https://www.psupress.org/books/titles/978-0-271-09345-1.html).

## Diagrams and manuscripts

### Earliest manuscript diagrams

The earliest surviving kabbalistic manuscripts, copied in Rome in the late thirteenth century, contain diagrams including a “Tree of Wisdom.” Fourteenth-century kabbalists increasingly produced arboreal maps on parchment.

See:

- [“The First Kabbalistic Tree?”](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/site/?p=927)
- [“Kabbalistic Trees in Italy”](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/site/?p=585)

### *Portae Lucis*, 1516

Likely the first printed kabbalistic tree image. It helped transform a manuscript practice into a reproducible European emblem.

### Kircher’s plate

Kircher’s 1652–1654 plate gave the Western occult tradition its most influential path topology.

### Lurianic *ilanot*

Lurianic trees could be extremely long scrolls showing:

- Adam Kadmon
- Circles and straightness
- Partzufim
- Worlds
- Divine names
- Copulation and gestation
- Light, vessels, shattering, and repair

They are not merely alternative decorative versions of Kircher’s diagram.

### Golden Dawn colored trees

Golden Dawn manuscripts and later Regardie publications used four trees or four color scales. Yesod changes from indigo through violet and dark purple to citrine flecked with azure, representing manifestation through the worlds.

### Tarot artists

- Pamela Colman Smith visualized the Golden Dawn-derived four Nines in the Rider–Waite–Smith deck.
- Lady Frieda Harris visualized Crowley’s revised titles Strength, Happiness, Cruelty, and Gain in the Thoth deck.

Fortune’s novels, particularly *The Sea Priestess* and *Moon Magic*, dramatize lunar, sexual, dream, and astral themes closely related to her Yesod doctrine.

### Alan Moore and J. H. Williams III

*Promethea* issue 14 constructs a narrative ascent through the Tree. Yesod is treated through sexual imagination, dream, and symbolic formation. **[MODERN]** These comics are creative Hermetic reception, not sources for Jewish Kabbalah.

# Controversies and disputes

## 1. Dating *Sefer Yetzirah*

**Traditional claim:** Abraham authored or received it.

**Documented evidence:** Medieval manuscripts and tenth-century commentaries.

**Scholarly reconstruction:** A late-antique or early-medieval layered work.

**Open dispute:** Its earliest recoverable context, the relative priority of recensions, and how much derives from late-antique cosmology, Jewish letter speculation, magic, or philosophical science.

## 2. Is Yesod in *Sefer Yetzirah*?

**Popular claim:** *Sefer Yetzirah* teaches the modern ten named sefirot, including Yesod as ninth.

**Finding:** It teaches ten *sefirot belimah* but does not name them Keter through Malkhut.

**Source of confusion:** Later kabbalists read their own tenfold system through the older text; Westcott then appended the medieval Thirty-Two Paths list.

## 3. Was Yesod always ninth?

No.

**[TEXT/SCHOLARSHIP]** The *Bahir* supplies the Tzaddik/foundation/pillar complex, but some early systems count the relevant potency seventh. Its ninth position was consolidated in thirteenth-century theosophical Kabbalah.

## 4. Zohar authorship

- **[TRADITION]** Shimon bar Yoḥai and companions, second century.
- **[SCHOLARSHIP]** Late thirteenth-century Castile, with Moses de León central.
- **[CURRENT REFINEMENT]** Multiple strata and a literary circle rather than a single homogeneous forgery.

Calling the Zohar either “simply second-century” or “simply a hoax by one man” erases the actual evidence and complexity.

## 5. Jewish sexuality versus modern “sex magic”

**[TEXT]** Yesod is explicitly genital, phallic, reproductive, and covenantal in medieval Jewish sources.

**[QUALIFICATION]** This does not make medieval Kabbalah identical with modern sexual magic. Circumcision, marital law, procreation, emission, divine union, Sabbath, and patriarchal lineage structure the Jewish symbolism.

**[MODERN]** Golden Dawn, Crowley, Fortune, Grant, and later practitioners reconfigure that symbolism through astral theory, Tantra, psychoanalysis, antinomianism, and ritual sex.

## 6. Moon attribution

**Hermetic standard:** Yesod = Moon.

**Jewish complication:** The Moon often symbolizes Malkhut/Shekhinah receiving the Sun/Tiferet. Yesod is the channel between them.

**Historical explanation:** Planetary ladder tables place Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, and Moon successively on the lower celestial sefirot, leaving Earth/Malkhut below. That table became especially stable through Christian Cabala and ceremonial magic.

## 7. Gabriel and Kerubim

**Popular claim:** Jewish Kabbalah universally assigns Gabriel and Kerubim to Yesod.

**Finding:** Jewish sources contain Gabriel, Kerubim, dreams, covenant, and lunar imagery, but the fixed one-to-one table is especially clear in Reuchlin, Agrippa, and Golden Dawn transmission.

**Absence of evidence:** The examined *Bahir*, Gikatilla, and Cordovero passages do not present the entire later package in one table.

## 8. “Shaddai El Chai”

Gikatilla and Cordovero discuss Shaddai, El Chai, and sometimes El Shaddai in connected ways.

The Golden Dawn fixes **Shaddai El Chai** as a compound name.

**Conclusion:** The components are medieval Jewish; the exact standardized formula is characteristically Hermetic.

## 9. Kircher versus Jewish trees

Kircher’s diagram is historically influential, not uniquely authoritative.

Jewish *ilanot* may differ in:

- Number and direction of visible channels
- Position of sefirot
- Inclusion of partzufim
- Circular versus linear structures
- Letter placement
- Treatment of Daʿat
- Whether a line is counted as a “path”

The Golden Dawn’s tarot overlay works within its own ritual system. It does not establish what the Ari or Gra “really meant.”

## 10. Tarot attribution

The Hebrew-letter/tarot system is early modern and occult:

- Court de Gébelin/de Mellet proposed ancient connections.
- Lévi popularized one arrangement.
- Golden Dawn created another.
- Crowley revised Heh/Tzaddi.
- Case and Waite retained other variants.

No medieval Jewish source mentions tarot.

## 11. “Pure Intelligence”

The “Pure Intelligence” text is traditionally presented as the Yetziratic description of Yesod.

**Finding:** It belongs to a later Thirty-Two Paths list printed with Westcott’s translation. It is not the ninth named sefirah in the oldest recensions of *Sefer Yetzirah*.

## 12. Gamaliel and “Obscene Ones”

The equation Gamaliel = ninth qliphothic order is documented in Western occult tables.

The translation “Obscene Ones” is philologically doubtful.

The full tenfold inverse tree evolved through Latin Cabala and Golden Dawn synthesis; it should not be projected unchanged into Zoharic or Lurianic Judaism.

## 13. Lilith’s placement

Sources variously present Lilith as:

- Demoness of the Other Side
- Consort of Samael
- Tenth qliphothic order
- Infernal ruler associated with the ninth
- Ruler of Gamaliel in modern practice

A clean “Gamaliel—Lilith” pair is useful within later occultism but historically composite.

## 14. Daʿat

[*Sefer Yetzirah*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah.1) insists:

> “Ten and not nine; ten and not eleven.”

Cordovero counts Keter and excludes Daʿat as an independent eleventh sefirah: [*Pardes Rimmonim* 3:3](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.3.3.7?lang=bi).

Other systems count Daʿat when Keter is concealed. Lurianic partzufim contain internal states of Daʿat without thereby making eleven simultaneously independent sefirot.

**Conclusion:** Daʿat is a real kabbalistic category but not normally an eleventh sefirah added to a complete ten. Occult diagrams that draw it as an extra circle represent one pedagogical convention.

## 15. Christian appropriation

Pico, Reuchlin, Ricius, Kircher, and Rosenroth preserved and disseminated important Jewish material. They also:

- Read it Christologically
- Detached it from halakhic and Jewish communal settings
- Claimed it confirmed Christian doctrines
- Sometimes used conversionary aims
- Combined it with Hermetism and magic

Both preservation and appropriation are historically true. One does not cancel the other.

# Open questions

1. **Earliest fixed ninth-position witness.** A manuscript-level study could more precisely identify the earliest surviving text or diagram in which Yesod is unambiguously numbered ninth.

2. **Formation of the angelic table.** Reuchlin clearly transmits Kerubim at nine, but the exact Jewish manuscript sources behind his complete hierarchy deserve closer reconstruction.

3. **Moon versus Malkhut.** More work is needed to trace when the planetary ladder decisively shifted lunar imagery from a predominantly Shekhinah/Malkhut context to Yesod in Christian tables.

4. **The qliphothic names.** Several names in the ten-order list are textually corrupt. A new philological edition comparing Hebrew, Latin, German, and Golden Dawn witnesses would be valuable.

5. **Gamaliel’s gloss.** No adequate Hebrew etymology for “Obscene Ones” was found. The route by which this gloss arose remains uncertain.

6. **Lilith’s ruler-status.** The precise first printed table unequivocally making Lilith ruler of Gamaliel, rather than tenth order or general demoness, remains to be established.

7. **Vital’s textual layers.** Yesod passages should be compared across Vital’s notebooks, Shmuel Vital’s arrangements, and Meir Poppers’s *Derekh Etz Chaim* redaction.

8. **Gra and Ari diagrams.** Modern diagrams bearing these names should be traced edition by edition instead of being treated as two invariant originals.

9. **Oral pathworking.** No evidence was found that Golden Dawn-style guided journeys through tarot-letter paths existed in medieval Jewish Kabbalah. Earlier contemplative letter and divine-name practices are documented, but direct continuity remains unproved.

10. **“Treasure House of Images.”** The exact pre-Fortune genealogy of this English title—from astral-light literature, Neoplatonic storehouse imagery, Golden Dawn papers, and psychological theory—deserves a separate source study.

11. **“Machinery of the Universe.”** It is securely attested in Golden Dawn/Crowley-derived tables. No medieval Jewish source examined uses an equivalent fixed title for Yesod.
