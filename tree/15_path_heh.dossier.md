# The Path of Heh: Research Dossier

## Method and evidentiary labels

This dossier uses the following labels throughout:

- **[Documented text]** — directly attested in the cited primary source.
- **[Scholarly reconstruction]** — a historical or philological conclusion advanced by modern scholarship.
- **[Kabbalistic tradition]** — a religious attribution accepted within one or more Jewish kabbalistic traditions, regardless of modern historical dating.
- **[Hermetic-occult attribution]** — an attribution made within Christian Cabala, Hermetic Qabalah, the Golden Dawn, Thelema, or a derivative occult school.
- **[Disputed]** — the witnesses, recensions, scholars, or occult authorities disagree.
- **[Modern invention]** — an attribution first documented in modern occultism and not found in the relevant ancient or medieval Jewish texts.
- **[Negative finding]** — the claimed association was not found in the cited primary text or belongs to a different textual layer.

The essential historical distinction is this:

> The letter **Heh**, its cosmological use in *Sefer Yetzirah*, and its symbolism in Jewish Kabbalah are old; “Path 15, Chokmah–Tiphareth, Emperor, four colour scales” is an early-modern and nineteenth-century Hermetic construction.

# The name

## Hebrew spelling and pronunciation

The square-script form is **ה**. The letter’s name occurs as הֵא, הֵי, or modern הֵא/הֵי, conventionally transliterated *he*, *heh*, or *hey*. In Tiberian and modern Hebrew it ordinarily denotes /h/, though word-final Heh may instead mark a vowel or serve as *mater lectionis*.

**[Documented linguistic fact]** Heh is the fifth letter and consequently has the ordinary gematria value **5**. Its Phoenician ancestor is conventionally transcribed 𐤄; Greek adapted the cognate sign as epsilon when the Semitic consonantal value was no longer needed.

## Does Heh mean “window”?

“Window” is the standard Golden Dawn, BOTA, and modern occult gloss. It supplied an attractive symbolism: Heh as an aperture through which light enters, hence revelation, sight, and the “opening of lights.”

**[Disputed]** This is not a secure historical etymology. The original names of the early Northwest Semitic letters are reconstructed imperfectly. Proposed ancestors of Heh include a human figure with raised arms and other pictograms; “window” is a traditional pedagogical interpretation rather than a demonstrated Proto-Sinaitic word-history. Unicode’s Phoenician chart establishes the historical sign-equivalence, but not “window” as the original pictorial meaning. See the [Unicode Phoenician names list](https://www.unicode.org/charts/nameslist/n_10900.html), the [Unicode Hebrew history discussion](https://unicode.org/versions/Unicode17.0.0/core-spec/chapter-10/), and the palaeographic discussion in this [Heritage Science study](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40494-023-00920-9).

“Breath” is likewise a powerful later homiletic meaning, not the lexical etymology of the letter-name.

## Shape history

**[Scholarly reconstruction]**

1. Proposed Proto-Sinaitic/Proto-Canaanite forms are pictographic and variable.
2. Phoenician **𐤄** regularized the sign as one member of a consonantal alphabet.
3. Paleo-Hebrew used regional descendants of that Phoenician form.
4. Imperial Aramaic altered and simplified its strokes.
5. Judeans gradually adopted the Aramaic-derived “Jewish” or “Assyrian” script, כתב אשורי, particularly in the Second Temple period.
6. The resulting square-script form became **ה**.

The lower-left stroke of square Heh remains detached. That gap became exegetically productive in rabbinic literature: it made Heh an image of the world as a structure with an opening for departure and another for return.

For the shift from Paleo-Hebrew to square/Aramaic script, see the [Bar-Ilan palaeographic study](https://cris.biu.ac.il/ws/portalfiles/portal/55985668/3511_Article_Text_15900_1_10_20171121.pdf).

## Heh in divine names

Heh occurs twice in the Tetragrammaton, **יהוה**:

- First Heh: commonly correlated with **Binah**, the supernal Mother.
- Vav: the six central or “male” sefirot, frequently personified as Zeʿir Anpin.
- Final Heh: **Malkhut/Shekhinah**, the receptive or manifest kingdom.

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** Cordovero gives the compact rule:

> “When it comes at the beginning of a word, it hints at Binah; at the end … Malkhut.”

The Hebrew is:

> הא — עצמה, בבואה בראש תיבה, הרמז לבינה. ובבואה בסוף תיבה, הרמז למלכות.

See [*Or Ne’erav*, Part VII, “Letter Heh”](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_VII%2C_Letter_Heh).

Vital’s *Etz Chaim* likewise says that Yod corresponds to Chokhmah, the first Heh to Binah, and the final Heh to Malkhut or the mouth/speech of the relevant *partzuf*. See [*Etz Chaim* 1:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.5).

This Tetragrammaton symbolism is Jewish and predates the Golden Dawn. It does not, however, turn Heh into a Chokhmah–Tiphareth path.

## Biblical prooftexts

None of the following verses names “Path 15.” They became components of broader sephirotic and alphabetic exegesis.

### 1 Chronicles 29:11

> לך יהוה הגדלה והגבורה והתפארת והנצח וההוד כי כל בשמים ובארץ לך יהוה הממלכה

“Yours, YHWH, are greatness, power, beauty, victory, and splendour … Yours is the kingdom.”

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** The verse supplied names or prooftexts for the seven lower sefirot: Gedullah/Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, Netzach, Hod, *kol* as Yesod, and Mamlakhah/Malkhut. It is background to the Tree’s vocabulary, not the origin of Heh’s occult path. [Hebrew and translations](https://www.sefaria.org/I_Chronicles.29.11).

### Proverbs 3:19

> יהוה בחכמה יסד־ארץ כונן שמים בתבונה

“YHWH founded the earth by Wisdom; He established the heavens by Understanding.”

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** This became a major prooftext for Chokhmah and Binah and is relevant to Heh through its first-Heh/Binah symbolism. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.3.19).

### Job 28

Job asks, “Wisdom—from where shall it be found?” The chapter associates wisdom with hidden cosmic routes.

**[Kabbalistic reception]** It informed medieval speculation concerning divine wisdom and its paths, though it does not enumerate a Fifteenth Path. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.28).

### Job 38:9

> בשומי ענן לבשו וערפל חתלתו

“When I made cloud its garment and thick darkness its swaddling-band.”

**[Documented medieval path text]** This is the explicit prooftext behind the Fifteenth “Constituting Intelligence,” not Job 28. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.38.9).

### Isaiah 26:4

> כי ביה יהוה צור עולמים

**[Documented rabbinic exegesis]** The unusual divine form ביה, *be-Yah*, supports the teaching that the worlds were created with Yod and Heh. See [Isaiah 26:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.26.4) and *Menachot* 29b below.

### Isaiah 57:15

The descriptions “High and Exalted,” “dwelling forever,” and “Holy is His name” contributed to the divine-title catalogue at the opening of *Sefer Yetzirah*. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.57.15).

# The Jewish tradition

## 1. *Sefer Yetzirah*

### Date and textual history

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** The work is attributed to Abraham; other transmission traditions connect it with Rabbi Akiva.

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** Proposed dates range widely. Much of twentieth-century scholarship placed its formation between approximately the third and sixth centuries CE. Peter Hayman’s critical work emphasizes that the earliest recoverable manuscript state appears only around the early tenth century and that the witnesses are too unstable to reconstruct a single pristine “original.” Tzahi Weiss and others have argued for still later stages of composition or redaction. The traditional Abrahamic attribution is therefore a religious attribution, not a historical dating.

See:

- [Peter Hayman, *Sefer Yeṣira: Edition, Translation and Text-Critical Commentary*](https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/sefer-yesira-9783161587955/)
- [Jewish Encyclopedia, “Yezirah, Sefer”](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/15084-yezirah-sefer)
- [Sefaria’s textual overview](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah.1-6)

### The thirty-two paths

The opening equation is:

> בשלשים ושתים נתיבות פליאות חכמה … בעשר ספירות בלימה ועשרים ושתים אותיות יסוד

“In thirty-two wondrous paths of Wisdom … through ten sefirot belimah and twenty-two foundation letters.”

**[Documented text]** The thirty-two paths are thus:

- Ten *sefirot belimah*.
- Twenty-two foundation letters.
- Three mothers: אמש.
- Seven doubles: בגדכפרת.
- Twelve simples: הוזחטילנסעצק.

The earliest text does not draw the familiar Kircher tree. The twelve simple letters are also described as twelve diagonal boundaries of cosmic space. Turning every letter into one of twenty-two lines between the later named sefirot is a diagrammatic interpretation, not a sentence found in *Sefer Yetzirah*.

### Heh’s class

> שתים עשרה פשוטות ה ו ז ח ט י ל נ ס ע צ ק

**[Documented text]** Heh is the first of the twelve simple letters. See [*Sefer Yetzirah*, Gra version, chapter 5](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah_Gra_Version.5).

The twelve simples govern twelve faculties, zodiacal constellations, months, and bodily “directors.” The precise matching varies by recension and commentary.

### The major recension variants

| Witness/tradition | Faculty | Sign | Month | Body |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Gra recension | Speech | Aries | Nisan | Right foot |
| Common/long recension | Speech | Aries | Nisan | Liver |
| Saadia recension | Sight | Aries | Nisan | Liver |
| Short recension/Raavad reconstruction | Sight | Aries | Nisan | Right hand |
| Cordoverian/Ramak table | Sight | Aries | Nisan | Right hand |
| Golden Dawn | Sight | Aries | Nisan retained indirectly | Head/face through zodiacal anatomy |

The Gra text reads:

> המליך אות ה׳ בשיחה … וצר בו טלה בעולם וניסן בשנה ורגל ימין בנפש

“He made Heh king over speech … and formed Aries in the universe, Nisan in the year, and the right foot in the soul.”

See [*Sefer Yetzirah*, Gra 5:10](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah_Gra_Version.5.10) and Aryeh Kaplan’s comparative recension table, p. 219, in his [*Sefer Yetzirah* edition](https://leonbahrmanministries.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Aryeh-Kaplan-Sefer-Yetzirah2.pdf).

**Finding:** Aries and Nisan are comparatively stable; the faculty and organ are not. Statements that *Sefer Yetzirah* simply assigns Heh to “sight” or “the right foot” without naming the recension suppress real textual variation.

### Saadia, Gra, and “Ari” versions

- **Saadia Gaon:** Heh—sight—Aries—Nisan—liver.
- **Gra/Vilna Gaon:** Heh—speech—Aries—Nisan—right foot.
- **Ari recension:** this label is used for a recension received through Lurianic circles. It should not be assumed that Isaac Luria personally composed every wording or diagram printed under that designation.
- **Cordovero:** normally follows sight/right hand in comparative tables.

**[Disputed]** “Saadia,” “Gra,” and “Ari” can designate textual recension, commentary, or diagrammatic tradition. Modern occult tables frequently conflate these categories.

## 2. The Thirty-two Paths appendix and the “Constituting Intelligence”

The familiar sentence about Path 15 does **not** belong to the early chapters of *Sefer Yetzirah*. It comes from a separate medieval tract, often printed as the “Thirty-two Paths of Wisdom.”

The Hebrew is commonly transmitted approximately as:

> הנתיב החמשה עשר נקרא שכל מעמיד, מפני שהוא מעמיד עצם הבריאה בערפלי טוהר…

“The fifteenth path is called the Establishing Intelligence, because it establishes the substance of creation in pure obscurity…”

It then invokes Job 38:9.

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** This tract has been associated with the thirteenth-century Castilian “Circle of Contemplation.” Its literary history is separate from that of *Sefer Yetzirah*, even when printers or translators bind the texts together. See the [Journal of the Western Mystery Tradition study](https://hermetic.com/jwmt/v1n3/32paths).

William Wynn Westcott explicitly acknowledged that his English “Thirty-Two Paths” came from the Hebrew printed by Johannes Stephanus Rittangelius in 1642. His version reads, in part:

> “The Fifteenth Path is the Constituting Intelligence…”

and speaks of creation in “pure darkness.” See Westcott, *Sepher Yetzirah* (1893), pp. 27–29, in the [IAPSOP scan](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1893__westcott___sepher_yetzirah.pdf).

Thus:

- **Heh as a simple letter, Aries and Nisan:** *Sefer Yetzirah*.
- **“Fifteenth Constituting Intelligence”:** separate medieval path tract.
- **Heh = that Intelligence = Chokhmah–Tiphareth:** Hermetic diagrammatic synthesis.

## 3. Rabbinic lore: this world created with Heh

*Menachot* 29b interprets Genesis 2:4:

> אל תקרי בהבראם אלא בה״י בראם

“Do not read *behibbar’am* but *be-Heh bera’am*: He created them with Heh.”

The passage links:

- This world with Heh.
- The world to come with Yod.
- The lower opening of Heh with the ease of leaving the moral order.
- The smaller upper opening with the return of the penitent.

See [*Menachot* 29b](https://www.sefaria.org/Menachot.29b.1-17).

**[Documented rabbinic text]** This is genuine Heh lore, but it knows nothing of Aries, the Emperor, Chokhmah–Tiphareth, or Path 15.

Genesis Rabbah and later commentators add that Heh is an “easy” or breath-like letter. This produces the homily that creation required no exertion. It is a theological reading of its articulation, not the historical etymology of the name.

## 4. Abraham and Sarah

Genesis 17 changes אברם, Abram, to אברהם, Abraham, adding Heh; Sarai likewise becomes Sarah. The *Bahir* interprets Abraham’s added Heh numerically: Abraham’s name totals 248, corresponding to the traditional count of bodily limbs or commandments.

See [*Bahir* §8/28](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir.28).

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** Heh therefore becomes a sign of fecundity, covenant, completeness, and expanded capacity. It is not presented there as the Emperor’s path.

## 5. *Sefer ha-Bahir*

### Date

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** Attributed to the first-century sage Neḥunya ben ha-Qanah.

**[Scholarly reconstruction]** The received book emerged in Provence in the late twelfth century, incorporating older materials.

The *Bahir* contains:

- The Abraham/Heh interpretation.
- Speculation on upper and lower Heh.
- Numerical and bodily symbolism.
- Early sephirotic and divine-name structures.

See [*Bahir* §106](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir.106).

**[Negative finding]** It does not identify a Chokhmah–Tiphareth line as “the Path of Heh,” nor does it supply the Golden Dawn correspondence set.

## 6. Alphabet midrash and the letters petitioning God

### *Otiyyot de-Rabbi Akiva*

This is a family of medieval alphabet midrashim transmitted under Rabbi Akiva’s name. Individual letters are expanded through acrostic, numerical, graphic, and divine-name interpretations. There are multiple recensions, so a single canonical “Heh chapter” should not be presumed. See [*Otiot de-Rabbi Akiva* in *Otzar Midrashim*](https://www.sefaria.org/Otzar_Midrashim,_Otiot_D'Rabbi_Akiva.1).

### The Zohar’s petition of the letters

At the beginning of the *Zohar*, the letters approach God in reverse order, each asking that creation begin with it. Bet is chosen because it begins ברכה, blessing.

**[Documented Zoharic narrative]** Heh receives no independent Path-15 teaching. Heh and Vav are retained within the divine Name rather than made the initial of creation. See [*Zohar*, Introduction](https://www.sefaria.org/Zohar%2C_Introduction.1.1-34.260).

The letter-contest belongs to the larger alphabet-midrash genre. It should not be attributed indiscriminately to *Otiyyot de-Rabbi Akiva* and the *Zohar* as though they were one text.

## 7. The Zohar

### Authorship

- **[Kabbalistic tradition]** Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and his circle in second-century Galilee.
- **[Scholarly reconstruction]** The central body emerged in late-thirteenth-century Castile, with Moses de León a principal author or redactor; current scholarship often speaks of multiple strata and a literary circle rather than one simple authorial event.

See the [Pritzker Edition’s publisher page](https://www.sup.org/books/cite/?id=4810) and Yehuda Liebes’s [study of Zoharic authorship](https://web.nli.org.il/sites/NLI/English/collections/jewish-collection/scholem/scholem-card-catalog/Documents/Yehuda-Liebes/ZOHAR-Yehuda-Liebes.pdf).

### Heh in the Zoharic system

Heh is treated primarily through:

- The two Hehs of YHWH.
- Binah and Malkhut.
- Mother and Daughter.
- Upper and lower worlds.
- Speech and manifestation.
- The conjunction or separation of the letters of the divine Name.

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** These are theosophical and exegetical letter-symbols. No securely identified Zohar passage establishes “Heh = fifteenth line from Chokhmah to Tiferet.”

## 8. Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah*

Joseph Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah*, composed around 1290, organizes divine names and scriptural language through the ten sefirot.

For Heh:

- The final Heh of YHWH signifies Malkhut.
- The upper and lower Hehs link Binah and Malkhut through the middle configuration.
- Tiferet is closely associated with YHWH in Gikatilla’s system.
- Chokhmah is associated with Yah.

See:

- [*Shaʿarei Orah*, Fifth Gate](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha%27arei_Orah%2C_Fifth_Gate%2C_Sixth_Sefirah.198)
- [*Shaʿarei Orah*, Eighth Gate](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha%27arei_Orah%2C_Eight_Gate%2C_Third_Sefirah.22)

**[Negative finding]** Gikatilla does not give Heh the later Golden Dawn archangel, angelic order, red colour scales, perfume, stone, Tarot card, or path geometry.

## 9. Cordovero: *Pardes Rimmonim* and *Or Ne’erav*

Moses Cordovero completed *Pardes Rimmonim* in Safed in 1548. It systematizes earlier Kabbalah, divine names, letter permutations, paths, and sephirotic relations.

**[Documented text]** Cordovero discusses the letters in multiple organizational schemes. In *Or Ne’erav*, Heh at the beginning of a word points to Binah and at the end to Malkhut. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne%27erav%2C_PART_VII%2C_Letter_Heh).

**[Disputed diagram history]** Posthumous printed editions of *Pardes* contain Tree diagrams with differing numbers and arrangements of lines. The letters are not consistently printed on those lines. It is therefore unsafe to call the Kircher diagram “Cordovero’s tree.”

The Hebrew text is available at [Sefaria’s *Pardes Rimmonim*](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim).

## 10. Luria through Ḥayyim Vital

Isaac Luria, the Ari (1534–1572), left no single authorized book called *Etz Chaim*. The received work is chiefly a redaction of his teachings by Ḥayyim Vital and subsequent editors.

Its major sequence is:

1. **Tzimtzum:** the initial withdrawal or concealment of infinite light.
2. **Kav:** a line or ray entering the vacated conceptual space.
3. **Adam Kadmon:** primordial configuration.
4. **Akudim, Nekudim, Berudim:** successively differentiated configurations.
5. **Shevirat ha-kelim:** the breaking of the vessels in the world of Nekudim.
6. **Partzufim:** reconfiguration of isolated points into relational “faces.”
7. **Tikkun:** repair, integration, and elevation of sparks.
8. **Four worlds:** Atzilut, Beriah, Yetzirah, and Asiyah, often recursively interpenetrating.

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** The Tetragrammaton is a principal structural key: Yod/Abba/Chokhmah; first Heh/Imma/Binah; Vav/Zeʿir Anpin; final Heh/Nukva or Malkhut. [*Etz Chaim* 1:5](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim.1.5).

**[Negative finding]** Vital’s *Etz Chaim* does not teach the Golden Dawn bundle “Path 15 = Heh = Aries = Emperor = Chokhmah–Tiphareth.” Its “lines,” *partzufim*, and divine-name permutations belong to a different conceptual and diagrammatic grammar.

| Tree | Heh commonly joins | Status |

| Gra letter-tree | Keter–Chokhmah | Later Jewish diagrammatic tradition |
| Kircher/Golden Dawn | Chokhmah–Tiphareth | Christian-Hermetic diagram |

## 12. Hasidic readings

### *Tanya*

There is no chapter of *Tanya* devoted to a “Path of Heh.”

The major Heh discussion is *Iggeret ha-Kodesh* 5. It explains that:

- Breath supplies the material basis of articulated letters.
- Heh is especially “light” or effortless.
- This world was created through Heh.
- The shape of the final/lower Heh signifies the extension of divine kingship through dimensions and directions.

See [*Tanya*, Part IV, *Iggeret ha-Kodesh* 5](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.5).

**[Documented Hasidic text]** This develops rabbinic and Lurianic letter theology. It is not an Aries–Emperor pathworking.

### Naḥman of Bratslav

Rabbi Naḥman frequently interprets letters, speech, breath, divine names, Torah, and the reconstruction of fallen language.

**[Negative finding]** No standard Breslov locus was found that treats “Path 15 of Heh” as a named doctrine. Presentations claiming a Rebbe Naḥman teaching about the Kircher line should supply a precise *Liqutei Moharan* citation.

## 13. Prayer, Torah, calendar, and Omer

### Prayer

Heh appears continually in:

- יהוה, conventionally read *Adonai* in prayer.
- יה, *Yah*.
- ה׳, a written circumlocution for the divine Name.
- Lurianic *kavvanot* using the four letters of YHWH.

The first Heh/Binah and final Heh/Malkhut symbolism affects many kabbalistic prayer intentions.

**[Negative finding]** There is no universally normative Jewish prayer assigned to a “fifteenth path between Chokhmah and Tiferet.”

### Torah

Particularly important loci are:

- Genesis 2:4, בהבראם, interpreted as “with Heh He created them.”
- Genesis 17:5, Abram becoming Abraham.
- The two Hehs of YHWH.
- Isaiah 26:4, Yah and the creation of two worlds.

### Calendar, sign, and tribe

- Heh—Aries—Nisan is explicitly *Sefer Yetzirah*.
- **Judah** is the dominant later Jewish tribe-month correspondence for Nisan, based on the order of encampment, offerings, and royal/Aries symbolism.
- Crowley’s *777* instead prints **Gad** in the relevant zodiacal row.

Thus “Heh = Judah” is not in the base *Sefer Yetzirah* sentence, while “Heh = Gad” belongs to an occult table and is not the standard Jewish Nisan attribution.

### Counting the Omer

Kabbalistic Omer practice normally maps the forty-nine days to seven lower sefirot, each included in the others. It does not map the days to the twenty-two Kircher paths.

**[Negative finding]** Heh’s numerical value 5 does not make the fifth day of the Omer “the Path of Heh.” The fifth Omer day in the standard sephirotic pattern is *Hod she-be-Ḥesed*. See [Hebcal’s Omer overview](https://lib.hebcal.co.il/events/counting-the-omer.html).

# The Hermetic and occult tradition

## 1. Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Pico’s *Conclusiones nongentae* included seventy-two Cabalistic conclusions. They Christianized Jewish kabbalistic materials, using divine-name speculation to argue for Christian doctrines.

**[Documented text]** Pico knew the thirty-two paths and letter techniques in a general Cabalistic context. See the [Latin *Conclusiones*](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Conclusiones_philosophicae%2C_cabalisticae_et_theologicae).

**[Negative finding]** Pico does not attest:

- “Path of Heh” as Chokhmah–Tiphareth.
- Heh as Aries on a drawn Tree.
- Heh as the Emperor.
- Four colour scales.
- Hemethterith.

Calling Pico an authority for the Golden Dawn Path 15 retrospectively projects a nineteenth-century system into 1486.

## 2. Reuchlin, 1517

Johannes Reuchlin’s *De arte cabalistica* presents Christian Cabala through Hebrew letters, divine names, number, and contemplative philosophy. The work is available in the [Badische Landesbibliothek scan](https://digital.blb-karlsruhe.de/blbihd/content/titleinfo/7174105).

A Latin letter discussion begins:

> “He littera quinta… cuius haec forma est: ה…”

It notices that Heh appears twice in the divine Name, associates one Heh with Binah, and comments upon the letter’s light aspiration. See the [Hebrew University-hosted excerpt](https://liebes.huji.ac.il/yehudaliebes/files/scechina_4.1.pdf).

**[Documented Christian Cabala]** Reuchlin’s Heh is a divine-name and letter-theology symbol.

**[Negative finding]** Reuchlin does not supply the Kircher/Golden Dawn Chokhmah–Tiphareth Heh path or a Tarot attribution.

## 3. *Portae Lucis*, 1516

Paulus Riccius’s Latin translation of Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah* appeared as *Portae Lucis* in Augsburg in 1516. Its title-page woodcut, attributed to Leonhard Beck, is an important early printed Christian-Cabalistic Tree.

See the [1516 title page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portae_Lucis_1516.jpg).

**[Documented artifact]** It is a milestone in diagram history.

**[Negative finding]** Its image is not the Golden Dawn’s fully lettered twenty-two-path Kircher arrangement.

## 4. Athanasius Kircher, 1652–1654

Kircher’s *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, volume II.1, is the decisive early printed witness for the diagram later adopted by the Golden Dawn. His Tree assigns Hebrew letters to twenty-two connecting lines. Heh occupies the Chokhmah–Tiphareth diagonal.

The plate can be examined in the [Cornell digital collection](https://digital.library.cornell.edu/catalog/ss:550272).

Kircher’s Latin Fifteenth Path reads:

> “Decima quinta semita dicitur Intelligentia constitutiua…”

and continues that it “constitutes creation in pure obscurity,” citing Job 38:9. See [Kircher’s Latin text, p. 314](https://www.esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm).

A close gloss is:

> “The fifteenth path is called the Constituting Intelligence, because it establishes creation in pure darkness.”

Kircher adds *semita apertionis luminum*, “the path of the opening of lights.”

**[Documented Christian Cabala]** This is the first securely identified source in the present dossier that combines the numbered Intelligence text with the early-modern diagrammatic environment from which modern Path 15 developed.

**[Disputed]** Kircher was synthesizing Jewish, Christian, Neoplatonic, astrological, and antiquarian materials. It does not follow that a pre-existing Jewish school used his exact letter-map.

## 5. Knorr von Rosenroth, 1677–1684

Christian Knorr von Rosenroth’s *Kabbala Denudata* translated and anthologized Zoharic, Cordoverian, Lurianic, and Herrera materials in Latin. It became a principal route by which later European esotericists encountered Kabbalah.

See:

- [E-rara’s *Kabbala Denudata* volumes](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/10759091)
- [Bill Heidrick’s volume index](https://www.billheidrick.com/Orpd/KRKD/index.htm)

**[Documented reception history]** Rosenroth helped make Jewish theosophical terminology available to Christian readers.

**[Negative finding]** The consulted witnesses do not establish Rosenroth as the inventor of Heh = Chokhmah–Tiphareth or Heh = Emperor. Those are respectively Kircher-diagrammatic and later Tarot developments.

## 6. Lévi and the Tarot

Éliphas Lévi’s *Dogme et rituel de la haute magie* appeared in 1854–1856. He treated the twenty-two Tarot trumps as corresponding to the twenty-two Hebrew letters and organized his work around that analogy. See the [French scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dogme_et_rituel_de_la_haute_magie_%28IA_b24884340_0001%29.pdf) and an [English translation of the Tarot discussion](https://www.rahoorkhuit.net/library/ceremonial/classics/pdf/transcendental/dogma_et_rituel_part_2.pdf).

Lévi’s basic sequential method was not identical with the Golden Dawn’s:

- Aleph began with trump I, the Magician/Juggler.
- Consequently, Heh tended toward trump V, the Pope/Hierophant, not the Emperor.
- Lévi did not possess the Golden Dawn’s Fool-as-Aleph ordering in its final form.

**[Historical correction]** “Lévi taught Heh = Emperor” is false if it means the mature Golden Dawn assignment. The Golden Dawn’s reshuffling of Tarot order, Hebrew letters, signs, and planets created that equation.

## 7. The Golden Dawn

The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn was founded in 1888 by William Wynn Westcott, Samuel Liddell MacGregor Mathers, and William Robert Woodman.

Its system combined:

- Kircher’s path geometry.
- *Sefer Yetzirah* letter classes and astrological correspondences.
- The later Thirty-two Intelligences.
- Tarot trumps.
- Four Worlds.
- Four colour scales.
- Renaissance and modern magical correspondences.

### Golden Dawn Heh formula

| Category | Golden Dawn attribution |
|---|---|
| Path | 15 |
| Connection | Chokmah–Tiphareth |
| Letter | Heh |
| Letter meaning | Window |
| Sign | Aries |
| Trump | IV, Emperor |
| Title | “Son of the Morning, Chief among the Mighty” |
| Intelligence | Constituting Intelligence |
| Directional quality | Descent from Chokmah toward solar Tiphareth; reciprocal initiatory ascent |
| Colour scales | Scarlet; red; brilliant flame; glowing red |

The Golden Dawn reasoning was synthetic rather than derived from a single ancient source:

1. Heh is fifth letter but fourth trump when the Fool is counted as zero/Aleph.
2. *Sefer Yetzirah* supplies Aries.
3. The Emperor’s ram-headed or ram-throne imagery fits Aries.
4. Kircher supplies the Chokhmah–Tiphareth geometry.
5. The Fifteenth Intelligence supplies the path-title.
6. The four-world colour doctrine produces the four reds.

*Book T* was publicly printed through Crowley’s *Equinox* material; see [*The Equinox* I.8](https://hermetic.com/_media/93beast.fea.st/files/section1/equinox/equinox_i_08_.pdf). Westcott’s path text is in his [1893 *Sepher Yetzirah*](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1893__westcott___sepher_yetzirah.pdf).

### The four colour scales

| World/scale | Heh/Aries colour |
|---|---|
| Atziluth, King Scale | Scarlet |
| Briah, Queen Scale | Red |
| Yetzirah, Prince Scale | Brilliant flame |
| Assiah, Princess Scale | Glowing red |

Some Golden Dawn manuscript descendants give the near-equivalent sequence “blood red, dark rose red, rich red, crimson.” This is a recension difference within occult colour tables, not a Jewish tradition.

## 9. The Goetic quinances

The Golden Dawn/Crowley table divides each zodiacal sign into six five-degree sections. For Aries:

| Aries degrees | Goetic spirit | Conventional number |
|---:|---|---:|
| 0°–5° | Bael | 1 |

## 10. Crowley’s Heh/Tzaddi exchange

*Liber AL vel Legis* I:57 states:

> “All these old letters of my Book are aright; but צ is not the Star.”

Crowley eventually concluded that Tzaddi belonged to the Emperor and Heh to the Star. In *The Book of Thoth* he wrote that “the positions of XVII and IV must be counterchanged.” See *The Book of Thoth*, pp. 9–10 in this [scan](https://zalbarath666.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/07/aleister-crowley-the-book-of-thoth.pdf).

His published reasoning included:

- Tzaddi suggested *tsar*, Caesar, sovereign, and hence Emperor.
- Heh, as a feminine letter of the Tetragrammaton, better suited the female figure of the Star.
- The Aries/Aquarius reversal produced a formal zodiacal symmetry around Pisces, analogous to the Golden Dawn’s Leo/Libra adjustment around Virgo.

### Three ways the exchange is represented

| Interpretation | Path 15 | Path 28 |
|---|---|---|
| Golden Dawn | Heh–Aries–Emperor | Tzaddi–Aquarius–Star |
| Letter/card swap | Heh–Star–Aquarius | Tzaddi–Emperor–Aries |
| Full “counterchanged positions” reading | Heh–Star–Aries on the old Path-15 geometry | Tzaddi–Emperor–Aquarius on Path 28 |

**[Disputed]** Crowley’s text, tables, deck labels, and the phrase “Aries and Aquarius are counterchanged” have generated competing reconstructions. His early *777* remains pre-swap. The mature Thoth presentation most visibly gives Heh to the Star and Tzaddi to the Emperor; whether signs, cards, letters, or geometric paths are the primary fixed units is the unresolved interpretive issue.

## 11. *Liber 231*

*Liber 231*, first printed in *The Equinox*, contains twenty-two paired sigils and oracular texts for the visible and adverse genii of the letter-paths. It predates Crowley’s fully public mature explanation of the Heh/Tzaddi switch.

See the [*Liber 231* scan](https://www.bibliotecapleyades.net/crowley/liber/libers/liber231.pdf).

**[Documented occult text]** The adverse names later used by Kenneth Grant for the Tunnels of Set derive from this complex.

**[Important numbering caution]** The verses are indexed from zero. The verse number is therefore not identical with the Kircher path number. “Verse 15” is not automatically “Path 15/Heh.”

## 12. Dion Fortune

Dion Fortune’s *The Mystical Qabalah* was published in 1935. Its contents include separate chapters on Chokmah and Tiphareth, general chapters on the paths, and a final chapter on the Qliphoth. See the [1935 bibliographic record](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Mystical_Qabalah.html?id=YkCxFg6efM4C) and [table of contents](https://nnty.fun/downloads/books/ETYNTKE/Magical%20Library%20%28UNORGANIZED%29/Magical%20Library/Fortune%2C%20Dion%20-%20The%20Mystical%20Qabalah.pdf).

**[Negative finding]** There is no chapter devoted to “the Sephira of Heh”: Heh is a letter-path, not a sephirah. Fortune’s book discusses the ten spheres individually, not all twenty-two paths in individual chapters. Consequently, no “chapter on this Sephira” exists to reproduce.

Her relevant contribution is methodological: paths represent subjective transitions between objective sephirotic states. Chokhmah is dynamic archetypal force; Tiphareth is the mediating solar center of sacrifice, illumination, and rebirth. A Heh pathworking in her framework would mediate those two ideas, but that synthesis is interpretive rather than a missing Heh chapter.

## 13. Regardie, Case, and Knight

### Israel Regardie

Regardie’s *A Garden of Pomegranates* (1931) and his publication of Golden Dawn documents from 1937 onward spread the Kircher–Golden Dawn tree, path titles, and Tarot correspondences.

**[Hermetic-occult attribution]** Regardie retained Heh–Aries–Emperor in his Golden Dawn expositions. He distinguished practical pathworking from historical Jewish Kabbalah only inconsistently by modern scholarly standards.

### Paul Foster Case

Case and BOTA retained:

- Heh.
- Aries.
- Emperor IV.
- Chokhmah–Tiphareth.
- Constituting Intelligence.

Case emphasized Heh as window, sight, reason, and the organizing authority that frames experience. His treatment of the Fifteenth Intelligence is available in [“The Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom”](https://www.mr-kaplan.com/Library/PFCase/32-Paths-of-Wisdom.html).

**[Documented occult divergence]** Case rejected or ignored Crowley’s Heh/Star reassignment in BOTA’s standard Tarot curriculum.

### Gareth Knight

Knight’s *A Practical Guide to Qabalistic Symbolism*, first published in two volumes in 1965, devotes its second volume to paths and Tarot. Knight explicitly warns that the flashing colours can be arbitrary and treats the system as practical symbolism rather than academic history. See the [publisher/book record](https://books.google.com/books?id=Tw-B_Hzcp9MC&num=20).

## 14. Pathworking traditions

**[Modern occult practice]** Golden Dawn-derived pathworking normally proceeds through:

1. Ritual preparation or banishing.
2. Contemplation of Heh and Aries.
3. Use of the path’s flashing colours.
4. Visualization of the Tarot key as a symbolic doorway.
5. Vibration of relevant divine or angelic names.
6. Imaginal ascent or descent between Chokhmah and Tiphareth.
7. Testing the vision by symbols and correspondences.
8. Written record and later critical review.

Recurring images include:

- A scarlet horizon or fiery desert.
- Ram horns.
- A ruler, architect, or martial initiator.
- A window or opening of light.
- Solar beauty receiving a flash of primordial Wisdom.
- In Thelemic work, the Star, Nuit, Aquarius, or a reversal of the Emperor formula.

**[Negative finding]** Such visionary scripts are not ancient Jewish practices described in *Sefer Yetzirah*, the *Bahir*, or the *Zohar*. Jewish letter meditation, permutation, divine-name contemplation, and Lurianic *kavvanah* are related historically but methodologically distinct.

## 15. Thelemic grades

The A∴A∴ grade structure is attached chiefly to sefiroth:

- Tiphareth: Adeptus Minor, 5=6.
- Chokhmah: Magus, 9=2.

**[Negative finding]** Heh/Path 15 does not possess an independent A∴A∴ grade. It links spheres associated with grades, but the line itself is not “the grade of Heh.”

# The Qliphah or tunnel

## Jewish background versus modern tunnel doctrine

Jewish Kabbalah has extensive doctrines of the *sitra aḥra*, shells, demonic powers, Samael, Lilith, and unholy counterparts. These are not identical with Kenneth Grant’s twenty-two Tunnels of Set.

The tenfold qliphotic names used by the Golden Dawn and Crowley are a Western occult synthesis. Grant’s tunnels are a further twentieth-century construction.

## The ten qliphoth in the Crowley/Golden Dawn table

| Sephirah | Qliphotic order | Common late-occult “prince” |
|---|---|---|
| Keter | Thaumiel | Satan and Moloch |
| Chokhmah | Ghagiel | Beelzebub |
| Binah | Satariel | Lucifuge Rofocale |
| Chesed | Gha’agsheblah/Gha’agsheklah | Astaroth |
| Geburah | Golachab | Asmodeus |
| Tiphareth | Thagirion/Thagiriron | Belphegor |
| Netzach | A’arab Zaraq | Baal |
| Hod | Samael | Adrammelech |
| Yesod | Gamaliel | Lilith |
| Malkhut | Lilith/Nahemoth | Naamah |

**[Hermetic-occult attribution]** Spellings and princes vary among Mathers, Crowley, Regardie, Grant, and later writers. The table is not a stable “classical Jewish list.”

For Path 15 specifically, the adjacent nightside spheres are therefore:

- Ghagiel, associated in this occult table with Beelzebub.
- Thagirion, associated with Belphegor.

Neither is “the demon prince of Heh” in any classical Jewish text.

## Ba’airiron

Crowley’s *777* places **Ba’airiron** in its path-15 qliphotic-order row.

**[Hermetic-occult attribution]** This is a path-level infernal correspondence in the *777* table and should not be confused with either endpoint qliphah or Grant’s tunnel-name.

## Hemethterith

Kenneth Grant’s *Nightside of Eden* appeared in 1977. Part Two expands the adverse genii of *Liber 231* into twenty-two Tunnels of Set. Hemethterith is the fifth tunnel chapter but corresponds to Kircher Path 15 because the letter-paths begin at Tree key 11.

| Field | Grantian attribution |
|---|---|
| Tunnel | Hemethterith |
| Dayside path | Heh/15 |
| Endpoints | Ghagiel–Thagirion |
| Source-name | Adverse sigil/name complex of Crowley’s *Liber 231* |
| Grant publication | *Nightside of Eden* (1977), Part II, chapter 5, around p. 183 |
| Independent demon prince | Not clearly supplied |
| Status | [Modern invention/occult elaboration] |

See the [table of contents and text scan](https://www.hempenings.com/Esoteric/Nightside-of-Eden-KennethGrant_text.pdf) and a [comparative tunnel table](https://www.qliphoth-archive.com/en/tunnels.html).

Grant treats the tunnels as subconscious, dreamlike, sexual, atavistic, and extraterrestrial or transhuman zones underlying the dayside paths. His method combines Crowley, Lovecraftian language, Tantra, Egyptian myth, Spare, and his own Typhonian magical experiences.

**Historical conclusion:** Hemethterith is not attested in *Sefer Yetzirah*, the *Bahir*, the *Zohar*, Cordovero, Luria, Kircher, or the original Golden Dawn papers. Its source-chain is:

> Crowley’s adverse genii and sigils → Grant’s 1977 tunnel interpretation → later qliphotic magic.

## Artists and diagrams

### Early Jewish diagrams

Kabbalistic trees appear in manuscript culture well before Kircher, but their geometry varies. They may map sefiroth, divine names, garments, *partzufim*, worlds, or processes without assigning the alphabet in Kircher’s order.

Examples include:

- A thirteenth-century manuscript diagram in the [Biblissima portal](https://portail.biblissima.fr/en/ark:/43093/ifdata70fbdaeec9d1ae238f5f80c07a3a026d4e76447a).
- The University of Haifa Ilanot project’s discussion of an early [“Tree of Wisdom” manuscript](https://ilanot.haifa.ac.il/site/?p=927).

### *Portae Lucis*

Leonhard Beck’s 1516 woodcut marks the visual entry of Gikatilla’s material into Christian print culture.

### Kircher’s plate

Kircher’s Tree is the decisive ancestor of the Golden Dawn’s standard geometry. Its importance lies not in being “the original Jewish tree” but in being reproducible, printed, encyclopedic, and later occultly adaptable.

### Golden Dawn coloured trees

Golden Dawn manuscripts placed the Tree in four colour scales corresponding to Atziluth, Briah, Yetzirah, and Assiah. These diagrams functioned as ritual and visionary instruments, not merely illustrations.

### Tarot artists

- **Pamela Colman Smith:** her 1909 Emperor bears Aries ram imagery, reflecting Waite’s Golden Dawn inheritance.
- **Lady Frieda Harris:** painted Crowley’s Thoth Tarot, including the Emperor and Star during 1938–1943; the cards embody Crowley’s revised letter doctrine.
- **Steffi Grant:** contributed art to Kenneth Grant’s Typhonian world.
- **Austin Osman Spare:** a major imaginal and artistic influence on Grant’s nightside interpretation.

## Poets and novelists

- **Aleister Crowley:** poet as well as occult systematizer.
- **W. B. Yeats:** Golden Dawn initiate; his poetry uses related cyclic, solar, royal, and visionary structures, but no single work can responsibly be called a documentary “Path of Heh poem.”
- **Arthur Machen and Algernon Blackwood:** Golden Dawn-connected novelists; again, Heh-specific influence is unproven.
- **Dion Fortune:** occult novelist whose fiction dramatizes ideas developed in her Qabalistic teaching.
- **Kenneth Grant:** consciously blended magical writing with Lovecraftian and surreal literary modes.

**[Negative finding]** Membership in the Golden Dawn does not by itself prove that a particular poem or novel encodes Path 15.

# Controversies and disputes

## 1. Dating *Sefer Yetzirah*

The work contains ancient materials but survives through late, divergent manuscripts. Dates from late antiquity to the early Islamic period remain in play. Traditional Abrahamic or Akivan authorship and critical historical dating answer different questions.

## 2. Is the “Constituting Intelligence” in *Sefer Yetzirah*?

No, not in the earliest recoverable chapters.

It is a later “Thirty-two Paths” tract frequently appended to the book. Westcott said where he obtained it; subsequent occult editions often erased that distinction.

## 3. What does Heh mean?

“Window” is a conventional occult meaning, not a settled Semitic etymology. “Breath” is a rabbinic and Hasidic theological development based on pronunciation.

## 4. Which faculty and organ belong to Heh?

Speech/right foot, sight/liver, and sight/right hand are all transmitted. Any table that gives one without its recension is incomplete.

## 5. Zoharic authorship

- Traditional: Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai.
- Scholem: principally Moses de León.
- Later scholarship: layered production associated with a Castilian circle, with de León central but not necessarily exclusive.

## 6. Christian appropriation

Pico, Reuchlin, Kircher, and Rosenroth preserved, translated, transformed, and disseminated Jewish texts. They also subordinated them to Christian theological projects.

“Christian Cabala” should therefore be described as both transmission and appropriation: it made Hebrew materials available to new audiences while frequently removing them from Jewish law, worship, language, and communal authority.

## 7. Kircher versus Jewish trees

Kircher’s geometry is neither the only possible twenty-two-line graph nor the unique Jewish Tree.

The decisive difference for Heh is:

- Ari/Gra received arrangement: Keter–Chokhmah.
- Kircher/Golden Dawn: Chokhmah–Tiphareth.

The early *Sefer Yetzirah* itself does not adjudicate this by naming the two endpoints.

## 8. Tarot origins and attributions

Historical Tarot developed as a European card game. The proposition that the trumps were ancient Egyptian, Mosaic, or kabbalistic hieroglyphs is unsupported by the earliest cards.

The letter–trump system developed through eighteenth- and nineteenth-century occultism, especially Court de Gébelin, de Mellet, Lévi, and the Golden Dawn. Its value as a symbolic system does not establish ancient authorship.

## 9. Emperor or Star?

- Golden Dawn, Waite, Case, Regardie: Heh–Emperor.
- Mature Crowley/Thoth: Heh–Star.
- Crowley’s exact movement of letters, signs, cards, and paths is internally difficult.

This is an occult-system dispute, not a variant reading of a Hebrew *Sefer Yetzirah* manuscript.

## 10. Daʿat

*Sefer Yetzirah* insists on:

> עשר ולא תשע, עשר ולא אחת עשרה

“Ten, not nine; ten, not eleven.”

**[Documented text]** Daʿat is therefore not an ordinary eleventh sefirah in that scheme.

**[Kabbalistic tradition]** Later systems treat Daʿat as knowledge, conjunction, an emergent state, or a sefirah counted when Keter is not counted. Lurianic *partzufic* configurations make it functionally important without simply overturning “ten and not eleven.”

**[Hermetic-occult attribution]** The Golden Dawn often depicts Daʿat as invisible, situated at or below the Abyss. Calling it “the eleventh sephirah” without qualification mistakes a conditional or emergent principle for an additional permanent emanation.

## 11. Nightside antiquity

The qliphoth have Jewish antecedents; Grant’s exact twenty-two tunnels do not. Hemethterith is a twentieth-century magical elaboration, not an ancient Hebrew demon secretly preserved in *Sefer Yetzirah*.

# Open questions

1. **What is the earliest manuscript of the separate Thirty-two Intelligences tract?**  
   The text’s association with the Castilian Circle of Contemplation is plausible, but its manuscript stemma and exact Hebrew wording require a dedicated codicological study.

2. **Which precise diagram should be called “the Ari tree”?**  
   Printed occult comparisons often treat a later received diagram as Luria’s autograph arrangement. The genealogy of individual Hebrew diagrams remains less simple than the label suggests.

3. **How directly did Kircher depend on Jewish diagrams rather than Christian intermediaries?**  
   His sources were extensive, but each line and letter on his plate cannot automatically be traced to a single Jewish exemplar.

4. **Did the Golden Dawn directly copy Kircher’s plate or receive its geometry through later compilations?**  
   Kircher is the clear printed ancestor, but intermediate transmission through Rosenroth, Lévi, French occultism, Freemasonry, and manuscript notebooks merits more precise documentation.

5. **What exactly did Crowley intend to counterchange?**  
   His verbal account, diagrams, old *777* tables, Thoth card labels, and later editorial corrections do not yield one uncontested answer.

6. **What is the earliest occurrence of “Heh means window”?**  
   It was entrenched by nineteenth-century occult tables, but its precise route from alphabet-name speculation into Golden Dawn doctrine remains insufficiently documented.

7. **Are “Gad” in *777* and “Judah” in Jewish Nisan tables independent systems or a copying error?**  
   The discrepancy should be preserved rather than harmonized without evidence.

8. **How should Grant’s Hemethterith be related to Crowley’s Heh/Tzaddi switch?**  
   Grant’s tunnel scheme is built from pre-existing adverse-genius tables but interpreted through later Thelemic doctrine. Different practitioners relocate or reinterpret its zodiacal and Tarot qualities.

9. **Can a specific Heh pathworking be attributed to the original Golden Dawn?**  
   The order transmitted symbols and skrying methods, but many detailed scripts sold today are late twentieth- or twenty-first-century compositions.

10. **Absence as result:**  
    No primary Jewish source examined here contains the complete entity now marketed as “The Path of Heh.” It is a composite whose components first cohere in the Golden Dawn era.
