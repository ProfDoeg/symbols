# Chesed: Research Dossier

## Method and evidentiary legend

This dossier distinguishes six kinds of statement:

- **[Text]** Directly documented in a primary source.
- **[Reconstruction]** Conclusion of modern historical or philological scholarship.
- **[Jewish tradition]** Received rabbinic, kabbalistic, liturgical, or Hasidic interpretation, whether or not historically demonstrable.
- **[Hermetic attribution]** A correspondence belonging to Christian Cabala, Renaissance magic, the Golden Dawn, Thelema, or later occultism.
- **[Disputed]** The evidence or interpretation is contested.
- **[Modern synthesis]** A recent harmonization, popular table, or practitioner interpretation whose precise antecedents are uncertain.

“Jewish Kabbalah” and “Hermetic Qabalah” below are therefore related historical traditions, not interchangeable vocabularies.

A crucial preliminary finding is that **Chesed is a sefirah, not one of the twenty-two letter-paths**. Consequently it has no intrinsic Hebrew-letter class, zodiacal month, tribe, Tarot trump, or Goetic quinance. Those data apply to paths. The incident paths of Chesed are treated separately below.

## The name

### 1. Hebrew form, meaning, and etymology

**[Text]** The word is חֶסֶד, consonants ח־ס־ד. Its numerical value in ordinary gematria is:

> ח = 8, ס = 60, ד = 4; total **72**.

This later invited comparison with the seventy-two-letter or seventy-two-name complex, but gematria alone does not establish that the sefirah was originally designed around the number seventy-two.

Biblical **ḥesed** is semantically richer than the single English word “mercy.” Depending on context, it can denote:

- kindness or beneficence;
- loyalty;
- covenant fidelity;
- gracious conduct;
- steadfast love;
- an act of favour performed within a relationship.

The Brown–Driver–Briggs lexicon consequently distributes the term among “goodness,” “kindness,” and “faithfulness,” rather than giving one universally adequate equivalent. [BDB, חֶסֶד](https://www.sefaria.org/BDB%2C_%D7%97%D6%B6%D6%AB%D7%A1%D6%B6%D7%93?lang=bi)

**[Reconstruction/Disputed]** Modern philologists dispute whether the earliest semantic core was spontaneous kindness, covenant obligation, reciprocal loyalty, or a wider relationship of mutual responsibility. A recent study argues that “reciprocal covenant” explains many otherwise difficult passages, but this remains a lexical proposal rather than a recovered ancient definition. [“Chesed: A Reciprocal Covenant”](https://www.thetorah.com/article/chesed-a-reciprocal-covenant)

For a sefirah, “lovingkindness” is often better than unqualified “mercy”: mercy can imply merely suspending punishment, whereas kabbalistic Chesed is also the positive outflow of life, blessing, abundance, and generosity.

### 2. Gedulah

The alternative name is גְּדֻלָּה, **Gedulah**, from the root גדל, “to become or make great.” It means greatness, magnitude, majesty, or magnificence.

**[Text]** Its major scriptural warrant is David’s prayer:

> **לְךָ יְהוָה הַגְּדֻלָּה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת וְהַנֵּצַח וְהַהוֹד**  
> “Yours, YHWH, are greatness, power, splendour, victory, and majesty.”

—1 Chronicles 29:11, [Hebrew and translations](https://www.sefaria.org/I_Chronicles.29.11?lang=bi)

**[Jewish tradition]** Kabbalists read the sequence as a scriptural catalogue of the seven lower sefirot:

| Verse-word | Kabbalistic reading |
|---|---|
| הַגְּדֻלָּה, “the greatness” | Chesed |
| הַגְּבוּרָה, “the power” | Gevurah |
| הַתִּפְאֶרֶת, “the splendour” | Tiferet |
| הַנֵּצַח, “the victory” | Netzach |
| הַהוֹד, “the majesty” | Hod |
| כֹל, “all” | Yesod |
| הַמַּמְלָכָה, “the kingdom” | Malkhut |

This is kabbalistic exegesis, not the plain historical sense of Chronicles. It explains why the fourth sefirah can be called either **Chesed** or **Gedulah**.

### 3. Abraham and the biblical name

**[Text]** Micah links the noun directly to Abraham:

> **תִּתֵּן אֱמֶת לְיַעֲקֹב חֶסֶד לְאַבְרָהָם**  
> “Grant truth to Jacob, ḥesed to Abraham.”

—Micah 7:20, [text and translations](https://www.sefaria.org/Micah.7.20?lang=bi)

Rashi reads this in relation to Abraham’s kindness, but does not by that alone construct a ten-sefirah diagram. [Rashi on Micah 7:20](https://www.sefaria.org/Rashi_on_Micah.7.20?lang=bi)

**[Jewish tradition]** Bahiric and Zoharic Kabbalah turns the verse into a structural correspondence: Abraham becomes the human exemplar or “chariot” of Chesed, Isaac of Gevurah, and Jacob of Tiferet.

### 4. Proverbs 3:19, Job 28, and Isaiah

These biblical Wisdom texts belong principally to the wider doctrine of creation through wisdom and the “paths of wisdom”; they do **not** name Chesed.

**[Text]** Proverbs 3:19 says that YHWH founded the earth through Wisdom and established heaven through Understanding. In later sefirah-language these are readily read as Ḥokhmah and Binah. The preceding verses also call Wisdom “a tree of life.” [Proverbs 3](https://www.sefaria.org/Proverbs.3?lang=bi)

**[Text]** Job 28 asks where wisdom and understanding are found and concludes that God knows wisdom’s “way” or “path.” [Job 28](https://www.sefaria.org/Job.28?lang=bi)

**[Text]** Isaiah 40:12–14 speaks of measuring creation and asks who has measured or directed the divine spirit. Later “Thirty-Two Paths” literature employed this vocabulary of measuring and paths. [Isaiah 40](https://www.sefaria.org/Isaiah.40?lang=bi)

**Finding:** none of these passages calls the fourth sefirah Chesed. Their relevance is contextual and retrospective. The direct biblical anchors for this sefirah-name are principally **ḥesed** itself, Micah 7:20, Psalm 89:3, and **Gedulah** in 1 Chronicles 29:11.

## The Jewish tradition: chronological dossier

### I. Biblical and rabbinic prehistory

**[Text]** Biblical ḥesed is an ethical, relational, and divine attribute. It is not yet a hypostasis numbered fourth in a tenfold emanational system.

Representative biblical complexes include:

- Exodus 34:6–7: God is “abundant in ḥesed and truth.”
- Psalm 89:3: “The world is built by ḥesed.”
- Micah 7:20: ḥesed is linked to Abraham.
- 1 Chronicles 29:11: Gedulah begins a sequence later read as the lower sefirot.

**[Jewish tradition]** Rabbinic literature makes *gemilut ḥasadim*, performance of acts of kindness, a pillar of communal and religious life. The later sefirah was therefore not created from an ethically empty term: it spiritualized an already central biblical-rabbinic virtue.

**[Historical caution]** It is anachronistic to translate every biblical occurrence of ḥesed as the fourth sefirah.

### II. *Sefer Yetzirah*

#### Date and recensions

**[Reconstruction/Disputed]** Suggested dates range from late antiquity to the early Islamic period. Some earlier scholarship proposed the second through sixth centuries. Peter Hayman’s critical edition emphasizes that secure evidence for the work’s circulation begins around the early tenth century and edits three major textual forms:

1. Short recension;
2. Long recension;
3. Saadyan recension.

[Peter Hayman, *Sefer Yeṣira: Edition, Translation and Text-Critical Commentary*](https://www.mohrsiebeck.com/en/book/sefer-yesira-9783161587955/)

**[Jewish tradition]** The work is traditionally attributed to Abraham, sometimes with teachings transmitted through the patriarchal or rabbinic past. That attribution is devotional/pseudepigraphic tradition, not a conclusion of critical history.

#### The thirty-two paths

**[Text]** The opening describes creation through thirty-two wondrous paths of wisdom and immediately analyzes them as ten sefirot plus twenty-two letters:

> **עשר ספירות בלימה ועשרים ושתים אותיות יסוד, שלש אמות ושבע כפולות ושתים עשרה פשוטות**  
> “Ten sefirot belimah and twenty-two foundation letters: three mothers, seven doubles, and twelve simples.”

—*Sefer Yetzirah* 1:1, [Sefaria with Saadia](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah.1.1?lang=bi&with=Saadia%20Gaon)

The thirty-two are therefore:

- ten *sefirot belimah*;
- three mother letters;
- seven double letters;
- twelve simple letters.

**[Text]** The work insists:

> **עשר ולא תשע, עשר ולא אחת עשרה**  
> “Ten, not nine; ten, not eleven.”

—*Sefer Yetzirah* 1:4, [text and commentaries](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah.1.4?lang=bi)

#### What is the fourth sefirah here?

In the common textual sequence, the first four sefirot are associated with:

1. spirit of the living God;
2. spirit from Spirit;
3. water from Spirit;
4. fire from Water.

The fourth is not called Chesed. Nor is it yet assigned El, Abraham, Jupiter, Tzadkiel, or the virtue of obedience.

**Core finding:** the claim “*Sefer Yetzirah* says Chesed is the fourth sefirah” is retrospective. It is legitimate inside later kabbalistic commentary, but not a literal description of the earliest recoverable text.

#### Saadia, Ari, and Gra versions

- **Saadia Gaon’s recension/commentary**, early tenth century, represents one major textual family and a philosophical-cosmological interpretation.
- The **Ari version** is a sixteenth-century Lurianic revision or selection transmitted through later kabbalistic printing and commentary.
- The **Gra version** reflects the eighteenth-century Vilna Gaon’s textual and interpretive decisions.

[Sefaria’s Gra-version introduction](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Yetzirah_Gra_Version.1.4?lang=bi&with=About)

**[Disputed]** Popular occult literature often speaks as though these are four clean ancient “editions” with interchangeable diagrams. Critical scholarship instead finds a fluid manuscript history; “Ari version” and “Gra version” include later editorial and interpretive activity.

### III. Early theosophical Kabbalah and the *Bahir*

**[Reconstruction]** The *Sefer ha-Bahir* emerged in Provence in approximately 1150–1200, while presenting itself as ancient rabbinic teaching. Its materials may preserve older motifs, but the surviving composition belongs to medieval Kabbalah. [Encyclopaedia Judaica overview](https://www.jewishvirtuallibrary.org/kabbalah)

The *Bahir* is decisive because it moves from the *Yetzirah*’s terse sefirot and letters toward symbolic divine powers, biblical personalities, commandments, limbs, and cosmic processes.

**[Text/Jewish tradition]** A passage quoted in later kabbalistic literature asks, “What is Chesed?” and answers that it is Torah, linking this to Abraham’s gratuitous hospitality—feeding and giving drink. [Recanati preserving the Bahir passage](https://www.sefaria.org/Recanati_on_the_Torah%2C_Yitro.1?lang=bi)

Other Bahiric materials connect Abraham with the completion or stabilization of the divine structure. [*Sefer ha-Bahir*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_HaBahir.6?lang=bi)

The complex can be summarized:

- Abraham performs kindness below;
- he becomes identified with the divine right;
- the world is sustained or “built” through ḥesed;
- Torah itself may be called Chesed because it mediates divine beneficence.

**[Historical caution]** Section numbering varies sharply among the Scholem, Margaliot, Kaplan, and online editions. Citations such as “Bahir §135” or “§131” must be checked against the named edition.

### IV. Castilian Kabbalah, Gikatilla, and the *Zohar*

#### Joseph Gikatilla, *Shaʿarei Orah*, c. 1290

Gikatilla organizes the sefirot through divine names. The section devoted to the fourth sefirah is in the Seventh Gate.

**[Text]** Chesed’s divine name is **El**. The name signifies the beneficent power that pleads for merit and tempers or overcomes destructive judgment. [*Shaʿarei Orah*, Seventh Gate, Fourth Sefirah](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha'arei_Orah,_Seventh_Gate,_Fourth_Sefirah?lang=bi)

Gikatilla’s major threads include:

- El as mercy and expansive divine power;
- Abraham as the bearer of Chesed;
- the divine right opposed and joined to the left;
- judgment requiring admixture with mercy;
- the priestly blessing and beneficent transmission;
- Psalm 52:3, “the ḥesed of El all day”;
- Exodus 34’s “El, compassionate and gracious.”

Elsewhere Gikatilla describes Abraham as bearing the banner of **El**, while Chesed can tip the scales toward compassion. [*Shaʿarei Orah*, Fifth Gate](https://www.sefaria.org/Sha'arei_Orah%2C_Fifth_Gate%2C_Sixth_Sefirah.198?lang=bi)

**Finding about the familiar hierarchy:** Gikatilla supplies strong medieval evidence for **El–Chesed–Abraham**. He does not, in this section, give the complete later formula “El–Tzadkiel–Chashmalim–Jupiter.” That composite should not be cited as if it were a single table from *Shaʿarei Orah*.

#### The *Zohar*

**[Jewish tradition]** The *Zohar* is attributed to Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and his circle in the second century.

**[Reconstruction]** The main body appeared in late-thirteenth-century Castile through Moses de León. Scholem argued for de León’s central authorship; more recent scholarship often speaks of a “Zoharic circle,” layered composition, and different strata rather than one author producing the entire corpus. The *Raʿaya Meheimna* and *Tikkunei Zohar* are generally treated as later strata. [National Library of Israel overview](https://www.nli.org.il/en/discover/judaism/jewish-bookshelf/zohar), [recent manuscript scholarship](https://academic.oup.com/book/60780/chapter-abstract/528593579)

#### Chesed in the *Zohar*

Recurring Zoharic identifications include:

- Chesed is the right side or right arm;
- Gevurah is the left;
- Tiferet mediates them;
- Abraham is Chesed;
- Isaac is Gevurah;
- Jacob is Tiferet;
- Chesed is water, white, expansive blessing, and the first day’s primordial light;
- priestly service arises from the right;
- unbounded Chesed requires Gevurah’s delimitation.

The *Zohar* reads **בהבראם**, “when they were created,” as **באברהם**, “through Abraham,” associating Abraham with the world built by Chesed. [*Zohar*, Vayeḥi](https://www.zohar.com/zohar/Vayechi/verses/431-466)

Abraham’s “crown of kindness” is linked explicitly to Micah 7:20. [*Zohar*, Va’etḥannan](https://www.zohar.com/zohar/Vaetchanan/chapters/21)

The right/left polarization of Abraham and Isaac appears repeatedly. [*Zohar*, Vayera](https://www.zohar.com/zohar/Vayera/chapters/11)

#### “Chesed is water”

This symbolism is not simply “Jupiter equals Water.” In Jewish theosophy, water represents diffusion, fertility, blessing, and undelimited giving; fire represents restriction, differentiation, and judgment. The Golden Dawn later absorbs “Water” as an elemental correspondence in its comparative tables, but its technical elemental system is not identical to the Zoharic metaphor.

### V. Cordovero: *Pardes Rimmonim* and *Or Neʿerav*

Moses Cordovero completed *Pardes Rimmonim* in Safed in 1548. It is the great systematic synthesis immediately preceding Luria.

#### *Pardes Rimmonim*

**[Text]** Cordovero treats Chesed/Gedulah as fourth, situated on the right and symbolized by the right arm. [*Pardes Rimmonim* 1:3](https://www.sefaria.org/Pardes_Rimmonim.1.3?lang=bi)

His larger system clarifies several points:

- the sefirot are neither independent gods nor mere names;
- each sefirah contains the others;
- Chesed therefore includes a Gevurah aspect, just as Gevurah includes Chesed;
- pure, undifferentiated expansion would be cosmically destructive;
- harmonious emanation depends on right, left, and middle;
- the divine essence is not exhausted by any sefirah.

Cordovero’s “channels” should not automatically be identified with the Golden Dawn’s alphabetically numbered Tarot paths. His printed works circulated with different diagrams, and the verbal descriptions in *Pardes* and *Or Neʿerav* are not always diagrammatically identical.

#### *Or Neʿerav*

*Or Neʿerav* is a pedagogical introduction to kabbalistic study, not simply an abbreviated Chesed treatise. It explains the necessity of ten sefirot and the relation between Ein Sof, emanation, and the forms through which divine action becomes knowable. [*Or Neʿerav*, part VI](https://www.sefaria.org/Ohr_Ne'erav%2C_PART_VI.1?lang=bi)

#### Ethical Chesed in Cordovero

In *Tomer Devorah*, Cordovero turns imitation of the sefirot into ethical discipline. To imitate Chesed means active, embodied kindness: sustaining others, receiving guests, caring for the vulnerable, accompanying the dead, and giving without merely reproducing resentment. This is a Jewish ethical application—not the Golden Dawn virtue “obedience.”

### VI. Luria through Ḥayyim Vital: *Etz Chaim*

Isaac Luria, the Ari, left relatively little writing. His cosmology was transmitted chiefly by disciples, especially Ḥayyim Vital. *Etz Chaim* is therefore “Lurianic” through Vital’s redaction and textual transmission.

[Hebrew *Sefer Etz Chaim*](https://www.sefaria.org/Sefer_Etz_Chaim?lang=bi)

#### Tzimtzum, vessels, and shattering

In compressed form, the Lurianic sequence is:

1. Ein Sof;
2. tzimtzum, divine contraction or concealment;
3. the kav, measured ray;
4. Adam Qadmon;
5. worlds/configurations of primordial emanation;
6. the world of points or Tohu;
7. shevirat ha-kelim, shattering of vessels;
8. descent and entanglement of sparks;
9. Tikkun, ordered repair through partzufim.

**[Text/Jewish tradition]** The lower vessels fail to sustain the intensity of the light. The “kings of Edom” become a biblical cipher for this primordial breakdown. Chesed is among the lower qualities implicated in the shattering. [Vital material summarized with *Etz Chaim* references](https://www.chabad.org/kabbalah/article_cdo/aid/380568/jewish/Shattered-Vessels.htm)

#### Chesed in Tohu and Tikkun

The problem in Tohu is not that Chesed is morally evil. Each quality exists in an isolated, absolutized form. Chesed without interinclusion cannot accept Gevurah’s limit; Gevurah without Chesed becomes sheer negation.

Tikkun establishes partzufim and interinclusion:

- Atik Yomin and Arikh Anpin relate to Keter;
- Abba to Ḥokhmah;
- Imma to Binah;
- Zeʿir Anpin contains Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, Netzach, Hod, and Yesod;
- Nukva or Malkhut receives and articulates the configuration.

Chesed is thus normally the right arm of **Zeʿir Anpin**, not a separate partzuf.

#### Four worlds

The four worlds are:

1. Atzilut, Emanation;
2. Beriah, Creation;
3. Yetzirah, Formation;
4. Assiyah, Action.

Each world possesses its own tenfold differentiation. Consequently “Chesed of Atzilut,” “Chesed of Beriah,” and so forth are legitimate Lurianic expressions. The Golden Dawn later places a divine name, archangel, angelic choir, and planetary sphere into a four-world hierarchy, but that exact table is a Western esoteric construction.

#### Tikkun and human action

Mitzvot, prayer, ethical action, intention, and the refinement of embodied life participate in raising sparks and restoring ordered relation. Chesed performed below can awaken or draw Chesed above. This is not equivalent to “invoking Jupiter,” although later occultists often place the ideas side by side.

### VII. The Gra’s Tree

The Vilna Gaon’s reading of *Sefer Yetzirah* stresses a carefully ordered relation between:

- ten sefirot;
- three mothers;
- seven doubles;
- twelve simples;
- spatial, calendrical, and bodily dimensions.

**[Jewish tradition]** The Gra’s diagrammatic tradition is used in Lithuanian and later Jewish-kabbalistic study.

**[Disputed]** “The Gra Tree” is not simply Kircher’s diagram with different labels. Nor is there one universally transmitted “Ari Tree.” Printed and manuscript diagrams differ in connections, placement of Daʿat, and whether letters are written on every channel.

The Golden Dawn’s Vav–Teth–Yod–Kaph lines touching Chesed should therefore not be projected wholesale onto the Gra or Ari. The Jewish diagrams are expressions of their own cosmological and exegetical systems, not defective Tarot diagrams.

### VIII. Hasidic readings

#### Shneur Zalman of Liadi, *Tanya*

The most directly relevant chapter is *Shaʿar ha-Yiḥud ve-ha-Emunah*, chapter 4.

**[Text/Hasidic tradition]** Creation ex nihilo is attributed to the divine quality called Gedulah or Chesed. Yet creation must be paired with Gevurah: concealment and limitation make finite beings possible. [*Tanya*, part II, chapter 4](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_II%3B_Shaar_HaYichud_VehaEmunah.4?lang=bi)

Further treatments include:

- **Iggeret ha-Kodesh 3:** Chesed as right arm and source of extension or longevity. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.3.3?lang=bi)
- **Iggeret 5:** Abraham’s acts of kindness arouse supernal Chesed. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.5?lang=bi)
- **Iggeret 10:** finite worldly giving and the search for a more unlimited divine charity. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.10?lang=bi)
- **Iggeret 13:** no divine quality acts in total isolation; Chesed contains Gevurah and Gevurah contains Chesed. [Text](https://www.sefaria.org/Tanya%2C_Part_IV%3B_Iggeret_HaKodesh.13?lang=bi)

#### Rabbi Naḥman of Bratslav

Naḥman repeatedly uses the right-side/Chesed complex:

- halakhic clarity and gratitude become vessels of ḥesed;
- the priestly or Abrahamic quality belongs to the right;
- charity, Torah, and the disclosure of divine kindness are interrelated;
- religious vitality requires integrating love and awe.

Relevant passages include *Liqquṭei Moharan* II:2, II:4, and I:13. [Sefaria text](https://www.sefaria.org/Likutei_Moharan?lang=bi)

There is no single universally recognized “Naḥman chapter on Chesed” corresponding to Fortune’s chapter XVIII.

### IX. Prayer, Torah, Omer, and calendar

#### Prayer

**[Jewish tradition]** The opening blessings of the Amidah are read kabbalistically as:

- Abraham/Chesed;
- Isaac/Gevurah;
- Jacob/Tiferet.

The first blessing concludes **Magen Avraham**, “Shield of Abraham,” and invokes ancestral love and beneficence. This is a kabbalistic overlay on a much older rabbinic prayer.

Priestly blessing is also associated with Chesed because the priest embodies the right side and blesses in love.

#### Torah

The *Bahir*’s “Chesed is Torah” does not mean that all Torah belongs exclusively to the fourth sefirah. Torah can be mapped differently according to its written, oral, concealed, revealed, merciful, and juridical aspects.

#### Counting the Omer

In the widely used kabbalistic scheme:

- week 1 = Chesed;
- day 1 = Chesed she-be-Chesed;
- day 2 = Gevurah she-be-Chesed;
- day 3 = Tiferet she-be-Chesed;
- day 4 = Netzach she-be-Chesed;
- day 5 = Hod she-be-Chesed;
- day 6 = Yesod she-be-Chesed;
- day 7 = Malkhut she-be-Chesed.

The **day of Chesed in the strictest sense is the first day**, while the whole first week explores Chesed’s seven internal aspects. [Chabad on the Omer qualities](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/67788/jewish/Discourse-25.htm), [Hebcal overview](https://lib.hebcal.co.il/events/counting-the-omer.html)

#### Month, tribe, and simple letters

**Finding:** Chesed has no intrinsic month or tribe in *Sefer Yetzirah*. Months, tribes, zodiacal signs, and bodily organs belong to the twelve **simple letters**, and the assignments vary between recensions. Adding a month or tribe to Chesed itself is a modern conflation unless a particular secondary system is explicitly named.

## The Hermetic and occult tradition: chronological dossier

### I. Pico della Mirandola, 1486

Pico’s *Conclusiones* contain forty-seven conclusions “according to the Hebrew Cabalists” and seventy-two “according to his own opinion.”

**[Text]** Thesis 40.37 places “piety” beneath Wisdom in the right-hand arrangement and connects it to Abraham:

> **in dextrali coordinatione subordinationem pietatis ad sapienciam**

“in the right-hand ordering, the subordination of piety to wisdom.”

Pico immediately turns the relation into a Christian claim about Abraham seeing Christ. [Pico, *Conclusiones* 40.37](https://www.esotericarchives.com/pico/conclus.htm)

This is important evidence for a fourth sphere rendered as **pietas**, but it is also a clear act of Christian reinterpretation.

**[Text]** Thesis 40.48 says, with deliberate independence from other Kabbalists:

> **Iupiter sit quarte, Mars quinte, Sol sexte**

“Jupiter is of the fourth, Mars of the fifth, the Sun of the sixth.”

[Pico, *Conclusiones* 40.48](https://www.esotericarchives.com/pico/conclus.htm)

This is one of the earliest clear printed witnesses for **Jupiter = fourth sefirah**. Pico himself labels the arrangement as his conclusion—“whatever other Kabbalists may say”—so it should not be retroactively cited as ancient Jewish consensus.

---

### II. *Portae Lucis*, 1516

Paulus Ricius’s Latin *Portae Lucis* translated portions of Gikatilla’s *Shaʿarei Orah*. Its title page contains the earliest famous **printed** sefirah-tree.

- It is not the earliest Jewish sefirah diagram.
- Medieval manuscript diagrams preceded it.
- It is an important bridge by which Jewish divine-name Kabbalah entered Christian print culture.
- Gedulah/Chesed could be rendered through Latin terms such as *magnificentia*, *gratia*, *pietas*, or *misericordia*, depending on translator and context.

[Center for Jewish Art record](https://cja.huji.ac.il/gross/browser.php?id=40577&mode=set), [reproduction](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sefirot_%28Paulus_Ricius%29.jpg)

J. H. Chajes’s study of kabbalistic visualization shows that there was no single primordial “Tree of Life” diagram from which every later tree mechanically descended. [Chajes, *The Kabbalistic Tree*](https://www.psupress.org/books/titles/978-0-271-09345-1.html)

---

### III. Johannes Reuchlin, *De arte cabalistica*, 1517

Reuchlin presents Kabbalah through a dialogue among a Jewish teacher, a Pythagorean, and a Muslim interlocutor. He had access to Gikatilla and to Ricius’s Latin translation. [Center for Jewish Art](https://cja.huji.ac.il/sch/browser.php?id=40611&mode=set)

His broad Latin vocabulary includes divine love, piety, greatness, and beneficence, but the later Golden Dawn table should not be attributed to him intact.

**[Christian-Cabalist attribution]** Reuchlin treats Kabbalah as a confirmation of Christian and Pythagorean truth. He helped spread:

- a tenfold hierarchy;
- Hebrew divine names as objects of contemplation;
- numerical and letter mysticism;
- Christian readings of Jewish esotericism.

[1517 scan metadata](https://books.google.com/books?id=vF9mG3DnbBUC), [modern Latin-German edition](https://www.frommann-holzboog.de/editionen/1706/170600211?lang=de)

**[Appropriation issue]** Reuchlin also defended Hebrew learning during the controversy over confiscating Jewish books. His Christian use of Kabbalah was therefore both preservative and supersessionist: he valued Jewish texts, yet made them testify to Christian claims their Jewish authors did not affirm.

---

### IV. Agrippa, 1531/1533

Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa’s *De occulta philosophia* supplies a major intermediate stage between Christian Cabala and the Golden Dawn.

**[Text/Hermetic attribution]** Agrippa describes divine influence proceeding through an angel—Zadkiel/Zedekiel—into Jupiter, producing clemency and pacifying justice. [*Three Books of Occult Philosophy*, book III](https://quod.lib.umich.edu/e/eebo/A26565.0001.001/1%3A18.10?rgn=div2&view=fulltext)

Agrippa also gives related Jupiter hierarchies involving:

- a planetary angel, Sachiel/Zadkiel;
- the intelligence Iophiel;
- the spirit Hismael;
- the magic square of Jupiter.

These should not be collapsed into one being. Later tables frequently confuse “archangel of Chesed,” “angel of Jupiter,” “planetary intelligence,” and “planetary spirit.”

### V. Athanasius Kircher, *Oedipus Aegyptiacus*, 1652–54

Kircher’s *Cabala Hebraeorum* in *Oedipus Aegyptiacus* printed the most influential ancestor of the modern Hermetic Tree.

[Latin transcription](https://esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm), [Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/A027084/page/n3/mode/2up)

#### The fourth “path”

At pages 312–13 Kircher gives:

> **Quarta semita vocatur intelligentia … receptacularis**

“The fourth path is called the … receptacular intelligence.”

He explains it as a boundary or receptacle into which superior intelligences descend and from which spiritual virtues emanate. [Kircher, p. 312–13](https://esotericarchives.com/kircher/cabala.htm)

The uncertain Latin reading *metalis/metalis* in OCR has produced English “measuring,” “arresting,” “cohesive,” and “receptacular.”

#### What this text is—and is not

**[Textual history]** This numbered list of thirty-two “Intelligences” is not part of the earliest recoverable *Sefer Yetzirah*. It circulated as a medieval or early modern appendix, was translated by Joannes Stephanus Rittangelius in 1642, printed by Kircher, and translated again by Westcott.

**[Hermetic attribution]** Fortune’s “Yetziratic text” is therefore a Western reception of this appendix, not a quotation from Hayman’s early *Sefer Yetzirah* recensions.

#### Kircher’s path layout

Kircher’s diagram placed twenty-two lettered links among ten spheres in a configuration substantially ancestral to the Golden Dawn Tree. The Golden Dawn retained the topology while altering and systematizing many astrological attributions.

For Chesed, the Kircher/Golden Dawn geometry has four incident paths:

| Path | Letter | Joins | Golden Dawn attribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | ו Vav | Ḥokhmah–Chesed | Taurus, Hierophant |
| 19 | ט Teth | Chesed–Gevurah | Leo, Strength/Lust |
| 20 | י Yod | Chesed–Tiferet | Virgo, Hermit |
| 21 | כ Kaph | Chesed–Netzach | Jupiter, Wheel of Fortune |

The Ari and Gra diagrams do not supply a universally matching alphabetic set. “Path 20,” for example, may mean an ordinal intelligence, a letter-channel, or a particular geometric line depending on the author.

### VI. Knorr von Rosenroth, *Kabbala Denudata*, 1677–84

Knorr’s two-volume Latin anthology made extensive Zoharic and Lurianic materials available to Christian Europe.

[Volume scan](https://archive.org/download/bub_gb_QzBBAAAAcAAJ/bub_gb_QzBBAAAAcAAJ.pdf), [e-rara record](https://www.e-rara.ch/zut/content/titleinfo/10759091)

Its vocabulary includes:

- **Chesed seu Gratia** — Chesed or Grace;
- Gedulah as greatness/magnificence;
- mercy, benignity, and love as contextual renderings;
- the right arm and the relation of mercy to severity.

**[Historical importance]** Knorr transmitted far more authentic Jewish material than a simple occult chart suggests. At the same time, selection, Latin Christian vocabulary, and later English condensation changed how the system was read.

The section *De decem numerationibus impuris* was an important conduit for later Western Qliphothic lists. Mathers’s *Kabbalah Unveiled* is an English adaptation from selected Knorr material rather than a translation of all *Kabbala Denudata*. [Mathers scan](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1912__mathers___kabbala_denudata.pdf)

### VII. Éliphas Lévi and the Tarot

**[Disputed popular claim]** Lévi is often called the first person to correlate the twenty-two Tarot trumps with the twenty-two Hebrew letters. Earlier writers in Court de Gébelin’s circle—notably the Comte de Mellet—had already proposed Hebrew-letter correspondences.

**[Historical role]** Lévi nevertheless made the Tarot-alphabet synthesis central to nineteenth-century occultism. In *Dogme et rituel de la haute magie* (1854–56) and *La clef des grands mystères* (1861), he treated the Tarot as a hieroglyphic key to Kabbalah.

[French *Dogme et rituel* scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dogme_et_rituel_de_la_haute_magie_%28IA_b24884340_0001%29.pdf), [National Library of Israel history](https://blog.nli.org.il/en/tarot_kabbalah/)

Lévi’s exact letter-card order is not the Golden Dawn’s. The Golden Dawn later placed the Fool at Aleph and regularized astrological attributions.

For Chesed itself, the critical point remains: **Tarot trumps belong to paths; the four numbered Fours belong to the fourth sefirah.**

### VIII. The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn

The Golden Dawn synthesized Kircher’s topology, Renaissance angelology, astrology, Tarot, alchemy, Enochian material, and Masonic-style grades.

#### Fifth Knowledge Lecture

The fifth Knowledge Lecture gives the familiar hierarchy:

- sphere of operation: Tzedek, Jupiter;
- divine name: El;
- archangel: Tzadkiel;
- angels: Chashmalim;
- symbolic quality: royal mercy and expansive order.

[Regardie, *The Complete Golden Dawn*, fifth Knowledge Lecture](https://s3.us-west-1.wasabisys.com/luminist/EB/R/Regardie%20-%20The%20Complete%20Golden%20Dawn.pdf)

This is the principal source of the modern compact formula:

> El → Tzadkiel → Chashmalim → Tzedek/Jupiter.

The levels are commonly explained as:

| World | Chesed level |
|---|---|
| Atziluth | El |
| Briah | Tzadkiel |
| Yetzirah | Chashmalim |
| Assiah | Tzedek/Jupiter |

**Caution:** Golden Dawn and Crowley tables sometimes distribute names differently in specialized columns. A heading such as “God-name in Assiah” in a later transcription should not erase the standard pedagogical four-world chain.

#### *Book T*: the four Fours

| Suit | Golden Dawn title | Astrology |
|---|---|---|
| Four of Wands | Lord of Perfected Work/Completion | Venus in Aries |
| Four of Cups | Lord of Blended Pleasure/Luxury | Moon in Cancer |
| Four of Swords | Lord of Rest from Strife/Truce | Jupiter in Libra |
| Four of Pentacles | Lord of Earthly Power/Power | Sun in Capricorn |

[Golden Dawn *Book T* text](https://zanotowane.pl/1123/8041/)

The four cards express stability, consolidation, possession, and the risk that stable form becomes stagnation.

#### Colour scales

| World/scale | Colour |
|---|---|
| Atziluth / King | Deep violet |
| Briah / Queen | Blue |
| Yetzirah / Emperor | Deep purple |
| Assiah / Empress | Deep azure flecked yellow |

These became the basis for coloured Golden Dawn Trees and later ritual implements.

#### Grade

**[Hermetic attribution]** Chesed corresponds to **Adeptus Exemptus, 7°=4□**. In Golden Dawn-derived narratives, this is the accomplished adept, lawgiver, organizer, or master of the manifested order below the Abyss.

In Crowley’s A∴A∴, the Adeptus Exemptus is expected to formulate an understanding of the universe and a proposal for its welfare, then surrender the attained self at the Abyss. [Crowley grade material in *The Equinox*](https://hermetic.com/crowley/equinox/i/ii/eqi02020c)

This grade doctrine is Western initiatory symbolism, not a Jewish rank.

### IX. Westcott’s “Fourth Path”

Westcott’s translation reads:

> “The Fourth Path is named Measuring, Cohesive, or Receptacular.”

It says that this intelligence contains the holy powers and communicates spiritual virtues derived from the primordial emanation. [Westcott, “The Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom”](https://hermetics.net/media-library/kabbalah/william-wynn-westcott-the-thirty-two-paths-of-wisdom/)

The 1893 *Sepher Yetzirah* edition prints it as an appendix. [Westcott scan](https://iapsop.com/ssoc/1893__westcott___sepher_yetzirah.pdf)

**Source-critical conclusion:** calling this “the *Sefer Yetzirah*’s description of Chesed” is conventional Golden Dawn language. More precisely, it is the fourth item of a later thirty-two-intelligences list associated with the *Yetzirah* in early modern Christian transmission.

This controversy does **not directly affect Chesed**, because neither the Heh nor Tzaddi path is incident to Chesed in the standard Kircher/Golden Dawn topology.

### XI. Dion Fortune, *The Mystical Qabalah*, 1935

Chapter XVIII is entirely devoted to Chesed. Because the chapter remains copyrighted in many jurisdictions, what follows is a comprehensive synopsis rather than a reproduction.

[Chapter XVIII scan](https://gnosticwaves.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/the-mystical-qabalah_dion-fortune_chesed.pdf)

#### Fortune’s full argument in outline

1. Chesed is the first sphere below the Abyss and the first aspect of the “manifested” Lesser Countenance.
2. It is balanced by Geburah.
3. Chesed is anabolic, constructive, organizing, and preservative; Geburah is catabolic, corrective, and destructive.
4. Both are kings: Chesed enthroned in peace, Geburah riding in war.
5. Chesed converts an abstract archetypal idea into an organized plan capable of later embodiment.
6. The adept of Chesed sees causal patterns behind astral images rather than becoming hallucinated by them.
7. The sphere is associated with “Masters” who translate high archetypal ideas into forms useful to humanity.
8. Adeptus Exemptus belongs here.
9. Jupiter signifies benign authority, law, stable government, magnanimity, and ordered social life.
10. The virtue is obedience—not servility, but disciplined participation in lawful order.
11. Its vices are bigotry, hypocrisy, gluttony, and tyranny: the corruptions of orthodoxy, corporate belonging, abundance, and authority.
12. The crowned king bears orb and sceptre.
13. Four produces the first solid, conventionally symbolized by tetrahedron or pyramid.
14. The equal-armed cross represents fourfold equilibrium.
15. The Four of Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles express different forms of stabilization and possession.
16. Chesed’s spiritual experience is the Vision of Love.
17. Chesed without Geburah would become indulgence, inertia, hypertrophy, or tyranny concealed as benevolence.
18. Geburah without Chesed would become sterile violence.

Fortune’s correspondence heading gives:

- El;
- Tzadkiel;
- Chashmalim;
- Jupiter;
- Vision of Love;
- obedience;
- the four characteristic vices;
- tetrahedron, pyramid, cross, orb, wand, sceptre, crook;
- the four Fours;
- the four colour scales.

#### The arm discrepancy

Fortune explicitly gives the **left arm**, reasoning that it holds the orb while the right hand wields Geburah’s sword. Jewish/Zoharic lists give **Chesed as the right arm** and Gevurah as the left.

This is not a translation problem. It is a real divergence between her occult microcosm and classical Jewish body symbolism.

#### Historical-context caution

Fortune’s praise of strong, ordered leadership reflects the interwar period and should not be treated as a timeless political teaching of Jewish Kabbalah. Her biological language of anabolism and catabolism is analogical occult psychology, not scientific proof of the sefirot.

### XII. Regardie, Case, Knight, and later interpretation

#### Israel Regardie

Regardie’s great historical role was to publish and organize Golden Dawn materials. *The Complete Golden Dawn* preserves the Knowledge Lectures, Qliphoth papers, grade structure, colours, and Tarot material that establish Chesed’s modern Hermetic profile.

He also psychologized the Tree in *A Garden of Pomegranates* and treated Jupiterian Chesed as magnanimous authority, order, synthesis, and expansion.

#### Paul Foster Case

Case retained the Kircher/Golden Dawn topology and much of its correspondence system while developing Builders of the Adytum. His Rosicrucian grade discussions associate Exempt Adept with Chesed’s mastery of ordered manifestation. [Case, *The True and Invisible Rosicrucian Order*](https://books.google.com/books?id=M5-G3QbtAp8C)

Case rejected Crowley’s Heh/Tzaddi revision and retained the Golden Dawn letter/trump assignments.

#### Gareth Knight

Knight’s *Practical Guide to Qabalistic Symbolism*, first published in the 1960s, treats Chesed as the level where form conceived in Binah becomes a coherent organizing pattern. He emphasizes social order, spiritual authority, and the balance of mercy and severity. [Book record](https://books.google.com/books?id=Tw-B_Hzcp9MC)

#### Pathworking

Common modern pathworking practices include:

- visualization of Chesed’s blue or violet sphere;
- invocation of El and Tzadkiel;
- contemplation of a crowned king holding orb and sceptre;
- use of Jupiter’s day and hour;
- Tarot meditation on the four Fours or the Wheel;
- ethical reflection on generosity bounded by justice;
- visionary ascent through Tiferet–Chesed or Netzach–Chesed.

These are modern Hermetic ritual methods. They are not described in the *Bahir*, *Zohar*, or *Etz Chaim* in this form.

## The Qliphah and Kenneth Grant’s tunnels

### 1. Jewish qelippot

Hebrew קְלִיפּוֹת, *qelippot*, means shells, husks, or rinds.

**[Jewish tradition]** Zoharic and Lurianic sources describe the *sitra aḥra*, “Other Side,” impure husks, mixed good and evil, and forces nourished by misdirected holiness.

In Lurianic cosmology, qelippot are bound to the shattering, fallen sparks, and the failure of vessels. Human action can feed them or release holiness from them.

**[Historical caution]** This is not yet necessarily a neat upside-down diagram with one standardized demon order and prince for each sefirah. Jewish literature contains several overlapping qelippah schemes, including imagery from Ezekiel’s storm wind, cloud, fire, and *nogah*.

### 2. Western tenfold Qliphoth

Knorr’s transmission and the Golden Dawn organized qliphothic material into an inverse tenfold hierarchy.

| Sefirah | Golden Dawn/later Qliphah | Common translation |
|---|---|---|
| Keter | Thaumiel/Thamiel | Bicephalous or contending powers |
| Ḥokhmah | Ghagiel/Zogiel/Oghiel | Hinderers or material appearances |
| Binah | Satariel/Sathariel | Concealers |
| **Chesed** | **Gagh Shekelah / Agshekeloh / Gha’agsheblah** | Disturbing Ones, Breakers in Pieces, Smiters |
| Gevurah | Golachab/Golahab | Burners with fire |
| Tiferet | Thagirion/Zamiel | Disputers or ugly/false beauty |
| Netzach | Aʿarab Zaraq/Ghoreb Zereq | Ravens of dispersion |
| Hod | Samael | Deceivers or poison of God |
| Yesod | Gamaliel | Obscene ones |
| Malkhut | Lilith/Nahemoth | Night spectre; whisperers/material shells |

The supplied list varies across Mathers, Crowley, Grant, and later Left-Hand-Path authors. A spelling difference may reflect manuscript corruption, typesetting, Hebrew back-transliteration, or deliberate reinterpretation.

### 3. Gha’agsheblah

The Golden Dawn paper says:

- Chesed belongs to the second infernal palace;
- its order is **Gagh Shekelah**, “the Disturbing Ones”;
- their symbolic forms are black cat-headed giants;
- alternative names include Aziel, Charariel, and Agniel.

[Golden Dawn “The Qliphoth”](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Qliphoth.html)

Regardie prints **Agshekeloh**, “Breakers in Pieces.” Crowley prints **Gha’agsheklah**, “Smiters.” The now-common **Gha’agsheblah** and **Gamchicoth** spellings belong to later transmission.

**Interpretive theme:** the shadow of generosity is uncontrolled excess—growth that consumes its beneficiaries, authority that becomes paternalistic tyranny, wealth that devours, or mercy that refuses necessary boundaries. This is a modern symbolic interpretation built from the Chesed–Gevurah polarity; it is not a literal translation of a securely reconstructed Hebrew phrase.

### 4. Astaroth

The Golden Dawn list assigns:

> Chesed — Ashtaroth

but immediately warns that these chiefs are attributed only “according to the opinion of some” and cannot be confined to one sphere. [Golden Dawn Qliphoth paper](https://www.tarrdaniel.com/documents/Thelemagick/gd/publication/english/Qliphoth.html)

Thus:

- **Astaroth as Chesed’s demon prince is documented Golden Dawn material**;
- it is not a teaching of the *Sefer Yetzirah*;
- it is not securely attributable to the classical *Zohar*;
- the Golden Dawn itself presents the allocation as uncertain.

The later name Gamchicoth, “Devourers,” made Astaroth especially attractive to modern practitioners, but this tidy interpretation is later than the primary Golden Dawn spelling.

### 5. Kenneth Grant’s Nightside

Kenneth Grant’s *Nightside of Eden* (1977) turns the reverse sides of the twenty-two paths into “Tunnels of Set.” [Text scan](https://www.hempenings.com/Esoteric/Nightside-of-Eden-KennethGrant_text.pdf)

There is no single “tunnel of Chesed,” because tunnels connect qliphothic spheres just as paths connect sefirot.

A principal Chesed-adjacent tunnel is:

- **Uriens**, reverse of Vav/Hierophant, joining the shadow of Ḥokhmah to Gha’agsheblah.

Other tunnels touch Gha’agsheblah according to the reversed Teth, Yod, and Kaph paths. Grant’s sigils, sexual-magical readings, Setian language, and dreamlike entities are twentieth-century Typhonian developments, not recoveries of ancient Jewish practice. [Tunnel index](https://www.qliphoth-archive.com/en/tunnels.html)

### Ari and Gra comparison

No source-critical one-line table can honestly say “Vav joins X on the Ari Tree” without first specifying which printed diagram and interpretive school is meant. The Ari’s teachings concern partzufim, worlds, and channels; the Gra’s letter diagram follows his recension of *Sefer Yetzirah*. Their letters and channels are not homologous with the Kircher–Golden Dawn ordinal paths.

**Finding:** the fixed set “16 Vav, 19 Teth, 20 Yod, 21 Kaph” is specifically the Kircher/Golden Dawn convention.

1. Medieval Jewish manuscript diagrams: late-thirteenth century onward, varied in topology.
2. *Portae Lucis*, 1516: famous early printed Tree.
3. Posthumous *Pardes Rimmonim* diagrams: multiple and not entirely consistent.

6. Gra trees: alternative Jewish letter/channel arrangements.
7. Golden Dawn coloured Trees: Kircher-derived topology with four colour scales and astrological/Tarot letters.
8. Crowley–Harris diagrams: Thelemic revisions and colour symbolism.

## Controversies and disputes

### 1. Dating *Sefer Yetzirah*

- **Traditional:** Abrahamic authorship.
- **Older scholarship:** often late antique, sometimes second–sixth century.
- **Current cautious reconstruction:** composite formation is possible, but firm manuscript and citation evidence becomes clearer near the early tenth century.
- **Consequence for Chesed:** the earliest text has ten sefirot but does not name the fourth Chesed.

### 2. Were the *Yetzirah*’s sefirot already the later sefirot?

The Hebrew *sefirah* can suggest counting, enumeration, or numerical dimension. The later emanational powers are a historical transformation of this vocabulary.

Calling the fourth *Yetzirah* sefirah “Chesed” is:

- normal in later commentary;
- misleading if presented as the original wording.

### 3. Authorship of the *Zohar*

- **Jewish tradition:** Rabbi Shimon bar Yoḥai and his circle.
- **Scholem’s reconstruction:** Moses de León was the central medieval author.
- **More recent reconstruction:** a late-thirteenth-century Castilian Zoharic circle, with multiple strata and later additions.
- **Open issue:** how much older material survives inside the medieval literary form.

### 4. Christian appropriation

Pico’s Chesed material is inseparable from claims that Kabbalah proves Christ. Kircher likewise places Jewish material inside a Christian universal history and at times uses hostile anti-rabbinic language.

The history is double:

- translations preserved and disseminated important Jewish sources;
- Christian Cabalists regularly removed them from Jewish law, language, and community;
- they made Jewish symbols bear Trinitarian and Christological meanings rejected by Judaism;
- later occultists often presented those Christian transformations as “ancient Hebrew wisdom.”

### 5. Kircher versus Jewish trees

The Kircher/Golden Dawn Tree is historically real and internally coherent as a Western occult system. It is not the universal Jewish diagram.

Differences include:

- topology;
- order and meaning of channels;
- letter placement;
- planetary allocation;
- treatment of Daʿat;
- use of Tarot;
- use of path grades and ritual initiations.

The dispute is not resolved by declaring one “correct” without specifying the textual community and purpose.

### 6. Tarot attribution

No ancient Jewish Kabbalah contains Tarot.

- Tarot cards developed in fifteenth-century Europe.
- Hebrew-letter theories arose in eighteenth-century French occultism.
- Lévi popularized them.
- The Golden Dawn revised the order.
- Crowley revised Heh/Tzaddi.
- Case and most Golden Dawn descendants rejected Crowley’s swap.

For Chesed, the stable distinction is:

- sphere = four Fours;
- Kaph path = Wheel of Fortune;
- Chesed does not itself equal the Wheel.

### 7. El, Tzadkiel, Chashmalim, Jupiter

These four correspondences do not first appear together in one ancient Jewish list.

- **El:** strong medieval Jewish authority in Gikatilla.
- **Jupiter:** explicitly fourth in Pico’s own 1486 thesis.
- **Zadkiel/Jupiter:** consolidated in Renaissance angelology, especially Agrippa.
- **Chashmalim:** biblical term, later transformed into a fixed angelic order.
- **Complete bundle:** Golden Dawn standardization.

### 8. Right arm versus left arm

Jewish Kabbalah says Chesed is the right arm. Fortune says left.

Possible causes include:

- viewing the Tree frontally;
- identifying diagrammatic left with the operator’s left;
- ritual mirroring;
- Fortune’s desire to place the orb in a passive hand and the Geburah sword in the active hand.

The sources do not support erasing the contradiction.

### 9. Qliphothic spellings and Astaroth

Gagh Shekelah, Agshekeloh, Gha’agsheklah, Gha’agsheblah, and Gamchicoth are not transparently identical Hebrew forms. Translations such as “Smiters,” “Disturbers,” “Breakers,” and “Devourers” reflect different recensions and reinterpretations.

Astaroth is documented in a Golden Dawn list, but that source itself says the assignment is uncertain and that the chiefs extend across many orders.

### 10. Daʿat

*Sefer Yetzirah*’s “ten, not eleven” is foundational.

In many Jewish systems:

- Keter is counted and Daʿat is not;
- or Daʿat is counted as the manifest integration of Ḥokhmah and Binah when Keter is left beyond the count.

Daʿat is therefore not ordinarily an independent eleventh sefirah.

Golden Dawn and Fortune diagrams sometimes show it as an “invisible sefirah” in the Abyss. This is a legitimate Hermetic convention but should not be retrojected as the uncontested Jewish position. [Chabad discussion of the two systems](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/137112/jewish/Two-Systems-of-Ten-Sefirot.htm)

### 11. “Classical lists”

There is no single timeless “classical list” containing divine name, archangel, angelic choir, planet, colour, vice, virtue, Tarot, perfume, animal, stone, and grade. The modern table is a stratified archive:

- Bible;
- medieval Kabbalah;
- Renaissance Christian Cabala;
- planetary magic;
- Kircher;
- Knorr;
- Golden Dawn;
- Crowley;
- Fortune;
- later comparative occultism.

## Open questions

1. What is the precise manuscript history of the numbered *Thirty-Two Paths of Wisdom* appendix before Rittangelius?
2. Which Hebrew text stands behind Kircher’s corrupt or uncertain *intelligentia metalis*?
3. How should the varying *Pardes Rimmonim* and *Or Neʿerav* channel descriptions be reconciled with their printed diagrams?
4. Which portions of the Bahiric Abraham–Chesed complex preserve older Jewish motifs?
5. How many authors and redactional strata produced the principal Zoharic discussions of Chesed?
6. Which Jewish angelological sources directly preceded Agrippa’s form Zadkiel/Zedekiel?
7. When did Chashmalim first become a fixed choir of the fourth sefirah rather than visionary beings or luminous phenomena?
8. What exact Hebrew form produced the Golden Dawn’s **Gagh Shekelah**?
9. Was the shift from “Disturbers/Breakers” to “Devourers/Gamchicoth” textual, phonetic, or interpretive?
10. What manuscript or intermediary stands behind the Golden Dawn’s Ashtaroth–Chesed chief list?
11. Did Fortune deliberately reverse the classical bodily attribution, or inherit a now-unidentified occult table?
12. Which “Ari Tree” should be used in any proposed line-by-line comparison: Vital’s circles, the yosher figure, a partzuf diagram, or a later printed school diagram?
13. Can all of Crowley’s comparative row-4 equations be historically sourced, or were some intuitive analogies?
14. How much of modern Chesed pathworking derives from original Golden Dawn manuscripts versus post-Regardie reconstruction?
15. When “Jupiter” appears in a Jewish kabbalistic work, is it an indigenous astrological correspondence, a Christian-Cabalist reimportation, or a later comparative gloss? Each occurrence must be dated separately.
